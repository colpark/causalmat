# Judge review: Acta_Materialia/10.1016_j.actamat.2021.116790

**Verdict:** accept with fixes. This is a structure-only review. No figures or crops were opened, and no panel citation or technique was ruled on; every cited panel is checked separately by the blind second read.

After the fixes the graph has 48 nodes, 67 edges and 20 spine nodes.

## 1. Spine

The spine is one connected, stage-ordered path from n1 to d1:

**n1** (gap: surface composition and structure of the Cantor alloy undetermined) -> **n2** (hypothesis: repeated annealing could decompose the single-phase solid solution) -> **n3** (one sample exposing a (110) and a (320) grain) and **n4** (comparison axes: orientation, temperature) -> **n5** (sputter-and-anneal cycles, 673-1000 K). From n5 the argument runs in two branches plus one control branch, as PROTOCOL.md allows:

- **surface chemistry:** n6 (Ni/Mn-enriched topmost layers) -> m1 (co-segregation in equal amounts, a surface not a grain-boundary process) -> p1; and n7 (Mn collapses above ~873 K) -> m2 (desorption, not bulk diffusion) -> d1.
- **surface structure:** n9 ((110) rough and anisotropic, ~1.2 A steps) -> n10 ((110) locally (2x1), disordered along [-110]); n11 ((320) unreconstructed (1x1), better ordered) -> m3 (facet succession corrugates the surface) -> n15 (~6.5 A rows on the terraces); n12 ((320) terrace-and-step, 0.5 and 1.0 A steps). n10 -contrasts-> n11 is what makes this one comparison rather than two lines. n9 and n12 cause p2.
- **bulk control:** n13 (single fcc phase, no precipitates) and n14 (homogeneous within 2-4 at.%), which answer the decomposition hypothesis n2 and are tied to the surface line by n13 -contrasts-> n6.

p1 and p2 both support d1. There is no PRF node: the packet (captions plus linked text, no introduction or methods) contains no use or test condition, and none was invented; see the MatMech tally below.

Every spine STR/PRP/PRF/MEC claim has incoming OBS evidence or a KNW premise: n6 (o1, o4), n7 (o2), m1 (o5), m2 (o25, o2), n9 (o8, o11, o13, o14 + k1), n10 (o12, o15), n11 (o17), m3 (o20 + k2), n12 (o18, o19 + k1), n15 (o20), n13 (o22 + k1), n14 (o24), p2 (o8); p1 is `basis: argued` and is reached from m1, n6, n13 and n14. OBS and KNW are never spine. Audits are 3 (a1, d2, d3), under the budget of 5, and each one bounds a claim: a1 (bulk EDS Mn depletion at the boundary) contradicts the surface-sensitive SAM reading behind n8; d2 bounds n6 (the annealing history the surface composition remembers); d3 bounds p1 (XPS and EDS Mn contents disagree).

v04 decision rules hold. The two surface-order claims are phase/identity (a surface crystal structure) while the two topography claims are microstructure/shape, which is the distinction v04 draws between "which structure is present" and "what it looks like". n8 is microstructure/distribution (where Mn is, i.e. segregation), not chemistry/composition. m2 is identification, not pathway, because desorption is picked out of a known catalogue by a decoupling experiment (the Si(111) witness, o25), which is exactly what r04 ruling #26 broadened the leaf to cover. Two calls I checked and left alone: o11 (the 64 eV (1x1) LEED mesh) evidences n9 rather than n10, which is defensible because the perpendicular a*, b* with |b*|/|a*| ~ sqrt(2) is the anisotropic (110) mesh behind the row topography, and n10 states the local (2x1) and the disorder, which o12 and o15 carry; and o14 and o19 keep `provenance: measured` although both histograms are "calculated from the STM image", since they reduce the same recorded height data rather than another OBS node's reading, which is how the rest of the batch types image-derived histograms (18 measured vs 5 derived).

## 2. Splits and merges

- **Split n12 -> n12 + n15.** The label stated two claims about the (320) surface that have different evidence and different causes. n12 keeps the terrace-and-step morphology with 0.5 and 1.0 A step heights (evidence o18, o19; the step heights follow from d(320) and d(320)/2, which is the k1 premise). New **n15** (STR/microstructure/shape, spine) carries the ~6.5 A rows running along [001] on those terraces (evidence o20; caused by the two-(110)-plus-one-(100) facet succession, which is m3 with the k2 model). The split was needed because m3 -explains-> n12 explained only half of the node it pointed at, while n12 -causes-> p2 uses only the other half (in p2 the (320) surface is the flat one). Edges: m3 -> n15 explains and o20 -> n15 evidences (measure_feature_metric) replace the edges into n12; n5 -> n12 produces is added so n12 keeps a spine parent, as n9 has for the (110) surface; n15 -> d1 supports is added. o20 -> m3 overlay_model_on_data (6.5 A read against the model's 6.48 A) is unchanged.
- **Not split: n9, n10, n11.** Each names two directions or two aspects, but in each case both halves rest on the same evidence and are used in the same place downstream, so splitting would change no inference. n10's (2x1) reconstruction and [-110] disorder are read from the same streaks (o12) and the same atomic image (o15), and both feed the contrast with n11; n9's roughness and its flat rows are one anisotropy claim; n11's "far better ordered" is the comparative that the n10 -contrasts-> n11 edge carries.
- **Merged: the second clause of n7 into d2.** "later preparations stay Mn-poor" was d2's claim word for word. d2 is the node that carries its evidence (o3, the two open Mn squares below the full-symbol line) and its qualifies edge into n6, while o2, the evidence on n7, reads only the collapse. n7 now states only "Above ~873 K the surface Mn content collapses"; nothing is lost.
- No other duplicate pairs. The readout/claim pairs (o1,o4/n6; o13,o14/n9; o18,o19/n12; o22/n13; o24/n14) are observations and the claims they evidence, not duplicates. m3 names the rows as the effect of the facet succession, which is what a MEC/pathway label does; n15 is the structural claim itself.

## 3. Source and read_from changes

- **o12: source figure -> text.** The label states the 30 eV beam energy, which is in the F3 caption only, and calls the diffuse intensity facet-related, which is in the F3 linked text only. F3 is tier C, so no panel of the packet shows either. Both are now in requires_unseen. o11, the other F3 LEED node, was already text for the same reason.
- **o20: read_from pixels -> annotation** (annotation_match "6.5A"). The node's metric is the string the packet lists for panel #F4c; only the "dark patches along the rows" half is a pixel reading. The panel is flagged annotated:false, but the rule keys on a string listed for the panel, and this one is.
- **m1: requires_unseen filled** (source stays inferred). m1's discriminating premise is that no Ni segregation is measured at the grain boundary (Figs S4/S5, in the F2 linked text, absent from the packet). Without it the graph's own n8 - the boundary IS Mn-enriched - points the other way.
- **p1: requires_unseen filled** (source stays inferred). The "~2 nm" in the label is the 2.2 nm sampling depth at 70 deg take-off, an F1 linked-text fact. It was listed on n6 but not on p1, which is the node that states it.
- **read_from kept as axis** on o1-o5, o14, o19: the readings are compositions, angles and step-height peaks taken against the plotted axes; the strings listed for #F1a/#F1b and #F4b are legend entries and axis titles, not the readings.
- **read_from kept as pixels** on o6, o7, o8, o10, o11, o12, o13, o15, o17, o18, o22, o24. - **read_from kept as text** on o25 and a1, the two text_only OBS with no panel in the packet; each already carries a read_from_note saying that none of pixels/annotation/axis applies.
- **source elsewhere:** every claim that needs an unseen fact is already text or inferred with the fact listed (grain orientations and STM/SAED field sizes from captions; sampling depths, the Si(111) witness experiment, the 2-4 at.% EELS/EDS variation and the boundary EDS composition from linked text). o2 is the closest call left standing: its "~873 K" is the text value, while the panel shows the break between the 870 K and 913 K points, and its image_note says exactly that.

## 5. MatMech tally (recorded after the graph was final; the graph was not edited for it)

Supports 1, contradicts 0, not covered 1.

- **M1 (Processing -> Structure): supports.** Cause "Ar+ ion sputtering and annealing cycles at various temperatures and durations" is n5; all three effects are on the spine and evidenced. Surface segregation of Mn and Ni: n6 with o1 (both rise with annealing temperature) and o4 (both rise with take-off angle), explained by m1 with o5 (the Ni-Mn difference stays at 14-15 at.%, i.e. equal amounts). Terraced morphology on (320): n12 with o18 and o19, and n15 with o20 for the rows. Anisotropic row-like structure on (110): n9 with o13, o14 and o8, and n10 with o12 and o15. MatMech's two knowledge premises for M1 (Mn has the lowest surface energy; the Mn-Ni mixing enthalpy is strongly negative) appear nowhere in the packet and are not in the graph, which is correct - they are not cause-effect pairs, and the linked text never states them.
- **M2 (Structure -> Performance): not covered.** The effect, "upper temperature limit for use in high vacuum and aerospace applications", has no counterpart in the graph, and should not have one: the packet is captions plus linked text with no introduction, and neither vacuum nor aerospace use is mentioned anywhere in it. What the graph does carry is the thermal ceiling itself - n7 (Mn collapses above ~873 K) -> m2 (desorption, evidenced by o25, the Si(111) witness) -> d1, with d2 recording the memory effect that follows an anneal above 873 K. Note also that the graph attributes that ceiling to Mn desorption, not to the terrace or row morphology that M2 names as its cause, so even the cause side of the pair is not the graph's link.
