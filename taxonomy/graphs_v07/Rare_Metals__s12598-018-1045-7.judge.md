# Judge review: Rare_Metals/s12598-018-1045-7

As-cast versus as-extruded Mg-4Zn-1La: microstructure, tensile properties and corrosion. This is a structure-only review. No figures or crops were opened, and no ruling was made on panel citations or techniques.

## Verdict
Accepted with fixes.

The spine runs:
- hypothesis n1 -> base system n2 and comparison axis n3;
- casting n4 -> as-cast GB network n5 -> homogenization n6 -> partial dissolution n7 -> extrusion n8;
- n8 -> grain refinement n9 (DRX n10 explains), ED-aligned stringers n11 and fragmented T-phase n11b;
- mechanical branch: n9 and n11b -> strength/ductility gain n13 (Hall-Petch n14 explains);
- corrosion branch: n11 -> lower cathodic kinetics n19 -> lower corrosion rate n18;
- n13 and n18 -> combined gain n23 -> conclusion n24.

There are 18 spine nodes, all connected from n1, and the path is stage ordered, with two branches (mechanical, corrosion). Every spine STR/PRP/PRF/MEC node has OBS evidence or a KNW premise: n14 rests on k2 (basis argued), and n10 has o7 plus k7. There are two audits, and both are fair: o9 (as-cast grains look larger than the stated 50 um) qualifies n9, and n26 (Fe above tolerance, Ni/Cu neglected) qualifies n25b. The graph now has 56 nodes and 75 edges.

## Splits and merges
- **n11 -> n11, n11b.**
  - n11: the stringers aligned along ED replace the GB network (distribution).
  - n11b: the undissolved coarse T-phase is broken into fine particles (feature_size).
  - The strength edges (causes -> n13, supports -> n15) moved to n11b. The corrosion edges (-> n19, -> n20) and the contrast with n5 stay on n11.
  - n8 produces both, and n7 causes both. o10 evidences both: assess_spatial_distribution -> n11, and inspect_local_feature -> n11b.
- **s2 -> s2, s2b.** s2 is the Zn/La-rich second phases with no Mg. s2b is the matrix, which holds Zn and ~0.3 wt% La. o5 evidences both.
- **k1 -> k1, k1b.** k1 is the precedent ~25/~52 deg peaks. k1b is a KNW/fact: Ce behaves like La. k1b is a premise for s1 and n16.
- **n25 -> n25, n25b.**
  - n25 is the comparison with ZE41.
  - n25b is a MEC/pathway: the Fe/Zn content differences explain the rate (argued).
  - New premise k8 (refs [4,32,33]). n26 now qualifies n25b.
- **o15** label trimmed to the cathodic/anodic reading. Ecorr moved to image_note.
- **Merges: none.** o1/o3 and o2/o4 are OM versus SEM readings of the same states, with distinct modalities.
- **n13 kept whole.** It is one tensile comparison, and splitting strength from ductility would leave a ductility node with no stated cause.
