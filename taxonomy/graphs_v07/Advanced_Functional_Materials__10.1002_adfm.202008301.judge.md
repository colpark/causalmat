## 1. Spine order

The spine reads HYP -> DES -> PRC -> STR -> PRP -> PRF -> DSC with MEC bridging, in 19 connected nodes:

- need n1 (Ni-rich NCM cycled to 4.5 V loses capacity and voltage at the interface) -> hypothesis n2 (a preformed P-based passivation interface should suppress it);
- three DES choices follow n2 and converge on one act: base system n3, the PH3 route n4 and the mass-ratio sweep n5, all feeding the conversion step n6;
- n6 produces the structure claim that carries the argument, the 1-2 nm amorphous LiTMPO4 interface n7, and feeds the cycling act n10;
- transport branch: n7 causes the 0.11 eV migration descriptor n8, which causes the rate capability n16 (jointly with the metallic-interface claim n21);
- protection branch: n7 supports the artificial-CEI bridge n9, which with n10 explains the two post-mortem structure claims n11 (fewer intergranular cracks) and n12 (R-3m survives to the surface) and the new n25 (clean surface, no thick film); n11 and n25 jointly cause the stable interfacial resistance n13 -> capacity retention n15; n12 causes the maintained phase transitions n14 -> voltage retention n17;
- trade-off branch: n7 supports n18, counteracted by the first-charge transport penalty n23; n18 supports the conclusion and the guidance n24;
- n15, n16, n17, n18 and the bulk-unchanged claim n22 support the conclusion n19.

n1 is the only spine node without an incoming spine edge and n19 the only one without an outgoing edge. Two parallel branches (interfacial transport; surface protection, itself forking at n11/n12) plus the thickness trade-off branch, as the graph's own notes state.

**Evidence.** Every spine STR/PRP/PRF/MEC claim is backed: n7 by o1, o2, o3, o4 and o8; n8 by o15 with the DFT premise k3; n9 by o19; n11 by o20; n12 by o21 with k5, qualified by o22; n13 by o23; n14 by o17 with k4; n15 by o9; n16 by o10; n17 by o18; n18 by o1 and o11. The off-spine claims are backed too: n20 by o6 with k1, n21 by o16 with k2, n22 by o5 and o7, n23 by o14, n25 by o19, n26 by o12 and o13.

**v04 rules** hold. n7 and n25 are STR/interface, not microstructure/distribution: both claims are the contact state of a reaction layer on the NCM surface, which is the r04 rule for that pair. n11 stays STR/microstructure/damage (cycling-opened intergranular cracks are loss of continuity, not crystallographic defects). n14 is STR/phase/fraction with `attrs.proxy: dQ/dV peak intensity`, which is what the leaf is for (degree of transformation and its evolution, proxy named in attrs), not phase/identity: n12 already carries the which-phase reading. n15, n16 and n17 are PRF/service_capability and not qualification or FOM: the paper applies no external threshold, spec or benchmark, only its own use conditions. n13 and n26 are PRP/value with `property_family: ionic_electrochemical` for the same reason. n8 is PRP/descriptor, not diagnostic: no KNW/lookup -> MEC/identification chain follows it; it feeds the rate claim directly. o5 and o7 are OBS/signal/reference_match with `match_to_reference` (empirical references, ruling #12), while o15 and o16 read the computed panels and carry `provenance: computed`. o4 is `provenance: derived` and has its derives edge from o3.

**Audits:** 3 (o11, o13, o22), within the budget of 5 and each one fair, because each changes the support of a spine claim. o11 is text_silent: from 2.5 C upward NCM-3 falls below the untreated NCM-0, which the paper never states and which sharpens the trade-off n18. o13 is text_silent: at the first cycle the pristine cell has the *lowest* polarization of all four, so it qualifies n26 rather than supporting it. o22 is text_silent and cross-checks the linked text's claimed R-3m -> Fd-3m -> Fm-3m decay against the panel, where no Fd-3m FFT is labelled anywhere, and so qualifies n12. No other node carries a `qualifies`, `contrasts` or `rules_out` edge and there is no DSC/limitation, so the audit ring is exactly these three.

## 4. Mode changes
