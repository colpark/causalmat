# Judge review: Advanced_Materials/10.1002_adma.201702037

Vacancy-driven gelation of defect-rich MoS2 nanoassemblies with four-arm PEG-SH. Judge: senior investigator (v07 part B, staff B6).

## Verdict

**Accept with minor fixes.** The spine reads as the paper's argument. It has 15 nodes, connected from HYP to DSC/conclusion: the defect gap and hypothesis lead to the Mo:S and MoS2-content sweeps, then synthesis and gel mixing, then vacancy density (s3), then C-S-Mo crosslinks (s4), then the chemisorption mechanism (m1). From m1 there are two branches: gelation (r1 -> f1 in medium -> f2 cell encapsulation) and stiffness (r2). Every spine STR/PRP/PRF claim and m1 has OBS evidence. All types, rels, ops and modalities are v04-valid, and the mm_ops fit the acts. There are 3 audits (o11 overall Raman attenuation, o14 Mo 3d components that are not spin-orbit doublets, o19 no 3 kPa plateau in F2d). All three are fair, and all bear on spine claims s4 and r1. There are 63 nodes in total, above the 25-40 aim. The side ring was not pruned because every node in it is valid and correctly cited.
