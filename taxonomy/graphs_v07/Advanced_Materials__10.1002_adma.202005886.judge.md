# Judge review: Advanced_Materials/10.1002_adma.202005886

**Verdict:** accept with fixes. This is a structure-only review. No figure or crop was opened, and panel citations and techniques were not ruled on.

After the fixes the graph has 44 nodes, 60 edges and 18 spine nodes. The spine is one connected, stage-ordered path with a single root (n1): need for a microscope-proof label -> hypothesis of selective fs-laser doping -> Er/Si bilayer base system and the fluence/separation sweep -> deposition -> laser writing -> HCl etching -> fake-spot writing -> crystalline silicon (n9) and crater geometry (n12) -> the heating/in-diffusion pathway m1 -> erbium incorporated (n10) -> the oxidation pathway m2 -> PL at 1530 nm (n13) and the etch-resistant emission (n14) -> the 6.6 mJ/cm2, 2.5 um down-select (n16) -> the two-level label read-out (n17) -> conclusion (n18). Two side branches (crater geometry n12, etch selectivity n14) and one control-style line (the fake spots, n8 -> n22) converge on n17, inside the branch budget.

Every spine STR/PRP/PRF/MEC claim now has OBS evidence or a KNW premise; m1 had neither before this review. The v04 decision rules hold: n9 keeps phase/identity with structure-sensitive Raman evidence while the new n21 takes the grain-size reading (HOW BIG -> feature_size), n11 stays chemistry/bonding for the Er3+ surrounding against n10's composition claim (WHAT is in the matrix), o8/o9/o11 stay morphology/distribution (WHERE the signal is) against o7's appearance, and o6 stays response/fitted_parameter because the thresholds come from the D^2-vs-log-E fit (k3). Audits are 2 (o16, the incompatible F2c/F3 fluence scales, text_silent; n20, the diffraction limit on pixel density), both fair and well inside the budget of 5. The node count, 44, is above the 25-40 aim; I did not prune, because the excess comes from the three splits below and the paper carries 15 distinct readouts over 4 figures.

## Splits and merges
- **n9 -> n9 + n21.** The label joined two claims with a semicolon. n9 keeps the phase identity (irradiated zones contain crystalline silicon grains: o1, o2, o8, with the k1 lookup). New **n21** (STR/microstructure/feature_size, spine false, source text) carries the grain-size reading of the 518 vs 520 cm-1 deviation, which the F2 linked text attributes to the finite small size of the grains; no panel shows a grain size, so it is a text claim with the fact listed. Edges: n9 supports n21, k1 premise_for n21.
- **n12 -> n12 + n23.** A size claim and a form claim in one label, separately evidenced and separately used. n12 keeps the lower id and the dimensions (about 0.5 um wide, 95 nm deep; o14 measure_feature_metric, o6 fit_model, o7) and becomes feature_size. New **n23** (STR/microstructure/shape, spine false) carries the rimmed donut form with the emptied centre; it is what the evaporation pathway m3 accounts for, so the n12 -> m3 `supports` edge moved to n23, and n6 produces n23, o7 evidences n23 were added.
- **n14 -> n14 + n22.** "Luminescent spots keep their emission after etching" and "spots written afterwards give none" are two claims about two objects with two different causes: HCl leaves incorporated Er untouched (n7), while a spot written after etching has no erbium left to incorporate (n8). n14 keeps the first; new **n22** (PRP/value, optical, spine false) takes the second, with n8 causes n22, o12 evidences n22 and n22 supports n17.
- **Merges: none.** The candidates were checked and rejected: o3 (F2c PL-vs-fluence curve) and o4 (F2b spectra) read different panel kinds of the same rise, so v04 keeps them apart; o2 and o16 differ in claim (a trend vs the cross-figure inconsistency, which is the audit); o8, o9 and o11 are three different maps; n13 (property claim) and o3 (readout) are different roles.
- **Not split:** m1 and m2. A MEC/pathway is by definition a chain (heating -> crystallisation -> in-diffusion; oxidation -> Er:O surrounding -> brighter emission); splitting a pathway into its steps would apply to every pathway node in the corpus. n4 keeps its two swept variables, which DES/variable_sweep explicitly allows ("the controlled variable(s)"). n17 keeps its two read-outs, because the dual read-out IS the single performance claim.
