- **p6 → F3a/F3b.** Same 25 µm bar in both. F3a's β grains run ~10–15 µm with a cleanly resolved boundary network; F3b's network is far denser at ~4–5 µm, and the α-Mg bands are more numerous and more strongly curved, as the image_note says.
- **p8 → F4a/F4b.** F4b shows one ~3 µm agglomerate of bright particles in the centre plus sub-micron particles on boundaries and inside grains, and carries no A or B markers although the caption places the two EDS points in it. Reading this as an agglomerate rather than the dispersion the text claims is correct, and `partial` is the right support level.

All 15 panel_ids used are present in the packet; none is invented.
