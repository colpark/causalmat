## Changes made

1. **v3** (XRD extra reflections, `#F2`). The 2θ list was wrong. Re-reading the axis, the starred La-only reflections are at ≈21, a group at 31/33/35/38 (only the ~36 one is strong), plus ≈50 and ≈67 — not "47 and 55". Label and `image_note` corrected; `image_support: partial` kept, since the 31–35 group sits barely above the noise floor at the printed 1000-count scale.
2. **a2** (Al₂Cu markers) — the overturned check. The node claimed the markers are drawn "only on the RE-free and Sm patterns". At magnification the two filled squares sit at ≈43° and ≈48° on the **Al-Cu-Mn-Sm trace only**; the RE-free trace carries no Al₂Cu marker at all. Label and `image_note` corrected. The audit is unaffected and in fact sharper: XRD marks Al₂Cu in one alloy of three while the F1d EDS identifies Al₂Cu inside the La alloy, so the Cu-consumption argument is not settled by this figure.
3. **Ruling, no change:** a2 keeps `figs: [F1, F2]` with `modality: diffraction_pattern`. The "split a node that reads two panel kinds" rule does not apply to `cross_check_consistency`, which is by definition a cross-figure act; the modality names the panel that carries the new content (F2).
