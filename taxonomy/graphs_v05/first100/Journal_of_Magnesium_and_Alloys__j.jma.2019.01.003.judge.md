## Spine

Mg alloys are too soft for sliding contact (d1) -> the route, not the reinforcement, decides the composite (d2) -> AZ31B, 10 vol.% fly ash, route as the comparison axis (d3-d5) -> stir casting and FSP as the two arms (d6, d7), plus the sliding test (d8) -> dispersion, interface state and grain size, each stating both arms (d9-d11) -> hardness (d13) -> wear rate (d14) and wear mode (d15), explained by d16 and d18 -> height loss under service conditions (d19) -> conclusion (d20). 18 spine nodes, one root. The two-arm device of putting both routes' states in one label keeps the contrast on the spine without doubling it, which is the right call here.
