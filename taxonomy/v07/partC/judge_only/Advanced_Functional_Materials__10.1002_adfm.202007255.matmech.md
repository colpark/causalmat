## Tetrahedron elements
- **Processing**: Coating of MnO particles on porous PP separator
- **Structure**: Formation of a dual-layer Li deposition with compactly arranged spheres at the bottom and sheet-like bulky Li on the top
- **Properties**: Coulombic efficiency–electrochemical property, dendrite-free deposition–mechanical property
- **Performance**: Improved cycling stability and capacity retention in Li||Li and Li||Cu cells
