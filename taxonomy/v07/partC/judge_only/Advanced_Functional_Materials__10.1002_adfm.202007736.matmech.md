### M3  Processing → Property
- cause: Presence of citrate during nucleation
- effect: Increased hydration state and altered oxygen-to-calcium ratio in CaOx crystals
- experiment: EDS and EELS chemical analysis | Energy Dispersive X-ray Spectroscopy (EDS) and Electron Energy Loss Spectroscopy (EELS) | params: Chemical characterization of ex situ CaOx samples synthesized with and without citrate | result: Citrate increases O:Ca ratio from ~5:1 in COM to ~6:1 in COD
  - [experimental result] EDS and EELS data show increased O:Ca ratio in presence of citrate, indicating formation of COD rather than COM.
  - [experimental result] MD simulations reveal higher water molecule to calcium ratio in clusters formed with citrate.
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Citrate forms tridentate coordination complexes with calcium, altering local hydration environment.
  - [referenced knowledge] Citrate reduces free calcium concentration by forming soluble complexes, favoring hydration-rich COD formation.
  - [deductive reasoning] Hence, citrate modulates the hydration state of CaOx by influencing both ion speciation and crystal chemistry.
