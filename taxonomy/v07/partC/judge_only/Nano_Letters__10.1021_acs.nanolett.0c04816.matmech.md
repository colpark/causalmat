### M2  Structure → Property
- cause: Partial dislocations and stacking faults induced by electric fields
- effect: Local conductance–electrical property
- experiment: Conductive Atomic Force Microscopy (cAFM) | Electrical transport measurements | params: Voltage applied (+45 V) during scanning after electric field treatment (−60 V for 5 s) | result: Ellipsoidal area of enhanced conductance with insulating core observed; cross-sectional correlation with SEM contrast confirms localized conductivity changes.
  - [experimental result] cAFM data show suppressed conductance directly below the AFM tip where dislocations are present.
  - [image description] Image shows localized reduction in conductance correlating with dislocation-induced lattice distortion.
  - [referenced knowledge] DFT calculations reveal bound negative charges at dislocations attract positively charged oxygen vacancies.
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Accumulation of oxygen vacancies near dislocations modifies Fermi energy and reduces available charge carriers.
  - [deductive reasoning] Thus, dislocation-induced charge trapping leads to local resistivity increase and lower conductance.
