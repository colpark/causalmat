### M1  Processing → Structure
- cause: Uniaxial compressive stress applied to ZnO nanoplatelets
- effect: c-axis preferred orientation of ZnO nanoplatelets
- experiment: X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis | XRD | params: Measurement before and after applying uniaxial pressure to ZnO nanoplatelet powder and disk | result: Enhanced (002) peak and suppressed (100), (101) peaks, indicating c-axis preferred orientation after compression
  - [experimental result] ZnO nanoplatelets have a hexahedral morphology with aspect ratio ~0.4, giving them shape anisotropy related to crystal orientation.
  - [image description] Figure shows enhanced (002) peak and suppressed other peaks in XRD after compression, indicating c-axis preferred orientation.
  - [referenced knowledge] Lotgering orientation factor confirms more than two-fold enhancement in c-axis orientation after compression.
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Piezoelectric nanocrystals can be aligned by uniaxial compressive stress due to shape anisotropy.
  - [deductive reasoning] Therefore, uniaxial compressive stress induces c-axis preferred orientation in ZnO nanoplatelets through torque-induced alignment.
### M2  Structure → Property
- cause: c-axis preferred orientation of ZnO nanoplatelets
- effect: Increased conductivity with decreasing stress at high pressure range
- experiment: Electrical transport measurements under pressure | I-V and I-P measurements | params: Pressure range from 163.5 to 483.1 MPa applied to ZnO bulk composed of c-axis oriented nanoplatelets | result: Conductivity increases with decreasing pressure from 450 to 200 MPa, then decreases below 200 MPa
  - [image description] Figure shows symmetrical Ohmic I–V characteristics with conductivity increasing as pressure decreases from 450 to 200 MPa.
  - [image description] Current exhibits linear relationship with pressure between 200–400 MPa, differentiating it from exponential regulation seen in single interface systems.
  - [referenced knowledge] Piezotronic effect involves strain-induced piezoelectric charges modulating carrier transport at interfaces.
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Statistical distribution of oriented nanocrystals leads to collective piezotronic effects across many interfaces.
  - [deductive reasoning] Thus, c-axis preferred orientation allows coordinated piezopotential generation across many interfaces, statistically modulating electron transport pathways through the bulk material.
