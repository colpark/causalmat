## Extracted mechanisms (MatMech)
### M1  Processing → Structure
- cause: Cyclic spark plasma sintering with liquid phase
- effect: BiSbTe alloy exhibits enlarged grains and abundant dislocations
- experiment: Electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) and scanning electron microscopy (SEM) | Microstructural analysis | params: Samples subjected to 1–5 SPS cycles | result: Grain size increases significantly after multiple cycles; large laminar structure forms after more than three cycles
  - [experimental result] EBSD and SEM results show significant grain growth after multiple SPS cycles
  - [image description] Figure 3a shows grain size increase from ~60 μm in sample 4C compared to smaller grains in 1C
  - [referenced knowledge] Liquid phase reduces edge free energy of grain boundaries, lowering critical driving force for grain growth
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Liquid-phase-aided sintering promotes mass transport and accelerates grain boundary migration
  - [deductive reasoning] Repeated heating/cooling cycles enhance dissolution-reprecipitation process, leading to preferential growth of larger grains
### M2  Structure → Property
- cause: Enlarged grains and abundant dislocations
- effect: Enhanced thermoelectric properties (higher power factor and lower lattice thermal conductivity)
- experiment: Hall effect measurements and Debye-Callaway model fitting | Electrical and thermal transport characterization | params: Samples with different numbers of SPS cycles | result: Carrier mobility increases with number of cycles; lattice thermal conductivity decreases due to increased dislocation density
  - [experimental result] Hall mobility measurements show increasing mobility with more SPS cycles
  - [image description] TEM images reveal high density of disordered dislocations in cycled samples
  - [referenced knowledge] Dislocations act as effective phonon scattering centers for mid-frequency phonons
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Reduced grain boundary scattering allows better charge transport while dislocations impede heat transfer
  - [deductive reasoning] Combined effects lead to simultaneous improvement in power factor and reduction in thermal conductivity
### M3  Property → Performance
- cause: Enhanced thermoelectric properties (higher ZT value)
- effect: Improved thermoelectric conversion efficiency
- experiment: Single-leg module efficiency measurement | Device performance testing | params: Samples with different processing conditions | result: Sample 4C achieves ≈5% efficiency, which is ≈56% higher than sample 1C and ≈85% higher than commercial BiSbTe
  - [experimental result] ZT value reaches ≈1.46 for optimized sample, representing ≈50% improvement over conventional processing
  - [image description] Efficiency measurement shows ≈5% conversion efficiency for best sample
  - [referenced knowledge] Higher ZT values directly correlate with improved device efficiency according to fundamental thermoelectric theory
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Simultaneous enhancement of power factor and reduction of thermal conductivity maximizes energy conversion efficiency
  - [deductive reasoning] Therefore, the improved ZT value from optimized processing translates directly into superior thermoelectric performance
