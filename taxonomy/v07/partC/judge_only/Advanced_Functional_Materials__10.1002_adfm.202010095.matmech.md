  - [deductive reasoning] Therefore, the textured microstructure prevents microcrack formation, maintaining electrode integrity and enhancing cycle life.
### M3  Structure → Property

  - [deductive reasoning] Therefore, the radially aligned microstructure improves electrochemical performance by enabling efficient Li+ transport and maintaining low resistance over cycles.
### M4  Processing → Performance
- cause: Phosphorous and boron doping
- effect: Enhanced cycle stability in lithium-ion batteries
- experiment: Differential electrochemical mass spectrometry (DEMS) and post-cycling spectroscopy | Gas evolution and surface analysis | params: Monitoring O2 and CO2 release during charging up to 4.8 V | result: B-LNCM showed minimal gas evolution, indicating superior oxygen stability and reduced surface degradation.
  - [experimental result] First-principles calculations show that frontal (102) facets have lower oxygen vacancy formation energy than lateral (003) facets.
  - [image description] B-LNCM has more lateral facets and fewer frontal facets, reducing oxygen release and surface degradation.
  - [referenced knowledge] Oxygen evolution triggers surface reconstruction and electrolyte decomposition, accelerating capacity fading.
  - [non-referenced_knowledge] Reduced oxygen loss and surface degradation preserve electrode structure and maintain performance over cycles.
  - [deductive reasoning] Therefore, phosphorus and boron doping enhance cycle stability by suppressing oxygen evolution and associated surface degradation mechanisms.
