## Tetrahedron elements
- **Processing**: PGC bio-nanosheets were synthesized by in situ regeneration of cellulose on polydopamine-reduced graphene oxide (PGO) templates, followed by physical assembly and chemical crosslinking using epichlorohydrin (ECH).
- **Structure**: The PGC bio-nanosheets formed a homogeneous porous hydrogel structure with a continuous and well-connected 3D network.
- **Properties**: conductivity–electrical property, mechanical strength–mechanical property, flexibility–mechanical property
- **Performance**: The hydrogel demonstrated stable electrical and mechanical performance in aqueous environments and in vivo implantation, and was used as an epidermal and implantable bioelectronic device for monitoring electrophysiological signals and accelerating wound healing.
