- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): The synthesis route of the eIM cathode is schematically illustrated in Figure  1a. In short, the Al atomic layers in $\mathrm{Nb}_{2}\mathrm{AlC}$ MAX were etched away by HF etchant to obtain the layered ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ MXene. Subsequently, negative $\mathrm{I^{-}}$ ions were intercalated in the interlayer gaps at a high potential driven by a constant electric field, and then fixed after converting it to the elemental state $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ at $1.6~\mathrm{V}$ (Details are given in the Experimental Section in the Supporting Information.)  As evidenced by the scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images, the pristine ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ MXene shows a typical concertina morphology (Figure  1b-i). The flakes are array stacked with a smooth surface with no visible impurities (Figure  1b-ii).[41,42] Additionally, the inherited Nb and C elements from MAX, O, and F, detected in the energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS) mapping data, stem from the surficial terminations (Figure  1c).[16] High-resolution transmission microscopy (HRTEM) images reveal that adjacent ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ flakes are spaced ${\approx}1.10~\mathrm{nm}$ apart (Figure  1d). Comparatively, the primary laminated microstructure remains intact after electrodeposition treatment, whereas the flat surface and interior galleries are completely covered by evident speckled clusters (Figure  1e). The magnified morphological details reveal the filled internal gaps. Further EDS data identifies the emerging cluster as iodine, in accordance with the Nb distribution, as shown in Figure  1f.[2] Unsurprisingly, the aIM electrode is similar in structure but less uniform (Figure S1, Supporting Information). The inserted/fixated $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ leads to an enlarged spacing of ${\approx}1.14~\mathrm{nm}$ , while the maintained parallel fringes represent the uniformity of the embedding without causing serious interior stress (Figure 1g).  X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns were analyzed to offer insights into the evolution of the phase composition. As shown in Figure  1h, the complete removal of Al atoms results in the disappearance of the peak at $39^{\circ}$ indexed to the (103) crystal plane of $\mathrm{Nb}_{2}\mathrm{AlC}$ MAX and the blueshift of the peak of the (002) plane from $12.9^{\circ}$ to $8.0^{\circ}$ , indicating thorough etching.[43] As for the eIM, after $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ inserting/fixating, the diffraction peak is further negatively shifted to $7.8^{\circ}$ , indicating an enlarged interlayer spacing. Other diffraction peaks of ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ at $33.9^{\circ}$ , $35.2^{\circ}$ , and $40.9^{\circ}$ remain unchanged. Additionally, Figure  1i depicts the Raman spectra, where the peak at $149~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ derived from the Nb-Al vibration disappears after etching; the peaks at 639, 266, and $130\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ , referred to as Nb-C and Nb-O vibrations, are enhanced and remain noticeable after electrodeposition.[44,45] A new peak detected at $114~\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$ , partially overlapping with Nb-O, is attributed to I–I.[38,46] To further unveil the surficial composition details, X-ray photoelectron  spectroscopy (XPS) was performed. As shown in Figure S2 (Supporting Information), apart from the four common elements of Nb, C, O, and F, a robust peak of I situated near $625~\mathrm{eV}$ emerged in the eIM sample. In particular, a detailed I3d spectrum is fitted and shown in Figure  1j, where the two strongest peaks at 631.8 and $620.2~\mathrm{eV}$ conform to II bonds derived from internal free $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ molecules.[47,48] Besides, the two manifest ${\mathrm{I-O(F)}}$ bonds at $619.2{\mathrm{~eV}}$ of $\mathrm{I}3\mathrm{d}_{5/2}$ and $634\mathrm{eV}$ of $\mathrm{I}3\mathrm{d}_{3/2}$ are also recognized since the strong adsorption capability stemming from both $\mathrm{Nb}_{2}\mathrm{C}$ and $\scriptstyle-\mathrm{F}/\mathrm{O}$ terminations leads to the partial bonding of I to the MXene host during insertion and subsequent oxidation.[2]

The incorporated insulating $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ hardly impairs the efficient electron transfer in ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ . On the contrary, ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ significantly facilitates the conversion reaction, which is clearly reflected by the rate capability. At an ultrahigh current density of $18\mathrm{~A~g~}^{-1}$ the capacity can reach up to $143\mathrm{mAhg^{-1}}$ , exceeding $70\%$ of $205\mathrm{mAh~g}^{-1}$ at 1 A ${\boldsymbol{\mathrm{g}}}^{-1}$ (Figure 2e). The ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ cathode exhibits low capacities of only $30\mathrm{mAhg^{-1}}$ at all rates (Figure S6, Supporting Information). The galvanostatic charge/discharge (GCD) profiles in Figure 2f show a flat discharge plateau even at $18\mathrm{Ag^{-1}}$ , remarkably different from all reported $\mathrm{I}_{2}.$ –metal battery systems with limited kinetics at high scan rates.[2,5,24,40,50] When the current density further increases to $20\mathrm{Ag}^{-1}$ , the battery delivers a capacity of ${\approx}131\mathrm{mAhg}^{-1}$ (Figure S7, Supporting Information). As evidenced by the absolute plateau slopes in Figure  2g, this value (∆voltage (mV)/∆capacity $(\mathrm{mAhg^{-1}})$ ) always remains within 1.5, indicating the desired insensitivity to current density. Strikingly, the plateau region offers exceptionally high contributions to capacity and energy density (enclosed area), up to $84.5\%$ and $89.1\%$ at 1 A ${\boldsymbol{\mathrm{g}}}^{-1}$ , respectively, which significantly improves the efficient output in the high-voltage region (Figure  2h). Figure S8 (Supporting Information) illustrates the plateau contributions to capacity and energy density at different rates, where the marked enclosed area represents the energy density. Consistent with the CV curves, the midpoint discharge plateau remains at $1.3~\mathrm{V},$ close to the theoretical value of $1.38~\mathrm{V}$ versus $\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}/\mathrm{Zn}$ $(Z\mathrm{n}^{2+}/Z\mathrm{n}$ vs SHE: $-0.76~\mathrm{V}$ ; Figure S9, Supporting Information). - Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): The prolonged cycle stability has always been a concern plaguing $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ redox systems, whose capacity fades due to the loss of $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ or reaction products $[\Gamma^{-}$ or $\mathrm{I}_{3}^{-})$ ) by parasitic shuttling.[52–54] Interestingly, using an eIM cathode, the trapping effect triggered by nanosized galleries efficiently resolves this issue. As shown in Figure  3a, at a high rate of $6\mathrm{~A~g^{-1}}$ , a remarkable cyclic lifespan of over $23~000$ cycles was achieved, maintaining a decent capacity retention of $80\%$ and achieving an extraordinary capacity decay rate of $0.0035\%$ per cycle. In stark contrast, the aIM cathode fails after 1500 cycles under the same conditions, similar to previously reported $\mathrm{I}_{2}{\cdot}Z\mathrm{n}$ batteries (Figure S11, Supporting Information). The ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ cathode shows satisfactory cycle stability but a poor capacity of less than $25\operatorname{mAh}\mathrm{g}^{-1}$ (Figure S12, Supporting Information). Strikingly, as shown in Figure  3b, the discharge plateau can always remain stable without evident shrinkage or disappearance. This can be partly attributed to the durable and efficient electron transfer, as shown by the EIS profiles (Figure S13, Supporting Information). The plateau regions nearly overlap between the 1st and $20~000\mathrm{th}$ , differing from the $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ –metal counterparts whose plateau attenuation bears the brunt as the cycle progresses.[24,50] The excellent prolonged capability coupled with the high discharge voltage close to the theoretical value of eIM obtained in this study touches the unrecorded peak among various $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ redox chemistry parameters, including Na, Mg, Zn, Li, Fe, Cu, and $\mathrm{Al}\cdot\mathrm{I}_{2}$ , as summarized in Figure  3c and Table S2 (Supporting Information).[2,24,40,55] In $\mathrm{I}_{2}/\mathrm{Li}$ batteries, the natural affinity of $\mathrm{Ti}_{3}\mathrm{C}_{2}\mathrm{T}_{\mathrm{X}}$ MXene to the I species has been verified.[5,24]  The restricted shuttle effect also benefits the anti-self-discharge performance as the formation of $\mathrm{I}_{3}^{-}$ has been effectively suppressed or confined. As depicted in the voltage versus time profiles, eIM cathode experiences a voltage decay from 1.6 to $1.33~\mathrm{V}$ after $48\mathrm{~h~}$ , delivering a slow self-discharge rate of $5.6\mathrm{mV}\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ , while the aIM cathode has a lower voltage of $1.24\:\mathrm{V}$ at a poor discharge rate of $7.5~\mathrm{mV}\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ (Figure 3d and Table S3: Supporting Information). Consequently, the long-lasting effective output in the high-voltage region is obtained. Owing to the support of an ultraflat voltage plateau, the obtained energy density reaches 259.3  Wh $\mathrm{kg}^{-1}$ at 1 A ${\boldsymbol{\mathrm{g}}}^{-1}$ , exceeding all reported aqueous MXene capacitors/pseudocapacitors and most aqueous metal- $\mathrm{I}_{2}$ batteries ( $\mathrm{_{Zn}}$ , Fe, and $\mathrm{Cu}\cdot\mathrm{I}_{2}$ ), as outlined in Figure 3e.[15,20] The eIM cathode obtained by the new electrodeposition method has excellent redox kinetics and long-term durability, benefiting from the confinement effect and adequate electron supply of MXene to I species. Moreover, compared to other MXene-based composite electrodes with intercalation/extraction features, the resultant conversion-type reaction suffers from a smaller volume change and collapse trend during cycling.

- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): The efficient trapping effect and rapid redox kinetics originate from the unique electronic structure and robust interaction between the ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ MXene host and the iodine species.[23] To reveal the fundamental mechanism, density functional theory (DFT) stimulation was applied to investigate the adsorption behavior and the induced electronic structure variation of the eIM cathode.[10,57,58] $\mathrm{Nb}_{2}\mathrm{C}$ surfaces were constructed by saturating the superficial Nb atoms in coordination with O groups, considering their dominant O terminations generated in the synthesis, thus forming a ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CO}}_{2}$ slab. Besides, the density of states (DOS) patterns near the Femi energy $(E_{\mathrm{f}})$ of the optimized adsorption systems around Nb sites were calculated to elucidate the influence of the electronic structure on the superior redox kinetics of the eIM cathode.[58,59] As revealed in Figure 5f and Figure S19 (Supporting Information), when iodine species $(\mathrm{I}^{-},\mathrm{I}_{2}$ and ${\mathrm{I}}_{3}^{-^{\prime}}$ ) are adsorbed by the ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ flakes, the DOS patterns only change slightly, indicating that the superior metallic properties of the ${\mathrm{Nb}}_{2}{\mathrm{CT}}_{\mathrm{X}}$ host are not attenuated. Consequently, efficient electron transfer at the interface area benefits the rapid redox kinetics of the eIM cathode.
