- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): In this work, inspired by the possibility that carbon supporting and metal mixing can increase MPI ability, we designed a series of new MPI tracers ${\mathrm{MFe}}_{2}{\mathrm{O}}_{4},$ /Carbon $\mathbf{\nabla}^{\prime}\mathbf{M}~\mathbf{=}~$ Mn, Co, or ${\mathrm{Zn}}$ ) and $\gamma\mathrm{-Fe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{3}$ /Carbon. The carbon supporting secondary $\mathrm{MFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}$ and $\gamma\mathrm{-Fe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{3}$ nanoparticles were prepared by pyrolysis of iron-containing MIL-88A at $450~^{\circ}C$ under an air or argon atmosphere and then coated with polydopamine (PDA) to increase their dispersibility in water (Scheme 1). The size, morphology, composition, and carbon supporting structure of doping SPIONs nanoparticles (denoted herein as $\mathrm{MFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}/\mathrm{C}(\varpi\mathrm{PDA}$ or $\gamma\mathrm{-Fe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{3}/\mathrm{C}(\varpi\mathrm{PDA})$ were determined by high-resolution transmission electron microscope (HR-TEM), scanning electron microscope (SEM), X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), and powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD). The results revealed that the carbon supporting metal ion doping $\mathrm{MFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}$ and $\gamma\mathrm{-Fe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{3}$ were successfully synthesized (the size of nanoparticles was $7{-}9~\mathrm{nm}$ ) and were coated with PDA. The metal ions doping nanoparticles together with the carbon support, imparted the $\mathrm{MFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}/\mathrm{C}(\varpi\mathrm{PDA}$ materials with outstanding MPI imaging performance. A ${\mathrm{ZnFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}}/{\mathrm{C}\mathrm{@PDA}}$ sample prepared by the pyrolysis of ZnFe-MIL-88A at $450~^{\circ}C$ under an air atmosphere affords an MPI signal of 1787.7, which was around 4.7 times higher than that determined for the commercial Vivotrax (383.2) under the same testing conditions. Both the in vitro cell culture and in vivo animal studies demonstrated that ${\mathrm{ZnFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}}/{\mathrm{C}\mathrm{@PDA}}$ was a highly biocompatible and effective MPI tracer. This work demonstrates that ${\mathrm{MFe}_{2}\mathrm{O}_{4}}/{\mathrm{C}\mathrm{@PDA}}$ through the pyrolysis of MOFs allowed tuning and optimization of MPI performance for cancer imaging, suggesting a new paradigm for the synthesis of imaging agents.
