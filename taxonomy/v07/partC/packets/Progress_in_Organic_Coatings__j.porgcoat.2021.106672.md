However,the stress strength of the composite film showed a decrease when the fillers content continued to increase,which was due to the aggregation of fillers at such high levels.

- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): The anti-corrosion coatings possess multiple furan groups and is able to react towards a maleimide end-capped polyurethane through DA reaction to result in thermally reversible cross-linked materials which are expected to exhibit heat-driven self-healing ability according to our previous research [24].To study the self-healing effect induced by the sunlight, the sample with the best anticorrosive performance (PCM2, 2 ${ \mathrm { ~ w t } } \% .$ ）was artificially scratched and healed by sunlight,and then analyzed by POM, SEM, stress-strain curves and EIS (Fig.7).The selfhealing performance of composite coatings was assigned to the dynamic formation of DA bonds.The DA cross-link points in the system would tend to break when the coating was subjected to the external forces.When the coating was heated, the polymer chains flowed freely and the DA bonds were re-formed under the thermal stimuli.As shown in Fig.S1,the sunlight was applied on the coatings.Due to the excellent photo-thermal conversion efficiency of the PDA nanoparticles and MXene,the coating can be heat to the glass transition temperature within short time. The temperature rapidly rose to $1 2 0 ~ ^ { \circ } \mathrm { C }$ within 1 min under sunlight with an intensity of $2 0 0 \mathrm { m W / c m ^ { 2 } }$ The repair process was also observed using POM(Fig.7a).A pre-scratched sample was put on a stage to observed the change of the crack under the sunlight.The POM photo clearly exhibited that the crack healed under a sunlight intensity of $2 0 0 \mathrm { m W / c m ^ { 2 } }$ for 1 min. The similar results can be obtained from the photos of SEM (Fig. 7b).  Moreover, the self-healing ability of the composite induced by sunlight can be evaluated by the stress-strain curve. The changes of mechanical properties after the self-healing behavior of the film were shown in the Fig.S2,the performance of composite can restore to more than $9 8 \%$ of the original for the first time,and for the second time it still maintained over $9 0 \%$ of the original. The anti-corrosion performance of coating before and after self-healing also was evaluated by EIS tests (Fig.7c). The corrosion resistance before and after self-healing was compared under immersion condition for1 day and the performance was almost not affected before and after self-healing.The above results indicated that the fabricated coatings possess great self-healing ability and maintain the same anti-corrosion effect as the original after selfhealing.
