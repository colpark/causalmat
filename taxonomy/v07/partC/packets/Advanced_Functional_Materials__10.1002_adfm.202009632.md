- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): The overall synthetic procedure, involving the shear exfoliation of presynthesized bulk $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ , conformal coating of the polydopamine layer, and carbonization, is depicted in Figure 1a. Briefly, a layered $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ (Figure 1b, presynthesized sample) was directly exfoliated into $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets by a controlled fluidic flow using water with added dopamine. With the assistance of fluid dynamics, dopamine molecules were intercalated into the gap between adjacent $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ layers. Meanwhile, dopamine was adsorbed on the surface of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets due to the strong adhesive ability of dopamine, resulting in dopaminecoated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ with an expanded structure. Then, a coating of polydopamine was created on the exfoliated dopamine-coated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ by the self-polymerization of dopamine under basic conditions. Polydopamine serves as a nitrogen-doped carbon source with controllable, conformal, and ultrathin coatings. Finally, the polydopamine-coated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ $(\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD})$ nanosheets were carbonized, resulting in alternatively assembled $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{N}$ -doped carbon $(\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C})$ nanosheets. Conventional $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ has a contact limitation between active sites and electrolytes due to the densely stacked structure of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ layers and also suffers from intrinsically insulating conductivity (Figure  1a). In contrast, the alternative heterogeneous stacking architecture of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ enables a large exposure of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ sheets to the electrolyte, and facilitates electron and ion transfer during Faradaic redox reactions.  Note that compact and layered $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ materials should be highly exfoliated for the preparation of ultrathin sandwichlike $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ structures in nanoscale interfacial engineering. In this work, we successfully demonstrated that the use of a Taylor–Couette flow confined in a small gap between two cylinders enables the delamination of layered $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ into mono- or few-layer flakes (Figure S1, Supporting Information). This fluid dynamic exfoliation process, using a strong shear force and efficient mass transfer, is suitable for the cost-effective and scalable manufacturing of exfoliated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ ( $\mathrm{ex}\mathrm{-MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets. By optimizing process parameters such as rotational speed, exfoliation time, and initial $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ concentration, we achieved a high exfoliation yield of $82.1\%$ and a high production rate of $5.74\textrm{g}\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ (Figure S2, Supporting Information). The introduction of dopamine into the Taylor–Couette flow helps stabilize the $\mathrm{ex}\mathrm{-MnO}_{2}$ sheets in aqueous dispersion by coating the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ surface with a dopamine surfactant. After centrifuging to remove unexfoliated 2D $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ , the collected dopamine-coated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets form an excellent colloid suspension in water with a typical Tyndall effect (Figure 1a); the colloidal suspension of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets remained unprecipitated for longer than 1 month. As dopamine contains amine and hydroxyl groups, its positively charged $\mathrm{NH}_{2}^{+}$ groups interact with negatively charged $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets via strong electrostatic interactions (Figure  1c and Figure S3, Supporting Information), while the hydroxyl groups of dopamine increase the negative surface charges of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets, as confirmed by zeta potential measurements (below $-30\mathrm{mV}_{\mathrm{i}}$ Figure S4, Supporting Information). This result indicates the successful conformal coating of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ sheets with dopamine molecules.  Figure  1d shows a high-resolution transmission electron microscope (HR-TEM) image of typical $\mathrm{ex}\mathrm{-MnO}_{2}$ with an ultrathin 2D nanosheet structure and wrinkled surface morphology. Dopamine molecules inserted into $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ layers with an expanded interlayer distance of $1.24~\mathrm{nm}$ compared to that of bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ $\left(0.73\mathrm{nm}\right)$ were also observed (Figure S5, Supporting Information). Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and atomic force microscopy (AFM) were used to determine the lateral size and thickness of the ex- $\mathrm{{MnO}}_{2}$ flakes. Dispersions were diluted and drop-cast onto a silicon wafer substrate, followed by washing and thermal treatments to remove the coated dopamine molecules. Based on SEM statistics, the $\mathrm{ex}\mathrm{-MnO}_{2}$ has a lateral dimension in the range from 19 to $1389\mathrm{nm}$ (Figure  1e and Figure S6, Supporting Information). A statistical AFM analysis results in an average thickness of $2.8\pm2~\mathrm{nm}$ for the $\mathrm{ex}\mathrm{-MnO}_{2}$ (Figure 1f and Figure S7, Supporting Information).

- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): After self-polymerization followed by carbonization, the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ nanosheet was characterized using microscopy and spectroscopy measurements. The HR-TEM images in Figure 2a,b reveal that the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ is coated with a carbon layer with a thickness of ${\approx}2~\mathrm{nm}$ , resulting in a core–shell structure of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ nanosheet. The lattice parameter of $0.24~\mathrm{nm}$ in the ordered crystal structure corresponds to the (100) plane (Inset of Figure  2b). Further examination by energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy elemental mapping of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ (Figure 2c) results in a total overlap of Mn, O, C, and N signals on the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ surface, indicating a uniform carbon coating on the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ . The chemical composition of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ before and after carbonization was further investigated using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS). Peaks of C 1s, N 1s, and O 1s are observed in $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ , indicating the presence of polydopamine on the surface of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ . After carbonization, the O 1s peak significantly decreased, as shown in the survey scan of Figure S8, Supporting Information. The high-resolution C 1s spectrum (Figure 2d) of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ exhibits a sharp peak for $\mathrm{C}{-}\mathrm{C}$ $(284.4~\mathrm{eV})$ , with weak peaks corresponding to $\mathsf{C}{-}\mathrm{N}$ (286.4  eV) and ${\mathrm{C}}{=}0$ (287.8  eV).[30] In addition, the high-resolution $\mathrm{N}$ 1s peak indicates the N-doped carbon coating for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ , while the nitrogen content is $2.3\%$ for the elements on the surface of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ . The composition ratio of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ and carbon was $90.2{:}9.8\mathrm{wt}\%$ based on the thermal gravimetric analysis results (Figure  S9, Supporting Information). To investigate textural properties of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ , the nitrogen adsorption and desorption were performed (Figure S10, Supporting Information). Based on the Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) analysis, the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ had a high specific surface area of $121.4~\mathrm{cm}^{3}~\mathrm{g}^{-1}$ , which was about sixfold higher than $18.2~\mathrm{cm}^{3}~\mathrm{g}^{-1}$ for the pristine bulk $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ . Compared to the porosity distribution of bulk $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ exhibited large mesopores and narrow pore size distribution. The formation of porous structure for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ is attributed to the restacking and agglomeration of ultrathin wrinkled $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ nanosheets. The superior textural properties of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ are expected to be suitable for easy-access ion transfer.  To better understand the intercalated crystalline structure of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ , X-ray diffraction (XRD) and Raman measurements were performed on bare bulk $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ , $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ , and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ samples (Figure  2e,f). The bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ exhibited a typical diffraction peak for birnessite ${\mathrm{K}}_{X}{\mathrm{MnO}}_{2}$ with an interlayer parameter d of $0.73\mathrm{nm}$ .[31,32] Compared to the bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ , two characteristic XRD peaks for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ which are indexed as (001) and (002), respectively, were significantly reduced (Figure  2e). The loss of the original crystalline structure for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ is attributed to the presence of mono- and few-layered $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ nanosheets.[33,34] After exfoliation and carbonization processes, the broad (002) peak was observed for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ , corresponding to amorphous carbon structure.[35,36] In addition, $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ exhibited decreased intensities of XRD peaks at (100) and (110) compared to the bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ . Therefore, the as-obtained $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{PD}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ had a birnessite form of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ .  Observation of the Raman spectrum (Figure 2f) for pristine $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ shows two characteristic Raman peaks at $645~\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ $(\nu_{1})$ and $578~\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$ $\left(\nu_{2}\right)$ , associated with the out-of-plane symmetric stretching vibration of $\scriptstyle{\mathrm{Mn-O}}$ in octahedral $\mathrm{MnO}_{6}$ , and the inplane symmetric stretching vibration of $\scriptstyle{\mathrm{Mn-O}}$ along the chains of the $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ framework.[37,38] Compared to the spectrum of bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ , the spectra of dopamine-coated $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ and $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ exhibit different Raman spectra, where the $\scriptstyle{\mathrm{Mn-O}}$ stretching mode shows significant broadening and weakening of both the out-of-plane and in-plane modes, possibly indicating full exfoliation of the bare $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ into mono- or few-layered $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ sheets. Two other Raman peaks of the D band $(1374\mathrm{cm}^{-1})$ and G band $(1588~\mathrm{cm^{-1}})$ ,[30] which are related to disorder in $\operatorname{sp}^{2}$ - hybridized carbon, were observed in both samples. The higher intensity ratio $I_{\mathrm{D}}/I_{\mathrm{G}}$ for $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ compared to that of $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/$ polydopamine is attributed to an increase in the microcrystalline graphitic carbon through pyrolysis in an inert atmosphere.

Based on a comparison summary of $19.7\ –\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ and other $\mathrm{MnO}_{2}$ -based materials with similarly high mass loadings reported in the literature (Figure  4f ),[14,29,41–57] it was concluded that $19.7\ –\mathrm{MnO}_{2}/\mathrm{C}$ is a superior high-mass-loading electrode with ultrahigh specific capacitance and impressive rate ability.
