- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): Characterization of Microfluidics-Synthesized ZIF-8 and BioZIF-8 MOFs. We designed a microfluidic chip with a two-spiral mixing channel to fabricate ZIF-8 MOFs (top panel of Figure 1A). The transverse shearing force in the double spiral channels results in more vigorous mixing than the straight channel. The spiral mixing channel shortens the mixing distance and reduces the mixing time, which is established approximately 46% faster than in a straight microchannel.46,47 Hence, such even and vigorous mixing results in the generation of size-controlled small particles.46 The microfluidic chip consists of two inlets (one is a centered inlet) followed by a two-spiral mixing channels and an outlet. The channel width measured $1200\mu\mathrm{m}$ and height about $60\mu\mathrm{m}$ . The zinc nitrate solution $23~\mathrm{mg/mL}$ of water) is passed through the first inlet and the organic ligand molecule 2-methylimidazole (2-MeIM) $(220~\mathrm{mg/mL}$ of water) is passed through the centered inlet. The solution mixes and assembles into ZIF-8 MOFs in the spiral channels (Figure S1). The injection flow rate is $35~\mu\mathrm{L}/$ min during the assembly of ZIF-8 MOFs. The prepared ZIF-8 crystals are almost monodispersed, which have a hydrodynamic size of ${\sim}477\pm54\mathrm{nm}$ as confirmed by dynamic light scattering (DLS) (Figure 1B). The average core size is ${\sim}373\pm10\mathrm{nm}$ under scanning electron microscopy (SEM) (Figure 1C). The hydrodynamic size and core size of ZIF-8 are similar to the MOFs prepared via batch reactor or other nonaqueous methods.48−50 On the other hand, the neat ZIF-8 MOFs prepared by the conventional aqueous method in the present work has a hydrodynamic diameter (HDD) of ${\sim}1253~\mathrm{nm}$ , and the core diameter is ${\sim}915~\mathrm{nm}$ (Figure 1A bottom panel illustrates the synthesis scheme; Figure 1B,C). The SEM image shows the cubic morphology of microfluidic ZIF-8 MOFs51,52 (Figure 1D) and typical crystal morphology of hydrothermally synthesized ZIF-8 MOF (Figure 1E). Powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern of both ZIF-8 crystals formed in various methods evolved similar prominent peak positions, including 011, 002, 112, 022, 013, and 222 confirming the sodalite structure,53 which is the typical structure of ZIF-8 (Figure S2A). ZIF-8 crystals formed in both microfluidics and hydrothermal methods display a positive zeta potential value of $\sim+30\mathrm{mV}$ (Figure S2B). We studied the reproducibility of the microfluidic method by comparing the ZIF-8 MOFs prepared using three separate chips. ZIF-8 MOFs in the SEM images have similar size and no apparent changes in the morphology of the crystals (Figure S3A). The hydrodynamic size of these MOFs is in a similar range with least differences, that is, 488, 479, and ${505~\mathrm{nm}}$ (Figure S3B). We studied the stability of the microfluidics-formed ZIF-8 MOFs on day 1 and 3 weeks later by characterizing the crystal structure, morphology, and DLS measurements (such as zeta potentials and HDD). The ZIF-8 MOFs showed negligible changes in the crystal structure, morphology, and DLS measurements (Figure S4) throughout the study period. However, upon exposure of ZIF-8 MOFs to acidic $\mathrm{pH}~6.0$ , the breakdown of the morphology is evident in the SEM images (Figure 5A− C).54,55 Furthermore, the zeta potential value varies upon exposure to the acidic $\mathsf{p H}$ (Figure 5D).

- Linked text (the paper's own sentences, as linked by the PDF parser): We prepared the biomolecules-encapsulated ZIF-8 MOFs (BioZIF-8 MOFs) using the same double spiral microfluidic chip. We used various biomolecules such as nucleic acid (siRNA), proteins (BSA), and antitumor drug doxorubicin (DOX) for encapsulation. PDMS does not absorb any biomolecules apparently under the flow (Figure S6); thus the loss during the reaction is neglectable. For the preparation of siRNA and DOX-encapsulated BioZIF-8, the biomolecules were injected along with the zinc nitrate precursor solution (Figure 2A), whereas for BSA the injection was along with 2- MeIM solution (Figure 2B). The injection flow rate was 35 $\mu\mathrm{L}/\mathrm{min},$ and the synthesis proceeded at room temperature. The morphology of BioZIF-8 MOFs siRNA@ZIF-8 (Figure 2C), DOX@ZIF-8 MOFs (Figure 2D), and ${\mathrm{BSA@ZIF}}{\mathrm{-}}8$ MOFs (Figure 2E) was shown by SEM analysis. The average hydrodynamic diameter of siRNA, DOX, and BSA BioZIF-8 MOFs measured 528, 766, and $722\mathrm{nm},$ , respectively (Figure S7). To confirm the encapsulation, we labeled the biomolecules with a fluorescent dye, for example, siRNA labeled with Cy5 dye (Figure S8A) and BSA labeled with fluorescein isothiocyanate (FITC) dye (Figure S8B). We confirmed the DOX encapsulation using UV−vis spectroscopy (Figure S8C). The surface charge of the BioZIF-8 MOFs is positive (Figure S9A). The incorporation of biomolecules did not affect the crystal nature of BioZIF-8 MOFs, because both neat ZIF-8 and BioZIF-8 MOFs displayed a similar $2\theta$ degree (Figure S9B). The ZIF-8 MOFs show varied surface area, pore-volume, and average pore size diameter after loading the biomolecules according to the BET results (Table S1).  We evaluated the encapsulation efficiency of microfluidicprepared BioZIF-8 MOFs. We labeled the biomolecules with a fluorescent dye to estimate the encapsulation capacity, for example, siRNA labeled with Cy5 and BSA labeled with FITC fluorescence dye. The DOX concentration was measured using UV−vis spectroscopy. The microfluidic approach allows more encapsulation of biomolecules into BioZIF-8 MOFs than hydrothermal synthesized (Figure $2\mathrm{F}{-}2\mathrm{H}_{.}$ ). The encapsulation percentage of the BioZIF-8 MOFs was $59\%$ for BSA, $47\%$ for DOX, and $53.5\%$ for siRNA prepared using the microfluidic approach, which is significantly higher than that of the biomolecule-encapsulated BioZIF-8 MOFs (BSA, $43\%$ ; DOX, $34.5\%$ ; siRNA, $37.3\%$ ) formed with the conventional method.
