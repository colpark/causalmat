Reviewed all listed panels (F4a-c TEM; F1a-c SEM; F2 XRD; F3 DSC) for the Al-Cu-Mn + La/Sm rare-earth study.

Key visual observations:
- F4a/b/c (TEM, 20 nm scale): show plate/lath-shaped theta'-type precipitates in a criss-cross pattern. Panel (a) has sparser, coarser, more clustered dark plates; (b) shows a denser array; (c) shows a very dense, fine, uniform crisscross network — consistent with progressive precipitate refinement across the three alloys (presumably Al-Cu-Mn, +Sm, +La).
- F1a/b/c (SEM, 10 µm scale): show a light-gray second-phase (eutectic) network outlining former grain/cell boundaries. (a) shows a coarse, continuous, interconnected network; (b) shows a much more fragmented, discontinuous, finer distribution of the same phase; (c) is similar to (b) but with two labeled feature types (A: blocky/irregular particles, B: fine dispersed particles), indicating at least two distinguishable morphologies of the grain-boundary phase.
- F2 (XRD): identifies Al and Al2Cu peaks common to all three alloys, plus distinct Al5Cu6La peaks appearing only in the La-containing alloy; no separate Sm-compound peaks are resolved.
- F3 (DSC): shows two thermal-reaction peaks (A near 550-600 °C, B near 640-650 °C) whose positions/shapes differ slightly among the three alloys, consistent with (but not proof of) different phase/eutectic content.
