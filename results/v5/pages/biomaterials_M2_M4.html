key — what the evidence permits
Because Co content sets Co2+ release magnitude (5Co-MBG releases fastest and most, 2Co-MBG less, over 7 days, per the previous step), and the western blot shows HIF-1alpha band intensity is faint for plain MBG and 2Co-MBG but clearly stronger for 5Co-MBG (with equal alpha-tubulin loading), the two results together show that HIF-1alpha protein level in BMSCs tracks the same ordering as Co2+ release across the three scaffold groups: more Co2+ release goes with more HIF-1alpha.
limits
  • Only three scaffold groups are compared (MBG, 2Co-MBG, 5Co-MBG); this is an ordering across categories, not a dose-response curve relating a measured Co2+ concentration to a measured HIF-1alpha level.
  • The western blot is a qualitative/semi-quantitative read (band intensity relative to alpha-tubulin loading control), not a quantified protein amount, and no timepoint within the 7-day release window is specified for when the blot was taken.
  • The scaffold groups differ in composition beyond Co2+ release (they are different materials, not a single material dosed with varying free Co2+), so material/compositional differences other than Co2+ release cannot be ruled out as contributing to the HIF-1alpha pattern.
  • obs1 shows HIF-1alpha only; it says nothing about VEGF, so the 'drives VEGF' portion of the conclusion is not supported by this evidence.
  • obs2 (relative survival with Scaffolds+Ampicillin at ~105%, ~81%, ~52% on days 1, 3, 7 versus a 100% blank and 110-118% drug-free scaffolds) concerns antibacterial/cytotoxicity survival with an antibiotic, not the HIF-1alpha/hypoxia pathway, and does not bear on this mechanism.
not identifiableWhether the HIF-1alpha increase reflects a specific hypoxia-mimetic mechanism (Co2+ stabilizing HIF-1alpha) versus a more general cobalt-driven cellular stress response, since the blot cannot separate these two explanations for elevated HIF-1alpha across the three groups.
