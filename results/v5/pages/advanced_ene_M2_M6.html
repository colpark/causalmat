key — what the evidence permits
Because charge is compensated by both V (V3+ to V4+ to V5+) and Fe (Fe2+ to Fe3+) valence changes, the w/ HCl cathode can draw on more than one redox couple, consistent with it delivering a first discharge capacity of ~91 mAh/g at 110 mA/g -- higher than the 50-65 mAh/g reported for earlier aqueous PBAs.
limits
  • The 91 mAh/g figure is a single first-discharge measurement on one composition (w/ HCl cathode); it does not partition how much capacity comes from the V-based versus the Fe-based redox couple.
  • The 50-65 mAh/g benchmark is drawn from earlier, different studies/materials rather than a controlled comparison run under the same conditions and rate, so the comparison is cross-study, not a matched experiment.
  • Capacity alone does not confirm that both valence changes are electrochemically active during this specific discharge at 110 mA/g; step 1's evidence for dual valence compensation came from separate characterization, not from this GCD trace.
  • One capacity value cannot establish a general relationship between number of active redox couples and capacity magnitude -- it supports an ordering/comparison at best.
