HYP

n1 HYP/gap
Reports disagree on whether MAPbI3 shows a low-temperature orthorhombic-tetragonal transition and dual PL emission
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Domain size, through the residual stress it stores, should govern the phase transition and PL behaviour of MAPbI3

DES

n3 DES/base_system
Use MAPbI3 perovskite as the archetypal system, one compound across every crystalline state
n4 DES/variable_sweepshown
Comparison axis: three archetypal crystalline states PC, HOC and SC, later a domain-size series from <20 nm to >500 um
n5 DES/route
Grow each state by a route chosen for it: spin-coating, temperature-gradient solidification, inverse temperature crystallization

PRC

n6 PRC/synthesis
Crystallize MAPbI3 from precursor solution by the three routes into PC films, HOC films and mm-sized single crystals
n15 PRC/treatment
Grind the as-grown PC, HOC and SC finely into nanoparticles under nitrogen
n17 PRC/stimulusshown
Expose an SC crystal to the atmosphere for 1 day, 1 month and 3 months, then split the aged crystal in half

STR

n7 STR/microstructure/feature_sizepartial
Domain size spans four decades: ~0.1 um (PC), ~10-100 um (HOC) and ~1e2-1e3 um (SC)
n8 STR/phase/identityshown
All three states are the same MAPbI3 phase of the same composition; their as-grown pattern differences are preferred orientation only
n9 STR/phase/latticepartial
Lattice expansion with temperature is near-linear in SC but non-linear in PC and HOC, in both phases
n10 STR/phase/lattice
Inhomogeneous residual lattice strain builds up toward the transition and its size depends on the crystalline state
n12 STR/phase/fractionshown
The orthorhombic-to-tetragonal transition of SC starts at the domain surface and moves inward, finishing within <10 K

PRP

n13 PRP/valueshown
As-grown emission below 150 K ranks with crystalline state: PC dual peak plus IR tail, HOC intermediate, SC a single peak
n14 PRP/valuepartial
Transition temperature rises and the transition window narrows as domain size grows
n20 PRP/valuepartial
Domains below ~50 nm stay tetragonal: single ~790 nm band at 80 K, no transition in the mapped window
n16 PRP/valueshown
After grinding all three converge on one tetragonal-like emission band that red-shifts smoothly, with no detectable transition below 150 K
n21 PRP/valueshown
Atmospheric ageing turns the SC surface into a PC-like dual emitter while the core stays single-peak

MEC

n11 MEC/pathway
The 2.0% unit-cell volume change stores residual stress between the two phases; the stress grows with domain size and resists transformation

PRF

n18 PRF/service_capability
Power-dependent PL acts as a gauge of the crystallinity of SC perovskite

DSC

n19 DSC/conclusion
Domain-size-dependent residual stress governs the orthorhombic-tetragonal transition and the PL of MAPbI3
d1 DSC/comparison
The domain-size axis reconciles the disparate literature: no transition in nanocrystals, dual emission in PC and small SC, abrupt transition in large SC
a2 DSC/limitation
The peak-width normalisation reference is inconsistent: the caption says sigma(700 K), the linked text sigma(100 K), and the data stop at ~270 K

KNW

k1 KNW/model
Williamson-Hall: size broadening is temperature independent, so the temperature-dependent part of the linewidth is inhomogeneous strain
k2 KNW/factshown
The unit-cell volume changes by 2.0% across the orthorhombic-tetragonal transition of MAPbI3
k3 KNW/precedent
Prior PL studies disagree: nanocrystals show no transition to 3 K, PC dual emission at 15 K, large SC one orthorhombic peak with an abrupt 150 K transition
k6 KNW/lookup
PL near 750 nm is the orthorhombic phase of MAPbI3 and PL near 780 nm the tetragonal phase

question, as the solver sees itRank the three samples by emission complexity below 150 K, and compare that ranking with the domain sizes from step 1.

depth 1causal strength associativedependency realinference follows, weaklylane other

mixed~0.1 um PC ... 1e2-1e3 um SC (domain sizes, from step 1) (spatial length scale (domain/grain size, in micrometers)) against two bands plus an IR tail (PC) ... one band (SC) (emission complexity, from obs1) (spectral feature count / qualitative PL band multiplicity (a dimensionless, ordinal descriptor of spectrum shape, not a length or magnitude))

combining proposition
The orderings correspond inversely across the same three samples: ~0.1 um PC shows two bands plus an IR tail, 1e2-1e3 um SC shows one band, HOC lies between on both axes.
limits
  • Three samples made by three different growth routes, so domain size is confounded with synthesis route.
  • Emission complexity is an ordinal count, not a continuous measure, so "inversely ranks" is the strongest form available -- no slope can be fitted.
  • Domain size (a spatial length scale in um) and emission complexity (the number/character of PL bands, e.g. 'two bands plus an IR tail' vs 'one band') are quantities of different kinds; the observations give no same-kind numeric measure of spectral complexity (e.g., bandwidth or peak spacing in energy/wavelength units) that could be substituted. The correspondence between the size ranking and the band-count ranking should be stated as a qualitative co-variation across the three samples, not as a validated inverse relationship between commensurate quantities.

scoring target: the combining proposition together with each named limit — 4 required elements. Stating that the causes cannot be separated, or that the result is not identifiable, without giving the combining proposition and its limits, earns nothing. The confound is the conclusion of the comparison, not a substitute for it.
