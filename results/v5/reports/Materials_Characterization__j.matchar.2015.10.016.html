Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

h1 HYP/hypothesis
Adding AlN as a sintering aid should promote densification of ZrB2-SiC composites

DES

d1 DES/base_system
ZrB2-30 vol% SiC with phenolic resin binder that pyrolyses to ~1.6 wt% carbon sintering aid
d2 DES/modification
Add AlN to the ZrB2-SiC powder mixture as a sintering additive
d3 DES/variable_sweep
AlN at 0, 1, 3, 5 wt%, each consolidated by hot pressing (HP) and by pressureless sintering (PS)

PRC

p0 PRC/treatment
Ball-mix powders with ZrO2 balls in ethanol (90 rpm, 60 min), rotary-dry at 90 C and sieve through 100 mesh
p1 PRC/forming
Hot press the powder mixtures (resin pyrolyses to carbon during heating)
p2 PRC/forming
Pressureless sinter the powder mixtures at the same temperatures as HP

STR

s1 STR/interface
In AlN-doped HP composites a thin Al-, Si-, B-, C-, N-bearing amorphous layer lines ZrB2/SiC grain interfaces
s2 STR/microstructure/porosity
HP composites with 1 and 5 wt% AlN reach ~100% relative density, above AlN-free HP (~97.5%)
s3 STR/microstructure/porosity
PS composites stay porous and porosity grows with AlN (relative density ~85.5% at 0 to ~70% at 5 wt%)
s4 STR/phase/identity
BN forms in HP5 during hot pressing
s5 STR/phase/identity
No AlN remains in HP5: the additive is consumed during hot pressing
s6 STR/phase/identity
Residual carbon graphitises into nano-multilayer graphite along HP5 grain boundaries
s7 STR/interface
PS5 grains are joined by thin amorphous boundary layers and binder-like glassy agglomerates
s8 STR/phase/identity
Aluminium oxycarbide Al2OC forms in PS5

PRP

r1 PRP/value
HP Vickers hardness peaks at ~20.3 GPa with 1 wt% AlN and falls slightly (~19.2-19.4 GPa) at 3-5 wt%
r2 PRP/value
PS Vickers hardness falls with AlN from ~12 GPa (0 wt%) to ~6.8 GPa (5 wt%)
b1 PRP/behavior_class
AlN-doped HP composites fracture predominantly intragranularly
b2 PRP/behavior_class
PS5 fractures predominantly intergranularly

MEC

m1 MEC/pathway
AlN reduces surface B2O3 to BN + Al2O3; the Al2O3-SiO2-based liquid drives liquid-phase sintering during hot pressing
m2 MEC/pathway
Gaseous products of AlN reactions stay trapped in the pores when no external pressure is applied, opposing densification
m3 MEC/pathway
Thin amorphous boundary layers make PS grain boundaries weaker than the grains, so cracks run along boundaries

DSC

c1 DSC/conclusion
AlN aids densification and hardness of ZrB2-SiC only under hot pressing; pressureless, it lowers density and hardness

KNW

k1 KNW/precedent
Residual carbon tends to graphitise at elevated temperature and high external pressure [40]
k2 KNW/precedent
AlN reduces the B2O3 surface oxide of ZrB2 to BN and Al2O3 (Eq. 1) [22]
