Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
317L SS esophageal stents are hard, cause extra injury and pain, and do not inhibit the cells that drive restenosis
n2 HYP/hypothesis
A soft, biodegradable Mg-based alloy should outperform 317L SS as an esophageal stent: degrade, alkalise the site and inhibit pathological cells

DES

n3 DES/base_system
Choose the Mg-Zn-Y-Nd alloy as the candidate biodegradable esophageal stent metal
n4 DES/variable_sweep
Compare Mg-Zn-Y-Nd with the incumbent 317L SS in surface, corrosion, immersion (1-30 d) and cell-culture (4 h-3 d) tests

PRC

n5 PRC/stimulus
Immerse both metals in Hank's solution at 37 C and retrieve after 1, 3, 7, 10, 15, 22 and 30 days

STR

n6 STR/microstructure/damage
Degradation opens a porous surface on Mg-Zn-Y-Nd that coarsens into a deep cellular network by 22-30 days; 317L SS stays flat
n14 STR/microstructure/shape
Both metal surfaces are smooth at the tens-of-nm scale; Mg-Zn-Y-Nd (~57 nm) is rougher than 317L SS (~32 nm)

PRP

n7 PRP/value
Mg-Zn-Y-Nd degrades far faster than 317L SS: much lower corrosion potential, ~3 decades higher corrosion current, higher immersion corrosion rate
n9 PRP/value
Immersed Mg-Zn-Y-Nd drives Hank's solution to pH ~10 and holds it there, above the blank control; 317L SS does not
n11 PRP/value
Mg-Zn-Y-Nd is less wettable than 317L SS: water contact angle about 60 vs 28 degrees
n12 PRP/value
Fewer cells adhere and fewer remain vital on Mg-Zn-Y-Nd than on 317L SS in all four cell cultures
n10 PRP/value
Mg-Zn-Y-Nd is much softer than 317L SS: microhardness about 54 vs 199 HV

MEC

n8 MEC/pathway
Mg dissolution consumes water and releases hydroxide (Mg + 2H2O -> Mg2+ + 2OH- + H2), so degrading Mg alkalises the medium

PRF

n15 PRF/service_capability
A degrading Mg-Zn-Y-Nd stent could neutralise the acidic esophageal tumour environment, an advantage over 317L SS
n16 PRF/service_capability
Mg-Zn-Y-Nd should suppress the cancer, fibroblast, squamous and inflammatory cells that cause stent restenosis
n13 PRF/service_capability
A softer, smooth Mg-Zn-Y-Nd stent should cause less injury and pain at implantation than 317L SS

DSC

n17 DSC/conclusion
Mg-Zn-Y-Nd is a promising biodegradable esophageal stent material with advantages over 317L SS

KNW

k1 KNW/fact
Mg corrodes in aqueous media by Mg + 2H2O -> Mg(OH)2 + H2, raising local pH
k2 KNW/precedent
Eca109, L929 and Het-1A cells participate in esophageal stent restenosis; macrophages are the main inflammatory cells
k3 KNW/fact
The focus of advanced esophageal cancer is chronically acidic because of inflammation and canceration
