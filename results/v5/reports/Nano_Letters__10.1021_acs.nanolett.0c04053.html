HYP

n1 HYP/need
Targeted in vivo delivery needs small, uniform, high-payload MOF carriers that also carry a targeting ligand
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Vigorous double-spiral micromixing should give smaller, more narrowly sized ZIF-8 than hydrothermal batch synthesis
n22 HYP/hypothesis
One-pass co-injection during the fast microfluidic assembly should encapsulate more biomolecule than hydrothermal batch synthesis

n3 DES/routeshown
Make ZIF-8 in a double-spiral microfluidic chip instead of the conventional hydrothermal batch route
n4 DES/variable_sweepshown
Comparison axis: microfluidic versus hydrothermal synthesis, for neat ZIF-8 and for siRNA-, DOX- and BSA-loaded ZIF-8
n5 DES/modificationshown
Coat the biomolecule-loaded ZIF-8 with a DNA (anti-CCL21) or RNA (A10) aptamer to add target recognition

PRC

n6 PRC/synthesisshown
Zn(NO3)2 and 2-methylimidazole streams meet in the double-spiral channel and assemble into ZIF-8 at room temperature
n7 PRC/synthesisshown
Cargo co-injected with the Zn stream (siRNA, DOX) or the 2-MeIM stream (BSA) is encapsulated as the crystals assemble in one pass
n8 PRC/depositionshown
In stage 2 of the three-spiral chip the aptamer is injected onto the just-formed BioZIF-8 and adsorbs on its surface

STR

n9 STR/microstructure/feature_sizeshown
Microfluidic ZIF-8 crystals are ~350-400 nm and narrowly sized; hydrothermal crystals are ~900-950 nm and more broadly sized
n10 STR/microstructure/shapeshown
Cargo loading and aptamer coating leave the rounded polyhedral ZIF-8 habit and the ~0.3 um particle size unchanged
n14 STR/microstructure/distributionpartial

PRP

n12 PRP/valueshown
Encapsulation efficiency is higher for the microfluidic route for every cargo (siRNA 53 vs 37%, DOX 47 vs 34%, BSA 59 vs 43%)
n15 PRP/valueshown
Surface charge reverses on coating: BSA@ZIF-8 +25 mV, anti-CCL21 DNA aptamer -16 mV, A10 RNA aptamer -14.5 mV

MEC

n11 MEC/pathway
Transverse shear in the double spiral mixes the reagents fast, so supersaturation is reached at once and nucleation outruns growth
n13 MEC/pathway
Cargo already present in the rapidly mixed stream is occluded by the nucleating framework, so more of it is trapped than in slow batch growth
n16 MEC/pathway
The polyanionic aptamer backbone binds electrostatically to the cationic ZIF-8 surface, which is why no coupling chemistry is needed
n19 MEC/pathway
The surface aptamer recognises its target (CCL21 in the lymph node, PSMA on prostate tumour cells) and holds the carrier there

PRF

n17 PRF/service_capabilityshown
Anti-CCL21 DNA aptamer-FITC-BSA@ZIF-8 accumulates in the draining inguinal and axillary lymph nodes more than the neat MOF
n18 PRF/service_capabilityshown
A10 RNA aptamer-FITC-BSA@ZIF-8 reaches the prostate tumour in higher amount than the neat MOF after tail-vein injection
n20 PRF/qualificationshown
Over 14 days the aptamer-BSA@ZIF-8 keeps liver and kidney serum markers inside their reference ranges with no organ histopathology

DSC

n21 DSC/conclusion
One integrated microfluidic chip makes aptamer-functionalised BioZIF-8 with a higher payload that targets lymph node and tumour
