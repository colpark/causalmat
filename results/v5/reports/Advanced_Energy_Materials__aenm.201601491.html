1The original graph

Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

h1 HYP/need
Aqueous rechargeable batteries need cathodes above the ~50-65 mAh/g delivered by earlier PBAs
h2 HYP/hypothesis
A V/Fe PBA with redox-active V and Fe sites could exceed Fe-only PBA capacity by multi-electron redox

DES

d1 DES/base_system
Vanadium hexacyanoferrate Prussian blue analogue (open MOF framework) chosen as aqueous cathode
d2 DES/variable_sweep
Synthesis route varied: simple mixing, co-precipitation without HCl, co-precipitation with HCl
d3 DES/method
Acidic aqueous electrolyte 0.5 M Na2SO4 + H2SO4, 0.45-1.15 V vs Ag/AgCl, chosen because V/Fe PBA is stable in acid

PRC

p1 PRC/synthesis
Co-precipitation keeps Fe(CN)6/V ratio constant; HCl added to retard PBA growth
p2 PRC/stimulus
Galvanostatic charging/discharging in the acidic aqueous electrolyte (up to 250 cycles)

STR

s3 STR/phase/fraction
Crystallinity rises (amorphous fraction falls): simple mixing < co-precipitation w/o HCl < w/ HCl
s6 STR/chemistry/composition
Charge is compensated by both V (V3+ to V4+ to V5+) and Fe (Fe2+ to Fe3+) valence changes
s8 STR/chemistry/composition
V and Fe ions dissolve during cycling, mostly from V sites, saturating after ~150 cycles
s2 STR/phase/identity
All routes give the cubic V3[Fe(CN)6]2-type PBA phase
s4 STR/chemistry/composition
As-synthesized formula Na0.4(VO)3[Fe(CN)6]2.12H2O with vanadyl ions and zeolitic/ligand water
s7 STR/phase/identity
PBA crystal structure preserved over 250 cycles (no amorphization)

PRP

r1 PRP/value
Electrochemical utilization and capacity retention increase with crystallinity
r2 PRP/value
Multi-electron redox: three reversible redox couples, Emid 0.54, 0.90, 1.08 V vs Ag/AgCl
r4 PRP/value
Charge-transfer resistance Rct accumulates with cycling while Re and Rfilm stay constant

MEC

m1 MEC/pathway
Residual amorphous region around crystallites acts as an extra interface (film resistance) hindering the electrochemical process
m2 MEC/identification
Three couples identified as V(III)/V(IV)O, Fe(II)/Fe(III), V(IV)/V(V) by precedent on V/Fe PBA films
a1 MEC/pathway
Alternative: capacity fade from structural degradation (amorphization or particle damage)
m5 MEC/pathway
V-site dissolution raises Rct, accumulating polarization and fading capacity; both saturate after early cycles
m3 MEC/pathway
High-rate capacity carried by kinetically facile Fe couple; V couples fade at high current

PRF

f1 PRF/service_capability
Initial discharge capacity 91 mAh/g at 110 mA/g, above earlier aqueous PBAs (50-65 mAh/g)
f3 PRF/service_capability
Capacity fades from 91 to ~55 mAh/g over 250 cycles, mostly in early cycles, at ~100% coulombic efficiency
f2 PRF/service_capability
Rate capability: ~54 mAh/g (57% of 94) at 3520 mA/g

DSC

c1 DSC/conclusion
Well-crystallized V/Fe PBA gives high aqueous capacity through V and Fe multi-redox; fading traces to V-site dissolution raising Rct

KNW

k1 KNW/precedent
Earlier aqueous PBAs: Cu/Fe ~55, Ni/Fe ~50-60, Cu/Ni/Fe 50-65 mAh/g; oxides/phosphates 60-80
k4 KNW/lookup
K-edge or pre-edge energy rises roughly linearly with transition-metal valence
k5 KNW/precedent
Electrodeposited V/Fe PBA films show three couples: V(III)/V(IV)O, Fe(II)/Fe(III), V(IV)/V(V)
k6 KNW/modelshown
Equivalent circuit Re-(CPE1||Rfilm)-(CPE2||Rct-W) separating resistances

limits
  • The 91 mAh/g figure is a single first-discharge measurement on one composition (w/ HCl cathode); it does not partition how much capacity comes from the V-based versus the Fe-based redox couple.
  • The 50-65 mAh/g benchmark is drawn from earlier, different studies/materials rather than a controlled comparison run under the same conditions and rate, so the comparison is cross-study, not a matched experiment.
  • Capacity alone does not confirm that both valence changes are electrochemically active during this specific discharge at 110 mA/g; step 1's evidence for dual valence compensation came from separate characterization, not from this GCD trace.
  • One capacity value cannot establish a general relationship between number of active redox couples and capacity magnitude -- it supports an ordering/comparison at best.
not identifiableWhether the capacity advantage over earlier aqueous PBAs (91 vs 50-65 mAh/g) is due mainly to the added Fe2+/Fe3+ redox contribution, the multi-step V3+/V4+/V5+ redox, or other unstated material differences (e.g., composition, defects) cannot be separated from the capacity number alone.
