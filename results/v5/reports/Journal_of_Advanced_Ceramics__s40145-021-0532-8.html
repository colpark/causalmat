1The original graph

Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Lead-free ceramics for pulsed-power capacitors reach Wrec >1.5 J/cm3 only at high fields (>160 kV/cm)
n2 HYP/hypothesis
A-site defect engineering (Sr vacancies) in BNT-BST relaxors should raise dielectric temperature stability and energy storage at modest fields

DES

n3 DES/base_system
BNT-BST relaxor ferroelectric ceramic (Bi,Na,Sr,Ba)TiO3 as host
n4 DES/modification
Raise Bi on the A site at the expense of Sr, leaving 0.5x Sr vacancies for charge balance
n5 DES/variable_sweep
Vacancy parameter x = 0, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08
n20 DES/down_select
Choose x=0.06 for temperature, frequency, fatigue and pulse-discharge tests

PRC

n6 PRC/forming
Sintering of BNS(0.245-1.5x)[]0.5xB(0.325+x)T ceramics (polished, thermally etched for SEM)

STR

n8 STR/defect/point
Sr-vacancy concentration on the A site rises with x
n10 STR/phase/lattice
TiO6 octahedral distortion strengthens with x
n7 STR/phase/identity
All compositions are single-phase pseudocubic perovskite with no secondary phase
n9 STR/phase/lattice
Unit-cell volume decreases slightly with x
n11 STR/microstructure/feature_size
Grains coarsen with x from ~1.0 to ~2.4 um in dense ceramics
n25 STR/phase/lattice
Unit-cell polarity (A-site cation and Ti-O vibrations) increases with Sr-vacancy content

PRP

n13 PRP/behavior_class
Relaxor character strengthens with x toward an ideal relaxor
n14 PRP/value
A second broad dielectric maximum at Tm2 emerges for x>=0.02 and moves to higher temperature with x
n16 PRP/value
P-E loops slim with x: Pr falls sharply (to ~2.4 uC/cm2 at x=0.06) while Pmax decreases only gradually
n26 PRP/value
The dielectric maximum at Tm1 is suppressed as x increases

MEC

n12 MEC/pathway
Sr vacancies break long-range polar order into polar nanoregions (PNRs) that respond dynamically to field and temperature
n15 MEC/pathway
Tm2 arises from thermal evolution of Sr-vacancy-affected PNRs, not from an abrupt structural phase transition
n24 MEC/pathway
Tm2 marks an abrupt structural (ferroelectric-paraelectric) phase transition

PRF

x=0.06 keeps TCC within +/-15% (base 150 C) from about 40 to 350 C
n18 PRF/figure_of_merit
x=0.06 gives the best storage: Wrec ~1.8 J/cm3 with eta ~72% at 110 kV/cm
n19 PRF/service_capability
x=0.06 keeps Wrec and eta stable from 30 to 150 C at 60 kV/cm
n21 PRF/service_capability
x=0.06 releases its stored energy as a fast pulse (t0.9 ~0.1 us)
n27 PRF/service_capability
x=0.06 keeps Wrec and eta with only a slight decrease from 1 to 100 Hz at 60 kV/cm
n28 PRF/service_capability
x=0.06 keeps Wrec and eta unchanged over 10^5 fatigue cycles
n29 PRF/service_capability
At 80 kV/cm the peak discharge current of x=0.06 stays roughly stable from 20 to 80 C

DSC

n23 DSC/conclusion
Sr-vacancy engineering yields BNT-BST ceramics (x=0.06) with wide-range dielectric stability, Wrec >1.5 J/cm3 and eta >70% below 160 kV/cm, candidates for pulsed-power capacitors
n22 DSC/comparison
x=0.06 stores more energy than reported BNT-based and other lead-free bulks at comparable fields

KNW

k1 KNW/fact
Ionic radius (CN 12): Bi3+ 1.35 A < Sr2+ 1.44 A
k2 KNW/model
Modified Curie-Weiss law; diffuseness gamma = 1 for normal ferroelectric, 2 for ideal relaxor
k3 KNW/lookup
BNT Raman regions: <200 cm-1 A-site cations; 200-400 Ti-O; 400-700 TiO6 octahedra; >700 A1/E LO overlap
k4 KNW/model
Wrec = integral of E dP on the discharge branch; eta = Wrec/(Wrec+Wloss)
k5 KNW/model
Wd = R * integral(i^2 dt) / V for discharge through load R
k6 KNW/lookup
Diffuse, weakened switching-current peaks and slim loops mark polar nanoregions replacing long-range ferroelectric domains
k7 KNW/fact
Charge balance: replacing 3 Sr2+ by 2 Bi3+ leaves one A-site vacancy (0.5x vacancies per x)
