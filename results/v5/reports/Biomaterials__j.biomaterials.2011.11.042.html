mixed0 -> 16.9 -> 20.8 mg/L at 7 days (Co2+ in medium for 0Co, 2Co, 5Co) (ion (cobalt) concentration released into solution, mg/L) against 290 -> 180 -> 127 m2/g (specific surface area (BET), m2/g — a static material/textural property, not present in the cited observations at all)

combining proposition
Release rises as surface area and pore volume fall -- 0 -> 16.9 -> 20.8 mg/L at 7 days against 290 -> 180 -> 127 m2/g -- so a texture-driven mechanism predicts the opposite order and is ruled out in that direction. It does NOT follow that cobalt content sets the release: **cobalt inventory and texture cannot be separated here**, because adding cobalt both lowers the texture and raises the inventory. The identifiable answer is that the two candidate causes are confounded by construction.
limits
  • Three scaffolds, one varying composition: inventory and texture are collinear.
  • Ruling out a direction is not ruling out a cause.
  • The proposition orders two different kinds of quantity side by side — a cumulative solution concentration (mg/L, from obs1) versus a specific surface area (m2/g, not contained in obs1–obs4 or in the cited previous-step pore-size result, which only gives pore diameter ~4-5 nm, not m2/g or cm3/g). Because no same-kind (e.g., concentration-vs-concentration, or area-vs-area) observation pair is available to test the texture-driven mechanism, the inverse-order argument rests on an unverified cross-kind comparison rather than on data given here; this should be flagged as a sourcing/kind limit rather than resolved by substituting different numbers, since none are available in the given observations.
