STR

As-sprayed Al2O3-YSZ is cubic ZrO2, while YSZ is mainly tetragonal ZrO2 with a little cubic phase
n6 STR/phase/fraction
Al2O3-YSZ coating is less crystalline (78.56%) than YSZ (close to 100%)
n9 STR/microstructure/feature_sizepartial
As-sprayed Al2O3-YSZ has larger cracks (average 26 um) than YSZ (12 um)
n12 STR/microstructure/damageshown

n15 STR/phase/fractionpartial

n19 STR/phase/identityshown

n7 STR/microstructure/distributionpartial

n22 STR/microstructure/feature_sizepartial
Grain size in Al2O3-YSZ decreases along the arrow toward the crack

PRP

n11 PRP/valueshown
Al2O3-YSZ undergoes a much larger micro-strain than YSZ during thermal cycling
n13 PRP/behavior_classpartial
Cracks run intergranularly in equiaxed-grain regions (new cracks) and transgranularly in columnar-grain regions (extended original cracks)
n23 PRP/value
Columnar grains partly block crack propagation in the Al2O3-YSZ coating

MEC

n8 MEC/pathway
Poor crystallinity raises microscopic stress concentration and tensile stress near cracks in Al2O3-YSZ
n16 MEC/pathway
The displacive c-to-t transition shrinks volume by about 2%, which with the existing connected cracks drives spalling
n5 MEC/pathway
No Al2O3 phase forms because some Al dissolves into ZrO2 as a solid solution during plasma spraying
n21 MEC/pathway
Some Al aggregates at grain boundaries as an amorphous phase instead of forming Al2O3
n14 MEC/pathwaypartial
After micro-strain, stress concentrates at grain boundaries of equiaxed grains but inside columnar grains, setting intergranular vs transgranular cracking

PRF

n17 PRF/service_capabilitypartial
Al2O3-YSZ coating does not peel after 5 cycles but spalls most seriously between 5 and 10 thermal cycles

DSC

n20 DSC/conclusion
Lower crystallinity (larger cracks, higher micro-strain) and the c-to-t transition shorten the thermal cycle life of Al2O3-YSZ relative to YSZ; columnar grains partly block cracks

KNW

k1 KNW/lookup
A t-ZrO2 peak near 43 deg and splitting of cubic peaks identify a high tetragonal content
k2 KNW/fact
The cubic-to-tetragonal ZrO2 transition is displacive with about 2% volume shrinkage
k3 KNW/precedent
Coatings with poor crystallinity develop microscopic stress concentration and tensile stress
k4 KNW/model
Griffith microcrack theory: strength depends on crack size, and a crack above critical size propagates rapidly
