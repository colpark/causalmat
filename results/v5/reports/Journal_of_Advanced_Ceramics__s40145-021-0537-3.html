1The original graph

Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Electrospun PVDF fiber membranes give weak, unstable piezoelectric output because of their loose structure; wearable PENGs need higher output
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Laminating oriented PZT/PVDF fiber membranes into a porous multi-layer film with PZT-promoted polar beta phase should raise PENG output

DES

n3 DES/modification
Add irregular-block PZT particles to the PVDF electrospinning solution as piezoelectric filler and beta-phase promoter
n4 DES/variable_sweep
Comparison axes: PZT 0-10 wt% (2 wt% steps) at ~220 um, and film thickness ~40-220 um (2-12 layers) at 10 wt%
n5 DES/architectureshown
Target a multi-layered porous fiber film: densified surface fiber layers enclosing separated internal fibers, sandwiched by Ni electrodes and PET

PRC

n6 PRC/formingshown
Electrospin PZT/PVDF (DMF/acetone) solution onto a rotating collector under high voltage to deposit oriented fiber membranes on Al foil
n7 PRC/formingpartial
Stack 2-12 fiber membranes and laminate under 15 MPa for 60 min at ~30 C into ~40-220 um fiber films

STR

n9 STR/phase/fractionshown
Adding PZT raises the PVDF beta-phase content of the fibers from ~76% to a maximum ~83% at 4 wt%, easing to ~81% at 6-10 wt%
n9b STR/phase/identitypartial
Electrospun PVDF fibers are beta-phase dominated where the cast film shows alpha and gamma phase
n10 STR/microstructure/porosityshown
Lamination leaves a porous interior of separated fibers under densified, flattened surface fiber layers
n10b STR/interfaceshown
Laminated fiber membranes merge into one film with no visible interface between layers
n8 STR/microstructure/orientationshown
Electrospun PZT/PVDF fibers are highly aligned along one direction

PRP

n11 PRP/valuepartial
Fiber films carry a stronger surface potential (more bound surface charge) than the 10 wt% PZT/PVDF cast film
n13 PRP/valueshown
Open-circuit output voltage of ~220 um fiber PENGs rises monotonically with PZT content, 7.5 V at 0 wt% to 62 V at 10 wt%
n14 PRP/valueshown
Output voltage of f-P0.10 fiber PENGs rises with film thickness, 6.75 V at 40 um to 62 V at 220 um
n18 PRP/valueshown
At equal thickness the fiber-film PENG gives 3.7x (100 um) and 3.4x (220 um) the voltage of the cast-film PENG
n23 PRP/valueshown
Fiber films are far more compliant and extensible than the cast film, most so perpendicular to the fibers

MEC

n12 MEC/pathwayshown
Aligned PVDF chain dipoles and poled PZT domain dipoles rotate under compression, changing net polarization and pumping electrons through the load

PRF

n15 PRF/service_capabilitypartial
220 um f-P0.10 fiber PENG delivers a maximum 136.9 uW at 10 MOhm load, 6.5x the cast-film PENG
n16 PRF/service_capabilityshown
The fiber PENG harvests human motion: finger pressing, fist beating, one-side bending and arm pressing all give voltage trains
n24 PRF/service_capabilityshown
Output of the f-P0.10 PENG is unchanged after 3000 compression and 3000 bending cycles

DSC

n17 DSC/conclusion
Porous multi-layered, highly oriented PZT/PVDF fiber films make flexible, stable PENGs with several-fold higher output than cast films, suited to human-motion harvesting
