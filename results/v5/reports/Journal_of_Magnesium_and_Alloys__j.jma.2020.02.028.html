Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Conventionally deformed or SPD-processed rare-earth-free AZ61 has unbalanced, moderate yield and tensile strength
n2 HYP/hypothesis
High stored deformation energy from ECAP plus an instantaneous electropulse should complete static recrystallization fast, giving ultrafine grains and strength with ductility

DES

n3 DES/base_system
Commercial hot-extruded AZ61 (Mg-6Al-1Zn) plate, no rare-earth addition
n4 DES/routeshown
Two-stage route: ECAP through a 160 deg die (20 deg outer arc), then electropulsing treatment (EPT) via copper electrodes
n5 DES/variable_sweep
Sweep ECAP temperature (373/423/473 K) and passes (2-5, 8), then EPT pulse duration (25-50 us) and time (5-20 min)
n11 DES/down_select
Variable-temperature ECAP chosen: 8 passes at 423 K then 3 passes at 373 K, since 373 K samples break at pass 5 and 423 K-5 softens by DRX

PRC

n6 PRC/treatment
ECAP of homogenized billets, route Bc, at 373, 423 or 473 K for 2-5 passes
n12 PRC/treatment
Variable-temperature ECAP: 8 passes at 423 K followed by 3 passes at 373 K
n14 PRC/treatment
Electropulsing treatment of the variable-temperature ECAPed samples at 25-30 us pulse duration for 5-10 min
n23 PRC/treatment
Homogenization of machined billets at 623 K for 4 h before ECAP

STR

n7 STR/defect/extendedshown
At 373 K, twins and deformation bands accumulate with passes up to 4 without dynamic recrystallization, storing deformation energy
n8 STR/microstructure/feature_sizepartial
Dynamic recrystallization refines grains at 423 K after 4-5 passes and at 473 K already after 3 passes
n13 STR/microstructure/feature_sizepartial
Variable-temperature ECAP refines grains markedly but leaves them non-equiaxed and elongated, retaining high dislocation density
n15 STR/microstructure/feature_sizeshown
EPT at 25 us-10 min completes static recrystallization into uniform ultrafine equiaxed grains of about 1 um
n16 STR/microstructure/orientationshown
The 25 us-10 min sample has a weakened basal texture and mostly high-angle boundaries; at 30 us texture strengthens slightly and HAGB fraction rises
n21 STR/microstructure/distributionpartial
Fine Mg17Al12 particles are dispersed within grains and along boundaries after ECAP+EPT
n22 STR/microstructure/feature_sizeshown
Starting material: as-extruded grains ~89 um with small recrystallized grains at boundaries; homogenization coarsens grains and leaves twins
n24 STR/microstructure/feature_sizeshown
For a given ECAP state, longer pulses or times take SRX from partial to complete to coarsened; high-temperature ECAP states end coarse and uneven

PRP

n9 PRP/valueshown
Yield strength rises with ECAP passes at 373 K to ~300 MPa at 4 passes; at 423 K it peaks (~230 MPa) at 4 passes then drops at 5
n18 PRP/valueshown
After EPT, 423K-8+373K-3+25us-10min: yield ~330 MPa, UTS ~448 MPa, strain ~0.15; yield slightly lower but UTS and elongation higher than before EPT

MEC

n10 MEC/pathway
Low-temperature ECAP accumulates dislocations and stored energy (strengthening); DRX at 423 K-5 consumes it and lowers YS, so ECAP temperature controls stored energy
n17 MEC/pathway
The high stored energy of variable-temperature ECAP plus an instantaneous high-energy pulse lets recrystallization finish in a short time before grains grow

DSC

n19 DSC/comparisonshown
ECAP+EPT AZ61 lies above all compared AZ61 routes (ECAP, forging, rolling, casting, FSP) in both YS and UTS
n20 DSC/conclusion
Variable-temperature ECAP with a 160 deg die followed by short electropulsing gives ultrafine recrystallized AZ61 with 448 MPa UTS and 15% elongation

KNW

k1 KNW/model
Yield stress as sum of Hall-Petch grain-boundary, Mg17Al12 precipitation and dislocation strengthening terms
k2 KNW/precedent
Literature AZ61 data (Table 4): conventional deformation and SPD routes give YS/UTS pairs used as comparators
k3 KNW/lookup
Fine equiaxed grains replacing twinned deformed grains mark recrystallization; retained twins and elongated grains mark incomplete recrystallization
