HYP

High-temperature dielectric films need higher permittivity with low loss while keeping polymer strength and thermal stability

DES

Poly(arylene ether nitrile) (PAEN), a high-temperature polymer, chosen as matrix
s4 DES/modificationshown
BST core carrying an MPTMS thiol layer decorated with Ag nanoparticles, used as hybrid filler
s5 DES/variable_sweepshown
Ag content of the hybrid filler set at 1, 3 and 5%, compared with pure PAEN

PRC

H2O2 hydroxylation of BST then MPTMS hydrolysis/coupling gives thiol-grafted BST-SH
s7 PRC/synthesis

s8 PRC/forming
BST@Ag particles compounded into PAEN to form composite films (30 wt% filler)

STR

Modified fillers are well bonded to PAEN; filler-matrix interfaces are nearly indistinguishable

DSC
