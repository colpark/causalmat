Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Photothermal SWNT agents need a surface coating giving high tumor uptake with low RES and skin retention
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Raising PEG density and chain length on the PMHC18 coating should prolong SWNT blood circulation and so shift biodistribution

DES

n3 DES/base_systemshown
Coat Hipco SWNTs with amphiphilic PEG-grafted poly(maleic anhydride-alt-1-octadecene) (PEG-PMHC18) by noncovalent wrapping
n4 DES/variable_sweepshown
Sweep PEG length (2 kDa, 5 kDa) against PEG density (5, 10, 25, 50, 100%): ten polymers
n13 DES/down_select
Select 10%-5kPEG-PMHC18 (half-life ~12-13 h) as the coating with balanced tumor, RES and skin uptake
d1 DES/method
Quantify SWNTs in blood and organs by the intensity of the Raman G-band (~1590 cm-1)

PRC

n5 PRC/synthesis
Synthesise PEGylated PMHC18 polymers at varied PEG:MHC18 monomer feed ratios
n6 PRC/synthesis
Sonicate SWNTs with each polymer, centrifuge at 21,000 g for 1 h and filter (0.1 um) to remove bundles and free polymer
n14 PRC/stimulus
Inject 10%-5k SWNTs i.v. (200 ul, 0.5 mg/ml) into 4T1 tumor mice; 2 days later irradiate one tumor with 808 nm laser at 1 W/cm2

STR

n7 STR/microstructure/distribution
Polymer-coated SWNTs are dispersed as single tubes or small bundles, tens to hundreds of nm long
n18 STR/chemistry/composition
The PEG-PMHC18 coatings differ in PEG grafting density, which tracks the PEG:MHC18 feed ratio

PRP

n15 PRP/value
SWNT-laden tumors heat to ~50 C under 808 nm irradiation versus a 1-2 C rise without SWNTs

MEC

n9 MEC/pathway
Denser and longer PEG shells prolong SWNT blood circulation (half-life from a few h up to ~20 h)
n10 MEC/pathway
Longer circulation lowers liver and spleen (RES) uptake of SWNTs
n10b MEC/pathway
Longer circulation raises tumor uptake of SWNTs
n11 MEC/pathway
Ultra-long circulation (>~16 h) makes SWNTs accumulate strongly in the skin dermis
n12 MEC/pathwayshown
Micro-vessel-rich dermis traps nanotubes that circulate for very long times

PRF

n16 PRF/service_capability
SWNT plus laser ablates irradiated tumors and suppresses regrowth over 14 days, unlike untreated, laser-only and SWNT-only groups

DSC

n17 DSC/conclusion
Optimising PEG density and length on PMHC18-coated SWNTs balances tumor uptake against RES and skin retention and enables effective in vivo photothermal tumor ablation
g1 DSC/design_guidance
A blood half-life around 12-13 h is optimal; heavier PEGylation (16-21 h) adds skin accumulation without enough tumor gain
l1 DSC/limitation
The mechanism of skin uptake of long-circulating SWNTs is not established and needs further study
l2 DSC/limitation
Ablation was incomplete in part: 2 of 7 large tumors were only partly burnt and 2 of 5 cleared tumors regrew after ~1 week

KNW

k1 KNW/fact
SWNTs absorb strongly in the NIR (808 nm) and convert the light to heat
k2 KNW/precedent
Earlier SWNT studies showed longer circulation lowers liver and spleen uptake
k3 KNW/lookup
Long-circulating nanoparticles accumulate in tumors by the enhanced permeability and retention effect
k4 KNW/fact
Skin dermis is rich in micro-vessels as small as ~5 um in diameter
