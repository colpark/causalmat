Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Flexible wearable electronics need micro-scale energy storage and sensors printable without binders, additives or metal collectors
n2 HYP/hypothesis
One aqueous additive-free MXene ink, if its rheology suits screen printing, can act as electrode, current collector, interconnect and sensor

DES

n3 DES/base_system
Take additive-free aqueous Ti3C2Tx MXene ink as the only solid of every printed component
n4 DES/variable_sweep
Compare two ink concentrations: H-MXene ~10 wt% (~100 mg mL-1) against L-MXene ~2 wt% (~20 mg mL-1)
n7 DES/down_select
Choose the H-MXene ink for all printing and drop the L-MXene ink
n13 DES/modification
Hybridise the MXene ink (30 wt%) with LTO or LFP powder (70 wt%) to make printable battery-electrode inks

PRC

n5 PRC/synthesis
Selectively etch Ti3AlC2 in LiF/HCl and delaminate the product into an aqueous few-layer MXene nanosheet ink
n8 PRC/depositionshown
Screen-print the H-MXene ink through a patterned mesh onto paper, PET, SiOx-coated steel, rubber, glass, wood and fabric
n14 PRC/depositionshown
Print a MXene current collector first, then the patterned MXene-LTO and MXene-LFP microelectrodes on each side of it

STR

n10 STR/microstructure/orientation
The printed microelectrode is a continuous film of MXene lamellae stacked parallel to the substrate
n15 STR/interfaceshown
LTO and LFP particles sit embedded in a crumpled MXene sheet network that merges continuously into the printed collector
c1 STR/microstructure/shape
The ink solid is delaminated few-layer MXene nanosheets, ultrathin and micrometre-wide
c3 STR/phase/identity
The printed microelectrodes contain crystalline MXene together with spinel LTO or olivine LFP

PRP

n6 PRP/value
The H ink has the elastic shear-thinning rheology screen printing needs; the dilute ink does not
n11 PRP/value
Areal capacitance grows with the number of printed layers, reaching ~1100 mF cm-2 at ten layers
n16 PRP/value
MX-LIMBs deliver ~88 uAh cm-2 at 20 uA cm-2 on a plateau near 1.8 V, falling to ~25 uAh cm-2 at 200 uA cm-2
c2 PRP/descriptor
The printed MXene film forms a continuous conductive network whose resistance falls steeply with the number of printed layers
c8 PRP/value
Printed MXene lines conduct well enough to serve as the metal-free current collectors and interconnects

MEC

n9 MEC/pathwaypartial
Shear flow through the mesh aligns the nanosheets while the instantaneous viscosity recovery freezes the printed line in place

PRF

DSC

Multitasking aqueous MXene inks print every part of high-performance flexible micro-supercapacitors, micro-batteries and a self-powered sensing system
