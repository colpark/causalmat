HYP

n1 HYP/need
Zinc-ion batteries need cathodes holding a high, stable capacity at high voltage over hundreds of cycles
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Co-inserted Zn2+ should convert K2MnFe(CN)6 into a framework that hosts divalent Zn2+ stably

DES

n3 DES/base_system
Use citrate-complexed KMnHCF, K1.6Mn[Fe(CN)6]0.94*0.63H2O, a vacancy-poor Prussian blue analogue, as cathode
n4 DES/route
Use 30 m KFSI + 1 m Zn(CF3SO3)2 electrolyte for a wide stability window and a 1.9 V redox potential

PRC

n5 PRC/synthesis
Controlled precipitation of KMnHCF at room temperature with citrate in the reactant
n7 PRC/stimulus
Galvanostatic cycling of the KMnHCF-Zn cell at 0.2 A/g for up to 400 cycles

STR

n6 STR/phase/identitypartial
As-prepared KMnHCF is monoclinic P21/n, a 3D open framework of N-coordinated Mn and C-coordinated Fe
n8a STR/chemistry/composition
Zn2+ becomes the dominant inserted cation, near 50 at% of the cathode metals from the 20th cycle
n8b STR/chemistry/composition
Mn is progressively lost from the cathode, falling to near zero by the 100th cycle
n9 STR/phase/identity
The electrode converts from monoclinic KMnHCF to rhombohedral KZnHCF, K2Zn3[Fe(CN)6]2*9H2O, within 20 cycles
n10 STR/phase/site_occupancyshown
Inserted Zn2+ takes the lattice sites of the framework rather than the Fe(CN)6 vacancies
n24 STR/chemistry/bondingshown
The MnN6 octahedra are completely replaced by ZnN4 tetrahedra linked to octahedral FeC6
n13 STR/chemistry/bonding
The d-pi covalent interaction that favours the monoclinic phase is suppressed when Zn2+ is inserted
n14 STR/chemistry/bonding
Coulomb attraction between Zn2+ and N- exceeds that between K+ and N-, favouring the rhombohedral phase
n23 STR/microstructure/shape
As-prepared KMnHCF forms 500-600 nm single-crystalline cubic particles with some surface defects

PRP

n16 PRP/behavior_class
After conversion the electrode stores Zn2+ by a single-phase solid-solution reaction, not the initial biphasic transition
n18 PRP/value
Reversible capacity settles near 100 mAh/g after the first 100 cycles, down from 138 mAh/g
n25 PRP/value
The converted electrode discharges on one plateau averaging 1.74 V, giving 150 Wh/kg

MEC

n11 MEC/pathway
Zn2+ relieves the electronic degeneracy and drives an intense Jahn-Teller distortion that breaks the MnN6 framework
n12 MEC/tradeoffshown
Zn2+ raises Coulomb attraction and weakens d-pi covalency, tipping the balance to the rhombohedral phase
n15 MEC/pathway
FeC6 linked to ZnN4 through longer cyanide bridges opens wider channels that accommodate divalent Zn2+
n17 MEC/identification
The Fe3+/Fe2+ couple is the main redox centre of the converted KZnHCF electrode

PRF

n19 PRF/service_capability
98% capacity retention from the 100th to the 400th cycle at 0.2 A/g with 99-100% coulombic efficiency

DSC

n20 DSC/conclusion
Zn2+-induced conversion of KMnHCF into KZnHCF yields a stable high-voltage zinc-ion cathode
n21 DSC/comparison
Electrochemically converted KZnHCF delivers about 100 mAh/g against 65 mAh/g for directly synthesised KZnHCF

KNW

k1 KNW/precedent
Earlier reports show Zn2+ switching between cavity sites and vacant Fe(CN)6 sites in PBAs
k2 KNW/model
Henkelman's theory: PBA phase stability is set by competing Coulomb attraction, d-pi covalency and Pauli repulsion
k3 KNW/fact
Degenerate electronic states destabilise an atomic configuration; Jahn-Teller distortion relieves the degeneracy
k4 KNW/lookup
JCPDS card 33-1061 is rhombohedral K2Zn3[Fe(CN)6]2*9H2O
k5 KNW/fact
Fe3+(CN)6 is larger than reduced Fe2+(CN)6, so oxidation contracts the lattice and shifts peaks higher
