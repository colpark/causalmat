Our argument graph in full, in stage order. Spine claims first, then the observation nodes with the panels behind them and the image verdict recorded for each.

HYP

n1 HYP/need
Biodegradable Mg implants corrode too fast and risk infection; a coating must slow corrosion and fight bacteria
n2 HYP/hypothesis
Loading the natural inhibitor lawsone into a PCL coating should raise corrosion resistance and add antibacterial activity

DES

n3 DES/base_system
PCL (biocompatible, slowly degrading polyester) chosen as the coating matrix on low-Al AZ31 Mg alloy
n4 DES/modification
Embed lawsone (2-hydroxy-1,4-naphthoquinone) in PCL as a non-toxic corrosion inhibitor and antibacterial agent
n5 DES/architectureshown
Bilayer coating: PCL-lawsone functional inner layer under a pure PCL top layer that limits inhibitor leaching

PRC

n6 PRC/treatment
Alkaline treatment of polished AZ31 to grow a temporary Mg(OH)2 passive layer before coating (AZ31-OH)
n7 PRC/deposition
PCL-lawsone inner layer then pure PCL top layer applied on AZ31-OH (PCL-LS); pure PCL coating as control

STR

n8 STR/chemistry/bonding
Alkali treatment leaves a hydroxyl-rich Mg(OH)2 oxide layer on the AZ31 surface
n9 STR/microstructure/distribution
PCL-LS coating fully and uniformly covers the pretreated substrate without visible defects
n9b STR/interface
No gap at the PCL-LS/oxide/substrate interfaces in cross-section
n10 STR/chemistry/composition
Lawsone resides in the inner PCL layer, beneath a lawsone-free PCL top layer
n18 STR/microstructure/feature_size
Polymer bilayer about 9-10 um thick on an oxide layer about 3.5-4 um thick

PRP

n11 PRP/value
Corrosion resistance in Hank's solution ranks AZ31 < AZ31-OH < PCL < PCL-LS; PCL-LS is the noblest
n11b PRP/behavior_class
Bare and alkali-treated AZ31 break down by pitting in polarization; coated samples show no pitting
n14 PRP/value
PCL-LS keeps its barrier impedance over 7 days of immersion while pure PCL loses it steadily
n16 PRP/value
PCL-LS coating is antibacterial against E. coli and S. aureus; AZ31, AZ31-OH and PCL are not
n19 PRP/value
Coatings make the surface hydrophobic (~96 deg vs 30-40 deg uncoated); lawsone does not change wettability
n20 PRP/value
Cross-cut adhesion of PCL-LS is better on AZ31-OH (4B) than on untreated AZ31 (3B)
n22 PRP/value
Coated samples give higher hFOB viability than uncoated at 3 and 5 days; lawsone does not reduce cytocompatibility

MEC

n12 MEC/pathway
The PCL layer acts as a physical barrier that blocks electrolyte penetration to the Mg substrate
n13 MEC/pathway
Released lawsone forms insoluble metal-lawsone complexes that fill active corrosion sites and inhibit corrosion
n21 MEC/pathway
Surface hydroxyl groups interact chemically with PCL groups and strengthen coating/substrate adhesion
n23 MEC/pathway
Lawsone quinone groups react with nucleophilic amino acids of bacterial membrane proteins and inactivate them

PRF

n15 PRF/service_capability
In 7-day Hank's immersion at 37 C, PCL-LS best protects AZ31: least H2, smallest pH rise, nearly intact surface

DSC

n17 DSC/conclusion
A PCL/lawsone bilayer coating gives biomedical AZ31 both durable corrosion protection and antibacterial activity

KNW

k5 KNW/precedent

k6 KNW/precedent

combining proposition
Putting the previous step's result together with this evidence shows that the hydroxyl-rich Mg(OH)2 oxide layer that alkali treatment leaves on AZ31 (present in the AZ31-OH sample) is not itself antibacterial: disc b (AZ31-OH) shows no inhibition halo on either E. coli or S. aureus plates, matching untreated AZ31 (disc a) and PCL (disc c). Only disc d (PCL-LS coating) produces a clear halo on both organisms. So the antibacterial effect belongs specifically to the PCL-LS coating, not to the alkali-formed oxide layer.
limits
  • The halo readout (obs1) is a qualitative presence/absence call, not a quantified measure of bacterial kill or potency
  • The evidence rules out the oxide layer alone and PCL alone as sufficient causes, but does not identify what within the PCL-LS coating produces the effect
  • No test isolates whether the oxide layer plays any supporting role in combination with the PCL-LS coating, since PCL-LS is only tested as fabricated
  • Single halo observation per disc/organism with no replicate or dose-response data given to weigh the reliability of the 'none' calls for discs a, b, c

scoring target: the combining proposition together with each named limit — 5 required elements. Stating that the causes cannot be separated, or that the result is not identifiable, without giving the combining proposition and its limits, earns nothing. The confound is the conclusion of the comparison, not a substitute for it.
