THE KEY UNDER AUDIT

Proposition: Because the same mesoporous PCO host (5-7 nm pores, hysteretic type IV isotherm) was used in both the Li and Na half-cells, the sharp contrast in cycling outcome -- Li/PCO retaining ~840-870 mAh/g through 40 cycles (initial reversible ~870) versus Na/PCO collapsing from ~870 mAh/g (1st discharge) to ~70 mAh/g by cycle 40 -- shows that the mesopore structure by itself is not sufficient to produce stable reversible capacity for both alkali ions. The identical pore architecture yields durable performance with Li but not with Na, so whatever the pores contribute (buffering, ion access) is evidently adequate for Li but is being overridden by some Na-specific process.
