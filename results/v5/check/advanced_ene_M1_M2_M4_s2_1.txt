Proposition: Combined with the prior step's finding that Zn-N bonds form at pyridinic-N sites (with the N 1s state shifting toward graphitic nitrogen), the DFT results at this step give a thermodynamic reason for that bond formation: charge transfer to zinc is more negative at every N-doped site (-0.032 to -0.037 e) than at pristine sites (-0.023 to -0.027 e, obs5), the transferred electron density sits in the zinc-substrate gap on N-doped graphene rather than as a detached lobe above pristine graphene (obs6), and the computed adsorption energies are negative at top/hollow/bridge sites on N-doped graphene (-0.072/-0.089/-0.077 eV) versus positive on pristine graphene (+0.030/+0.014/+0.029 eV, obs7).
