the proposition it supports

The single low-angle XRD reflection together with the TEM channel stripes and regular pore lattice show that 2Co- and 5Co-MBG retain an ordered mesoporous channel structure with pores ~4-5 nm n8, while the type IV hysteresis, the fall in N2 uptake, and the reduced pore-size-distribution peaks show that Co lowers mesoporosity, dropping surface area from 290 to 180 and 127 m2/g and pore volume from 0.30 to 0.19 and 0.15 cm3/g n9

the assumption the inference needs

The XRD reflection and the TEM fields imaged are taken as representative of the bulk ordered structure, and the N2 sorption-derived surface area, pore volume and pore-size distribution are taken as reliable quantitative measures of mesoporosity across the three compositions writer inference

end to end
  • Small-angle XRD shows a single low-angle reflection in all three samples (MBG, 2Co-MBG, 5Co-MBG), at 2theta ~1.37, ~1.47 and ~1.35 degrees respectively obs1.
  • TEM images show parallel channel stripes over ~200 nm and a regular pore lattice with a period of ~5-6 nm obs2.
  • N2 adsorption isotherms for all three samples show a type IV hysteresis step at P/P0 ~0.45-0.6, consistent with a mesoporous solid obs3.
  • Together this diffraction, imaging and isotherm evidence establishes that 2Co- and 5Co-MBG keep an ordered mesoporous channel structure with pores of about 4-5 nm n8.
  • N2 uptake at P/P0 ~1 falls with cobalt content, from ~8.7 mmol/g (0Co) to ~5.6 mmol/g (2Co) to ~4.35 mmol/g (5Co) obs4.
  • The pore-size distribution peaks at ~5 nm for 0Co and 2Co (with the 2Co peak lower) and shifts to ~4.2 nm for 5Co obs5.
  • These reductions in gas uptake and pore-size peak, together with surface area falling from 290 to 180 to 127 m2/g and pore volume from 0.30 to 0.19 to 0.15 cm3/g, establish that adding cobalt lowers the mesoporosity of the glass even while the ordered channel architecture from step 1 is retained n9.
  • Taking the retained ordered mesoporous structure and reduced mesoporosity as given, the Co2+ concentration measured in the release medium rises with time for both doped scaffolds, reaching ~8.9/17.9/20.8 mg/L for 5Co and ~4.7/10.5/16.9 mg/L for 2Co at 1/3/7 days, while 0Co stays at 0 obs1.
  • This dose- and time-dependent release establishes that Co2+ release is set by cobalt content, with 5Co-MBG releasing faster and more than 2Co-MBG, both rising over 7 days n11.
  • A western blot shows the HIF-1alpha band faint for MBG and 2Co-MBG but clearly stronger for 5Co-MBG, with equal alpha-tubulin loading controls across lanes obs2.
  • This blot establishes that the released Co2+ acts as a hypoxia mimic, raising HIF-1alpha in BMSCs, which in turn drives VEGF n12.
  • Separately, cumulative ampicillin release from 2Co-MBG is measured at ~340 ug at 8 h, ~670 ug at 24 h, ~780 ug at 72 h, and ~795 ug at 168 h where it plateaus obs3.
  • This release curve establishes that 2Co-MBG releases ampicillin over roughly the first 72 hours before the release plateaus n15.
  • Relative bacterial survival with ampicillin-loaded scaffolds falls to about 105%, 81% and 52% at days 1, 3 and 7, compared with the blank scaffold held at 100% and drug-free scaffolds at 110-118% obs4.
  • This survival data establishes that ampicillin-loaded 2Co-MBG lowers bacterial survival relative to both the blank and the drug-free scaffolds n16.
  • Taking the Co2+ release rate, the hypoxia-mimic mechanism and the antibacterial performance from step 2 as given, the same HIF-1alpha blot (faint for MBG and 2Co-MBG, clearly stronger for 5Co-MBG, equal alpha-tubulin) is reused to reaffirm that released Co2+ raises HIF-1alpha in BMSCs and drives VEGF n12.
  • The same bacterial-survival series (~105%, ~81%, ~52% at days 1, 3, 7 versus blank at 100% and drug-free scaffolds at 110-118%) is reused to reaffirm that ampicillin-loaded 2Co-MBG lowers bacterial survival versus the blank and drug-free scaffolds n16.
  • No panel in this step supplies a new mechanism directly linking the hypoxia-mimic pathway to the antibacterial pathway; the step restates the two established effects side by side rather than connecting them writer inference.
