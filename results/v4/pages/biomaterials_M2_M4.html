  • This mechanistic claim rests on western blot data in which the HIF-1alpha band is faint for MBG and 2Co-MBG but clearly stronger for 5Co-MBG, with equal alpha-tubulin loading controls ruling out a loading artifact obs2.
  • Carrying the same hypoxia-mimic/HIF-1alpha/VEGF mechanism forward as established, the trace treats it as the basis for a second, separate claim about scaffold performance n12.
  • That second claim is that ampicillin-loaded 2Co-MBG lowers bacterial survival relative to a blank control and to drug-free scaffolds n16.
