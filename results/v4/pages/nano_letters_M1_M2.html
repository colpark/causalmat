what remains open

end to end
  • The N2 isotherm shows adsorption/desorption hysteresis from P/P0 ~0.5 to 1.0 with an uptake of ~110 cm3/g obs1.
  • The pore size distribution inset peaks at ~5.5 and ~6.9 nm with a tail below 4 nm obs2.
  • SEM shows agglomerated faceted particles of ~250-500 nm with open gaps between them, though the internal mesopores are not resolved at this length scale obs3.
  • Together these observations establish that PCO is mesoporous, giving a hysteretic type IV isotherm and pores mostly 5-7 nm n5.
  • Taking this mesoporous pore structure as given, the Li cell shows a first discharge with a long plateau at ~1.05 V reaching ~1330 mAh/g, and a first charge of ~870 mAh/g with a plateau near ~2.0 V obs2.
  • Across subsequent Li cycling, charge capacity rises from ~870 mAh/g at cycle 1 to ~1050 mAh/g near cycle 17 and settles back to ~840 mAh/g by cycle 40, with coulombic efficiency ~96% obs3.
  • This cycling record establishes that Li/PCO keeps ~840-870 mAh/g after 40 cycles at 0.1 C, starting from an initial reversible capacity of ~870 mAh/g n7.
  • The Na cell shows a first discharge of ~865 mAh/g with a plateau near ~0.15 V, a first charge of only ~470 mAh/g, and just ~60 mAh/g remaining by cycle 40 obs1.
  • Na cycling further shows discharge capacity collapsing from ~870 mAh/g at cycle 1 to ~480 mAh/g at cycle 2 and down to ~70 mAh/g by cycle 40 obs4.
  • This record establishes that Na/PCO fades from ~870 mAh/g on first discharge to ~70 mAh/g after 40 cycles at 0.1 C, a sharp contrast to the stable Li behavior even though both electrodes were built on the same mesoporous, 5-7 nm pore architecture characterised in step 1 n8.
  • Because the pore structure from step 1 was held as the common starting point for both alkali systems, the divergence in fade behavior implies that the mesoporosity alone does not account for cycling stability, and some other Na-specific mechanism must be responsible, though the packet does not characterise it directly writer inference.

weakest step: step 2 — Step 2 links the step-1 pore structure to the Li/Na performance contrast, but the porosity data (obs1-obs3 of step 1) describe the pristine electrode, not a post-cycling structural state, so the claim that the same mesoporous architecture underlies both the stable Li result and the failed Na result rests on an unverified continuity assumption rather than direct post-mortem evidence.
