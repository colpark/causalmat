key — the combining proposition
Together, the volumetric time-course (A) and the photographic appearance of the irradiated site (B) show that the reduction in tumor volume seen in the SWNT+Laser group over 14 days corresponds to a visibly flat, dark (ablated) tumor site, whereas the control groups that grew to ~5-8x their starting volume also appear visibly raised (unresolved) in the photo. The photo corroborates that the numeric suppression in A reflects actual physical tumor ablation at the treated site rather than a measurement artifact, and the volume curve shows that this visible flattening is accompanied by a statistically significant (p<0.0001), sustained (14-day) suppression rather than a transient effect.
limits
  • The photograph (B) is a qualitative, apparently single-snapshot/representative image per group, not a quantified or statistically powered measurement, so it cannot by itself confirm the p<0.0001 group-level effect reported in A
  • Neither observation establishes the cellular or histological mechanism of ablation (e.g., photothermal cell death vs. other tissue damage)
  • The pairing does not extend the evidence beyond the 14-day window of A or beyond whatever single timepoint the photo in B was taken at
  • The photo gives only a visual/appearance descriptor (flat dark mark vs raised) and cannot substitute for or add a numeric magnitude to the volume data

generator spine_edgedepth 2lane otherdependency realinference follows, weaklycausal strength associative
key — the combining proposition
Taken together, Observation A (blood SWNT signal persists longer with higher PEG density and with 5 kDa versus 2 kDa PEG, e.g. 10%-2k cleared by 12 h vs 5%-5k cleared by 24 h) and Observation B (skin Raman maps show dense, extended SWNT signal for 100%-5k versus only a few sparse spots for 10%-5k) are mutually consistent with formulations that circulate longer also showing greater skin deposition, since both readouts move in the same direction with increasing PEG density.
limits
  • Observation A's specific paired example (10%-2k at 12 h vs 5%-5k at 24 h) varies PEG density and PEG molecular weight simultaneously, so density's individual contribution to the circulation-time trend is not isolated from that pair alone
  • Observation B does not report a blood clearance/circulation time for the actual imaged formulations (100%-5k, 10%-5k), so the skin-accumulation comparison is not tied to a directly measured blood half-life for those same samples
  • Observation A is a time-course readout of blood decay while Observation B is a single-endpoint spatial/imaging readout of skin signal density, so the pair assumes rather than tests that a longer circulation window translates into more deposition
  • The variable shared across A and B is PEG density/chemistry, not circulation duration itself, so the causal step from prolonged circulation to skin accumulation is inferred from parallel trends, not measured directly within the same specimens
not identifiableWhether prolonged blood circulation itself drives skin accumulation, or whether PEG density independently increases skin deposition regardless of circulation time, cannot be separated from this evidence, because PEG density covaries with both blood persistence (Observation A) and skin signal density (Observation B) by construction.
