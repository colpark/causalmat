Panels confirm the printed numbers. F6a: Cu2S and RGO-Cu2S both show a broad reduction wave near -1.2 to -1.3 V with peak currents around -40 to -45 mA/cm2, while Pt is essentially flat on this scale. F6b (magnified Pt) shows Pt's own reduction/oxidation currents span only about -0.4 to +1.1 mA/cm2 — consistent with the reported ~-0.45 mA/cm2 Pt peak, and explaining why Pt needed a separate magnified panel. F5a shows J-V curves with the same ordering: RGO-Cu2S (~16 mA/cm2) > Cu2S (~15) > Pt (~13) for Jsc, and Voc also tracks the same order, converging near 0.55-0.56 V.

The combination supports a consistent rank ordering between counter-electrode electrocatalytic activity (I3-/I- reduction current in CV) and photovoltaic output (Jsc, and to a lesser extent Voc): RGO-Cu2S ≥ Cu2S >> Pt in both datasets. That correlation is real and visible directly in the panels.

What it leaves open: the CV is a three-electrode measurement of the counter electrode alone in a model electrolyte, not the full assembled cell, so it cannot by itself establish that counter-electrode catalysis is the mechanism driving the Jsc/Voc differences rather than some other cell-level factor. No replicate curves or error bars are shown, so the magnitude of the ranking's robustness is unverified from these panels alone.
