Coulombic efficiency (open triangles, right axis) for all three routes clusters near 95–100% with no visually distinguishable separation between conditions.

What this justifies: the figure is the direct source of the capacity-ranking and first-cycle-value claims — no inference or extrapolation is needed, and the two measurements are essentially two different readouts of the same single panel.

What it leaves open: the panel is descriptive, not mechanistic — it shows that HCl-treated co-precipitation outperforms the other routes but gives no evidence here for why (e.g., particle morphology, phase purity, or composition differences that might mediate a "service_capability" or structure-value link). It also gives no error bars/replicate counts, no data beyond 250 cycles, and no indication that HCl's effect on capacity is distinguishable from other synthesis differences that may co-vary between routes.
