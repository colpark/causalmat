The combination does not account for the downstream Tg/bonding observation. The upstream evidence (FTIR new bands + the earlier-established TGA mass-loss pairing) supports only that a distinct CH2/Si-O-bearing species is chemically present and thermally quantifiable on the BST particle surface after silane treatment — consistent with, but not proof of, covalent Si-O-BST grafting versus physisorption (per the recorded caveats). The DSC panel is a separate, downstream measurement on BST@Ag/PAEN composites: it shows Tg is depressed 19-30 C relative to pure PAEN, but nothing in the FTIR/TGA evidence measures interfacial adhesion, chain-mobility restriction, or plasticization mechanism. The Tg shift is equally consistent with plasticization by residual/unreacted silane, particle-matrix interfacial slippage, or dispersion effects as with "improved bonding." No calibration links the FTIR/TGA signal magnitude to the size of the Tg shift, and the BST@Ag composite differs from the BST-SH sample characterized by FTIR, leaving the causal link between surface chemistry and Tg change asserted but not measured.
