Partial account, with a clear gap.

What matches: F10a (photocurrent) and the earlier-established PL ordering both rank Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS > Pd-ZIS > Ag-ZIS > ZIS. F8b's H2 evolution rates (125.4 > 34.5 > 3.2 > 1.5 umol/h) and F8a's cumulative time-course reproduce that identical order for those same four samples throughout the whole irradiation window, not just at one endpoint. So the upstream charge-separation proxy (photocurrent+PL) is consistent with, and plausibly explains part of, the downstream photocatalytic performance ranking (PRF/service_capability = H2 evolution) for the four samples it actually covers.

What it leaves open: F8a/F8b also report Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS and Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS, which rank 2nd and 3rd in H2 evolution — but neither was measured by photocurrent or PL, so the upstream evidence says nothing about them. Also, the fold-differences are wildly mismatched in scale: photocurrent spans roughly 5x (2.1 vs 0.4 uA/cm2) while H2 evolution spans 83.6x (125.4 vs 1.5 umol/h), so charge-separation ranking cannot, by itself, be the sole quantitative driver of the H2 rate gap — other factors (cocatalyst chemistry, active-site density, redox kinetics) are left unaccounted for.
