The three panels are consistent with, but do not fully establish, the downstream PV result.

F6a shows RGO-Cu2S and Cu2S with large, nearly overlapping reduction peaks near -1.2 to -1.25 V (~-43 and ~-41 mA/cm2), while Pt is essentially flat at this scale. F6b, the magnified Pt trace, confirms Pt does have a real but much smaller redox response (peak oxidation current ~1.1 mA/cm2, asymmetric loop), so the near-zero appearance in F6a is a scale artifact, not absence of activity — Pt is simply ~40x less active for this sulfide/polysulfide couple. F5a shows Jsc and Voc both ranked RGO-Cu2S > Cu2S > Pt, matching the CV activity ranking.

This combination supports a qualitative correlation: counter-electrode catalytic activity toward the S2-/Sn2- redox couple tracks with photovoltaic Jsc/Voc ranking, plausible via lower charge-transfer resistance improving redox regeneration. It does not establish causation — no fill factor, series/charge-transfer resistance (e.g., EIS), or PCE data are shown here, and RGO-Cu2S vs Cu2S have nearly identical CV peaks (~43 vs ~41) yet show a larger Jsc gap (16.0 vs 15.0) than that similarity would predict. Voc's dependence on CE catalysis specifically is also left unexplained by these panels alone.
