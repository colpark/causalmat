Report on [0001] deformation stress vs. step 2 value:

F10a (a specific [0001] tensile test at RT) shows plastic flow via kink bands (confirmed by the kink-band microstructure in F10b) proceeding at a stress of roughly 55–80 MPa, serrated and plateauing near 75–80 MPa, out to ~45% plastic strain.

Step 2's report for the same [0001] orientation (F2a/F2b) treats it very differently: the yield-stress plot marks [0001] only with a "fracture" square (~350 MPa at RT, ~255–260 MPa at 300°C, black "X" markers on the stress-strain panels), and the stress-strain curves in F2b/F2c show [0001] as a near-vertical elastic line rising straight to that fracture point with no plotted plastic strain — i.e., no yield/plastic regime is reported for [0001] in the summary figure.

Comparison: the kink-band flow stress in F10a (~60-80 MPa) is roughly 4-5x lower than the fracture stress attributed to [0001] loading in F2. This implies that when kink bands nucleate, [0001]-oriented crystals can deform plastically along a low-stress route that bypasses the high-stress elastic-to-fracture path shown as the default [0001] behavior in F2.
