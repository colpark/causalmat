The two panels are temporally consistent but do not jointly establish a mechanistic link.

F9 (XRD) shows that after 5 cycles the coating gives strong t-ZrO2 peaks (a marked peak near 43° and a split doublet near 73–74°, matching the t-ZrO2 reference sticks), while after 10 cycles those same peaks are markedly reduced in the inset boxes. F8 (photographs) shows the coupon fully intact at 5 cycles, with corner spallation first appearing by 10 cycles and then persisting/slightly enlarging through 40 cycles.

Together, this supports only a temporal correlation: the interval (5→10 cycles) over which the t-ZrO2 signature weakens is the same interval over which corner spallation first appears, consistent with a link between tetragonal-phase degradation and onset of local coating loss/service-capability decline.

What it leaves open: the XRD trace is not shown to be taken from the spalled corner specifically, so there is no spatial co-registration proving the phase change occurred at the failure site rather than elsewhere on the coupon. No monoclinic-ZrO2 reference is shown, so whether the weakened t-ZrO2 signal reflects t→m transformation, loss of coating material, or beam/sampling variation is undetermined. Causation (phase change driving spallation, vs. both being consequences of a third process) is not established, only coincidence in cycle number.
