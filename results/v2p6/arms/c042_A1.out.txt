Combination supports: The EDS maps (F5e) show C, Zn, N filling the whole particle outline while P appears only as sparse, scattered dots within that same outline for both the DNA- and RNA-aptamer samples — consistent with P (aptamer phosphate backbone) being a minor, non-uniformly distributed component riding on the ZIF-8 particle rather than a bulk constituent. The zeta-potential panel (F5d) shows charge inversion from a strongly positive value for bare BSA@ZIF-8 to negative values for both aptamer-conjugated samples. Together these support the qualitative conclusion that aptamer (anionic, phosphate-bearing) has been associated with the BSA@ZIF-8 particle surface, consistent with successful aptamer modification/conjugation.

What is left open: the sparse P signal does not establish uniform coverage, surface-vs-interior localization, quantitative loading, or covalent attachment — it only shows presence within the particle outline. The zeta shift shows a charge state change but not mechanism (adsorption vs conjugation) or aptamer integrity/folding. On values specifically: the stated upstream numbers (+25, -16, -14.5 mV) are close to but not identical to the printed caption (+23.5, -15.7, -13.8 mV; also "eV" is a typo for mV); given visible error bars on the bar chart, the exact printed digits read as approximate/rounded rather than precise bar-top values.
