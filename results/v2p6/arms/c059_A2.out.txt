What the combination supports: a consistent correlation — greater visible-light absorption (broader, redshifted edge) tracks with greater MO degradation, and this ordering is stable across all three co-doped compositions and all three measured time points, not just an endpoint coincidence.

What it leaves open: the UV-vis data establish an optical property (absorption), not a causal mechanism for the degradation rate. Neither panel shows the light source used in the degradation assay (visible vs UV), electron-hole pair lifetime, surface area/crystallite size, or radical/charge-transfer measurements, so whether enhanced visible absorption actually drives the higher degradation — versus confounds like differing surface area, defect density, or dopant redox chemistry that also vary with Ce content — is not established by these two panels alone. The evidence is consistent with the claim but does not mechanistically prove it.
