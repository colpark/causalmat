The XRD panel (F6a) for HP5 shows indexed reflections only for ZrB2, SiC, C (graphite), and BN — no peaks marked AlN or Al2O3, consistent with the claim [XRD] that no AlN/Al2O3 phase was detected. The hardness panel (F2b) shows the HP curve rising from ~18.0 GPa at 0 wt% AlN to ~20.3 GPa at 1 wt%, then easing to ~19.4 and ~19.2 GPa at 3 and 5 wt%, matching the stated [MECH:hardness] values.

Combined, the two panels support only a co-occurrence: in hot-pressed samples, AlN addition raises and then plateaus hardness even though AlN itself is not resolvable as a distinct crystalline phase by XRD at any addition level shown. This is consistent with AlN acting via a mechanism that does not require a detectable separate AlN phase — e.g., solid solution, grain-boundary effect, or a reaction product too dilute/amorphous for XRD — but the XRD panel by itself cannot identify which mechanism, since XRD only rules out AlN and Al2O3 as detectable phases, it does not identify what did happen to the added AlN or why hardness peaks at 1 wt% and declines slightly thereafter. The two panels together do not establish causation between AlN's phase fate and the hardness trend; they only show both facts are true simultaneously for HP5. Confirming a causal/mechanistic link (e.g., densification, grain refinement, or solute effects) would require additional evidence (density, grain size, or microstructural data) not present in these two panels.
