What the combination supports: the EDS map (upstream, structural) and the JMAK kinetics (downstream, mechanistic) are consistent with a shared narrative — a surface-decorating C/Co catalyst phase on C10 particles co-occurring with a lower Avrami exponent (1.32) than the uncatalyzed C0 case (1.59), which the authors would read as a shift toward more interface/surface-controlled (vs. bulk nucleation-and-growth) desorption kinetics upon catalyst addition.

What it leaves open: the EDS map only images C10, so there is no matched EDS comparison for C0 to show the catalyst/surface layer is actually absent or different there — the contrast with η=1.59 is inferred, not imaged. The EDS map is also a single static micrograph (no link to any specific temperature or time point in the JMAK series), so it cannot on its own explain why η changes with temperature within either alloy, nor establish that the surface C/Co layer is mechanistically causal for the lower exponent rather than merely co-present. The linear JMAK fits establish the kinetic exponents but not the physical nucleation/growth model each η value implies.
