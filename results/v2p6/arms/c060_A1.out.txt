Panels F4a–e show dielectric constant (and loss) vs. temperature at four frequencies for x=0, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08.

Claim A is well supported: at 1 kHz (black curve) the single peak drops from a value consistent with ~6750 at x=0 (a) to ~2700 at x=0.08 (e). A distinct second feature, explicitly labeled Tm2, appears in every doped composition (b–e, x≥0.02) but is absent/unlabeled at x=0 (a, single broad peak only). Tm2 shifts rightward across the series, from roughly 200 °C (b) toward ~250–260 °C (e), matching the stated ~200→260 °C range. One caveat: in panel b the Tm2 region reads more as a shoulder/plateau than a sharp maximum, becoming a clearly resolved peak only from c onward — so "second maximum" is a slightly generous description at x=0.02 specifically.

Claim B cannot be evaluated from these panels at all: none of F4a–e show a TCC (temperature coefficient of capacitance) curve or ±15% dashed reference lines — they are dielectric constant/loss plots, a different figure. So the upstream evidence here accounts fully for the Tm1/Tm2 (PRP-value type) observation but leaves the TCC/±15% qualification claim entirely unaddressed/open — it would need to be checked against a separate TCC figure not included in this set.
