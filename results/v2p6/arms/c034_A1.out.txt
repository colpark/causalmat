The upstream DFT trace (A) gives ΔG_H* for Pd(111)/AgxPd1-x(111)/Ag(111): -0.31, -0.08, 0.12, 0.28, 0.49 eV — plotted in F9a exactly as reported, forming a volcano-like ordering with Pd0.75Ag0.25 (-0.08 eV) closest to thermoneutral. The downstream measurement (B) and panels F8a/F8b show photocatalytic H2 evolution on the corresponding AgxPd1-x-ZIS cocatalyst composites: Ag0.25Pd0.75 (125.4) > Ag0.5Pd0.5 (57.3) > Ag0.75Pd0.25 (43.5) > Pd (34.5) > Ag (3.2) µmol/h, with F8a confirming the same rank order across 5 h of irradiation.

The rank order of measured H2 rates matches the rank order of ΔG_H* proximity to zero exactly, composition-for-composition (best-to-worst identical in both datasets). This supports treating the computed H* adsorption free energy as the mechanistic driver of the alloy-composition activity trend, consistent with a Sabatier-type optimum at Pd0.75Ag0.25.

What it leaves open: the DFT is computed on idealized bare Pd/Ag(111) surfaces, while the measured rates are for AgxPd1-x deposited as a cocatalyst on the ZIS (ZnIn2S4) semiconductor — so light absorption, charge separation/transfer at the ZIS interface, particle size/morphology, and possible surface segregation are not modeled and could independently affect the observed rates; the match is a rank-order correlation, not a demonstrated causal mechanism, and no intermediate charge-transfer or kinetic data connect the two.
