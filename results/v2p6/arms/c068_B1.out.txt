Panel F2a is the discharge-capacity/coulombic-efficiency plot itself: it directly shows the three curves (co-precipitation w/ HCl, co-precipitation w/o HCl, simple mixing w/o HCl) ranked red > blue > black across all ~250 cycles, with initial values reading roughly 91, 81, and 61 mAh/g respectively, matching Observations A and B exactly. This panel is the source of the downstream capacity data, not an independent check on it, so it confirms the numbers were read correctly but adds no new evidence about crystallinity or PRP/PRF mechanism.

Given the earlier-established HRTEM result, the combination supports only that the capacity ranking (w/ HCl > w/o HCl > simple mixing) and the HRTEM finding of residual amorphous regions in the best-performing (w/ HCl) sample co-occur in a way consistent with the paper's narrative that amorphous content limits capacity via interfacial resistance. It does not establish that amorphous fraction differs across the three routes (only the w/ HCl sample was imaged), does not measure resistance/impedance to demonstrate the proposed mechanism, and cannot support a dose-response relationship since the morphology data is a single qualitative snapshot while the capacity data is a quantitative multi-sample series. The evidence is a plausible, unverified co-occurrence, not a demonstrated causal link.
