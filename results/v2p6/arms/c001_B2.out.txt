Viewed F6a, F6b, F5a. F6a: full CV comparing Pt, Cu2S, RGO-Cu2S — reduction peak near -1.25 V at roughly -41 to -43 mA/cm2 for the two Cu2S-based electrodes vs a flat Pt trace (~-0.45 mA/cm2, confirmed by the magnified F6b). F5a: DSSC J-V curves, ranked RGO-Cu2S (Jsc ~16.0, Voc ~0.555) > Cu2S (~15.0, ~0.535) > Pt (~13.0, ~0.525), consistent with the printed axes and curve order.

The combination supports only an ordinal correlation: the catalytic-activity ranking from CV (RGO-Cu2S ≈ Cu2S >> Pt) matches the device-performance ranking in J-V (RGO-Cu2S > Cu2S > Pt for both Jsc and Voc), so the CV/SEM story already established is directionally consistent with, and offers a plausible catalytic explanation for, the downstream PV figures.

It does not "account for" the J-V result quantitatively or mechanistically. Magnitudes don't transfer: CV shows RGO-Cu2S and Cu2S almost identical (~43 vs ~41) while J-V clearly separates them (16.0 vs 15.0 Jsc, 0.555 vs 0.535 Voc); CV shows a huge gap over Pt (~100x) while the J-V gap is modest (16.0 vs 13.0, ~0.555 vs ~0.525). CV is a counter-electrode/dummy-cell measurement, J-V is a full-device measurement dependent on photoanode, dye, electrolyte and series resistance too — no sample-matched or fill-factor/efficiency data bridges the two, so the link remains inferred, not measured.
