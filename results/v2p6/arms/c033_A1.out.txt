F2a (GCD, w/ HCl curve, red circles): discharge capacity falls steeply from ~90 to ~65 mAh/g over the first ~20-50 cycles, then declines slowly to ~55 mAh/g by cycle 250, with coulombic efficiency ~100% throughout.

The combination supports a temporal correspondence: the interval of fastest capacity fade (first 20-50 cycles) coincides with the interval of fastest Rct rise, and both curves flatten into a slower decline/rise regime afterward, while Rfilm and Re remain unchanged — consistent with growing charge-transfer resistance (not film or electrolyte resistance) tracking the capacity loss. It does not establish causation: no mechanistic link (e.g., SEI composition, particle cracking, loss of active material) is shown connecting Rct to capacity, the near-100% CE argues against simple active-material loss, and the large, growing error bars on Rct after cycle 150 weaken confidence in the later-stage trend.
