Combined, this justifies only a qualitative link: the composition-dependent PL branch structure (SC single band < HOC intermediate < PC clearly split, plus an IR tail) co-occurs with the same ~150 K onset temperature where XRD shows the PC (004) two-phase coexistence band, supporting the reading that PC's structural bi-phasic behavior underlies its dual PL emission, with HOC/SC showing weaker or absent structural splitting. It leaves open: no phase fraction is given by either technique, no quantitative correlation between coexistence width and emission-branch intensity ratio, and no direct proof that the two PL bands map one-to-one onto the two XRD structural branches rather than correlating only by shared onset temperature.
