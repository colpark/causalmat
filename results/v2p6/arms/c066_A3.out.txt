Both panels confirm the stated numbers. F6a (XRD of HP5) shows peaks indexed only to ZrB2, SiC, C (graphite), and BN — no AlN or Al2O3 peaks are marked, consistent with claim A. F2b shows Vickers hardness vs. AlN wt% for HP (red) and PS (blue) samples: the HP curve rises from ~18 GPa at 0% to a peak ~20.3 GPa at 1%, then eases to ~19.4 GPa (3%) and ~19.2 GPa (5%) — matching claim B.

What the combination supports: for the hot-pressed (HP) route, the absence of AlN/Al2O3 reflections in the HP5 XRD (5 wt% AlN sample) together with the hardness trend supports the inference that AlN is not present as a separate/retained crystalline second phase detectable by XRD in HP samples, and that hardness peaks at low (1 wt%) AlN addition before modestly declining, staying above the 0% baseline through 5 wt%. This is consistent with AlN acting via grain-boundary/microstructural or minor solid-solution effects rather than forming a bulk crystalline addition-derived phase, at the doping levels tested.

What it leaves open: XRD absence does not rule out AlN present below detection limit, as amorphous/glassy phase, or as a thin intergranular film — it only shows no crystalline AlN/Al2O3 reflections in the one HP5 (5 wt%) pattern shown; the 1 and 3 wt% samples' XRD is not shown here, so whether the same phase picture holds at the hardness peak (1 wt%) is not directly evidenced. No causal mechanism (grain size, densification, phase purity) linking the XRD result to the hardness peak/decline is established by these two panels alone — only a compositional coincidence across the HP series.
