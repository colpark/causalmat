The combination supports that the aptamer-functionalized ZIF-8 particles (i) carry a detectable but sparse aptamer/P signal and (ii) do not produce overt blood-biomarker or histological abnormality over 3–14 days at either administration route. It does not establish that the microstructure/EDS finding causes or explains the biochemistry result — they are parallel, not mechanistically connected.
