Together the evidence supports only a correlational, non-monotonic pattern: 2Co-MBG, which releases less Co2+ than 5Co-MBG at every timepoint, produces the significantly higher VEGF response, while 5Co-MBG (more Co2+) yields VEGF near blank/MBG baseline. So higher Co2+ release does not track with higher VEGF — the relationship is not simple dose-proportionality. This leaves open why: no mechanism (e.g., cytotoxicity, an optimal ion-concentration window, HIF-1α pathway saturation) is shown; no dose-response curve between the two Co levels is tested; and the ion and VEGF assays are separate measurements, not a paired causal experiment, so causal attribution of the VEGF pattern to the ion levels is not established by these panels alone.
