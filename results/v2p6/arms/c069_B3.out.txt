This combination supports only a correlational, not a causal, link: gross thrombus coverage (B) improves with BVLD content (BVLD-PPAmF and BVLD/NO-PPAmF are cleanest), matching the earlier-established thrombin-activity result, while fibrinogen/gamma-chain adsorption (A) drops equally across all three functionalised coatings regardless of BVLD, exactly as the earlier finding described. Together they reinforce that fibrinogen-adsorption reduction is a shared PPAmF-functionalisation effect, not a BVLD/thrombin-specific one, and that visible thrombus reduction tracks BVLD/thrombin activity instead.

What this leaves open: no measurement here ties fibrinogen/gamma-chain levels to the specific photographed thrombus outcome on the same samples/timepoints; the photos are qualitative macroscopic grading, not a quantified co-assay, so no causal chain from "less fibrinogen" to "less thrombus" is established, nor is the mechanism behind the uniform fibrinogen drop identified.
