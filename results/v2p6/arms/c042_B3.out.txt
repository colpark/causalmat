This upstream evidence (EDS + zeta potential) does account for the downstream observation in the limited sense already settled: the charge reversal to net-anionic, combined with P detected inside the same C/Zn/N particle outline in both samples, supports that an anionic, phosphorus-bearing species (the aptamer) is associated with the cationic BSA@ZIF-8 particle — consistent with electrostatic attachment.

It leaves open, and the panels confirm rather than resolve: (1) zeta potential is a single bulk value, so it cannot localize the anionic species or confirm a coating geometry; (2) the sparse, dot-like P signal (visibly weaker/discontinuous versus the dense C/Zn/N fields) argues against a continuous, surface-spanning phosphorus shell — no uniform "layer" is established; (3) neither measurement distinguishes purely electrostatic adsorption from partial covalent/coordination binding; (4) no quantitative aptamer density or surface coverage is given. So the evidence supports association/adsorption of the aptamer with the particle, not a continuous coating, a specific binding mechanism, or a coverage extent.
