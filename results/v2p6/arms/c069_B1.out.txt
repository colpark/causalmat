What it leaves open: within the three functionalized coatings, fibrinogen rank order does not match the photographic rank order — BVLD/NO-PPAmF has the highest (not lowest) fibrinogen/gamma-chain signal of the three yet the cleanest photograph, better than BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF despite their lower fibrinogen readings.
