The upstream ICP-AES trend (A: Zn at% rising from 0 to ~50% by cycle 20, settling near 47%) and the downstream DFT structural models (B: MnN6→MnN3→MnN4 coordination shift, with tetrahedral ZnN4 linking to octahedral FeC6) are consistent in direction but not tightly coupled. F3b confirms A's bulk composition trend across pristine/4th/20th/60th/100th cycles. F5d–f (labeled by nominal stoichiometry K0.25Zn0.25-, K0.25Zn0.5-, and ZnMnFe(CN)6, not by cycle number) show that as modeled Zn fraction increases, Mn's coordination sheds N-ligands and a tetrahedral ZnN4 site appears bonded to FeC6, which the caption ties to Jahn-Teller distortion and an enabled phase transition.

Together, this supports the qualitative picture that increasing Zn uptake accompanies a local coordination reorganization around Mn and the emergence of Zn-tetrahedra linked to the Fe sublattice. It leaves open, however, whether the specific at% values measured at cycle 20/60/100 (A) map onto the nominal x=0.25/0.5/1 compositions modeled in B — the DFT panels are stoichiometry-indexed, not cycle-indexed. Because A is a bulk elemental measurement with no site resolution, it cannot itself confirm that the Zn sits in the tetrahedral sites B depicts, nor pin the ~47 at% plateau to saturation of those specific sites; that site assignment rests on the computational model, not on direct structural measurement at the corresponding cycle.
