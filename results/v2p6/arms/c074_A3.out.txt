What the combination supports: the two measurements are consistent with a mechanistic story in which the ternary Co-Fe-Cr (oxy)hydroxide's intermediate, more evenly balanced Co2+→Co3+/Co3+→Co4+ free-energy landscape (relative to the binary CoCrOOH, CoFeOOH, and Co(OH)2 endpoints) correlates with lower overpotential/faster onset in neutral OER LSV. Both are real, correctly-read panels from the same paper, so the pairing is not fabricated.

What it leaves open: this is a correlation between a computed thermodynamic descriptor (DFT ΔG, presumably on a fixed lattice/idealized structure) and a separate electrochemical measurement (LSV in 0.5M KHCO3) — the panels do not show a direct causal or mechanistic link (e.g., no shared intermediate, no in-situ spectroscopy tying computed Co-oxidation steps to the observed current). Neither panel states or demonstrates that the DFT-predicted balanced ΔG landscape is the cause of the LSV performance ranking; that inference, if drawn in text, would need to rely on the paper's discussion, not on these figures alone. Also note CoFeCrOOH (DFT) and Co5Fe3Cr2 (LSV) are presumably the same ternary material family but the figures don't confirm identical stoichiometry/naming correspondence.
