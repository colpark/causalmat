What it leaves open: the panels give no quantitative phase-fraction or porosity numbers, no explanation for the small mid-discharge SAXS deviation (particle rearrangement vs. minor void formation vs. electrolyte ingress), and being bulk/ensemble-averaged techniques, neither rules out local/heterogeneous microstructural change.
