Panels confirm the described data: F3c shows near-vertical, non-shifting color contours across the q-range and full discharge/charge window (no progressive structural signature); F3d shows the I(q) curves for OCV through 3V essentially overlapping, consistent with only a small drop near q~0.03-0.1 A-1 at 0.11/0.02V; F2d shows the capacity crash from ~870 (cycle 1) to ~480 (cycle 2) to ~70 mAh/g by cycle 40.

The upstream pair (structural stasis by SAXS + stalled, low AIMD oxidation degree for Na-Co3O4) is consistent with the downstream fade and supports reading the fade as driven by poor sodiation/limited conversion activity rather than volume-change-induced structural failure — i.e., the mechanism identified upstream is not contradicted by, and offers a qualitative explanation for, why capacity is low and falling rather than being caused by a scattering-visible volumetric collapse.

What it leaves open: SAXS samples only two voltage points in the first cycle, so it says nothing about structural behavior over cycles 2-40 where most of the fade occurs; the AIMD result is a single 2 ps computed snapshot, not a kinetic or capacity-scale quantity, so it cannot explain the specific magnitudes (870→480→70) or the sharp cycle-1-to-2 drop; and other fade mechanisms (SEI growth, irreversible side reactions, contact loss) are not ruled out.
