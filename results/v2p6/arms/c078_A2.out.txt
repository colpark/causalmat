The combination supports the downstream claim only partially. F3b (ICP) shows Zn at% rising from 0 in pristine material to ~50% by the 20th cycle and staying near 47% through the 100th cycle, confirming steady Zn incorporation with cycling — this is solid compositional (STR/chemistry/composition) evidence. F5d–f (DFT-built polyhedra) show that as nominal Zn content increases across three fixed stoichiometries (x=0.25, x=0.5, full Zn substitution), the Mn coordination shifts from octahedral MnN6 → distorted MnN3 → tetrahedral MnN4, and tetrahedral ZnN4 becomes linked to octahedral FeC6, which the authors say enables the subsequent phase transition. Together, composition (A) and modeled coordination change (B) form a coherent qualitative story: more Zn → different Mn/Zn coordination → structural transition, supporting a link between composition and site occupancy/phase.

What it leaves open: F5d–f are DFT-constructed structural models for three chosen stoichiometries, not experimentally resolved structures matched to the specific cycle numbers (4th, 20th, 60th, 100th) reported in F3b. There is no direct experimental structural probe (e.g., XRD, EXAFS) in these panels tying a measured at% at a given cycle to the corresponding coordination state — the mapping between ICP composition and DFT coordination geometry is inferred/illustrative, not measured in situ.
