Findings from the panels (aenm.202003419, Zn-plating on N-doped carbon host, "CnC HS" = N,C-codoped/zincophilic host vs "C HS" = undoped carbon host):

SEM (F3d, F3e — panels d/e of the first image): after Zn plating, the CnC HS surface (d) shows a comparatively flat, compact deposit with only scattered platelet features, whereas the C HS surface (e) is densely covered with fine needle/flake-like Zn protrusions across the whole 10 µm field — consistent with the schematic in panels f/g showing zincophilic sites giving spacious nucleation → lateral growth → homogeneous Zn film, versus non-zincophilic surfaces giving dense nucleation → tip growth → dendrites.

Electrochemical (F4, second image): F4a shows CnC HS maintaining Coulombic efficiency near 95–100% over ~200 cycles, while C HS drops to near 0% around cycles 40–50, indicating short-circuit/failure. F4b (long symmetric-cell voltage trace) shows C HS developing large voltage spikes and an explicit "C HS battery failure" event around 2700–4300 min, while CnC HS stays flat and stable through 7000+ minutes. F4e (rate-varied symmetric cycling) shows the same pattern: C HS voltage becomes erratic/spikes as current density increases (~2500–3500 min), while CnC HS remains stable.

Justified conclusion: the zincophilic (CnC) host measurably suppresses dendritic/uneven Zn deposition and yields a longer-lived, more stable Zn plating/stripping interface (higher, steadier CE; no observed short-circuit) than the undoped carbon host, under the specific current densities/areal capacities tested (1–4 mA cm⁻², 0.5–4 mAh cm⁻²).

What remains uncertain: these are single representative traces/cells (no error bars, replicate counts, or statistics shown), the mechanism (Zn–N bonding) is only inferred from XPS/Raman shifts (panels a–c) plus schematic, not directly imaged at the bond level; performance beyond ~200 cycles / 7000 min or at other current densities, temperatures, or in full cells (only one full-cell device photo, F4g, no full-cell cycling data shown here) is not established by these panels alone.
