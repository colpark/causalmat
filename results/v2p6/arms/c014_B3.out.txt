It leaves open: (1) only two conditions overlap between the panels (RGO-Cu2S, Cu2S; Pt has no porosity measurement), so no dose-response link between porosity and Jsc/Voc is shown; (2) as already noted, GO/RGO addition confounds morphology change with RGO's own intrinsic surface area/conductivity, so porosity cannot be isolated as the causal driver of the PV gain; (3) F5a shows only raw J-V curves — no fill factor, efficiency, or other PRF/figure-of-merit values are given, so no quantitative figure-of-merit link to porosity is established, only a qualitative same-direction ranking.
