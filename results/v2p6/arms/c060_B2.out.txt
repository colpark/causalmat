What the combination supports: the smooth, monotonic Tm2 shift with composition (A) is consistent with — and adds to — the earlier Raman-based conclusion that Tm2 reflects a continuous/diffuse dielectric response rather than a discrete structural transition. The TCC stability (B) is a plausible technological consequence of such a broad, diffuse maximum (flat permittivity over a wide range), but it is a device-performance metric, not independent structural evidence, so it does not itself test the transition-vs-diffuse question.

What is left open: the prior qualifications still stand unresolved — Raman was only measured at x=0.06, so the "diffuse origin" claim is unverified at x=0.02, 0.04, 0.08 even though Tm2 appears there too; no PNR- or vacancy-specific signature is shown; only 1 kHz data are given (no frequency dispersion, the standard relaxor/PNR test); and TCC flatness, while suggestive, does not mechanistically confirm PNR involvement or rule out other origins for Tm2.
