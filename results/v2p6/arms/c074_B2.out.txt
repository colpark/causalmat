The combination supports a correlation: the ternary composition, which DFT places at an intermediate (not extremal) ΔG for both individual Co oxidation steps, is the one that shows the best overall OER kinetics (lowest onset among the tested set). That is consistent with — but does not establish — a mechanistic story where balanced two-step energetics enables faster net turnover.

It leaves open: (1) η10 is a lumped multi-step OER kinetic outcome, not decomposed into the Co2+→Co3+/Co3+→Co4+ steps DFT resolves, so no mechanistic pathway link is demonstrated, only co-occurrence; (2) the F1a labels (CoCrOOH, CoFeOOH, CoFeCrOOH, Co(OH)2) carry no explicit stoichiometry, so exact identity with F5c's Co5Fe3Cr2/Co5Fe5/Co5Cr5/Co is not confirmed by the panels themselves; (3) no causal or quantitative test ties the computed ΔG values to the measured overpotential.
