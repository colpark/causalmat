Only two of the four panels bear on this question. F5a (ion-release profiles) shows Co2+ release rising with soaking time and scaling with cobalt loading: 0Co-MBG releases no detectable Co2+ at any time point, while 2Co-MBG and 5Co-MBG both release increasing amounts over 7 days, with 5Co-MBG consistently higher than 2Co-MBG — a clear, monotonic dose response in cobalt content for Co2+ release into solution. F9 (western blot) shows HIF-1a band intensity strongest in 5Co-MBG, weaker in 2Co-MBG, and weaker still (though present) in plain MBG — consistent with cobalt dose promoting HIF-1a expression, though the blot is a single unquantified replicate with no loading-normalized densitometry shown, so the 2Co vs 0Co ordering is only qualitative.

However, no VEGF panel was actually supplied. The images labeled F11a and F11b are the same drug-release/antibacterial figure (ampicillin release kinetics and bacterial survival with/without ampicillin) — they contain no VEGF data and no cobalt-dose comparison at all. So while cobalt content and HIF-1a expression together support a dose-response story, the evidence provided does not include any VEGF measurement, and the claim that HIF-1a and VEGF describe "one dose response" cannot be assessed from these images.
