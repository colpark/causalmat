The upstream resistivity/PF result only explains PF, and it does so incompletely (Seebeck data are absent, and x=0 already shows PF isn't resistivity alone). It does not extend to ZT: at 300 K the PF ranking (x=0.01 highest, x=0.005 lowest of the doped set) is inverted relative to the ZT ranking (x=0.005 highest, x=0.01 second). Since ZT = PF·T/κ, this mismatch means thermal conductivity (not shown in either panel supplied) must differ enough across compositions to override the PF ordering — the resistivity/PF story cannot account for why x=0.005 gives the best ZT at 300 K. So the upstream evidence justifies the PF-vs-resistivity co-variation already recorded, but it does not account for the ZT/figure-of-merit ranking; that ranking is left open, pointing to an unaddressed thermal-conductivity contribution.
