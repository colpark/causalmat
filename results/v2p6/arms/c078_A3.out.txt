Partially. Claim A (ICP-AES: Zn rises from 0 to ~50 at% by cycle 20, holding ~47 at% after) is directly confirmed by F3b, whose green Zn bars go from 0 (pristine) to ~50 at% at the 20th cycle and stay ~46–47 at% at the 60th/100th cycles — quantitative match.

Claim B (DFT: Mn polyhedron goes MnN3→MnN4, with grey ZnN4 tetrahedra linking to FeC6) is only partly borne out. F5d–f show a progression with increasing Zn stoichiometry (Zn0.25 → Zn0.5 → full Zn substitution): MnN6 → MnN5 → MnN4, and F5f does show grey ZnN4 tetrahedra bridging to octahedral FeC6, consistent with the linkage claim. But the intermediate coordination in F5e is labeled MnN5, not MnN3 as the upstream text states — a discrepancy the panels do not resolve.

What's left open: the DFT models use idealized substitution levels (0.25, 0.5, full Zn), not the actual cycle-20/60/100 compositions from ICP, so the two evidence lines aren't tied to the same material state, only to the same qualitative trend (more Zn → more Zn-N coordination, altered Mn site). The caption itself hedges ("could attribute," "could happen"), so the phase-transition mechanism remains a proposed rationalization, not a confirmed observation.
