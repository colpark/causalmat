What the combination supports: x=0.01 is consistently the best-performing composition in both power factor and ZT, so the same doping level that maximizes PF also yields the highest figure of merit — a coherent, mutually reinforcing pair of observations rather than a contradiction.

What it leaves open: PF itself is monotonically decreasing above ~370 K, while ZT keeps rising until ~500–525 K before turning over. Neither panel shows thermal conductivity, so the PF data alone cannot account for where the ZT peak sits or why ZT continues climbing after PF has started falling — that shape must be governed by κ(T), which is outside these two figures. The link between "best PF" and "best ZT" composition is therefore supported, but the specific ZT peak location/temperature dependence is not explained by PF alone.
