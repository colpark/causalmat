The combination partially extends but does not fully account for the downstream claim. Observation B is DFT modeling at three explicit Zn stoichiometries (K0.25Zn0.25-, K0.25Zn0.5-, and ZnMnFe(CN)6), directly showing site-level structure: as Zn content increases in the model, Mn's coordination drops (MnN6→MnN3→MnN4, attributed to Zn2+-induced Jahn-Teller distortion) while grey tetrahedral ZnN4 units appear and link to octahedral FeC6, i.e., Zn is modeled as occupying specific N-coordinated framework sites. This does address site_occupancy in a way A alone cannot, and together with A (bulk Zn at% rising toward ~47–50% by the 20th cycle, F3b) it supports a coherent narrative: increasing Zn content is accompanied by Zn entering tetrahedral framework sites and reorganizing Mn coordination, motivating the proposed phase transition.

What remains open: the DFT stoichiometries (0.25, 0.5, 1.0 Zn per formula unit) are idealized model inputs, not derived from diffraction or site-resolved data on the actual cycled electrode; there is no experimental (e.g., Rietveld, EXAFS) confirmation that real cycled samples at ~47–50 at% Zn adopt these specific modeled occupancies. The correspondence between ICP's bulk at% trend and the DFT compositions is asserted, not measured together on the same sample.
