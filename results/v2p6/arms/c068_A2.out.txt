Figure 2a (the only panel provided, listed twice) is a discharge-capacity-vs-cycle-number plot with coulombic efficiency on a secondary axis, showing three curves for three synthesis routes: co-precipitation w/ HCl (red), co-precipitation w/o HCl (blue), and simple mixing w/o HCl (black).

What the panel confirms: at every cycle shown, the ordering is w/ HCl > w/o HCl > simple mixing, with initial values of roughly 91, 81, and 61 mAh/g respectively — matching measurement A exactly. The w/ HCl (red) curve's first point sits at ~90–91 mAh/g, matching measurement B. So the panel is direct, sufficient support for both stated measurements; nothing in A or B goes beyond what is plotted.

What it leaves open: the panel only reports the electrochemical outcome (capacity, coulombic efficiency) as a function of cycle number and synthesis label — it does not show or explain why the HCl-assisted co-precipitation route yields higher capacity (e.g., no structural, morphological, phase-purity, or particle-size data are in this panel). So the evidence establishes the correlation between synthesis procedure and downstream capacity ranking/magnitude, but the mechanistic link — what property change caused by HCl addition drives the capacity improvement — is not addressed by this figure and remains open.
