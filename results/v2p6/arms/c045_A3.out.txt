This is a same-figure, same-paper correlation between the two host structures, plausible as a causal mechanism (sharp platelets promoting shorting/dead-Zn/dendrite failure) but not proven as causal by these panels alone — no intermediate diagnostics (e.g., cross-sections, dendrite-growth time series, or shorting confirmation) are shown here linking morphology directly to the CE failure mode. It also does not establish generality beyond this cycling condition (1 mAh cm⁻² at 4 mA cm⁻²) or rule out other differences between CnC and C hosts (composition, porosity, conductivity) as contributing causes.
