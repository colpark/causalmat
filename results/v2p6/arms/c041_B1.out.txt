The sparse, non-uniform P signal leaves surface coverage extent uncertain, so it cannot establish that enough aptamer is present to drive targeting. Other explanations for the fluorescence difference (altered surface charge, size, or nonspecific biodistribution shifts from the coating) are not ruled out by either dataset.
