It leaves several things open. First, the efficiency panel omits x=0 entirely, so the large PF gap between doped and undoped samples cannot be checked against efficiency at all. Second, the mid-tier ordering doesn't fully track: x=0.005 has the lowest PF among doped samples but an efficiency comparable to (not clearly below) x=0.04, whose PF is higher. Third, efficiency is a model output depending on thermal conductivity/ZT as well as PF, neither shown here, so PF alone cannot be said to determine the efficiency ranking—only to be correlated with it for the extremal case (x=0.01).
