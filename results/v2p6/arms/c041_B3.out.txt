F6b then shows that particles bearing the DNA aptamer produce a brighter (greener, higher-intensity) ex vivo signal in both inguinal and axillary lymph nodes than the neat BSA@ZIF-8 (cyan, lower intensity), for both subcutaneous and footpad injection routes. Together these support that aptamer-bearing particles reach/accumulate in lymph nodes more than uncoated particles, consistent with the aptamer conferring enhanced lymph node delivery/targeting capability.
