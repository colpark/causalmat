The provided panels do not include a VEGF measurement at all. F9 is a western blot showing HIF-1α band intensity increasing qualitatively across MBG < 2Co-MBG < 5Co-MBG (with α-tubulin as a loading control of even intensity), which is consistent with cobalt content promoting HIF-1α expression. F5a-d are ion-release curves (Co2+, SiO4^4-, Ca2+, PO4^3-) versus soaking time for the same three compositions — Co2+ release rises with cobalt content (0 < 2Co < 5Co-MBG), but this panel reports elemental release, not VEGF protein. So the evidence justifies only a HIF-1α dose response: band intensity increases with cobalt content in a single blot, one biological readout, no replicate quantification or statistics shown. It does not justify any claim about "one dose response" spanning both HIF-1α and VEGF, because no VEGF panel is present among the images supplied — VEGF expression versus cobalt content is simply not shown here. Any statement that HIF-1α and VEGF together trace a single cobalt dose-response curve is therefore unsupported by this image set; that would require a VEGF figure (e.g., ELISA or blot) that was not provided.
