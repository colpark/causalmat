What is left open: the EDS spectrum is qualitative (peak presence/intensity, no quantified atomic percentages), so it cannot establish a dose-response link between actual Ce loading and the antibacterial trend — it only confirms Ce/Er are present in the material, not that composition scales monotonically with the nominal 0.1/0.2/0.3 labels. The mechanism connecting composition to activity (e.g., surface chemistry, ROS generation) is not shown in these panels.
