Together, this supports the claim that the aptamer is incorporated into (or associated with) the MOF particle at the level of composition (P present, if sparse) and that aptamer-functionalized particles reach/accumulate more strongly in draining lymph nodes than the non-aptamer control, i.e., the aptamer coating plausibly enhances lymph-node delivery or retention.

What it leaves open: EDS is a static, single-particle compositional map — it does not establish P is exclusively from the aptamer (no independent elemental control ruling out other P sources), nor does it quantify aptamer loading or confirm intact aptamer function. The fluorescence comparison shows a difference in signal intensity/color between aptamer and neat MOF but is qualitative (color-scale imagery, not stated replicate numbers or statistics here) and does not itself demonstrate the mechanism (active targeting vs. passive/size effects) or downstream service capability/therapeutic efficacy — it only shows greater local accumulation in these two lymph node sites at the imaged timepoint.
