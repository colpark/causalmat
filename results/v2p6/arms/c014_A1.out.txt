It leaves open: (1) no porosity/BET data for Pt is shown, so the Pt comparison in F5a cannot be explained by the porosity evidence at all; (2) no FF, PCE, or explicit "figure of merit"/PRF value appears in either panel, so any efficiency-metric claim is not directly supported; (3) causation is not established — RGO's conductivity, not pore structure, could equally explain the Jsc/Voc gain between RGO-Cu2S and plain Cu2S.
