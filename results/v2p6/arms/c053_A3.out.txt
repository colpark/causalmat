What the combination supports: good interfacial bonding at the 1% loading (SEM) is consistent with the very small tensile-strength drop from pure PAEN to 1% Ag (79.3 → 78.3 MPa, essentially within error bars), i.e., strong adhesion at low filler content correlates with near-retained strength.

What it leaves open: the SEM evidence shown is only for the 1% sample. The further decline in tensile strength at 3% and 5% Ag (74.4, 73.5 MPa) is not directly explained by any interface micrograph at those higher loadings — no SEM panel here shows whether gaps, agglomeration, or debonding appear as filler content increases. So the data support a link between good 1%-level interfacial adhesion and modest strength retention, but do not establish the mechanism behind the additional strength loss at higher Ag content; that would require interface images at 3% and 5% loadings, which are not among the panels provided.
