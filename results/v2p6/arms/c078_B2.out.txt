The panels change the picture from the earlier "co-occurrence, not mechanism" verdict. F3b is the printed ICP-AES bar chart: it confirms observation A exactly (Zn rises from 0 at pristine to ~50 at% by the 20th cycle, then holds near 46-47 at% at the 60th/100th). F5d-F5f are DFT-built structural models keyed to increasing Zn stoichiometry (x=0.25, 0.5, then full ZnMnFe(CN)6), and they are explicitly site-resolved: as Zn content rises, the Mn coordination polyhedron is shown converting MnN6 → MnN5 → MnN4, and in F5f grey ZnN4 tetrahedra appear and link to FeC6 octahedra within the same 3D framework.

Together, this justifies more than co-occurrence: the DFT series ties specific Zn:metal ratios to specific, located changes in framework coordination (Mn losing N-neighbors, Zn occupying N-sites, ZnN4-FeC6 linkage), i.e., a genuine site-occupancy account of how added Zn is accommodated in the host lattice, and why that accommodation is described as enabling a subsequent phase transition.

What remains open: the DFT structures are idealized/computed models at chosen stoichiometries, not direct experimental site-occupancy data (no EXAFS, neutron, or Rietveld occupancy refinement shown) on the actual cycled electrode; nothing here confirms the real ~47 at% plateau material matches the F5f end-member structure rather than a disordered intermediate, so the tie between the ICP plateau and the specific ZnN4/MnN4 model remains a modeled, not measured, correspondence.
