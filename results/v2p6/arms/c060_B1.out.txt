Panels confirm the numbers: Tm1 falls from ~6750 (x=0, panel a) to ~2700 (x=0.08, panel e); Tm2 first appears at x=0.02 (~200 C, panel b) and shifts to ~230-260 C by x=0.08 (panel e), all as a broad, single hump per composition with no split or kink.

The upstream result (smooth, discontinuity-free Raman softening at x=0.06 plus the composition-shift of Tm2) is consistent with, but does not by itself predict, the downstream TCC behavior. The combination supports one thing: at x=0.06, where both a continuous phonon evolution and a diffuse Tm2 were established, the TCC curve staying within +/-15% across ~40-340 C (spanning both Tm1 and Tm2) fits a picture in which a smooth, non-abrupt dielectric anomaly produces no sharp property discontinuity - the "no structural transition" reading and the "temperature-stable capacitance" reading are mutually consistent for that one composition.

What it leaves open: the x=0.08 TCC overshoot near 230-290 C, coincident with its Tm2, is not accounted for by any upstream evidence, since Raman was only measured at x=0.06. Whether x=0.08's overshoot reflects a larger-magnitude diffuse anomaly (still no discrete transition) or a genuinely different, more abrupt mechanism at higher x cannot be determined from what's shown - the qualification about compositional generality applies directly here.
