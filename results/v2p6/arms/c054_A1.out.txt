What the combination supports: the SEM images are consistent with the claim of even particle dispersion without large agglomerates at the loadings shown, and this co-occurs with a modest, monotonic decline in tensile strength as filler/Ag content increases — i.e., the strength drop is not obviously explained by visible agglomeration, since none is apparent even at the highest loading (5%).

What it leaves open: the SEM evidence is qualitative (single field of view per sample, one scale bar) and not a quantitative dispersion/particle-size distribution measurement, so "no large agglomerates" is an impression, not a statistic. Neither the SEM panels nor F7a establish the mechanism linking dispersion to the strength decline (e.g., interfacial debonding, stress concentration, reduced chain mobility) — the images show topology, not fracture-mode analysis tied causally to the PRP/value drop.
