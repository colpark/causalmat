Panels F4a-e (dielectric constant vs. temperature, 1 kHz-1 MHz) verify claim A directly: the 1 kHz Tm1 maximum falls from ~6750 (x=0, single broad peak, no Tm2) to ~4300, ~3500, ~3300, ~2700 across x=0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08; a distinct second maximum Tm2 is labeled starting at x=0.02 and its position shifts rightward from roughly 190-200 C (x=0.02) to ~230 C (x=0.04), ~250 C (x=0.06), ~260 C (x=0.08) — consistent with the stated ~200→~260 C trend. So the upstream evidence for A is well-supported by these images.

Claim B (the TCC/±15% stability curve) is a different plot that is not among the five panels supplied here — none of F4a-e show temperature coefficient of capacitance. The Tm panels only show that x=0.04-0.06 develop a broad, relatively flat plateau between the two maxima (consistent with a mechanism that could produce stable capacitance over a wide range) and that x=0.08 has a visible dip/valley between Tm1 and the still-rising Tm2, which is at least qualitatively compatible with an overshoot near 230-290 C. But this is circumstantial, not a measurement of TCC itself. The combination supports A fully and offers a plausible mechanistic rationale for B's shape, but does not verify B's specific ±15% limits or values — that claim remains unconfirmed by the images provided.
