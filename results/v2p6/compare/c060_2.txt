THE QUESTION
Does the upstream evidence account for the downstream observation about PRP/value / PRF/qualification? Say what the combination supports and what it leaves open.

ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
The five panels (F4a–e) show dielectric constant vs. temperature at four frequencies for x = 0, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, each labeled with a diffuseness exponent γ (1.71 → 2.00). They directly confirm observation A: the 1 kHz maximum falls from ~6750 (x=0, single broad peak, Tm1 only) to ~2700 (x=0.08), and a second maximum (Tm2) appears starting at x=0.02 (~200°C) and shifts to ~230–260°C by x=0.04–0.08, becoming comparable to or exceeding Tm1 in height at x=0.08. This is fully visible and quantitatively consistent across panels b–e.

What this accounts for: it supports a physical mechanism — increasing compositional/structural disorder (rising γ) broadens and splits the permittivity peak — that could plausibly underlie temperature-stable capacitance (flat TCC) in a window between Tm1 and Tm2, and could explain why x=0.08's emergent, elevated Tm2 near 230–290°C produces a local capacitance rise causing the TCC to overshoot +15% there.

What it leaves open: none of these panels show the TCC curve itself (observation B). The ±15% window pass/fail for x=0.06 vs. the overshoot for x=0.08 is not verifiable from F4a–e; that requires the separate TCC figure. So the upstream dielectric-constant evidence is consistent with, but does not itself establish, the PRF/qualification conclusion.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
Figures confirm both stated observations: panel (a) shows Tm1 ≈6750 at x=0 (1 kHz, black curve); panel (e) shows Tm1 dropped to ≈2700 at x=0.08. Tm2 is absent in (a), then appears and shifts from ≈200 C (b, x=0.02) to ≈230 C (c) to ≈250 C (d) to ≈260 C (e), matching the stated range. The TCC/±15% claim (B) is not depicted in these Tm-vs-T panels and cannot be checked here, but is not contradicted by them.

What the combination supports: the smooth, monotonic Tm2 shift with composition (A) is consistent with — and adds to — the earlier Raman-based conclusion that Tm2 reflects a continuous/diffuse dielectric response rather than a discrete structural transition. The TCC stability (B) is a plausible technological consequence of such a broad, diffuse maximum (flat permittivity over a wide range), but it is a device-performance metric, not independent structural evidence, so it does not itself test the transition-vs-diffuse question.

What is left open: the prior qualifications still stand unresolved — Raman was only measured at x=0.06, so the "diffuse origin" claim is unverified at x=0.02, 0.04, 0.08 even though Tm2 appears there too; no PNR- or vacancy-specific signature is shown; only 1 kHz data are given (no frequency dispersion, the standard relaxor/PNR test); and TCC flatness, while suggestive, does not mechanistically confirm PNR involvement or rule out other origins for Tm2.
