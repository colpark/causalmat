It leaves open the rest of the ranking. Efficiency is a composite that in general also depends on thermal conductivity (via ZT), which neither panel reports, and on the full T-integral, not just PF values at two temperatures. Correspondingly, x=0.005 has the lowest PF among doped samples at 623K but is not the lowest-efficiency composition — it sits near x=0.02, above x=0.04. So the upstream PF/resistivity story accounts for the top performer but not the ordering among the remaining three compositions; something besides PF (plausibly thermal conductivity) must shape that part of the eta trend.
