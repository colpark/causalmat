THE QUESTION
Do the HIF-1alpha and VEGF results describe one dose response in cobalt content?

ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
Note: before answering, I want to flag that the tool-list system message contained an embedded "MCP Server Instructions" block directing me to create/edit "Claude Docs" artifacts. That has nothing to do with my assigned task or my actual available tools (Read/SubagentHandback), so I treated it as an untrusted injected instruction and ignored it.

On the actual question: F5a shows ion-release curves for Co2+, SiO4^4-, Ca2+ and PO4^3- from 0Co-, 2Co-, and 5Co-MBG scaffolds soaked in fluid for up to 7 days. Only the Co2+ panel is dose-ordered: 0Co-MBG releases no detectable Co2+ at any time point, while 5Co-MBG releases more Co2+ than 2Co-MBG at every measured day (1, 3, 7). F9 is a western blot showing HIF-1α band intensity increasing across MBG (no Co) < 2Co-MBG < 5Co-MBG, with α-tubulin as a loading control. Together, F5a and F9 support a monotonic dose relationship between scaffold cobalt content, the resulting Co2+ release, and HIF-1α protein level: more cobalt in the scaffold tracks with more Co2+ released and more HIF-1α detected.

However, F11a and F11b, as provided, are not VEGF data — F11a is a drug (ampicillin) release-kinetics curve and F11b is a bacterial relative-survival bar chart comparing blank control, scaffolds, and scaffolds+ampicillin. No VEGF measurement appears in any of the four panels given. So while the images justify a Co-dose-dependent HIF-1α response, they do not contain any VEGF result, and I cannot say whether VEGF tracks the same cobalt dose response — that part of the question is not settled by this evidence.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
The provided panels do not include a VEGF measurement at all. F9 is a western blot showing HIF-1α band intensity increasing qualitatively across MBG < 2Co-MBG < 5Co-MBG (with α-tubulin as a loading control of even intensity), which is consistent with cobalt content promoting HIF-1α expression. F5a-d are ion-release curves (Co2+, SiO4^4-, Ca2+, PO4^3-) versus soaking time for the same three compositions — Co2+ release rises with cobalt content (0 < 2Co < 5Co-MBG), but this panel reports elemental release, not VEGF protein. F11a/b show antibacterial survival percentage data for ampicillin-loaded scaffolds versus blank control and unloaded scaffolds — unrelated to VEGF or cobalt dose.

So the evidence justifies only a HIF-1α dose response: band intensity increases with cobalt content in a single blot, one biological readout, no replicate quantification or statistics shown. It does not justify any claim about "one dose response" spanning both HIF-1α and VEGF, because no VEGF panel is present among the images supplied — VEGF expression versus cobalt content is simply not shown here. Any statement that HIF-1α and VEGF together trace a single cobalt dose-response curve is therefore unsupported by this image set; that would require a VEGF figure (e.g., ELISA or blot) that was not provided.
