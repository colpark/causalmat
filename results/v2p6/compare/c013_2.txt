ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result

So the combination supports only that resistivity/PF differences are broadly consistent with the compositional trend in transport, and that x=0 is uniformly worst on both PF and ZT. It leaves open the mechanism linking PF to ZT quantitatively, the role of thermal conductivity (unmeasured here), and why x=0.005 outranks x=0.01 in ZT despite lower PF — that inversion is unexplained by the PF evidence alone.
