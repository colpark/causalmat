ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
The two datasets support different, non-overlapping conclusions, and the pairing itself does not establish a causal chain from A to B.

FTIR (F2a): the red BST-SH trace alone shows new peaks at 2930, 2864 cm⁻¹ (C–H stretches) and 1122 cm⁻¹, absent from BST and BST-OH. This is solid, direct spectroscopic evidence that the thiol-functionalization step grafted an organic (silane-type) layer onto the particle surface — i.e., surface chemistry/bonding changed with the BST-SH treatment.

DSC (F5a): Tg falls from 176 °C (pure PAEN) to 146/149/157 °C as BST@Ag loading goes 1%→3%→5%, a monotonic drop then partial recovery with filler content — evidence of a plasticization/chain-mobility (PRP/value) effect in the composite.

Together, the two panels are consistent with a plausible narrative (surface functionalization changes interfacial chemistry, which in turn alters polymer chain mobility and Tg), but the evidence shown does not connect them mechanistically: F2a is about BST-SH vs BST/BST-OH particles, not about BST@Ag composites, and F5a's samples are BST@Ag/PAEN blends at varying Ag%, not varying SH-functionalization. No panel here directly links surface chemistry (SH grafting) to the Tg/PRP values — that link, if claimed in text, is not settled by these two figures alone.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
The upstream FTIR/TGA pairing (settled) establishes that BST-SH particles carry a real, quantifiable, chemically distinct surface species (CH2/Si-O groups, ~1% mass loss) — a claim about BST particle surface chemistry. The downstream DSC observation is a Tg drop in PAEN composites (176 C pure PAEN → 146/149/157 C at BST@Ag 1/3/5%). These are different objects: the FTIR/TGA evidence characterizes BST-SH particles alone, while the DSC panel (F5a) is on BST@Ag/PAEN composites, and neither panel nor caption confirms that the BST used in these composites is the BST-SH-functionalized material versus plain BST or BST-OH. So the upstream evidence cannot, on its own terms, be tied to the specific samples in the Tg measurement.

Even granting that link, the FTIR/TGA data speak only to "a surface species exists and is quantifiable" — they carry no information about polymer-chain mobility, interfacial coupling, or crystallinity/packing disruption, which are what would mechanistically explain a Tg shift. The upstream evidence therefore cannot account for why Tg drops ~20-30 C upon compositing, nor for the partial recovery trend with increasing Ag% (146→149→157 C). What is left open: whether the silane species (or any BST-SH grafting) is causally responsible for the Tg changes at all, versus filler loading, dispersion, or Ag itself.
