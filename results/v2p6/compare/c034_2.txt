F8b's H2 rates rank identically for the same compositions (note the paper's "AgxPd1-x" labels equal DFT's "Pd1-xAgx" labels — Ag0.25Pd0.75=Pd0.75Ag0.25, etc.): 125.4 > 57.3 > 43.5 > 34.5 (Pd) > 3.2 (Ag). This is a Sabatier-principle match — the alloy whose ΔG_H* sits closest to thermoneutral (Pd0.75Ag0.25) gives the fastest H2 evolution, while pure Pd (too strongly binding, ΔG=-0.31) and pure Ag (too weak, ΔG=0.49) both underperform the alloys. F8a's time-resolved curves are simply the cumulative trace of the same rates and add no independent evidence.

So the DFT thermodynamics plausibly account for the rank order of intrinsic HER activity across the AgPd composition series. What is left open: the DFT is computed on isolated (111) facets, not on the actual ZIS-supported AgPd cocatalyst; the measured rate is a whole-photocatalyst (ZIS+cocatalyst) quantity that also depends on light absorption and charge separation/transfer at the heterojunction, which ΔG_H* alone cannot address. Correlation, not a proven causal mechanism, is what the combination establishes.
