What it leaves open: no SEM panels for BST@Ag1% or BST@Ag3% were provided, so the graded, monotonic mechanical decline across all four compositions has no matching graded microstructural series — only the two extremes are pictured. The parallel trends are consistent with but do not prove that the visualized surface/agglomeration state causes the ductility loss.
