ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
Yes, the DFT trend accounts for the downstream photocatalytic observation, but only partially and non-monotonically.

DFT (F9a) gives |ΔG_H*| ordering: Pd0.75Ag0.25 (0.08) < Pd0.5Ag0.5 (0.12) < Pd0.25Ag0.75 (0.28) ≈ pure Pd (0.31) < Ag (0.49). Smallest |ΔG_H*| = most favorable H adsorption/desorption balance for HER. Measured H2 rates (F8a/F8b): Pd0.25Ag0.75 (125.4) > Pd0.5Ag0.5 (57.3) > Pd0.75Ag0.25 (43.5) > Pd (34.5) > Ag (3.2) >> ZIS.

Combination supports: the two Ag-rich-moderate compositions (Pd0.5Ag0.5, Pd0.25Ag0.75) with near-zero ΔG_H* do outperform pure Ag and pure Pd, consistent with the volcano-type HER argument that near-thermoneutral H* binding favors activity, and DFT correctly identifies pure Ag as the worst HER catalyst among the alloys.

What it leaves open: the best-performing composition experimentally (Pd0.25Ag0.75, 125.4 µmol/h) is not the one with the lowest |ΔG_H*| (that's Pd0.75Ag0.25, which is only mid-pack experimentally at 43.5). So the rank order of the two datasets is inverted for the Pd-rich alloys — the simple ΔG_H* metric alone does not explain why Pd0.25Ag0.75 is best. Also, all DFT values are isolated-metal-surface calculations on AgxPd1-x(111), while measurements are on ZIS-supported nanoparticles in a full photocatalytic system, so charge separation, light absorption, and heterojunction/interface effects (not captured by ΔG_H*) likely also contribute to the observed rate ordering.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
Panels confirm the data: F9a reproduces the DFT ΔG_H* series exactly (Pd −0.31, Pd0.75Ag0.25 −0.08, Pd0.5Ag0.5 0.12, Pd0.25Ag0.75 0.28, Ag 0.49 eV). F8b reproduces the H2-rate series exactly (Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS 125.4 > Ag0.5Pd0.5 57.3 > Ag0.75Pd0.25 43.5 > Pd 34.5 > Ag 3.2 > ZIS 1.5 μmol/h), and F8a shows the same ranking sustained over 5 h of irradiation.

Ag0.25Pd0.75 and Pd0.75Ag0.25 name the same alloy composition, so the downstream best-performing catalyst is the same composition the upstream DFT identifies as most thermoneutral (|ΔG_H*| smallest). Moreover the full ranking matches across all five compositions: ascending |ΔG_H*| (Pd0.75Ag0.25 < Pd0.5Ag0.5 < Pd0.25Ag0.75 < Pd < Ag) tracks descending H2 rate exactly. This justifies treating computed ΔG_H* as a good predictor of the composition-dependent trend in photocatalytic H2 evolution rate, a stronger, series-wide correlation than the earlier single-composition XPS-DFT pairing.

It leaves open: dG_H* is computed on a bare Pd/Ag(111) surface while the rate is measured on ZIS-supported alloy particles, so the mechanistic bridge (electronic structure → adsorption energy → surface HER kinetics) is not measured on the same object as the photocatalytic output. Absolute activity also depends on light absorption, ZIS-alloy charge transfer, and particle/surface-area effects not addressed by dG_H* alone, so the correlation supports but does not prove that HER thermodynamics alone drives the observed rate differences.</message>
