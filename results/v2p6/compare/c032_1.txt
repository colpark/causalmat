ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
Combined, A (YSZ SEM crack network unchanged between the pre-cycle and 5-cycle views, F4b/F4c) and B (Al2O3-YSZ coupon photographs: intact through 5 cycles, corner spallation appearing by 10 and persisting/growing to 40, F8) support only a narrow, coincidental point: at the shared early time point (0–5 cycles), a stable, unchanged crack network in YSZ co-occurs with an intact, unspalled coupon in the photographic series. It leaves open, and does not establish, any causal or mechanistic link between microstructure and downstream damage/service capability, for several reasons. The evidence therefore cannot support a claim that microstructural (crack) stability or change drives, predicts, or accounts for the spallation, damage progression, or resulting service capability observed in F8.
