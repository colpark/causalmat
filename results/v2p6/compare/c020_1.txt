Combined, this upstream evidence supports the claim that Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS has both the highest charge-separation/transport signal (photocurrent, the PRP/value-type quantity) and the highest photocatalytic H2 output (the PRF/service_capability-type quantity), and that the ranking of photocurrent among ZIS, Ag-ZIS, Pd-ZIS, Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS matches the ranking of H2 rate for those same four samples — i.e., the two properties covary consistently where directly comparable.

What it leaves open: the figures show correlation, not a demonstrated causal mechanism linking photocurrent to H2 rate; photocurrent was only measured for 4 of the 6 compositions (no Ag0.5Pd0.5 or Ag0.75Pd0.25 photocurrent trace), so the non-monotonic composition-dependence in H2 rate is not independently checked against photocurrent for those two intermediate ratios; and neither panel addresses stability, quantum efficiency, or why the 0.25 Ag:0.75 Pd ratio specifically optimizes performance.

Partial account, with a clear gap.

What it leaves open: F8a/F8b also report Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS and Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS, which rank 2nd and 3rd in H2 evolution — but neither was measured by photocurrent or PL, so the upstream evidence says nothing about them.
