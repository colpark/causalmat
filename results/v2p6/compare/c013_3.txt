What it leaves open: PF does not account for the ZT ordering. x=0.01 has the highest PF at every temperature, yet x=0.005 has the higher ZT at 300 K — the opposite order. Nothing in these two figures establishes a causal or even purely correlative link between the PF and ZT curves; thermal-conductivity data would be needed to close that gap.
