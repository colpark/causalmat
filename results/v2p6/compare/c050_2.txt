ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
The combination supports only a plausibility link, not a demonstration. The upstream SEM/photo result establishes that bare BST is smooth (~70 nm) while Ag-treated particles are rough, coated, and larger, and that this modification co-occurs with color darkening as Ag% rises — but only as two discrete states (bare vs. "modified"), with no graded series and with Ag content, particle size, and coating thickness confounded together. So the link between the structural observation and the dielectric trend remains an unconfirmed but consistent hypothesis, not an accounted-for result.
