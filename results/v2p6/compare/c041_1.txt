The upstream data account for the downstream observation only in a weak, permissive sense: they establish that the aptamer is present at/near the particle surface, which is a necessary precondition for it to function as a lymph-node-directing ligand and thus for enhanced accumulation to be plausible. But the upstream evidence does not itself explain *why* fluorescence rises — it gives no evidence of aptamer functional activity, coverage density, or specific target engagement (e.g., CCL21-mediated binding). The sparse, non-uniform P signal leaves surface coverage extent uncertain, so it cannot establish that enough aptamer is present to drive targeting. Other explanations for the fluorescence difference (altered surface charge, size, or nonspecific biodistribution shifts from the coating) are not ruled out by either dataset. The mechanistic link between "aptamer attached" and "greater lymph-node uptake" is asserted, not demonstrated, by this evidence pair.
