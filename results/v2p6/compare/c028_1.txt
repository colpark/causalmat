ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
Only two of the four panels bear on this question. F5a (ion-release profiles) shows Co2+ release rising with soaking time and scaling with cobalt loading: 0Co-MBG releases no detectable Co2+ at any time point, while 2Co-MBG and 5Co-MBG both release increasing amounts over 7 days, with 5Co-MBG consistently higher than 2Co-MBG — a clear, monotonic dose response in cobalt content for Co2+ release into solution. However, no VEGF panel was actually supplied.
