ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
Yes, the combination supports a real (if partial) link between the two, more than the earlier pristine-only comparison did. Panel F3a's cycle-indexed XRD shows two new peaks (~16.5°, ~21.9°) emerging after just 4 cycles and growing while the original KMnHCF peaks weaken through the 20th, 60th, and 100th cycle traces; panel F3b's ICP shows Zn at% rising in step over the same cycle points (4th→20th→100th), climbing to ~50 at% by cycle 20 and holding near 47 at% thereafter. This justifies saying the structural change (new peaks, weakening host peaks) and the compositional change (rising, then plateauing Zn) co-occur and progress together across cycling. It does not establish that the new XRD peaks specifically index to a Zn-containing phase (no refinement/indexing against the KZnHCF reference pattern shown at bottom of F3a is reported in the given data), nor that the bulk Zn at% from ICP resides in the sites producing those new peaks rather than elsewhere (e.g., surface deposit or side phase). So the evidence supports co-occurring compositional and structural change with cycling, but not a confirmed mechanistic or site-resolved link between Zn uptake and the specific new phase.
