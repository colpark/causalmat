This supports the claim that the same crystallographic phase/fraction is retained (persists) throughout cycling, consistent with a "single-phase" or near-single-phase reaction mechanism for this system.

What it leaves open: this is a 2-theta window (16-21°) only, so peaks outside this range (which could reveal a minority second phase) are not shown. Intensity loss is not quantified (no Rietveld fit or peak-area analysis given), so the actual phase fraction, degree of amorphization, or extent of any structural disordering cannot be determined from this panel alone. No lattice-parameter/peak-shift analysis is shown here, so whether unit-cell parameters change continuously (solid-solution) versus staying literally fixed is not distinguished.
