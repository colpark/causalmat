ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
The upstream SEM series (F3a→F3b→F3e) documents a real morphological progression: bare BST is smooth and ~70 nm, BST-SH shows a rougher surface consistent with the silane functionalization step, and BST@Ag5% is visibly coated, rougher, and agglomerated into larger clusters. This supports that a surface layer (silane, then Ag) was actually deposited on the particles, which is the physical prerequisite for the interfacial-polarization argument used to explain why Ag-coated BST/PAEN composites have higher dielectric constants than pure PAEN — consistent with F6a showing all three loadings (1%, 3%, 5%) well above pure PAEN (~3.35 at 100 Hz).

What it leaves open: SEM is shown only for bare BST, BST-SH, and BST@Ag5% — not for BST@Ag1% or BST@Ag3%. So the imagery confirms coating occurred at the top loading but does not itself demonstrate a graded increase in coating extent/thickness across 1%→3%→5% that would mechanistically account for the monotonic ranking 5%>3%>1%>>PAEN seen in F6a. That dose-dependent trend rests on the dielectric measurement alone; the SEM evidence establishes plausibility of the coating-driven mechanism but not a quantitative link between Ag loading and microstructural change at intermediate compositions.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
The four panels support the upstream claims but only partially bridge to a downstream "microstructure/distribution/PRP" story.

SEM (F3a/b/e): F3a shows bare BST as small, fairly uniform, faceted particles (~70 nm scale bar visible); F3b (BST-SH) and F3e (BST@Ag5%) both show coarser, agglomerated, rougher-surfaced particle assemblies. This is consistent with claim A: modification (silane or Ag coating) roughens and enlarges the apparent particle surface. However, these are all dry powder/filler micrographs, not composite cross-sections — they show particle-level morphology change, not how particles are distributed within the PAEN matrix. So they justify "surface modification changes particle morphology" but leave dispersion/distribution inside the polymer, and any interfacial mechanism linking morphology to dielectric response, unestablished.

F6a (dielectric constant vs frequency) exactly reproduces claim B: BST@Ag5% > 3% > 1% >> pure PAEN across the full frequency range, with ~100 Hz values (~6.05 vs ~3.35) matching the stated numbers.

Combination: the SEM and dielectric evidence are individually well supported and consistent in direction (more/rougher coating tracks higher dielectric constant), but the panels do not show composite microstructure, particle distribution, or a causal/mechanistic link between morphology and the property value — that inference remains open.
