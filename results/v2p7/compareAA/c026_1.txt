What the panels justify: N-doping of the graphene hollow spheres (CnC HS) makes the electrode more zincophilic and improves reversible Zn plating/stripping. DFT charge-transfer calculations (F2b) show N-doped graphene pulls substantially more electron density toward adsorbed Zn (~-0.03 to -0.037 e across Top/Hollow/Bridge sites) than pristine graphene (~-0.02 to -0.027 e), consistent with stronger Zn binding at N sites. Together this supports the conclusion that N-doping stabilizes Zn deposition and extends cycle life relative to undoped carbon.
