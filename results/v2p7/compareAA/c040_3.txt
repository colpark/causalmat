ANSWER 1 -- evidence only
On the actual question: F5e's EDS maps show C, Zn, N filling the particle outline in both the Anti-CCL21 DNA and A10 RNA aptamer-BSA@ZIF-8 samples, with P appearing only as sparse dots in the same footprint — evidence that the aptamer (via its phosphate backbone) is physically incorporated into the ZIF-8/BSA composite, i.e., successful conjugation/synthesis. F6e shows that 9h post-injection, the tumor field is brighter for A10 aptamer-BSA@ZIF-8 than for BSA@ZIF-8 alone.

This combination supports only that the A10 particles used in the biodistribution study do in fact contain aptamer, a necessary precondition for any aptamer-mediated targeting explanation of the brighter tumor signal. It does not mechanistically account for the downstream observation: EDS was run on dry particles from a synthesis characterization, not on the same material used in vivo, and gives no information about circulation, receptor binding, cellular uptake, or targeting efficiency. It also provides no elemental contrast against the neat BSA@ZIF-8 control, so it cannot show the aptamer is selectively responsible for the fluorescence difference rather than some other formulation variable. In short, F5e establishes aptamer presence (a structural precondition); it leaves the actual targeting/service-capability link — why that presence translates into greater tumor accumulation — entirely open.

ANSWER 2 -- the same evidence, plus the earlier result
The EDS maps (F5e) show C, Zn, and N filling the same particle outline for both the DNA- and A10 RNA-aptamer-BSA@ZIF-8 samples, consistent with these being framework/protein elements distributed throughout the particle, while P appears only as sparse, scattered dots within that same outline — consistent with a much lower-abundance, aptamer-derived phosphorus signal rather than a bulk constituent. This supports that the aptamer is chemically present on/in the particle but at low density relative to the ZIF-8 framework itself; it does not establish surface vs. core localization, quantify loading, or independently confirm the P signal's chemical origin beyond compositional inference.

The ex vivo images (F6e) show the tumor panel brighter (more yellow/orange in the pseudocolor scale) for the A10 aptamer MOF than for the plain BSA@ZIF-8 at 9h, supporting greater fluorophore accumulation/retention in tumor tissue for the aptamer-bearing construct.

Together, the EDS result establishes that the aptamer is physically incorporated (P present, sparse) on the same particles used in vivo, which is a necessary precondition for attributing the brighter tumor signal to aptamer-mediated targeting. But the combination does not establish mechanism (targeting affinity vs. general biodistribution), does not quantify P loading against tumor brightness, and rests on single representative images per condition/timepoint — so any causal service-capability claim beyond "aptamer present + higher tumor signal observed" remains open.
