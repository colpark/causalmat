F8 shows VEGF secretion by BMSCs: Blank ~60, MBG ~100, 2Co-MBG ~430 (P<0.05 vs each of the others), 5Co-MBG ~115 pg/mL.

Combination assessment: the upstream ion data justify that 2Co and 5Co scaffolds do release different, dose-ordered amounts of Co2+, giving a plausible mechanistic input (Co as a hypoxia-mimetic stimulus) for a VEGF effect. But the data do not account for the downstream pattern: VEGF is highest at the lower-Co (2Co) condition and drops back to near-baseline at the higher-Co (5Co) condition, i.e., a non-monotonic response, whereas Co2+ release itself is monotonic and highest for 5Co. Nothing in these two panels explains why higher Co exposure fails to sustain or exceeds the VEGF response — that requires an unmeasured factor (e.g., a cytotoxic/threshold effect at higher Co concentration), which this evidence does not test. Also unresolved: the F5a caption mismatch means the identity of that curve's labeled quantity should be verified against the source text.
