Upstream XRD (F6a) is run only on the HP5 specimen (hot-pressed, presumably the 5 wt% AlN composition) and marks reflections for ZrB2, SiC, graphite, and BN; no peaks are marked for AlN or Al2O3. Downstream, the HP hardness curve (F2b) rises from ~18.0 GPa (0 wt%) to a peak ~20.3 GPa at 1 wt% AlN, then eases to ~19.4 and ~19.2 GPa at 3 and 5 wt%.

Together these support one narrow point: at the highest AlN loading (5 wt%, HP5), no crystalline AlN or Al2O3 phase remains detectable, so the modest hardness value at 5 wt% cannot be attributed to a retained AlN (or oxide) second phase acting as a reinforcement or as a residual hard/soft inclusion — whatever AlN added was seemingly consumed, reacted, or rendered amorphous/sub-detection-limit.

This leaves open: (1) phase identity at 0, 1, and 3 wt% AlN is not shown, so the rise-then-plateau/decline shape of the hardness curve (especially the peak at 1 wt%) cannot be explained by phase data at hand; (2) no density, grain-size, or microstructural data are given to identify the actual mechanism (densification, grain refinement, grain-boundary chemistry) behind the hardness trend; (3) BN's origin/role and its relation to AlN additions are not addressed.
