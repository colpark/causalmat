What this combination supports: the two panels are internally consistent with each other's stated numbers, and both concern the same compositional family (Co paired with Fe and/or Cr), so it is plausible the authors intend CoFeCrOOH (DFT) and Co5Fe3Cr2 (LSV) as the same ternary catalyst, with its intermediate/balanced two-step energetics offered as a rationale for its fastest onset of the OER current.

What it leaves open: the DFT panel does not show CoFeCrOOH as lowest on the rate-limiting (larger) barrier — CoFeOOH is lower there — so the free-energy diagram alone does not establish why the ternary outperforms the Fe-only binary experimentally. The panels also don't establish that the DFT model's electrolyte/mechanism assumptions match the neutral-electrolyte LSV condition; that link is inferred, not demonstrated by these two crops.
