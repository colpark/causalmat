F6a (HP5 XRD): peaks indexed only to ZrB2, SiC, graphite, and BN. No AlN or Al2O3 reflections appear anywhere in the pattern.

F2b (Vickers hardness vs. AlN wt%): the HP curve rises from ~18.0 GPa (0%) to a peak ~20.3 GPa (1%), then eases to ~19.4 GPa (3%) and ~19.2 GPa (5%) — a shallow, near-flat trend after the initial jump, well above the separately plotted PS curve.

What the combination supports: since HP5 (the 5 wt% AlN body) shows no crystalline AlN or Al2O3, the added AlN does not survive as a distinct hard secondary phase in the hot-pressed material — it is evidently consumed, likely reacting to help form the BN detected, or otherwise incorporated (e.g., into a boride/oxide solid solution or grain-boundary phase XRD can't resolve). This is consistent with the HP hardness plateauing rather than tracking AlN content linearly, i.e., hardness is not being driven by retention of free AlN/Al2O3.

What it leaves open: XRD was run only on HP5, not on the 0/1/3 wt% HP samples, so whether AlN was ever crystalline at lower loadings, and exactly when/how it reacts, is unknown. The mechanism behind the 0→1% rise and the mild 1→5% decline (grain size, density, grain-boundary chemistry) is not identified by these two panels; any amorphous phase is invisible to XRD.
