Together these support the qualitative link: the Mg alloy's much greater electrochemical activity (consistent with active corrosion/hydrogen evolution consuming protons and generating hydroxide) plausibly drives its sustained alkalization, while the comparatively noble, low-current SS shows no such rise and instead trends acidic. So the direction of the two datasets is mutually consistent.

Left open: polarization is a short, single-point measurement and cannot by itself account for the pH curve's specific time course (the plateau near day 22, or SS's rise-then-fall-to-6.1 pattern).
