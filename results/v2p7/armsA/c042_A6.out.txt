Panels confirm the reported measurements. F5e shows, for both the DNA-aptamer and RNA-aptamer BSA@ZIF-8 samples, dense C/Zn/N signal filling the particle outline while P appears only as scattered, sparse points within the same outline — matching claim A exactly. Together, this is consistent evidence that aptamer conjugation adds phosphorus (from the nucleic-acid backbone) at low, spatially sparse abundance relative to the bulk ZIF-8/BSA framework, and that this conjugation coincides with a sign reversal of surface charge from positive to negative. The combination supports concluding that the aptamer is present on/in the particle and that its addition correlates with reduced/reversed zeta potential — i.e., a compositional change (P incorporation) co-occurring with a surface-charge change.

What it leaves open: EDS mapping is qualitative/2D-projected and cannot establish P is exclusively from the aptamer (it could include residual buffer or other trace phosphorus sources) or quantify aptamer loading; sparse P dots do not prove uniform aptamer coverage. Zeta potential is a bulk, ensemble surface-charge measurement and does not by itself demonstrate that the charge reversal is mechanistically caused by the aptamer's phosphate backbone specifically, only that it is temporally/compositionally associated with aptamer addition. No causal or structural link between the P distribution pattern and the specific magnitude of zeta potential shift is established by these two panels alone.
