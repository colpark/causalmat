F2d confirms the capacity fade: ~870→~480 mAh/g (cycles 1-2) collapsing to ~70 mAh/g by cycle 40, with rising but imperfect Coulombic efficiency.

What the combination supports: during cycle 1, the mesoscale/nanoscale structure probed by SAXS (this q-range, roughly nm-scale features) does not undergo a detectable bulk structural collapse or phase transformation coincident with the large first-cycle capacity drop; the SAXS data argue against major electrode-mesostructure degradation as the immediate cause of the cycle-1→2 capacity loss.

What it leaves open: the SAXS series only covers the first discharge/charge, not the many subsequent cycles over which capacity falls to ~70 mAh/g, so it cannot speak to whether structural change accumulates later. It also cannot identify the actual mechanism (SEI growth, contact loss, chemistry/identification change, or capability limits) driving the long-term fade — that remains undetermined by this evidence.
