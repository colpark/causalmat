Together, the evidence supports the correlational conclusion: the same synthesis route that yields smaller, narrower-distributed ZIF-8 particles also yields higher encapsulation efficiency across three unrelated cargo types, a reasonably robust pattern since it replicates over siRNA, DOX, and BSA.

What it leaves open: these are two separate assay panels (size measured on unloaded/parent particles, encapsulation measured on loaded batches), so no single-particle or paired measurement ties size directly to loading — the link is inferred, not shown. The synthesis routes likely differ in more than size (surface area, porosity, crystallinity, defect density), any of which could independently drive encapsulation differences, so size/microstructure cannot be isolated as the causal variable from this figure set alone.
