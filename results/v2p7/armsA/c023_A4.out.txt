Both panels confirm the tabulated HP numbers exactly. F2a (relative density, HP curve, red): ~97.5% at 0 wt% AlN, jumps to ~100% at 1 wt%, dips slightly to ~99.3% at 3 wt%, returns to ~100% at 5 wt% — matching measurement A. F2b (Vickers hardness, HP curve): ~18.0 GPa at 0 wt%, peaks at ~20.3 GPa at 1 wt%, then falls to ~19.4 GPa at 3 wt% and ~19.2 GPa at 5 wt% — matching measurement B.

Together these support: for hot-pressed samples, near-full densification (≥99.3%) coincides with the highest hardness values, and the low-porosity condition (1 wt% AlN, ~100% dense) also gives the peak hardness. This is consistent with porosity reduction contributing to the hardness gain from 0→1 wt%. The PS (pressureless-sintered) curves in both panels, sitting far below HP in both density (dropping from ~85% to ~70%) and hardness (~12 to ~7 GPa), further support that density level tracks hardness level across the two processing routes.

What is left open: from 1 to 5 wt% AlN, HP density stays essentially flat/near-saturated (99.3–100%) while hardness declines modestly (20.3→19.2 GPa). Porosity/density alone does not explain this decline — these two panels show no grain size, phase, or microstructure data, so the mechanism (e.g., grain growth, secondary-phase formation) is not established here. No PRP or "value" metric appears in these panels; that link cannot be assessed from this evidence.
