Upstream evidence: F2a (FTIR) shows new absorption bands at 2930, 2864 and 1122 cm-1 present only in the BST-SH spectrum (absent in BST and BST-OH), consistent with new C-H stretching and C-S/S-related vibrations from a thiol-bearing surface modifier grafted onto the particles. F2b (photograph) shows that after standing 2h in a water-toluene mixture, BST-OH settles into the lower (aqueous) layer while BST-SH disperses/floats in the upper (toluene, organic) layer.

Does the upstream account for the downstream? Partially, by inference rather than direct mechanistic proof. The FTIR result establishes that BST-SH's surface chemistry is distinct from BST-OH (new organic/thiol-related bonds present only on BST-SH), and the photo shows BST-SH is more hydrophobic/organophilic (partitions to toluene) while BST-OH is more hydrophilic (stays in water). Together these two independent observations are consistent with the standard causal story: surface bonding chemistry changes wettability, which governs which liquid layer the particles prefer. This is what the combination supports.

What it leaves open: the panels do not show a direct link connecting the specific new bonds to the partition behavior — no intermediate contact-angle, zeta-potential, or chemical-assignment (peak-by-peak identity of the 2930/2864/1122 cm-1 bands as thiol-derived) is given here, so the causal chain is plausible but not demonstrated within these two panels alone. Also not addressed: any quantitative dispersion stability, particle loading value, or PRP-specific property changes.
