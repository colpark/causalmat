The upstream photocurrent data (F10a) and downstream H2-evolution data (F8a, F8b) agree in rank order for the four samples they share: ZIS < Ag-ZIS < Pd-ZIS < Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS, both in transient photocurrent density (~0.4→0.9→1.5→2.1 uA/cm2) and in H2 evolution rate (1.5→3.2→34.5→125.4 umol/h) and cumulative H2 amount over 5 h. This concordance supports the claim that improved charge separation/transfer (the upstream photocurrent signal) tracks with, and plausibly underlies, higher photocatalytic H2-evolution capability (the downstream service outcome) for these four compositions.

What it leaves open: the H2-evolution panels (F8a, F8b) also report two intermediate bimetallic compositions, Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS (57.3 umol/h) and Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS (43.5 umol/h), that fall between Pd-ZIS and Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS in activity — but no photocurrent trace was shown for these samples, so the photocurrent evidence cannot confirm the same charge-separation ordering holds across the full Ag:Pd ratio series, nor explain why 0.25:0.75 is the optimum ratio rather than the endpoints. Additionally, photocurrent is a correlate, not a demonstrated causal mechanism; other factors (light absorption, active-site density, cocatalyst dispersion) are not ruled out as contributors to the downstream H2-evolution differences.
