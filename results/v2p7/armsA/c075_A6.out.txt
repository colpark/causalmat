F5d shows Rct climbing steeply from ~3 ohm at cycle 0 to ~17-20 ohm by cycle 60-100, then continuing more gradually to ~24-28 ohm by cycle 150-250 with wide error bars; Rfilm and Re stay flat and small throughout. F2a shows the "w/ HCl" capacity (red) falling steeply over roughly the first 20-50 cycles and then declining slowly to ~55 mAh/g by cycle 250, with CE near 100%.

The two curves are temporally consistent in a rough sense: both show a fast early change followed by a slower, longer tail, which is compatible with rising interfacial charge-transfer resistance contributing to the early steep capacity loss and to some of the later slow fade. That correspondence is what the combination supports — a plausible link between growing Rct and capacity decay, with Rfilm/Re ruled out as major contributors since they stay flat.

It leaves open: (1) the F5d caption does not state which synthesis condition (w/ HCl, w/o HCl, or simple mixing) the EIS was run on, so pairing it specifically with the red "w/ HCl" curve is an assumption, not stated; (2) correlation only, no causal mechanism shown; (3) Rct plateaus/scatters after cycle 150 (large error bars) while capacity keeps slowly declining through 250, so the EIS data alone does not account for that continued tail of fade.
