The combination supports a consistent rank order across both electrochemical and device measurements: RGO-Cu2S ≥ Cu2S >> Pt in reduction current, and the same order in Jsc, with Pt also showing the lowest Voc. This is consistent with the qualitative claim that stronger catalytic (reduction) activity at the counter electrode tracks with better photovoltaic output — i.e., the upstream CV evidence is directionally consistent with and helps rationalize the downstream Jsc/Voc figure-of-merit differences.

What it leaves open: the panels show only correlation in rank order, not a quantitative or mechanistic bridge (e.g., no charge-transfer resistance/EIS data here linking CV peak current magnitude causally to Jsc or Voc). Voc differences in particular are not obviously explained by counter-electrode reduction kinetics alone, since Voc is more commonly governed by recombination/junction behavior; the CV and J-V panels alone do not establish that the counter-electrode catalytic activity is the causal driver of the Voc trend, only that both happen to follow the same material ordering.
