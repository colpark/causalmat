What it leaves open: the images do not explain the mechanism behind the decline. Absence of visible agglomerates does not identify what is driving the strength loss (e.g., interfacial debonding, stress concentration at rigid particles, filler-matrix adhesion). The SEM is qualitative/local (single fields of view, no quantitative dispersion or interface metric), so it cannot on its own account for a monotonic mechanical trend — it only rules out large-scale clumping as the obvious cause.
