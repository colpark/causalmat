It does not establish that catalysis causes the Jsc/PCE difference: the panels contain no charge-transfer-resistance (EIS), fill-factor, or PCE values, and no control for other differences between cells (light-harvesting layer, series resistance, dye loading). So the CV-to-J-V correspondence is consistent with but does not prove the mechanistic account; magnitude of Voc differences (0.555/0.535/0.525) is not resolvable from F5a at this scale.
