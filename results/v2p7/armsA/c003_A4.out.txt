Yes, the two upstream measurements are mutually consistent and jointly support the downstream claim that adding 10 wt% C@Co does not change the phase identity or the thermodynamic plateau pressures of the Mg90Ce5Y5 hydrogen-storage alloy. F3a (XRD) shows the milled C10 pattern retains all four host phases (CeMg12, Y5Mg24, Ce2Mg17, Mg) seen in C0, with only one weak extra peak (labeled 5, Co) near 44.5°, indicating the additive is present in small amount without forming new bulk compounds or disrupting the host structure. Together, unchanged phase identity (structure) plausibly accounts for unchanged plateau pressure (thermodynamic value), since plateau pressure is set by the hydride-forming phases, which the additive does not alter.
