What this leaves open: the data are transport/output measurements only — they say nothing directly about downstream "value" or "service capability" claims (e.g., practical device utility, durability, conversion efficiency, or performance under real operating conditions/duty cycles). No mechanism is shown for why fiber morphology or PZT loading produces these gains, no data exist beyond 10 wt% PZT or outside the tested resistance range, and no long-term stability or absolute-efficiency figures are given. So the evidence supports the voltage/power trends themselves but does not by itself establish broader service-capability or value conclusions beyond those trends.
