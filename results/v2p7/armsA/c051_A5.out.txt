Both panels were opened and inspected directly.

F2a (FTIR) shows three separate spectra — BST, BST-OH, BST-SH particles. Only the BST-SH trace carries distinct new bands at 2930, 2864 cm-1 (C-H stretches) and 1122 cm-1 (likely C-S or Si-O-C), confirming claim A: these features are unique to the thiol-functionalized particle surface, absent from bare BST and from BST-OH. This supports a conclusion that BST-SH particles carry new organic/thiol-related surface chemistry not present on the other two particle types.

F5a (DSC) is a different sample series entirely: PAEN matrix alone and PAEN filled with BST@Ag at 1, 3, 5%. Tg drops from 176 C (neat PAEN) to 146/149/157 C with filler, rising modestly with Ag loading — matching claim B.

The combination does not causally connect: F2a's chemistry evidence concerns BST-SH particle surface groups, while F5a's thermal data concerns BST@Ag-loaded PAEN composites — no BST-SH sample appears in the DSC panel, and no PAEN-bonding evidence appears in the FTIR panel. So the upstream FTIR evidence documents new surface functionality on BST-SH particles, but it does not itself demonstrate or explain the Tg depression/trend seen for the (different) BST@Ag/PAEN composites. Whether thiol surface chemistry mediates matrix bonding or plasticization behind the DSC trend is left open — not shown by these two panels.
