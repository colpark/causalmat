All three panels were opened and inspected.

F6a shows three CV traces (Pt, Cu2S, RGO-Cu2S) with a sharp cathodic (reduction) peak near -1.2 to -1.3 V vs SCE: Cu2S and RGO-Cu2S reach roughly -40 to -45 mA/cm2 (the two curves overlap closely and are hard to rank precisely by eye), while Pt is essentially flat near zero, consistent with reported peaks of ~-43/-41 mA/cm2 vs ~-0.45 mA/cm2. F6b (magnified Pt) confirms Pt does have a small redox feature (~1.1 mA/cm2 anodic, a fraction of a mA/cm2 cathodic), just two orders of magnitude smaller than the sulfide counter electrodes. F5a shows J-V curves with the same ranking as claimed: RGO-Cu2S highest current density (~16), Cu2S middle (~15), Pt lowest (~13), curves converging near ~0.55-0.56 V.

Combination supports: the ordering of electrocatalytic reduction activity (RGO-Cu2S ≈ Cu2S >> Pt) at the counter electrode co-varies with the ordering of short-circuit current in the full device (RGO-Cu2S > Cu2S > Pt), consistent with counter-electrode catalysis toward the redox couple as a contributor to device current. It does not establish causation: no charge-transfer-resistance (EIS) data are shown here, the Voc differences are small and unexplained by CV alone, RGO-Cu2S vs Cu2S CV peaks are too close to explain their ~1 mA/cm2 Jsc gap, and F5a's caption is truncated ("J-V curves and"), leaving unclear whether these are illuminated PV curves alone or paired with another dataset (e.g., dark J-V or EIS) needed to close the mechanistic link.
