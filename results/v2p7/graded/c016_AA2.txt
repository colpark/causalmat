What it leaves open: the FTIR alone does not prove the new bonds are the actual NO-donor species responsible for the release kinetics — no time-resolved FTIR, no quantitative bonding-to-flux correlation, and no control (e.g., partial loading or intermediate time points) linking bond loss to flux decay is shown.
