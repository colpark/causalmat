Other explanations for the fluorescence difference (altered surface charge, size, or nonspecific biodistribution shifts from the coating) are not ruled out by either dataset. The mechanistic link between "aptamer attached" and "greater lymph-node uptake" is asserted, not demonstrated, by this evidence pair.
