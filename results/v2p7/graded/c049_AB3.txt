F3a-b-e are SEM images of dry BST powder at three modification stages (bare, BST-SH, BST@Ag5%), showing progressive roughening/coarsening consistent with heavier surface coating. The SEM series (bare, BST-SH, Ag5%) also does not match the tensile series (PAEN, Ag1%, Ag3%, Ag5%) composition-for-composition, so no direct correlation can be drawn across the full set. No causal mechanism for the elongation drop is established by this evidence.
