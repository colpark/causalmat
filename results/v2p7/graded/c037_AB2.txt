Does the upstream PF evidence account for the downstream ZT observation? Only partially. PF alone does not explain the ZT ranking: x=0.01 has the highest power factor at 300 K, yet it is x=0.005 that has the highest ZT there — the two rankings invert at that temperature. Since ZT = PF·T/κ, this inversion implicates thermal conductivity (κ) as the deciding factor, but neither panel shows κ, so the combination cannot confirm why x=0.005 outperforms x=0.01 in ZT despite lower PF. What the two panels jointly support: PF and ZT trends are both consistent with the stated values, and doping (x=0.01) clearly boosts PF over undoped material. What remains open: the mechanism linking PF to ZT (i.e., the thermal-conductivity contribution) is not addressed by these two panels and would require a separate κ figure.
