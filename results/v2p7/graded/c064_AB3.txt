The combination supports a correlational link between surface state and first-cycle kinetics, but not a demonstrated mechanism.

F4c (EDS) shows C (blue) and Co (red) concentrated on C10 particle surfaces/edges, consistent with the earlier-established HRTEM corroboration that this is a real carbon(+Co) coating layer at particle edges. What it leaves open: this is a same-sample correlation, not a causal isolation — C0 and C10 differ in bulk composition as well as coating, so the coating's independent contribution to kinetics is not isolated. Neither panel quantifies coating coverage/uniformity or links it mechanistically (e.g., via activation energy) to the kinetic curves, and whether C or Co (or both) drives the effect remains unresolved.
