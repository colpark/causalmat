ANSWER 2
The EDS maps (F5e) show C, Zn, and N filling the same particle outline for both the DNA- and A10 RNA-aptamer-BSA@ZIF-8 samples, consistent with these being framework/protein elements distributed throughout the particle, while P appears only as sparse, scattered dots within that same outline — consistent with a much lower-abundance, aptamer-derived phosphorus signal rather than a bulk constituent. The ex vivo images (F6e) show the tumor panel brighter (more yellow/orange in the pseudocolor scale) for the A10 aptamer MOF than for the plain BSA@ZIF-8 at 9h, supporting greater fluorophore accumulation/retention in tumor tissue for the aptamer-bearing construct.

Together, the EDS result establishes that the aptamer is physically incorporated (P present, sparse) on the same particles used in vivo, which is a necessary precondition for attributing the brighter tumor signal to aptamer-mediated targeting. But the combination does not establish mechanism (targeting affinity vs. general biodistribution), does not quantify P loading against tumor brightness, and rests on single representative images per condition/timepoint — so any causal service-capability claim beyond "aptamer present + higher tumor signal observed" remains open.
