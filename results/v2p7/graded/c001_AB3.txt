The combination supports a correlational, order-consistent story: the same ranking (RGO-Cu2S ≥ Cu2S >> Pt) holds for both counter-electrode reduction-catalysis magnitude (CV) and cell photovoltaic output (Jsc, Voc). What it leaves open: CV at the counter electrode primarily reports catalytic/charge-transfer behavior there, which mechanistically governs fill factor and series resistance, not Jsc or Voc, which are set mainly by light harvesting and photoanode/electrolyte charge-transfer kinetics. No measurement here (CV or J-V alone) establishes a causal or quantitative link from CE catalytic activity to the specific Jsc/Voc gains — only a parallel ranking. Efficiency/fill-factor/PCE values, impedance data, or a stated mechanism connecting CE kinetics to Jsc/Voc would be needed to close that gap; none of that is shown in these three panels.

ANSWER 2
Panels confirm the reported values: F6a shows CV curves for RGO-Cu2S (~-43 mA/cm2), Cu2S (~-41 mA/cm2), and a near-flat Pt trace (~0); F6b magnifies Pt to show its actual reduction peak (~-0.45 mA/cm2); F5a shows J-V curves with RGO-Cu2S > Cu2S > Pt in both current density and voltage, matching Jsc ~16.0/15.0/13.0 and Voc ~0.555/0.535/0.525.

The upstream evidence partly accounts for the downstream observation: the ordinal ranking matches (RGO-Cu2S ≥ Cu2S >> Pt in reduction activity; RGO-Cu2S > Cu2S > Pt in Jsc and Voc), so it is consistent with counter-electrode polysulfide-reduction activity contributing to photovoltaic output. It does not account for the magnitudes. The CV gap between Pt and the sulfide electrodes is enormous (~90-fold), yet the J-V gap is modest (Jsc drops only ~20-19%, Voc only ~5-6%), so raw reduction current cannot be the sole or linearly acting determinant of Jsc/Voc. Conversely, the RGO-Cu2S vs Cu2S CV difference is small (~43 vs ~41), yet both Jsc (16.0 vs 15.0) and Voc (0.555 vs 0.535) show a real, consistent gap — a difference CV alone does not obviously predict in size. No fill-factor/series-resistance data are shown here to explain why Pt's catastrophic CV deficit doesn't translate proportionally to PV loss, and no matched-sample correlation is given, so the structure/activity-to-PV-performance link remains directional/qualitative, not quantitatively established.
