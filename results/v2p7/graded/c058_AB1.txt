What is left open: EDS is shown for only one composition, so there is no elemental/compositional confirmation or quantification for the plain TiO2 or Er0.5Ti-O samples individually, and no dopant concentration values that could be correlated with the graded magnitude of PL suppression. The prominent C peak is also unexplained (likely coating/substrate) and not addressed by either panel.

Panels viewed: F5b (EDS spectrum, Ti/O peaks, dominant C peak, small Er ~1.4 keV and Ce ~4.8 keV peaks) and F7 (PL spectra, TiO2 > Er0.5Ti-O ≈ half > Er0.5Ce0.2 lowest, all peaking 390-420 nm).

The upstream evidence establishes that Er0.5Ti-O and Er0.5Ce0.2 samples do contain detectable Er and Ce alongside the Ti-O host (EDS), and that the detected Er is present as Er3+ associated with a Ti-O framework (EDS+XPS combination, per the earlier finding). This is consistent, qualitatively, with the downstream PL trend: progressive addition of dopant (Er, then Er+Ce) tracks with progressively lower 390-420 nm PL intensity, i.e., composition correlates with quenching of the TiO2 defect/oxygen-vacancy emission.
