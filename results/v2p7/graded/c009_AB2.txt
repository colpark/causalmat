F8b (panel b, same four temperatures) shows a much faster series: at 300 C the curve plateaus around 15 min (close to the ~11 min cited for C10) and at 280 C finishes by ~40 min, matching "C10 finishes in <40 min even at 280 C." So the combination supports: the two alloys' desorption kinetics differ in the direction and rough magnitude the activation-energy numbers predict (lower Ea, faster desorption for the +10 wt% CQCo alloy). It leaves open: the panel/figure captions as printed are garbled and never explicitly state "panel a = C0, panel b = C10" — that assignment is inferred from matching numbers, not read directly off the image labels, and the mechanism by which CQCo addition lowers Ea is not shown in these panels.
