The downstream DSC observation is a Tg drop in PAEN composites (176 C pure PAEN → 146/149/157 C at BST@Ag 1/3/5%). These are different objects: the FTIR/TGA evidence characterizes BST-SH particles alone, while the DSC panel (F5a) is on BST@Ag/PAEN composites, and neither panel nor caption confirms that the BST used in these composites is the BST-SH-functionalized material versus plain BST or BST-OH. So the upstream evidence cannot, on its own terms, be tied to the specific samples in the Tg measurement.

The upstream evidence therefore cannot account for why Tg drops ~20-30 C upon compositing, nor for the partial recovery trend with increasing Ag% (146→149→157 C). What is left open: whether the silane species (or any BST-SH grafting) is causally responsible for the Tg changes at all, versus filler loading, dispersion, or Ag itself.
