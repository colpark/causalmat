THE QUESTION
Does the upstream evidence account for the downstream observation about PRP/value / PRF/service_capability? Say what the combination supports and what it leaves open.

ANSWER 1
The three panels support the claim well, with one caveat. F9a (DFT) gives ΔG_H* values that rank, from most favorable (near zero) to least: Pd0.75Ag0.25 (-0.08) < Pd0.5Ag0.5 (0.12) < Pd0.25Ag0.75 (0.28) < Pd (-0.31) < Ag (0.49). F8b's H2 rates rank identically for the same compositions (note the paper's "AgxPd1-x" labels equal DFT's "Pd1-xAgx" labels — Ag0.25Pd0.75=Pd0.75Ag0.25, etc.): 125.4 > 57.3 > 43.5 > 34.5 (Pd) > 3.2 (Ag). This is a Sabatier-principle match — the alloy whose ΔG_H* sits closest to thermoneutral (Pd0.75Ag0.25) gives the fastest H2 evolution, while pure Pd (too strongly binding, ΔG=-0.31) and pure Ag (too weak, ΔG=0.49) both underperform the alloys. F8a's time-resolved curves are simply the cumulative trace of the same rates and add no independent evidence.

So the DFT thermodynamics plausibly account for the rank order of intrinsic HER activity across the AgPd composition series. What is left open: the DFT is computed on isolated (111) facets, not on the actual ZIS-supported AgPd cocatalyst; the measured rate is a whole-photocatalyst (ZIS+cocatalyst) quantity that also depends on light absorption and charge separation/transfer at the heterojunction, which ΔG_H* alone cannot address. Correlation, not a proven causal mechanism, is what the combination establishes.

ANSWER 2
The three panels support a coherent, correlational link between the computed H* free energy and the photocatalytic H2 rate, but only at the level of trend, not mechanistic proof.

F9a (DFT) ranks |ΔG_H*| from optimal to poor: Pd0.75Ag0.25 (-0.08) < Pd0.5Ag0.5 (0.12) < Pd0.25Ag0.75 (0.28) < Pd (-0.31) < Ag (0.49). F8b's measured H2 rates rank in the matching order: Pd0.75Ag0.25/"Ag0.25Pd0.75" (125.4) > Pd0.5Ag0.5 (57.3) > Pd0.25Ag0.75 (43.5) > Pd (34.5) > Ag (3.2), and F8a shows this same ordering holds continuously over 5 h of irradiation, not just at one endpoint. So the upstream Sabatier-type evidence (near-thermoneutral H* binding at the 3:1 Pd:Ag ratio) does account for why that composition gives the best downstream H2-evolution capability, and the two datasets are mutually consistent in ranking and in the outsized gap between Pd0.75Ag0.25 and the rest.

What it leaves open: the DFT is computed on idealized bare metal (111) surfaces, not on the actual ZIS-supported nanoalloy/heterojunction that produces the F8 measurements, so effects from the ZIS interface, charge transfer, light absorption, particle size, or real surface composition are not modeled. The match is a rank correlation across five points, not a quantitative or causal test.
