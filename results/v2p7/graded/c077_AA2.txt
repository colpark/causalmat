Both panels are consistent with, and jointly support, a Zn-for-K/Fe/Mn compositional/phase change in the electrode.

What is left open: neither panel establishes mechanism (ion exchange vs. dissolution–reprecipitation), quantifies phase fraction, localizes Zn (bulk vs. surface/electrolyte residue), or rules out a persisting mixed-phase/two-phase coexistence rather than full conversion.
