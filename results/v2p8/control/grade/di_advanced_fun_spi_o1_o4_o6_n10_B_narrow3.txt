Panel F2d (peak-width, Δσ/σ, Gaussian-width metric) shows: SC holds width near 100% from ~100–140 K then drops sharply to ~50% right at 150 K and stays there; PC's (242) width spikes to ~260% around 150–155 K before relaxing back toward ~100–150% at higher T; HOC's (004)/(400) width similarly spikes to ~128% near 150–155 K before decaying.
