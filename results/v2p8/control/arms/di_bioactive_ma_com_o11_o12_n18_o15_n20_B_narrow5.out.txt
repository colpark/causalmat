What it leaves open: no quantitative thrombus mass, occlusion percentage, or statistics are given — the comparison is qualitative/photographic only, so the ordering is descriptive, not tested. The images do not establish that reduced thrombus is mechanistically caused by the reduced platelet adhesion/spreading shown by SEM/BIO (versus, e.g., differences in fibrin, coagulation cascade, or flow effects) — only that the two phenomena co-occur across the same five surfaces.
