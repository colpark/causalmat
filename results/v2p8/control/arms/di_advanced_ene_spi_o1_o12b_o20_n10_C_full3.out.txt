The two panels I was given (F1a, XRD Rietveld; F3b, ICP at% vs. cycle number for K/Fe/Mn/Zn) are the same underlying data that the "earlier established result" was already built from: F1a is the single-phase Rietveld fit (monoclinic framework confirmation) and F3b is the K/Fe/Mn/Zn bar chart showing Zn rising from 0% (pristine) to ~51% at cycle 20 and settling near 46-47% by cycles 60-100, exactly matching the trend already described. So this "measurement" is not an independent line of evidence layered on top of the earlier result — it is the same XRD/ICP pair restated. Reading it "together with" the earlier result therefore adds nothing beyond what that result already states: co-occurrence, in the same electrode material, of (i) a well-refined open-framework monoclinic phase at the pristine time point and (ii) a bulk Zn content that climbs to and plateaus near ~47-51 at% with cycling. This combination is consistent with, and supports, Zn2+ becoming the dominant inserted cation in a material that starts out as an open, well-ordered host framework — but it settles nothing new about STR (whether that P21/n framework structurally persists once cycling reaches the Zn plateau) or about site_occupancy (whether the inserted Zn actually occupies framework coordination sites rather than residing elsewhere, e.g. as surface/interphase species or in a converted phase). Those remain open because no post-cycling diffraction (structure at cycle 20+) or site-resolved technique is shown in either panel; F3b is a bulk, phase-blind elemental measurement.
