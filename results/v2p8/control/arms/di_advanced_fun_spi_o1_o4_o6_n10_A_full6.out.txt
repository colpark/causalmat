What it does not settle: the sign and magnitude of the anomaly are opposite between SC (sharp narrowing, ~100%→~50%) and PC/HOC (sharp broadening, peaking at ~242% and ~128%). Narrowing versus broadening implicate different local mechanisms — e.g., loss versus onset of domain/strain heterogeneity, or grain-boundary and powder-averaging effects specific to PC/HOC — so this panel cannot say whether SC, PC, and HOC are undergoing the identical phase transformation with a preparation-dependent lattice response, or genuinely different transitions that happen to share a transition temperature. Peak-width data also cannot by itself identify the space groups involved or confirm a symmetry change; that requires additional structural evidence (e.g., peak splitting/positions, which is not in this panel).
