What this settles: the gross photographic/macroscopic record corroborates, at the whole-device level, the ranking already established by SEM morphology and platelet counts — BVLD/NO-PPAmF leaves the tube lumen and foil surface visibly cleanest, 316LSS and PPAmF are the most thrombogenic, and BVLD-PPAmF/NO-PPAmF fall in between. This supports treating BVLD/NO-PPAmF as the surface with the least gross thrombus formation under this 2 h blood-circulation service test, consistent with (not independently proving beyond) the reduced platelet adhesion/activation finding.

What it leaves open: the images are qualitative photographs with no quantification (no thrombus area, mass, or occlusion percentage measured), apparently one sample per condition, and no statistics — so the ordering is descriptive only. The panels say nothing about mechanism, and they do not establish how this 2 h ex vivo/in vivo result translates to longer-term clinical service capability or durability.
