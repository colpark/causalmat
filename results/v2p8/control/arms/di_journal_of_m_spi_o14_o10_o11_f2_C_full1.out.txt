The three panels examined: F8b is a bar chart of H2 evolution rate for six compositions (ZIS 1.5, Ag-ZIS 3.2, Pd-ZIS 34.5, Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS 125.4, Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS 57.3, Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS 43.5 umol/h) — this is the same underlying dataset already used in the established result. F8a is new information: it shows cumulative H2 evolution over the full 5 h irradiation time course for all six samples, and the ranking (Ag0.25Pd0.75 > Ag0.5Pd0.5 > Ag0.75Pd0.25 > Pd-ZIS > Ag-ZIS ~ ZIS) holds consistently at every time point, not just the 5 h endpoint. F10a is the transient (on/off cycled) photocurrent trace for only four samples (Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS, Pd-ZIS, Ag-ZIS, ZIS) — the same four already covered in the established result, with no photocurrent data for Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS or Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS.

Read together with the established result, this combination supports that the matching rank order between photocurrent and H2 evolution for the four overlapping compositions is not a one-off snapshot: it is stable across the entire irradiation period, since F8a shows the same ordering holding continuously through 5 h and F10a shows the photocurrent ordering repeating consistently across illumination cycles. This strengthens confidence that the correlation is reproducible over time for those four samples.

It leaves open exactly what the earlier qualifications already flagged: Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS and Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS still have no photocurrent measurement at any time point, so whether their intermediate H2 evolution rates correspond to intermediate photocurrent cannot be checked — the full six-composition dose-response mapping remains incomplete, and the causal question (composition driving photocurrent and H2 evolution independently vs. photocurrent driving H2 evolution) is still unresolved.
