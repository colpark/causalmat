Panel 5B, read directly, contains information the earlier synthesis said was absent: it shows error bars on all five bars and significance brackets with *** for two comparisons — (1) 316LSS+PPAmF as a group significantly exceed the three PPAI-modified surfaces (BVLD-PPAmF, NO-PPAmF, BVLD/NO-PPAmF) combined, and (2) BVLD-PPAmF+NO-PPAmF together significantly exceed BVLD/NO-PPAmF alone. Panel 5A again shows the same five SEM fields: many spread dendritic platelets on 316LSS/PPAmF, fewer/less-spread on BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF, and only a few round unspread platelets on BVLD/NO-PPAmF.

Read together with the earlier result, this settles that the stepwise reduction in platelet count on BVLD/NO-PPAmF is not merely a descriptive ordering but a statistically supported drop below both the untreated controls and the single-agent surfaces — i.e., combining BVLD and NO-PPAmF treatment produces a statistically significant, additional suppression of platelet adhesion beyond either agent alone, co-occurring with the SEM-documented loss of spread/activated morphology on that same surface. This upgrades the earlier "descriptive, not tested" qualification specifically for the count data.

It leaves open: the brackets test grouped comparisons (all-low-surfaces vs all-high-surfaces; single-agents vs combined-agent), not a direct pairwise test of BVLD/NO-PPAmF against BVLD-PPAmF or NO-PPAmF individually, so exact significance of the combined surface versus each single surface separately is not shown. The SEM morphology remains qualitative and untested statistically, and no mechanism for the suppression is established.
