What this supports: for this one composition, photocatalytic H2-evolution efficiency is wavelength-dependent in a manner consistent with the material's own light-absorption profile — i.e., where the sample absorbs more strongly, quantum yield is higher, and where absorption falls off, yield falls off too. This is consistent with (but does not itself prove) that photon absorption at each wavelength, not some wavelength-independent process, governs how efficiently absorbed light is converted to H2.

What it leaves open: the panel gives no AQY data for ZIS, Ag-ZIS, or Pd-ZIS, so it cannot show whether this same wavelength-tracking behavior holds across the composition series, nor can it be tied back to the earlier photocurrent/H2-rate ordering.
