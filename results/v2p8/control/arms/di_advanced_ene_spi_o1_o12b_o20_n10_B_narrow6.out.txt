Panels F5d–f show three refined/modeled structures across a compositional series (K0.25Zn0.25MnFe(CN)6 → K0.25Zn0.5MnFe(CN)6 → ZnMnFe(CN)6), each with an inset showing the local Mn–N polyhedron and bond distances, plus the extended framework with Mn (purple), Fe (orange), and, in F5f, added grey ZnN4 tetrahedra. As Zn content in the nominal formula rises across d→e→f, the Mn coordination polyhedron visibly changes shape and the inset label changes from MnN6 (implied/d) to MnN5 (e) to MnN4 (f), with grey tetrahedra appearing in f and touching the orange FeC6 octahedra in the drawn structure.

This justifies: within this three-structure series, increasing Zn substitution is associated, in the modeled/refined structures shown, with progressive loss of N ligands around Mn (fewer-coordinate Mn polyhedra) and with the appearance of ZnN4 tetrahedra that are drawn as connected to FeC6 octahedra. That is, the figure supports a structural correlation between Zn content and both Mn coordination-number change and Zn siting/connectivity in these three specific compositions.

It leaves open: whether these are refinements of actual cycled electrode states or computed/hypothetical end-member structures is not shown here, so no link to the cycle-indexed ~47 at% Zn plateau from the ICP data can be drawn. The captioned causal claim ("could attribute to intensified Jahn-Teller effect") is explicitly hedged, not demonstrated. Nothing here shows K or vacancy site occupancy, phase symmetry/space group at each step, or that this sequence is the actual pathway traversed during real cycling rather than a set of discrete compositional snapshots.
