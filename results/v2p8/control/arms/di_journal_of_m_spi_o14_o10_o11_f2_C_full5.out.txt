The new panel (F8a, H2 evolution amount vs. irradiation time, 0-5h) shows that for all six samples the H2 output rises roughly linearly over the full 5-hour run, and the ranking present at the endpoint — Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (~628 umol) > Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS (~290) > Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS (~220) > Pd-ZIS (~170) > Ag-ZIS ≈ ZIS (~10-15) — matches the rates reported in F8b (these endpoint values are essentially the F8b rates multiplied by 5 h). What this settles in combination: for the four samples common to both photocurrent and H2 measurements, the rank ordering already shown to match between steady photocurrent and H2 evolution rate is not an artifact of a single time-point measurement — it holds continuously across the full irradiation period, since the time-resolved curves are near-linear and preserve the same order throughout. This adds confidence that the correspondence between photocurrent ranking and H2-evolution ranking reflects a stable, reproducible trend over the reaction course rather than an incidental coincidence at one sampling instant.

What it leaves open: F8a still provides no photocurrent data for Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS or Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS, so the composition series remains only partially covered on the photocurrent side, exactly as before. The time-course data does not supply a quantitative or unit-comparable relationship between current density and gas-evolution rate, and it does not distinguish whether composition drives photocurrent and H2 evolution independently versus photocurrent differences causally contributing to H2 evolution differences — that confound is untouched by this measurement.
