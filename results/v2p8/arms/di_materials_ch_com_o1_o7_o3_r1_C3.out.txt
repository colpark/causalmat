The established result supports the following conclusion: for the HP composites, and especially the 1 and 5 wt% AlN specimens reaching ~100% relative density, the near-full-density value is genuine rather than a measurement artifact. This is because two independent lines of evidence agree — the bulk density measurement and the SEM fracture-surface image — with the SEM showing no open pores and large flat transgranular fracture facets (fracture running through grains rather than around residual porosity), which is the microstructural signature expected of a well-consolidated, pore-free material. So the justified claim is: the reported relative density figures reflect a real, observably dense and well-consolidated microstructure, corroborated by direct inspection, not just a number from one measurement technique.

What it leaves open/uncertain:
1. Because density is a single bulk average, it cannot by itself say whether any residual porosity (if present at the lower-density compositions) is open or closed, or how it is spatially distributed.
2. SEM only shows one local fracture surface qualitatively — it cannot quantify a density value or provide a composition-resolved (0/1/3/5 wt% AlN) trend; there is no wt%-resolved porosity data from SEM to match against the specific values (~97.5%, ~99.3%, ~100%) at each composition. So the correspondence between SEM appearance and density value across the series is qualitative consistency only, not a demonstrated quantitative trend.
3. Neither observation identifies the mechanism by which AlN addition raises relative density (e.g., acting as a sintering aid or via grain-boundary effects) — the pair confirms the resulting dense, pore-free, transgranular-fracture state, but says nothing about why or how AlN produces it.

In short: the fully-dense state at high AlN content is corroborated and trustworthy, but the porosity distribution at lower densities, any quantitative SEM-density correlation across compositions, and the causal mechanism of AlN's densification effect all remain unestablished by this result.
