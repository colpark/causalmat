Panel F8c (Journal of Materials Science & Technology, j.jmst.2021.12.003) is a dual-axis plot: left axis shows UV-Vis absorbance (a.u.) as a continuous curve for the Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS sample from 200-800 nm, right axis shows discrete AQY(%) points at five wavelengths: 400 nm @ 18.3%, 420 nm @ 15.8%, 450 nm @ 6.5%, 500 nm @ 4.7%, 550 nm @ 0.19%.

What this settles: the measurement is directly supported by the panel — AQY does decline monotonically from 18.3% at 400 nm to 0.19% at 550 nm for this one sample (Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS), and the drop in AQY visually coincides with the falloff of the absorbance curve past ~400-450 nm, consistent with the claim that AQY tracks the absorption edge. This supports the figure_of_merit conclusion that photocatalytic activity (as quantified by AQY) is wavelength-selective and correlates with light absorption for this specific catalyst composition.

What it leaves open: the panel shows only five single-point AQY values with no error bars, replicate measurements, or statistical treatment, so precision/reproducibility of each AQY value is unstated. There is no comparison to other Ag/Pd ratios or to undoped ZIS in this panel, so whether this composition is optimal, or how "tracking the absorption edge" compares quantitatively (e.g., overlap with a specific absorption onset wavelength) is not established — the correlation is visual/qualitative only. Reaction conditions, light source calibration, and AQY measurement methodology are not shown in the panel itself.
