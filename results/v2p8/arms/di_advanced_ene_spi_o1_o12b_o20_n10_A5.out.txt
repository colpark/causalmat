The three crystal-structure diagrams (F5d–f) show the local Mn coordination polyhedron shrinking in a stepwise fashion as Zn content rises: MnN6 (Mn–N ≈1.98–2.28 Å) at low Zn (K0.25Zn0.25MnFe(CN)6), MnN3 (≈2.15–2.20 Å) at intermediate Zn (K0.25Zn0.5MnFe(CN)6), and MnN4 (≈2.08–2.26 Å) in the fully Zn-substituted end member (ZnMnFe(CN)6). This directly supports the stated claim that Zn addition drives a progression MnN6 → MnN3 → MnN4 in the polyhedral models. Panel f additionally shows grey tetrahedra dispersed among the purple/orange octahedral framework, consistent with ZnN4 tetrahedra linking to FeC6 octahedra as stated; this connectivity is visually clear only in panel f — panels d and e do not show unambiguous grey ZnN4 tetrahedra, so the "grey ZnN4 linking to FeC6" claim is supported for the fully substituted composition but not separately confirmed at the two intermediate Zn levels from the images alone.

What is left open: the panels are polyhedral/ball-and-stick structural models with bond-length annotations, not raw diffraction or spectroscopic data, so whether MnN6/MnN3/MnN4 reflect refined crystallographic site occupancies (e.g., from Rietveld refinement) or idealized/averaged local motifs cannot be determined from the crops alone. The mechanistic attribution to an "intensified Jahn-Teller effect" is asserted in the caption, not something the geometry alone proves. Quantitative Zn/Mn site-occupancy fractions and whether the phase transition itself was independently confirmed (e.g., by diffraction) are not shown in these three panels.
