The panel (F8c) supports the claim exactly as stated: it plots wavelength-dependent apparent quantum yield (AQY, right axis) together with the UV-vis absorbance spectrum (left axis) for the Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS sample, with labeled data points at 400 nm@18.3%, 420 nm@15.8%, 450 nm@6.5%, 500 nm@4.7%, and 550 nm@0.19%. This confirms a monotonic decline in AQY from 18.3% at 400 nm to 0.19% at 550 nm, and the decline visually tracks the falloff of the absorbance curve, which drops steeply between roughly 380–450 nm (the absorption edge region) before leveling off toward baseline near 500–550 nm. So the measurement is well-supported as a correlation/action-spectrum argument: photocatalytic activity (AQY) closely mirrors the material's light-absorption profile, consistent with the absorbed photons being the ones doing the photocatalytic work.

What it leaves open: this single panel does not establish causation or mechanism — it does not identify which absorbing species (ZIS host, Ag0.25Pd0.75 cocatalyst, or a heterojunction/plasmonic state) is responsible for the photoresponse, nor does it report quantum efficiency error bars, reproducibility, or how AQY was measured (wavelength bandwidth, actinometry). It also says nothing about long-term stability, comparison to undoped ZIS or other Ag:Pd ratios within this panel alone, or whether the correlation with absorbance is quantitatively fitted (e.g., overlay/normalized action spectrum) versus qualitative visual tracking. No PRF (probable root cause/figure_of_merit) mechanistic claim beyond "AQY tracks the absorption edge" is licensed by this figure alone.
