Panels F5d–f (compositions K0.25Zn0.25MnFe(CN)6 → K0.25Zn0.5MnFe(CN)6 → ZnMnFe(CN)6, i.e., a series of increasing Zn content) are site-resolved crystal-structure models. They show the Mn coordination polyhedron changing (MnN6-type environment → MnN3 → MnN4) as Zn content rises, and gray ZnN4 tetrahedra appearing and bonding directly to FeC6 octahedra within the same 3D metal-cyanide lattice, with the caption attributing this to Jahn-Teller distortion from Zn2+ and calling it the trigger for a phase transition.

Combined with the earlier result (single well-ordered monoclinic P21/n host framework as-prepared; bulk Zn at% climbing to ~47-50% by cycle 20), this measurement supplies exactly the missing site-resolved link: it shows that as Zn content in the same MHCF-type lattice increases, Zn occupies N-coordinated metal sites within that framework (not some separate phase), forming ZnN4 units wired into the Fe-C6 sublattice, while forcing Mn out of its original octahedral site. Together they support that the Zn2+ taken up during cycling is structurally accommodated inside the host framework identified initially, by substituting into/near the transition-metal N-coordination sites, and that this occupancy itself drives a coordination/phase change rather than Zn merely accumulating as a separate bulk phase.

What remains open: the panels are a synthesized/modeled compositional series (x=0.25, 0.5, 1.0), not confirmed samples pulled from the electrode at cycle 20, so the ~47 at% cycling plateau cannot be matched to a specific one of these structures; whether 47% corresponds to saturation of ZnN4 sites specifically, versus panel f's full end-member (100% Zn, no K/Mn framework left), is not shown; and no cycle-indexed in-operando structural data are given, so the timing/reversibility of this MnN6→N4 transition during actual cycling is still unresolved.
