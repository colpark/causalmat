The three panels (F5d-f) give a composition-graded, site-resolved structural series: as Zn content rises (K0.25Zn0.25 → K0.25Zn0.5 → fully Zn-substituted ZnMnFe(CN)6), the Mn coordination polyhedron itself changes (MnN6-type environment reducing toward MnN3, then MnN4), and grey ZnN4 tetrahedra appear and bond directly to the FeC6 octahedra of the host lattice. This is exactly the "where" information the earlier ICP/XRD result lacked: it shows Zn incorporated at N-coordinated sites inside the same Mn-N/Fe-C framework type identified as the as-prepared monoclinic phase, not merely present somewhere in the electrode.

Read together, the combination supports a coherent picture: the open Mn-N/Fe-C host framework established at cycle 0 is the same kind of framework that, as Zn uptake climbs toward the ~47 at% plateau, structurally accommodates that Zn by forming ZnN4 units linked to FeC6, with Mn's local coordination correspondingly distorted (attributed to a Jahn-Teller effect from Zn2+). This ties the bulk compositional trend to a specific, framework-internal structural mechanism rather than leaving Zn's location ambiguous.

What remains open: the panels are modeled endmember/intermediate compositions (mole fractions of Zn per formula unit), not explicitly indexed to cycle number, so the correspondence between panel (e)/(f) and the actual cycle-20 ~47 at% ICP plateau is inferred, not directly measured at that cycle.
