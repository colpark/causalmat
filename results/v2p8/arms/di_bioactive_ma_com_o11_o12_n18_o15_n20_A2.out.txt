What this supports: the photographic/visual gradient described — heavy thrombus on bare 316LSS and plain PPAmF, intermediate (partial) clearing with either BVLD- or NO-loaded PPAmF alone, and the least visible thrombus/most open lumen and cleanest foil for the combined BVLD/NO-PPAmF sample — is directly visible in both panels and is consistent between the cross-section (B) and longitudinal foil (C) views.

What it leaves open: these are single representative images per group with no visible quantification (thrombus mass/area), no stated replicate number, no error bars or statistics, so the apparent ranking cannot be confirmed as statistically robust or consistent across replicates from the image alone. The images also do not establish mechanism (why BVLD and NO combine to reduce thrombus) or performance beyond the 2 h timepoint.
