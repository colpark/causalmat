This adds a wavelength-resolved, photon-accounting basis under the one sample that anchors the top of the established four-sample order.

What it leaves open: AQY was measured only for Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS, not for Pd-ZIS, Ag-ZIS, or ZIS, so this measurement cannot extend the established cross-sample ranking to a third metric — it does not show that AQY ordering also follows photocurrent/H2-evolution ordering across samples.
