1What the earlier step established

Together, the SEM morphology and the BIO platelet counts show that BVLD/NO-PPAmF has both the fewest adhered platelets (~5x1e2 cells/mm2, lowest of the five surfaces) and a morphology dominated by a few round, unspread platelets, in contrast to 316L SS and PPAmF (32 and 41x1e2 cells/mm2, many spread dendritic platelets) and to BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF alone (15.5 and 11x1e2 cells/mm2, fewer spread platelets but still present). Combining the two measurements shows a reduced platelet number (BIO) co-occurring with reduced platelet spreading/activation shape (SEM) specifically on BVLD/NO-PPAmF -- a joint reduction in adhesion and activation that neither the count alone (which says nothing about the shape/activation state of the platelets present) nor the SEM images alone (which give no quantitative count) can show.

qualifications recorded on it

  • No statistics or error bars are reported alongside either measurement, so the apparent stepwise ordering across the five surfaces is descriptive, not tested
  • SEM morphology is reported qualitatively per surface (many/fewer/a few) and is not tied numerically to the counted platelet densities from the same fields, so the correspondence between 'few round platelets' and '5x1e2 cells/mm2' is descriptive, not a measured relationship
  • The pair says nothing about the underlying surface-chemistry or biochemical mechanism producing the suppression -- only that count and morphology move together across surfaces

The photographs (F6b tube cross-sections, F6c explanted thrombus strips) show the same five-surface ordering already seen at the cellular level: 316L SS and PPAmF have lumens/tubes filled with dark thrombus (heaviest clot burden), BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF are only partly occluded, and BVLD/NO-PPAmF has a nearly clean lumen and foil (least clot). Read together with the established SEM+BIO result — that BVLD/NO-PPAmF uniquely combines the lowest platelet count with the least-spread, least-activated platelet morphology — the combination supports a step up in scale: the platelet-level suppression (fewer, rounder, less-activated platelets) on BVLD/NO-PPAmF is accompanied by a macroscopic, whole-vessel reduction in gross thrombus formation after 2 h of blood circulation. Neither measurement alone carries this: the photographs show only gross clot extent with no cellular resolution, and the SEM/BIO pair (as already qualified) shows only platelet-level counts and morphology with no evidence about whether that translates into less thrombus at the device/lumen scale. Together they justify saying that reduced platelet adhesion and activation on BVLD/NO-PPAmF co-occurs with, and plausibly underlies, reduced gross thrombosis on that same surface, tying the cellular finding to a functionally relevant outcome. What remains open: the photographs are descriptive with no measured thrombus area, mass, or statistics, so the ordering is qualitative, not tested. No step establishes a causal link — thrombus formation also depends on fibrin/coagulation-cascade activity not captured by platelet counts or SEM, so the photographic result could partly reflect mechanisms independent of platelet adhesion. Flow/patency function and underlying surface chemistry remain unaddressed by any of these measurements.

arm C — the earlier result alone · combined 0.00, limits 0.25, 0 contradiction(s)
The established result supports the conclusion that BVLD/NO-PPAmF suppresses platelet adhesion in two independent, convergent respects simultaneously: it has the lowest measured platelet count of the five surfaces tested (~5x1e2 cells/mm2, versus 32 and 41x1e2 on 316L SS and PPAmF, and 15.5 and 11x1e2 on BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF alone), and the platelets that are present on it are morphologically unspread (a few round platelets) rather than the spread, dendritic, activated morphology seen on the other surfaces. Because these are two distinct measurement modalities (a quantitative count from BIO and a qualitative shape assessment from SEM) that point the same direction on the same surface, the pairing justifies saying BVLD/NO-PPAmF shows a joint reduction in both platelet adhesion (number) and platelet activation (shape/spreading state), a claim that neither measurement alone could support — the count alone says nothing about the shape of the platelets that did adhere, and the SEM images alone give no quantitative comparison across surfaces. This leaves several things open. First, the ordering across the five surfaces is descriptive only: no statistics or error bars are reported, so whether the differences (including the gap between BVLD/NO-PPAmF and the next-lowest surfaces) are statistically robust is untested. Second, the SEM morphology and BIO counts were not tied together numerically within the same fields of view, so the specific correspondence between "few round platelets" and "~5x1e2 cells/mm2" is an inference from parallel trends, not a directly measured joint relationship. Third, the result is silent on mechanism — it does not explain what surface-chemistry or biochemical property of BVLD/NO-PPAmF produces this dual suppression, only that the two readouts move together across surfaces.
