Candidate answer:
Panel F1c shows a {111} high-resolution X-ray diffraction pole figure taken over the area delimited by the red ellipse (marked in the preceding panel, presumably an image of the polycrystalline film). Because each grain has a distinct crystallographic orientation, this pole figure resolves the individual orientation spots/reflections belonging to the different grains within that ellipse-marked region. A single grain for the follow-up (in-situ, in-3D) strain measurement is then picked out by selecting one specific, well-isolated {111} orientation spot/peak in the pole figure, i.e., choosing the grain whose orientation gives a distinguishable diffraction peak that can be tracked on its own during the thermal cycling.
