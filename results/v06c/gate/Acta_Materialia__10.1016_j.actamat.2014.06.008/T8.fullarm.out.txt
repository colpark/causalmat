Panel F4a: All three measured traces (red Lab Cycle, green Syn Cycle 1, blue Syn Cycle 2) are much shallower/less steep than the cyan ideal thermoelastic reference line — the reference line rises from about -0.24% at 25°C to +0.05% near 155°C (a very steep slope), while the measured traces span the same strain range only over a much wider temperature range (roughly 50-350°C), meaning the measured out-of-plane elongation increases far more slowly with temperature than the ideal/reference slope predicts.

Regarding retracing: the traces do not simply retrace the same path on heating and cooling — each cycle traces an open loop (hysteresis) rather than following one line back and forth, i.e., the cooling branch lies at a different (offset) position from the heating branch for the same temperature, so the response is only "quasi-reversible" (it returns near its starting strain value after a full cycle but does not overlap the heating path).
