Panel F1c is a high-resolution X-ray diffraction pole figure (a Laue-type angular map) plotting rotation around the x axis (deg) versus rotation around the y axis (deg), color-coded for diffracted intensity (blue = low, red/orange = high). The map shows a scatter of many discrete high-intensity spots (orange/red blobs) across the angular field, each spot corresponding to a distinct diffraction peak from an individual crystallite/grain within the polycrystalline film — i.e., the pole figure resolves the individual orientation "fingerprints" of many grains in the illuminated area (the area delimited by the red ellipse referenced in the caption, presumably from a companion panel).

A single grain for the subsequent in-situ 3D strain measurement is picked out by selecting one isolated diffraction spot in this angular map — here explicitly marked with a red dashed circle around one relatively isolated peak located near (~13.0°, -0.4°) in (y-rotation, x-rotation) space. This spot is well separated from neighboring peaks, allowing its rocking-curve position to be tracked without overlap/contamination from adjacent grains, so that this specific grain's orientation can be indexed and used to set up the diffraction condition for the follow-up 3D strain mapping under thermal cycling.
