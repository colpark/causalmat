In the xy phase map of panel F6a at Tu = 50 °C (before heating begins), the phase value is not uniform across the grain: it varies spatially in a mottled, banded pattern of alternating high (red/orange, roughly +1.5 to +2.4 rad) and low (blue, roughly -2 to -2.4 rad) regions, forming elongated stripe-like domains that run roughly parallel to one another across the grain's interior (visible near y ≈ -0.5 to 0.5 µm, x ≈ -1 to 0.7 µm). This heterogeneous striped pattern indicates that even at this initial, unloaded/unheated baseline state the grain already contains pre-existing internal strain heterogeneity (residual lattice distortion from growth/annealing) rather than a flat, uniform phase — consistent with the paper's motivation that intragranular strain heterogeneity in the polycrystal has not previously been imaged in 3-D and needs a baseline before load/heating is applied.
