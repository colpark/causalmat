Across panels F5a, F5c, and F5e, the intense diffraction peak's spread (width) in the qx–qy slices broadens as thermal cycling progresses, with the increase occurring specifically along the qx axis (the black lines in F5c and F5e mark this widening). Since F5a shows the earliest set of heating/cooling maps and F5c/F5e are later-cycle panels where the explicit qx-broadening is called out, the pattern indicates that as thermal cycling continues (successive heating/cooling steps imposing compressive/tensile TEC mismatch strain), the qx-direction spread of the peak grows greater from F5a to F5c to F5e. This is consistent with increasing intragranular strain heterogeneity building up within the grain over repeated thermal cycles, while the average (center-of-mass) strain response appears along qy.
