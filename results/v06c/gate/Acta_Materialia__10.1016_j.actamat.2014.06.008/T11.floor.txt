Context from a materials paper:
- How strain heterogeneity inside one grain of a polycrystal evolves under load, and how its neighbours set it, has not been imaged in 3-D in situ
- Temperature from room temperature to about 270 C and back in 25 C steps (about 0.03% mismatch strain per step), over successive cycles
- Thermal cycling of the film on silica: TEC mismatch imposes in-plane compression on heating and tension on cooling
- On heating, strain heterogeneity in the grain grows mainly along x and falls back quasi-reversibly on cooling; it stays uniform along z
