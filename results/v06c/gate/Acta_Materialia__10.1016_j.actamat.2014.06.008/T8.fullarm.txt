Context from a materials paper:
- How strain heterogeneity inside one grain of a polycrystal evolves under load, and how its neighbours set it, has not been imaged in 3-D in situ
- Polycrystalline Au film chosen for high X-ray scattering power, large elastic anisotropy and oxidation resistance
- Physical vapour deposition of a 475 nm Au film on fused silica
- Flame anneal 30 s at 800-900 C to grow the grains laterally
- Film has a strong {111} fibre texture: nearly all grains <111> out-of-plane, a few <100> grains
- Among g1's neighbours, g3 and g6 are strongly misoriented in-plane from g1, while g2, g4 and g5 are nearly aligned with it
- Dislocations cannot cross the boundaries to the misoriented neighbours along x, so they pile up and raise x-heterogeneity; their reversible motion gives quasi-reversibility
