- 2D bismuthene has not been used in nanomedicine because engineering it with the needed structure and composition is hard
- Bulk layered Bi as feedstock: puckered layers with 5.62 A channels wide enough to take water (~3.2 A)
- Five freeze-thaw cycles (-80 C) with bath sonication exfoliate bulk Bi into oxidised nanosheets (bismuthene oxide)
- Free-standing lamellar nanosheets ~200 nm across and ~14 nm thick
- Bismuthene is a semiconductor with an optical band gap of ~0.69 eV
- Under 660 nm light (100 mW cm-2) bismuthene generates singlet oxygen
- Synergy: photothermal ablation and ROS killing act together, clearing residual cells that regrow after PTT alone
