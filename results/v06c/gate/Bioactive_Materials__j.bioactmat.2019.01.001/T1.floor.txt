Context from a materials paper:
- A biodegradable metal stent for tracheobronchial stenosis needs a suitable degradation rate in airway fluid and compatibility with airway epithelium
- Compare three typical biodegradable metals: high-purity Mg, high-purity Zn and pure Fe
- Static immersion of the three metals in GS or SBF for up to 28 d
- Corrosion products on all three metals in both media are carbonates and phosphates
- In GS, less chloride and adsorbed mucin and bicarbonate keep the protective product film intact, which slows corrosion
- Degradation is slower in GS than in SBF, most strongly for HP-Mg (28 d: 0.78 vs 2.0 mm/y)
