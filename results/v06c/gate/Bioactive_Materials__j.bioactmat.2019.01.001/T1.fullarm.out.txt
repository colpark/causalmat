Panel F3c (crop 8874df6ae34b97a9f4f47c1dbeafa8320c7f7bf45608a4033bec6d62fdc2f495_C.jpg) is a bar chart of corrosion rate (mm/y) for HP-Mg, HP-Zn, P-Fe in GS vs SBF at 7, 14, 21, and 28 days.

The panel supports the claim: HP-Mg is consistently slower-degrading in GS than in SBF, and it shows the largest GS-vs-SBF gap among the three metals at every timepoint (asterisks mark significant GS<SBF differences for HP-Mg at 7d, 21d, and 28d). The 28d values read approximately 0.78 mm/y (GS) vs ~2.0 mm/y (SBF), matching the claim's numbers.

However, the relationship is not static over 7–28 d — it changes markedly: at 7d the GS/SBF gap is largest (GS ~0.1 vs SBF ~3.4 mm/y, roughly a 30-fold difference), and this gap narrows steadily through 14d (~0.2 vs ~2.5) and 21d (~0.45 vs ~2.05) to 28d (~0.78 vs ~2.0, less than 3-fold difference). This convergence occurs because the SBF rate declines over time while the GS rate rises over time, so although GS remains slower throughout, the protective effect of GS relative to SBF weakens progressively across the immersion period.
