The Fig. 1 composite trace (top, blue) for RZSZ-0 (0 vol% excess ZrSi2) shows two dominant phase sets matching the target reaction: ZrB2 (square markers dominate most of the pattern, e.g. the tall (101) peak near 40°, plus (100), (001), (002), (110)/(220), (102), (200), (311), (201)) and SiC (triangle markers at (111), and peaks near 59° and 76°/222). These are the two expected reaction products from 2ZrSi2 + B4C + 3C = 2ZrB2 + 4SiC.

However, the pattern is not phase-pure: several low/minor peaks in the composite trace are labeled with the inverted-triangle (ZrO2) and star (WSi2) symbols in the low-angle region (roughly 22–33° 2θ), indicating trace zirconia (likely surface oxidation of Zr-bearing powder) and tungsten silicide (likely from WC milling-media contamination reacting during hot pressing) impurities. So based on this XRD panel alone, the composite is primarily ZrB2 + SiC as intended, but it also contains minor secondary/impurity phases (ZrO2 and WSi2) beyond the two target reaction products.
