In panel F6 (RZSZ-15, 15 vol% excess ZrSi2), the image is explicitly labeled and shows: the in-situ SiC that formed is "nano-sized" — i.e., fine, sub-micron (nanoscale, well below the 500 nm scale bar) equiaxed particles. These nano-sized SiC grains sit dispersed among/on the surfaces of the larger, elongated ZrB2 platelets, appearing as small granular clusters decorating the platelet surfaces and occupying the spaces between adjacent ZrB2 platelets, rather than forming a separate coarse matrix phase.
