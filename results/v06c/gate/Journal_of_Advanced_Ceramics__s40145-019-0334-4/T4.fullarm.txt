Context from a materials paper:
- ZrB2-SiC ceramics need higher density and toughness; covalent bonding limits their sinterability
- In-situ reaction of ZrSi2, B4C and C with excess ZrSi2 as sintering aid should give dense, self-toughened ZrB2-SiC
- Make ZrB2-SiC in situ by Reaction (1): 2ZrSi2 + B4C + 3C = 2ZrB2 + 4SiC
- High-energy ball milling of ZrSi2/B4C/C powders, then in-situ reactive hot pressing
- Reaction (1) completes: composite is ZrB2 + SiC with trace WSi2 and ZrO2, no starting-powder peaks
- Exothermic Reaction (1) locally melts/deforms ZrSi2; the liquid aids densification and transports Zr, B to fast-growing ZrB2 planes
