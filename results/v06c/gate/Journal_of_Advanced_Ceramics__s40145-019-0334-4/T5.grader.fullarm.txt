Candidate answer:
In panel F6 (SEM fracture surface of RZSZ-15, excess ZrSi2 = 15 vol%), the ZrB2 grains appear as elongated, rod-/plate-shaped platelets (labeled "ZrB2 platelets") rather than equiaxed grains. Several of these platelets are visible across the field of view, oriented in different directions, with smooth, faceted, relatively straight-edged surfaces typical of anisotropic crystal growth. They are interspersed with numerous small, granular nano-sized SiC particles that coat the platelet surfaces and fill the space between them. This elongated platelet morphology is not an isolated occurrence — it recurs multiple times throughout the visible fracture surface (at least 4-5 distinct platelets are marked/visible), indicating that elongated ZrB2 grain growth is a widespread/characteristic feature of this microstructure rather than a rare exception.
