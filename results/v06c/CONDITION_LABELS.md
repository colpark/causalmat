# Condition labels (v06c part 2)

Every given panel carries a condition label: the sample name from the caption span, and the level where the caption or linked text states it. From the linked text only the level is taken, and only when it is one of the graph's sweep levels. The sentence itself is never handed over, because it often states the observation (ceramic F8: "the formed SiC grains were submicron sized"). A label at or beyond an intervene decision is withheld. A panel whose own sentences name several levels is marked as a series panel, and the intervene cut withholds it.

Every verdict is model against model.
