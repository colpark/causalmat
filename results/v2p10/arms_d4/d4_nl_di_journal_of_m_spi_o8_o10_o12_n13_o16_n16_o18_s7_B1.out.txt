Read together with the earlier result, this settles that the specific composition whose ZT-peak location could not be attributed to the PF peak (which sits at x=0.01) or to the mobility plateau (x=0.005-0.02) is not merely a local optimum within this work's own doping series — it is also the highest RT ZT among five compositions spanning four independent literature reports on related Mg3Sb2-based n-type materials (varying Te vs Se doping, with/without Mn, different Bi:Sb ratios). So the combination upgrades the earlier "internal peak, cause unidentified" finding to "internal peak that is also state-of-the-art in this cross-study snapshot," strengthening the practical significance of that peak without resolving its mechanism.

It leaves open exactly what the earlier result already flagged as unresolved: no PF, mobility, or thermal-conductivity values are given for any of the four reference compositions, so the comparison cannot say whether this work's ZT advantage over the literature arises from the same mu_H/PF-linked mechanism, from a thermal-conductivity difference, or from the differing doping chemistry (Se vs Te, presence/absence of Mn) across the five compositions. It is also a single-temperature, five-point snapshot with no error bars or dose-response across the reference series.
