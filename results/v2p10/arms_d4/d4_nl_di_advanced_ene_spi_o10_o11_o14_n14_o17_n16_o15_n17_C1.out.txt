This measurement (via the n14->n16 link) settles that the CnC HS vs C HS distinction is a real, co-occurring structure-and-performance divide: the same two host materials that differ chemically (nitrogen-linked spectral shift, present vs absent) and morphologically (flatter/sparser vs dense sharp-platelet zinc deposits) are also the two materials whose plating overpotential curves are nearly indistinguishable at low current density but separate to roughly -0.05 V vs -0.10 V at high current density. So it supports the claim that whatever differentiates these two hosts structurally/chemically also differentiates them electrochemically, and that this electrochemical gap is current-density-dependent — small or negligible at low rate, larger at high rate. That is a legitimate, if modest, finding: the hosts are not electrochemically equivalent, and their divergence is rate-dependent rather than constant.

What it leaves open is substantial. It does not identify which factor is causally responsible: the nitrogen chemical-environment change, the morphology difference, or some other unmeasured structural/compositional difference between the hosts could each independently or jointly produce the overpotential separation — the pairing cannot decompose this because chemistry and morphology co-occur by construction in the same samples. It also cannot establish a dose-response or magnitude relationship, since one side (chemistry/morphology) is only qualitatively described — spectral shift present/absent, deposits described as flat/sparse versus dense platelets, with no coverage or size quantification — while the other side (overpotential) is a continuous, rate-resolved voltage measurement; mismatched quantity types block any claim about how much structural difference yields how much electrochemical difference. Finally, the pyridinic/graphitic nitrogen-state assignment and any Zn-N bonding remain the paper's interpretive claim, not something the underlying spectral or morphological observations directly establish, and neither of those observations was itself quantified.
