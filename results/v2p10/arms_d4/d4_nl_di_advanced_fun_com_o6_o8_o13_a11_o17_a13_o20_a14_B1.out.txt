Panel F5d shows individual tumor-volume-vs-time curves for six groups. BNs-BSA+808nm (blue): all five curves stay near zero through ~day 14, then four of five rise sharply (to 250-700 mm3) by day 20-22, one stays flat. BNs-BSA+Lasers (660+808nm combined, red): all five curves stay flat near zero throughout the full ~22-day observation. PBS, BNs-BSA alone, Lasers alone, and BNs-BSA+660nm all show unrestrained, rapidly rising growth in every animal.

Combined with the established photothermal result (alpha=6.4 L g-1 cm-1 linear NIR absorbance plus tau_s=133.4s/eta=19.4% conversion efficiency, both measured on the same phase-identified majority-Bi/minor-Bi2O3 material), this in vivo panel supports that the demonstrated 808nm photothermal heat generation translates into a real but incomplete and non-durable therapeutic (ablative) effect in vivo: BNs-BSA+808nm alone produces an initial tumor-growth arrest consistent with heat-induced ablation, but this control is not durable -- regrowth in 4/5 tumors after day ~14 indicates the single-wavelength photothermal effect alone does not reliably achieve complete/permanent ablation at the dose/exposure used. The combination further supports that full, durable suppression (BNs-BSA+Lasers, presumably 660+808nm together) requires the additional 660nm component (likely a phototherapeutic/photodynamic contribution) layered onto the 808nm photothermal effect established earlier -- i.e., the material's proven 808nm heat-conversion capability is necessary but insufficient alone for durable tumor control.

What remains open: this in vivo panel does not identify which phase (Bi vs Bi2O3) or morphology drove either the initial arrest or the later regrowth; it does not quantify actual intratumoral temperature, dose, or heat delivered, so it cannot confirm the 19.4%/133.4s in vitro parameters are what operated in vivo, nor whether regrowth reflects insufficient heating, incomplete tumor coverage, or recurrence from surviving cells; and it does not isolate the 660nm laser's individual contribution to the combined group's success (BNs-BSA+660nm alone still grew unrestrained, so 660nm alone is insufficient, but its exact mechanistic role alongside 808nm-driven heat is not established by this figure).
