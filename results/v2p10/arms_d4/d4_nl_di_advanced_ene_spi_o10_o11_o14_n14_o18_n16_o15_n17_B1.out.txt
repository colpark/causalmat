Figure 4a shows CnC HS sustaining ~93–100% Coulombic efficiency out to ~205 cycles, while C HS drops to 0% CE around cycles 40 and 48 and its data series ends near cycle 50. Combined with the earlier three-way co-occurrence result (nitrogen-spectral shift, flatter/sparser Zn morphology, and lower 39 mV vs 43 mV nucleation overpotential, all tracking together on CnC HS relative to C HS), this new cycling-stability measurement adds a fourth correlate to the same two-sample comparison: the same sample (CnC HS) that shows the nitrogen-linked chemistry, the more compact/flatter zinc morphology, and the lower nucleation overpotential is also the sample that survives far longer and more stably in Coulombic-efficiency cycling, whereas C HS—with its dense sharp-platelet morphology and higher nucleation overpotential—fails abruptly (CE collapsing to zero) by cycle ~40–48.

What the combination supports: a consistent four-way co-occurrence across a single CnC HS vs. C HS comparison, in which the sample with the "better" nitrogen chemistry, flatter morphology, and lower nucleation barrier is also the one with far superior long-term Coulombic-efficiency stability. This strengthens the plausibility of a link between interfacial/nucleation behavior and cycling longevity, since lower nucleation overpotential and more uniform morphology are mechanistically the kind of things that could suppress the dendritic/short-circuit failure implied by CE collapsing to zero.

What it leaves open: this is still correlation across only two samples, not causation. The panel does not identify why C HS fails (e.g., dendrite penetration, short circuit, side reactions) nor demonstrate that morphology, nitrogen chemistry, or nucleation overpotential specifically causes the improved cycling stability rather than some other unmeasured structural/compositional difference. No intermediate-cycle morphology or overpotential data link the early nucleation measurement to the late-cycle failure mechanistically; the two measurements (early nucleation vs. long-term CE) are on different timescales and cannot be shown to be mechanistically connected rather than merely co-varying. Sample-to-sample variability and reproducibility (replicate cells) are also not addressed.
