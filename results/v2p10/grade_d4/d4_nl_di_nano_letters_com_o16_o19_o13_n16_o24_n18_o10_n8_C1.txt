This measurement (read as PRF/service_capability evidence) settles that Na-Co3O4's limited sodiation reactivity is a comparative, not just isolated, finding. Taken jointly, the retained crystalline oxide phase, absence of metallic Co, near-invariant SAXS pore-scale scattering, and the MD oxidation-degree ranking (Na-Co3O4 ~36% at 2 ps, plateauing after 0.8 ps, versus ~59% for Na-Co3S4 and ~91% for Li-Co3O4) support the conclusion that Na-Co3O4 undergoes an intrinsically lower and earlier-saturating degree of oxidation than either the Li-based analog or the Na-sulfide analog.
