This measurement justifies the conclusion that the phase-identified bismuthene material (majority hexagonal Bi with a minor Bi2O3 trace) does not just absorb 808 nm NIR light in a linear, concentration-dependent way (alpha = 6.4 L g-1 cm-1) but actually functions as a working photothermal agent: it converts absorbed light into heat at a defined efficiency (eta = 19.4%) with a characteristic heating time constant (tau_s = 133.4 s).
