The ZT data show that the composition with the lowest resistivity and highest power factor at 300 K, x=0.01, is not the composition with the highest ZT at 300 K: at 300 K, x=0.005 (~0.84) exceeds x=0.01 (~0.78), and ZT falls further and monotonically through x=0.02-0.04 (0.63-0.69).
