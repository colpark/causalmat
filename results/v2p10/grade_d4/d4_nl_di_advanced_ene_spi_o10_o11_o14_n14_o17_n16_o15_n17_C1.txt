This measurement (via the n14->n16 link) settles that the CnC HS vs C HS distinction is a real, co-occurring structure-and-performance divide: the same two host materials that differ chemically (nitrogen-linked spectral shift, present vs absent) and morphologically (flatter/sparser vs dense sharp-platelet zinc deposits) are also the two materials whose plating overpotential curves are nearly indistinguishable at low current density but separate to roughly -0.05 V vs -0.10 V at high current density. So it supports the claim that whatever differentiates these two hosts structurally/chemically also differentiates them electrochemically, and that this electrochemical gap is current-density-dependent — small or negligible at low rate, larger at high rate.
