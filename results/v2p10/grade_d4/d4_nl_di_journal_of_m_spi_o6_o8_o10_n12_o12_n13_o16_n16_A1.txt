What it leaves open: the 300 K comparison is not representative of overall performance — the panel shows all doped compositions converge and rise sharply above 400 K, peaking around 450-525 K at ZT ~1.2-1.3, well above the n-Bi2Te3 reference (~1.0 peak), with x=0.01 (not x=0.005) becoming the best performer at those higher temperatures. So the 300 K figures alone do not establish which composition is optimal overall, nor do they capture the material's actual peak/average-ZT advantage, which is the more relevant metric for thermoelectric device performance across its full operating range.
