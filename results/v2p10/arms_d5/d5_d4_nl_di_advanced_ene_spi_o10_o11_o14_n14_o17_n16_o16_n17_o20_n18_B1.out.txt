Panel F4f shows only CnC HS (pre-deposited Zn on CnC HS foil) in a full electrolytic Zn-MnO2 battery: charge and discharge capacity decline only modestly, ~1.0 to ~0.9 mAh, over 500 cycles, and the inset shows the 1st and 100th cycle voltage-capacity profiles nearly overlapping. No C HS full-cell trace is shown for comparison in this panel.

What the combination supports: the earlier result established that CnC HS remains stable (within ~0.03 V) out to 7100 min in symmetric-cell cycling while C HS spikes and fails by ~4400 min — tying CnC HS to durable performance in one test geometry (symmetric Zn||Zn cell, voltage-based, time-domain). F4f adds a second, independent measurement type and cell geometry (full Zn-MnO2 battery, capacity- and cycle-number-based) on CnC HS alone, and it likewise shows minimal degradation and reproducible cycling behavior (1st vs 100th cycle overlap) over an extended run (500 cycles). Together, these two results converge qualitatively: CnC HS's good long-term behavior is not confined to a single cell configuration or a single metric — it shows up as stability in symmetric plating/stripping voltage and as retained capacity/reproducible profiles in a working full battery. This strengthens confidence that CnC HS confers a genuine, generalizable durability advantage rather than an artifact of the symmetric-cell setup.

What it leaves open: F4f contains no C HS full-cell data, so the two-sample ordering (CnC HS more stable than C HS) established earlier cannot be extended into this full-battery context — there is no direct comparison here, only a within-CnC-HS-alone observation. The measurement kinds still do not align (minutes-to-failure vs. capacity-retention-over-cycles), so no quantitative or dose-response relationship can be drawn between the two results, only convergent qualitative support. Causality is still unaddressed: nothing here indicates whether the nitrogen chemistry, morphology, or overpotential gap drives either the symmetric-cell stability or the full-cell capacity retention; all prior causal and confounding caveats (only two samples compared in the original pairing, unquantified spectral/morphology claims, potential unmeasured host differences) remain fully in force.
