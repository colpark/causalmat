The inset shows the 1st and 100th cycle voltage profiles nearly superimposed in shape and plateau position, supporting the claim that the electrochemical charge/discharge mechanism (voltage plateaus, polarization) is essentially unchanged between early and later cycling, i.e., no major shift in reaction pathway or large increase in polarization by cycle 100. Together this supports a conclusion of good capacity retention and stable cycling behavior for the pre-deposited Zn/CnC HS foil electrode over 500 cycles at whatever fixed current density was used.
