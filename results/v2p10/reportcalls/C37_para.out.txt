The chain starts with two separate readings: XRD (pattern b, F2b) showing that sample S2 keeps only its starting phases -- alpha-Al2O3, cBN, ZrO2 -- from 1550 to 1700 C with no new peaks, and SEM (panel a, F7a) showing sample S1's cBN/SiAlON boundaries are cleaved and gap-free. Because these come from two different samples, all they support is that S1 and S2 differ in both phase behavior and interface look; there is no link within one sample. Step 2 needed exactly that missing link, so it is handed a new SEM image (panel b, F7b) of S2 itself: a protruding cBN grain beside a matching ~2 um socket, with the Al2O3 grains still clean-faceted. Now the unreacted-phase result and an interface result both describe S2, showing that its unreacted phase set co-occurs with a debonded, pulled-out cBN interface -- though only as co-occurrence in one local spot, not a mechanism. Step 3 then needed that within-S2 phase/interface pairing to make sense of new flexural strength data, where S2 is weakest (~80, 98, 170, 145 MPa) against S1's rise to 465 MPa and S3's flat ~170-195 MPa band, letting S2's poor interface/phase state be associated with its low strength, though still without any mechanism connecting them and without S2's own strength fluctuations tracking the constant phase reading.
