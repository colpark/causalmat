The chain starts with two measurements on sodiated PCO: SXRD showing the same reflections persisting through discharge/charge with only modest intensity loss, and XANES showing the white line staying strong (~1.25 at ~7728 eV, unlike Co foil) with charged spectra between CoO and Co3O4. Neither alone rules out a hidden metallic Co fraction or bulk amorphization, but together they support that the crystalline oxide is largely retained and Co is not reduced to metal. That conclusion was needed to read the next measurement, SAXS contours staying nearly straight with I(q) overlapping except a small drop at ~0.03-0.1 A-1 at 0.11/0.02 V: since a metallic conversion would create new particle/void interfaces visible to SAXS, the flat scattering confirms no pore-scale reorganization accompanies the minor chemical change. That combined structural/pore picture was then needed to interpret an MD result, oxidation degree at 2 ps ranking Li-Co3O4 (~91%) above Na-Co3S4 (~59%) above Na-Co3O4 (~36%, plateauing after 0.8 ps), showing the experimentally confirmed minor conversion corresponds to comparatively lower, early-saturating reactivity than the other chemistries. That comparative-reactivity conclusion was finally needed to interpret cycling data, ~865 mAh/g on 1st discharge falling to ~470 on 1st charge and ~60 by the 40th cycle, as consistent with, and a candidate structural explanation for, the steep capacity fade.
