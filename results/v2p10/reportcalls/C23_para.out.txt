The chain starts with two separate measurements on the same composite: FTIR shows new bands at 2930, 2864 and 1122 cm-1 only in BST-SH, pointing to CH2 groups and a Si-O linkage, while TGA shows composites lose about 1% weight between 180-250 C that pure PAEN does not lose. Read together, these confirm each other: the chemical species FTIR sees is shown by TGA to be a real, quantifiable mass component, and the mass TGA loses is shown by FTIR to belong to that distinct species rather than to PAEN itself.

Step 2 needed that confirmed-species result to make sense of new DSC data: pure PAEN's Tg is 176 C, but the composites come in lower, at 146, 149 and 157 C for 1/3/5% Ag, rising with Ag content. Because the interfacial species was already established as present, its co-occurrence with the depressed, Ag-graded Tg values is read as consistent with the species disrupting chain packing.

Step 3 then added permittivity and loss data: composites show a stability knee near 130-135 C versus about 155 C for pure PAEN. This needed the Tg-plus-chemistry pairing from step 2 to show the composites are shifted downward on a second, independent property axis, consistent with a shared origin in the interfacial layer and/or filler.
