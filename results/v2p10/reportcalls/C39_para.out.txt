The chain starts with two single-panel observations that cannot be combined: the XRD trace (F2c) shows an hBN marker present at 1500 and 1600 C but absent at 1400 C, supporting only a phase-transformation-onset reading on its own, while the SEM micrograph (F7a) shows cleaved, gap-free facets at the boxed cBN-grain/SiAlON boundary, supporting only a good-bonding reading for that one sample; since neither panel states a shared specimen or a shared temperature axis, no link between hBN formation and bonding quality can be drawn, and step 2 was handed exactly that limit. Step 2 then adds a second SEM view (F7b, a protruding cBN grain beside a ~2 um socket with cleanly faceted Al2O3 grains), but because it too lacks a stated temperature or specimen identity, it cannot be placed on the F2c temperature series either, so the same "no combined proposition" verdict carries forward unchanged. Step 3 finally adds flexural-strength values across composites S1, S2, S3 from 1400-1600 C. Against the standing limitation, this only shows that S1's strength rises to its highest value (~465 MPa) at 1600 C, the same temperature where hBN appears in F2c-a temperature coincidence, not a demonstrated mechanism, since sintering temperature also drives densification and grain growth, and S2 and S3 do not show the same rising pattern.
