The chain begins with XRD showing SiAlON phases formed alongside alpha-Al2O3 and cBN, paired with an SEM image of boxed cBN grains and SiAlON regions whose boundaries show cleaved facets with no gaps -- together these support that the reaction-formed SiAlON sits at gap-free, well-bonded cBN interfaces. Step 2 needed a contrasting case, since the first result only showed bonded grains and had no debonded example to compare against; the new SEM panel supplies exactly that, showing a different cBN grain protruding beside a deep ~2 um socket with clean-faced Al2O3 nearby. Combined with the handed-in result, this reveals bonding is not uniform on the fracture surface -- some grains sit gap-free, at least one pulled free -- giving associative (not chemically verified) support linking gap-free morphology to bonding. Step 3 needed a macroscopic property to connect to this morphological contrast, since step 2 had only images and no numbers; the new K_IC measurements across three samples (S1, S2, S3) supply that, showing S1 -- the sample where gap-free bonding was documented -- also has the highest K_IC (~5.6 MPa m^1/2), with its scatter (2.4, 2.9, 5.6) plausibly matching a surface containing both bonded and debonded grains, though no specimen-to-grain linkage or chemical confirmation ties the numbers to the morphology causally.
