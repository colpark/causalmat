The chain starts with two structural measurements that only mean something together: a single low-angle XRD reflection in MBG, 2Co- and 5Co-MBG, and TEM images showing parallel channel stripes over ~200 nm and a pore lattice with a ~5-6 nm period. Combined, they cross-confirm a genuine periodic mesostructure rather than an artifact, in all three samples. Step 2 needed that confirmed mesostructure before it could mean anything to add the release curve (~340 ug at 8 h, ~670 at 24 h, ~780 at 72 h, ~795 at 168 h): without step 1 there would be no basis for calling the releasing material "mesostructured," so the pairing associates the confirmed mesoporous carrier with a graded, plateauing release, while flagging that pore size, bulk uniformity, symmetry, Co-content trends, and rate cannot be derived from either measurement alone. Step 3 then needed that release timeline to interpret the survival data (~105%, ~81%, ~52% at days 1, 3, 7 versus ~100% blank and 110-118% drug-free): together they associate the mesostructured carrier's drug delivery with a time-extended, drug-specific antibacterial effect, while leaving the release-survival timelines unaligned and no mechanism linking the release plateau to the continuing survival decline.
