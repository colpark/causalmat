The chain starts from an EDS spectrum showing Ti, O, and small Er and Ce peaks, which confirms Er and Ce are physically present in the particles; this composition check was needed before the PL result (band at 390-420 nm strongest for TiO2, about half for Er0.5Ti-O, lowest for Er0.5Ce0.2) could be read as tracking stepwise Er-then-Ce incorporation rather than some unidentified variable, giving a conclusion that dopant addition tracks a progressive PL drop. Step 2 needed that PL ordering on hand to interpret a new degradation measurement (60 min degradation ~46% for Er0.5Ce0.2, ~41% Ce0.3, ~31% Ce0.1, ~20% TiO2, same order at 20 and 40 min): at the two samples shared by both measurements, TiO2 and Er0.5Ce0.2, lowest PL co-occurs with highest degradation, so the pair supports an association the PL or degradation data alone could not, though only at those two endpoints, with unresolved mechanism and dopant identity. Step 3 needed that PL-degradation association to interpret a third measurement, antibacterial colony counts showing Er0.5Ce0.2Ti-O light plates with fewest colonies for both strains while dark plates stay covered, showing the same TiO2/Er0.5Ce0.2 endpoint contrast now tracking in the same direction across a light-dependent antibacterial metric, still confined to those two endpoints and without a demonstrated mechanistic pathway or dopant-ion resolution.
