The chain starts from two NEXAFS measurements: A shows N 1s features near 398 and 401 eV only in CnC HS, not C HS; B shows that after zinc deposition the 398 eV feature collapses while 401 eV grows taller. Together these establish that the nitrogen species found only in CnC HS undergoes a spectral change specifically tied to zinc plating. Step 2 needed that result to interpret a new morphology observation (flat, sparse zinc platelets on CnC HS vs. a dense mat of sharp platelets on C HS): pairing them shows the nitrogen-linked spectral shift and the flatter morphology co-occur in the same material. Step 3 needed that chemistry-plus-morphology pairing to make sense of a new overpotential measurement (plating curves nearly coincide at low current density but separate to about -0.05 V vs -0.10 V at high density), showing the same two materials also diverge electrochemically as rate increases. Step 4 needed that result to interpret new Coulombic efficiency cycling data (CnC HS holds 93-100% CE to about 205 cycles; C HS collapses to zero by 40-48 cycles), showing durability divergence co-occurs with the earlier distinctions. Step 5 needed step 4 to interpret new capacity-fade data (1.0 to 0.9 mAh over 500 cycles, 1st and 100th cycle profiles overlapping), which corroborates the favored host's stability using a second metric over a longer horizon.
