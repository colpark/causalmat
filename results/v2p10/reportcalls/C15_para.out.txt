The chain starts from two XRD observations: one labels YSZ reflections as t-ZrO2 and Al2O3-YSZ reflections as c-ZrO2, while the other shows a small (012) peak at ~43 deg and split peaks at ~35, 60 and 74 deg only in the YSZ pattern. Together these establish that YSZ is predominantly tetragonal and Al2O3-YSZ is cubic, since the peak-shape detail explains why the phase labels were assigned. Step 2 needed that phase call as a frame for the new orientation-change data (~4.3-11 deg per interval at 5 cycles falling to ~1 deg by 20 cycles in Al2O3-YSZ, versus steady 0.7-2.3 deg in YSZ), so it could show the phase difference co-occurs with a diverging micro-strain evolution. Step 3 needed that strain divergence to interpret the new crack-image evidence (longer, linked cracks and a wide horizontal crack in Al2O3-YSZ), establishing that the same coating with large decaying strain also develops connected cracks. Step 4 needed that strain/crack co-occurrence to place the new spallation timeline (intact at 5 cycles, corners spalled at 10 cycles, growing slightly to 40 cycles), showing the strain/crack signature precedes spallation onset in time, though no mechanism or general relationship is established at any step.
