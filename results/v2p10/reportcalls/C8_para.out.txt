The chain starts with TEM showing dense new dark nanoparticles after reduction and Raman showing only bulk-Bi modes with oxide bands absent; together these establish a new nanoparticle phase consistent with metallic Bi, since TEM alone gives no composition and Raman alone gives no location. Step 2 needed that phase call to interpret the new absorbance data (linear A/L at 808 nm, R2=0.9999, alpha=6.4 L g-1 cm-1): combined, they show the same Bi-identified material is also a strong, linear NIR absorber. Step 3 needed that absorption result to make sense of IR-imaging temperatures rising with concentration and power while water stayed cold: combined, they show the absorbing material actually heats under irradiation in a dose-dependent way, extending 'strong absorber' to 'photothermal heater.' Step 4 needed that heating result to interpret the tumour curves, where all five BNs-BSA+Lasers tumours held at zero but four of five 808 nm-only tumours regrew: combined, they show the demonstrated photothermal capability corresponds to a durable complete response only with the added laser, while 808 nm alone gives an incomplete, non-durable effect.
