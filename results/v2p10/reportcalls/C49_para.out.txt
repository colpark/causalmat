Step 1 opens with two measurements taken together: FTIR bands at 2930, 2864 and 1122 cm-1 appearing only in the BST-SH spectrum, read as evidence of a covalently grafted MPTMS layer (Si-O, CH2) on the BST particles, and a DSC series showing Tg at 176 C for neat PAEN falling to 146, 149, 157 C for BST@Ag at 1, 3, 5% Ag. Together these support that the silane-grafted, Ag-loaded filler changes the composite's glass transition relative to the unfilled resin. Step 2 needed that conclusion as its baseline because it supplies the only established composition-dependent result (the four Tg values) against which a new measurement can be judged. The new measurement -- dielectric constant declining only ~10% from 100 Hz to 1 MHz for every sample -- is placed beside the Tg series and shows a contrast: Tg varies with filler loading, but the frequency-dependent dielectric trend does not differ across compositions. The step still carries forward step 1's limits: the grafting-versus-Ag-content confound is unresolved, there is no per-sample dielectric number to pair quantitatively with the four Tg values, no dose-response is established, and because one measurement is frequency-domain and the other temperature-domain, nothing can be concluded about how dielectric constant behaves as temperature approaches Tg.</message>
