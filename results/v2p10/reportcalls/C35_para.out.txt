The chain starts with XRD showing SiAlON phases (Si4Al2O2N6, Si1.8Al0.2O1.2N1.8) forming alongside alpha-Al2O3 and cBN, paired with an SEM panel where boxed cBN grains and SiAlON regions show cleaved facets with no gaps at their boundaries; together these support that the reaction-formed SiAlON is associated with gap-free, well-bonded cBN-matrix interfaces. Step 2 needed that conclusion because it described only one bonding condition; the new panel, showing a different cBN grain protruding beside a deep ~2 um socket next to cleanly faceted Al2O3 grains, supplies the missing contrasting case, letting the two panels together show that bonding is not uniform across the sample -- some grains are gap-free while at least one has pulled free. Step 3 needed that bonding-variability finding because the fractography alone had no link to any performance measure; the new flexural-strength data (S1 rising to ~465 MPa at 1600 C, S3 flat ~170-195 MPa, S2 ~80-170 MPa) let the two be read together only as a loose associative narrative -- that variable bonding is one plausible contributor to variable strength across temperatures and series -- without tying any specific bonding state to any specific strength value.
