The chain starts from two Hall measurements: carrier concentration n_H rises from ~0.5e19 to 3.3e19 cm-3 across x=0.005-0.04, while mobility mu_H (~125-170 cm2/Vs) stays above every literature value at comparable n_H, together showing Se doping adds carriers without a mobility penalty relative to reference samples. Step 2 needed that carrier/mobility result to interpret a resistivity measurement (rho falling from ~650 to ~12-23 micro-ohm m at 300 K, with doped rho rising with T while undoped rho falls): combined, they show the resistivity drop is consistent with rising n_H driving conductivity up, unopposed by any mobility loss. Step 3 needed that resistivity/mobility picture to make sense of a power-factor measurement (PF peaking at x=0.01, ~29 at 300 K and ~22 at 623 K, above x=0.005 and x=0's at most ~5): together they show the PF peak sits on the same favorable, mobility-preserved low-rho regime rather than being an artifact of resistivity behaving badly. Step 4 needed that PF/resistivity link to interpret an efficiency measurement (eta at Th=623 K ~11.8% for x=0.01 versus ~9.5-10.6% elsewhere): combined, they show the composition favored by the resistivity/PF trend is also the composition with the highest calculated efficiency.
