The chain starts with two NEXAFS measurements: sharp N 1s features near 398 and 401 eV appear only in CnC HS, not C HS, and after zinc deposition the 398 eV feature collapses while the 401 eV feature grows taller. Together these show the nitrogen species specific to CnC HS undergoes a chemical-environment change that tracks zinc plating. Step 2 needed that sample identity to interpret a new morphology measurement (flat, sparse zinc platelets on CnC HS versus a dense sharp-platelet mat on C HS): it could now say the nitrogen-linked spectral shift and the flatter morphology occur in the same sample. Step 3 needed that two-axis pairing to place a new measurement, nucleation overpotentials of 39 mV (CnC HS) versus 43 mV (C HS), onto the same two-sample comparison, extending co-occurrence to three axes. Step 4 needed that three-way result to add long-term cycling data (C HS spiking and failing near 4400 min, CnC HS stable to 7100 min) as a fourth co-occurring axis, still only an ordering, not a causal link. Step 5 needed that four-way lab result to place a final measurement, a cell stack powering a phone, as a narrative continuation from characterization to device demonstration, without tying the device's function back to any specific lab-measured property.
