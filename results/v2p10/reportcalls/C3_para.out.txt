The chain starts with two NEXAFS measurements: sharp N 1s features near 398 and 401 eV appear only in CnC HS, not C HS, and after zinc deposition the 398 eV feature collapses while the 401 eV feature grows taller. Together these establish that CnC HS's nitrogen species undergoes a spectral change specifically tied to the zinc-plating step. Step 2 needed that settled nitrogen-spectral result to interpret a new morphology observation (flat, sparse zinc platelets on CnC HS versus a dense mat of sharp platelets on C HS): pairing them shows the nitrogen-spectral shift and the flatter morphology co-occur in the same sample, extending the distinction from one axis to two. Step 3 needed that two-axis co-occurrence to place a new pair of nucleation-overpotential values (39 mV on CnC HS, 43 mV on C HS) into the same comparison, showing the lower overpotential also tracks with the same sample, extending co-occurrence to three axes. Step 4 needed that three-way result to add long-term cycling data (C HS spikes and fails near 4400 min; CnC HS stays stable to 7100 min), extending the tracking to a fourth axis, cycling life. Step 5 needed all four prior axes to interpret a new full-cell capacity measurement (capacity falling from about 1.0 to about 0.9 mAh over 500 cycles, with 1st and 100th cycle profiles nearly overlapping) as extending the same CnC HS sample's favorable symmetric-cell behavior into a practical full-cell capacity-retention outcome, though only for CnC HS, with no matched C HS full-cell run to compare against.
