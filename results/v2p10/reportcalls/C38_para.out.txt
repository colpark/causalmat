The chain starts from two panels for different samples: XRD (F2b) shows S2 has only its starting phases (alpha-Al2O3, cBN, ZrO2) with no new peaks across 1550-1700 C, while SEM (F7a) shows S1's cBN/SiAlON boundaries are cleaved and gap-free. Because these come from different samples, together they only suggest that different binder chemistries differ in both phase behavior and interface morphology, not a within-sample link. Step 2 needed exactly that missing piece: the new SEM panel F7b gives S2 its own interface picture -- a protruding cBN grain beside a ~2 um socket, with clean-faceted Al2O3 grains -- letting the unreacted phase set (from F2b) finally be paired with an interface morphology for the same sample, S2, rather than borrowing S1's data. Step 3 then needed a mechanical-property reading, since the S2 phase/interface pairing said nothing about toughness. The new K_IC values show S2 has the lowest readings among S1, S2, S3, so this lets the sample with lowest toughness be the same sample already carrying the joint phase/interface picture, producing a combined phase-identity/interface/toughness account -- but only for S2, and only as co-occurrence, not mechanism.
