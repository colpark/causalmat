The chain starts with two structural probes of PCO on sodiation: SXRD shows the same reflections persisting with only modest intensity loss, and Na XANES shows a strong white line unlike Co foil, with charged spectra between CoO and Co3O4. Combined, these establish the oxide stays crystalline and Co stays oxidized -- something neither shows alone, since XRD can't rule out a small metallic fraction below detection, and XANES can't rule out bulk amorphization. Step 2 needed that result to interpret SAXS: contours stay nearly straight except a small drop at 0.11/0.02 V, which only becomes meaningful paired with the prior finding, showing the minor chemical change isn't accompanied by pore-scale reorganization. Step 3 needed that combined structural/microstructural picture to place a new MD result -- Na-Co3O4's oxidation degree (~36%) ranks below Na-Co3S4 (~59%) and Li-Co3O4 (~91%) and plateaus early -- giving a comparative, simulation-side counterpart showing Na-Co3O4's sodiation reactivity is intrinsically limited. Step 4 needed that "limited reactivity, retained structure" conclusion to interpret the cycling data (870 to 480 to 70 mAh/g by cycle 40): together they show the severe capacity fade cannot be explained by extensive oxide conversion or microstructure collapse, since both are already shown to be limited, leaving the actual driver of fade unidentified.
