The chain starts with two N 1s NEXAFS measurements: A finds sharp features near 398 and 401 eV only in CnC HS, none in C HS, marking nitrogen species unique to that material; B shows that after zinc deposition the 398 eV feature collapses while the 401 eV feature becomes taller, so those features change specifically with zinc plating. Together A and B establish that the nitrogen environment in the nitrogen-containing material shifts in step with zinc deposition. Step 2 needed that result to interpret a new C 1s measurement showing the 290.1 eV carbon feature disappearing after zinc deposition while the 284.9 and 288.3 eV features persist. Alone, this carbon result shows only a selective carbon change; paired with the nitrogen result, it shows the same zinc-deposition step selectively removes one nitrogen environment (398 eV) and one carbon environment (290.1 eV) together, leaving the other nitrogen and carbon features intact. That co-occurring selectivity narrows the reactive site from any generic surface change to a single specific environment, identified as pyridinic nitrogen.
