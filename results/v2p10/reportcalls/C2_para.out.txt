The chain starts from two NEXAFS measurements: sharp N 1s features near 398 and 401 eV appear only in CnC HS, and after zinc deposition the 398 eV feature collapses while the 401 eV feature becomes taller. Together these establish that the nitrogen species unique to CnC HS shows a spectral change specifically tied to the zinc-plating step. Step 2 adds a zinc-morphology observation (flat, sparse platelets on CnC HS versus a dense mat of sharp platelets on C HS); it needed the spectral result to know which sample was which, letting the two differences be paired onto the same material comparison rather than treated separately. Step 3 adds overpotential data showing the two hosts' plating plateaus nearly coincide at the lowest current density but separate to about -0.05 V versus -0.10 V at the highest; it needed the chemistry/morphology pairing to anchor which material carries the electrochemical gap, extending the co-occurrence to performance under rate. Step 4 adds long-term cycling data (CnC HS stable to 7100 min, C HS spiking from 2600 min and failing near 4400 min), needing the prior pairing to carry the same two-material identity into a lifetime/failure axis. Step 5 adds full-cell capacity data for CnC HS alone (about 1.0 to 0.9 mAh over 500 cycles, 1st and 100th profiles overlapping), needing the established CnC HS stability finding to show consistency at the device level, though only for that one material.
