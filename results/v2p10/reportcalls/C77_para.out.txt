Combining these establishes a mismatch: the composition with the largest power factor is not the composition with the largest room-temperature ZT. On its own, this bar chart could invite the reading that the paper beats the literature because it has a superior power factor. But it needs step 1's result to rule that out: since step 1 already showed the highest-PF sample in this work's own series isn't its highest-ZT sample, PF superiority can't be the reason this work's ZT beats the literature figures. The combination therefore shows PF is not a sufficient explanation for the favorable comparison, while leaving the actual driver, and any thermal-conductivity role, unidentified.
