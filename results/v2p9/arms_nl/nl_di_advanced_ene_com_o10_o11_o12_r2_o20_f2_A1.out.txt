Figure 4A (the only panel to open) is a step-rate test: specific discharge capacity vs. cycle number, with color-coded blocks for current densities from 55 up through 3520 mA/g and then back down to 55 mA/g. The trace shows a stepwise decline — roughly 94–98 mAh/g at 55 mA/g, dropping through the 110/220/440/880/1760 steps to a minimum of ~54–60 mAh/g at 3520 mA/g — and then a partial recovery to ~78 mAh/g when the current is returned to 55 mA/g at the end.

This directly supports the stated measurement and justifies the conclusion that the V/Fe PBA cathode retains a substantial fraction (roughly half to 60%) of its low-rate capacity even at a 64x higher current density (3520 vs 55 mA/g), and that the capacity loss at high rate is largely, though not completely, reversible: capacity comes back to ~78 mAh/g rather than fully to the initial ~94–98 mAh/g. That gap (78 vs ~96) is the key thing the figure leaves open — the plot alone does not distinguish whether the ~18–20 mAh/g shortfall is due to permanent rate-induced degradation, ordinary cycle-number fade accumulated over the ~65 cycles of the test, or both. The figure also does not report charge capacity or Coulombic efficiency trends, does not test rates beyond 3520 mA/g, and gives no information on longer-term cycling stability at any single fixed rate — so durability/service life beyond this one rate sweep is not settled by this panel.
