What this justifies: the material exhibits genuine photothermal conversion under 808 nm irradiation, with spot temperature increasing monotonically with both agent concentration and incident power density, and negligible heating in the water-only control - solid qualitative/semi-quantitative support for photothermal (PTA-type) activity, i.e., PRP/value in the sense of "does it convert light to heat, and does it respond dose/power-dependently."
