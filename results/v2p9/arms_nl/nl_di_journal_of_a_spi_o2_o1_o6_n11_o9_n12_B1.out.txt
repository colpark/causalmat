The image measurement shows that within the boxed region of Al2O3-YSZ, cracks lengthen and link up, and a new wide horizontal crack forms near the bottom, between "before" (F4e) and "after five thermal cycles" (F4f). This is a direct micrograph-level record of cumulative microstructural damage accruing specifically in the cubic-phase (Al2O3-YSZ) coating over the earliest cycling interval.

Read together with the established result (cubic-phase Al2O3-YSZ shows large per-interval orientation change, ~4.3-11 deg, concentrated at 5 cycles, decaying by 20 cycles, versus YSZ's steady low 0.7-2.3 deg), the combination supports that the large early micro-strain signal in Al2O3-YSZ is not just an abstract orientation-change number but corresponds to visible, physical crack growth and linkage in the same coating over the same early cycling window — i.e., the strain proxy and the observed damage track together in timing (both concentrated early) and in which coating they occur in (Al2O3-YSZ, not YSZ). This ties the micro-strain measurement to a tangible damage mode (crack extension/coalescence) rather than leaving it as an uninterpreted number, and reinforces that the cubic-phase coating is the one accumulating structural damage.

What it leaves open: no crack-growth image is shown for YSZ at matched cycle counts, so the contrast in damage accumulation between the two coatings is not directly imaged, only inferred from the orientation-change contrast established earlier. The cycle count for F4f ("five thermal cycles") only overlaps the early part of B's cycling range, so no imaged link is established between the strain decay by 20 cycles and any corresponding plateau or slowing of crack growth. Mechanistically, why cubic phase produces both larger orientation change and this cracking pattern remains unaddressed — no phase-to-damage mechanism is given. The measurement is a single before/after pair on one boxed region, not a quantitative crack-density or crack-length metric, so no quantitative correlation with the degree-per-interval numbers in B can be drawn, only qualitative co-occurrence of early-cycle damage and early-cycle high orientation change in the same material.
