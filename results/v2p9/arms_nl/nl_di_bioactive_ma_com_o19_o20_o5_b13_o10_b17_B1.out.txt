Figure 8 shows photocatalytic degradation of MO (%) vs time, with the order Er0.5Ce0.2Ti-O (~46%) > Er0.5Ce0.3Ti-O (~41%) > Er0.5Ce0.1Ti-O (~31%) > TiO2 (~20%), consistent at 20, 40, and 60 min.

Combined with the earlier settled result (same particle system chemically confirmed by XPS/EDS to contain Er3+ tied to the Ti-O framework, with visible-light absorption edge extended past 420 nm and largest in the Ce0.2 sample), this new functional measurement adds a third, independent line of evidence that tracks with composition and with the absorption result: the same Ce0.2-doped material that shows both confirmed Er-related doping and the largest red-shift in absorption edge is also the one with the highest photocatalytic degradation activity, and all doped samples outperform undoped TiO2 at every timepoint tested. Together, this supports a coherent functional correlation: doping (of this Er/Ce co-doped TiO2 system) is associated with enhanced visible-light-range photocatalytic activity, and the non-monotonic Ce-loading dependence (activity peaks at Ce0.2 rather than rising or falling monotonically with Ce content) mirrors the non-monotonic peak in absorption red-shift at the same composition — i.e., the compositional optimum for optical shift and the compositional optimum for degradation activity coincide.

What this still leaves open: the degradation assay measures overall photocatalytic performance, not the mechanism producing it. As before, nothing here establishes whether it is the confirmed Er3+ species or the oxidation-state-undetermined Ce that drives either the optical shift or the degradation enhancement, since Ce0.2 is only the sample identity common to both measurements, not evidence isolating Ce's causal role. It also does not establish that the absorption-edge shift itself (rather than surface area, crystallite size, charge-carrier separation, or other unmeasured factors) is the proximate cause of the degradation gains — the coincidence of optima is correlational, not mechanistic. It still does not resolve lattice-incorporation versus surface-association of the dopant, nor does it add any oxidation-state information for Ce. So the three-way combination upgrades the claim from "chemically confirmed doping co-occurs with an optical shift" to "the same compositional series also shows a matching non-monotonic functional (degradation) benefit peaking at Ce0.2," but a mechanistic chain from confirmed dopant identity through absorption shift to degradation activity remains unestablished.
