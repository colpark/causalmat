Panel F11b (Biomaterials 2011.11.042) is a bar chart of relative bacterial (or cell) survival percentage over days 1, 3, and 7 for three groups: Blank control (set to 100%), Scaffolds (drug-free), and Scaffolds+Ampicillin. The plotted values match the stated numbers: at day 1 the three groups are roughly overlapping (~100, ~109, ~104%); by day 3 Scaffolds+Ampicillin drops to ~81% while Scaffolds alone rises to ~118%; by day 7 Scaffolds+Ampicillin falls further to ~52% while Scaffolds alone stays elevated at ~116%. Asterisks appear over the Scaffolds+Ampicillin bars at days 3 and 7 only, consistent with the caption stating significance (P<0.05) for the ampicillin-loaded group versus both blank control and drug-free scaffolds.

What this supports: the measurement directly substantiates a time-dependent antibacterial/growth-inhibitory service_capability (PRF) attributable specifically to the ampicillin loaded in the scaffold — plain scaffold material without drug does not suppress survival (it tracks with or slightly exceeds blank control), whereas the drug-loaded scaffold produces a progressively larger, statistically significant reduction in survival by days 3 and 7. So the capability claim "drug-eluting scaffold confers antibacterial/cytotoxic effect over the first week, increasing with time" is well supported by this panel.

What it leaves open: the panel does not establish the mechanism (killing vs. growth inhibition vs. assay artifact), does not specify what "survival" is being measured (bacterial CFU vs. an MTT/viability assay on a cell line — the axis label alone doesn't disambiguate), and does not address duration or capability beyond day 7, reversibility, dose-response, or performance against other/multiple bacterial strains. Day-1 lack of significance also leaves open how quickly the effect onsets. No panel here addresses release kinetics of ampicillin from the scaffold itself.
