What it leaves open is substantial. No spot-level chemical or EDS analysis was done at either site, so it is not actually confirmed that the socket region lacks SiAlON — the entire contrast rests on morphology, not measured composition. The XRD phase identification is a bulk/global measurement for the whole sample, not verified locally at either the gap-free grains or the socket, so composition at both specific sites is inferred, not directly measured. The comparison is a single static fractographic snapshot on one sample, not a controlled experiment (no SiAlON-free control, no matched conditions), so it cannot rule out alternative explanations for the debonding such as local porosity, residual stress, grain orientation, or a fracture-test artifact.
