The F6a plot shows dielectric constant vs. frequency (100 Hz–1 MHz) for pure PAEN and the three BST@Ag composites (1%, 3%, 5%), each curve nearly flat, declining only ~10% across six decades of frequency, while the curves are clearly separated by loading: pure PAEN ~3.3→3.0, 1% Ag ~5.3→4.7, 3% Ag ~5.6→5.0, 5% Ag ~6.1→5.4.

Read alone, this measurement only shows frequency stability of permittivity and a loading-dependent baseline; it says nothing about morphology or thermal transitions.

Combined with the earlier SEM/DSC result (bare vs. Ag-coated BST morphology, and Tg dropping from 176 C neat to 146-157 C in composites, ordered but not quantitatively tied to coating extent), the two together support a broader picture of "service_capability": the composites are not just thermally altered (lower, filler-dependent Tg) but also electrically functional and stable across the operating frequency range typically used for dielectric/capacitor or embedded-capacitance applications — i.e., the Ag-modified BST filler simultaneously modifies polymer chain mobility (Tg) and raises/stabilizes the dielectric constant, and the dielectric response does not collapse or drift with frequency the way one might expect if the composite were structurally compromised or the filler poorly dispersed. That combination supports a claim that these composites remain serviceable as dielectric materials despite (or alongside) the coating-induced Tg reduction.

What remains open: the frequency-stability data are not matched to temperature-dependent measurements, so the combination cannot establish whether dielectric performance holds up specifically near or above the lowered Tg values (146-157 C), which is the regime where service capability would actually be stressed. It also cannot establish a mechanistic or quantitative link between coating extent/Ag loading and the specific slope or magnitude of dielectric decline — only that all four samples happen to be frequency-stable and ordered by loading, mirroring (but not causally connecting to) the Tg ordering.
