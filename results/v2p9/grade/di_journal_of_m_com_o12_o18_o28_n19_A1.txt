This measurement settles the thermodynamics side of the PRP/value question: the equilibrium (van't Hoff) plateau pressure of the hydride is essentially unchanged by adding 10 wt% CQCo. Since plateau pressure at a given temperature reflects the enthalpy/entropy of the hydriding reaction, this supports the conclusion that the catalytic additive does not measurably alter the thermodynamic stability of the magnesium hydride phase — it is not acting by shifting the reaction equilibrium. Any benefit of CQCo addition (if there is one) would have to come from kinetics (reaction rate, activation energy, nucleation) rather than from changing Pressure-composition-temperature equilibrium values.
