The CV panels show Cu2S and RGO-Cu2S with large reduction peaks (~-41 and ~-43 mA/cm2 near -1.25 V) versus Pt's negligible ~-0.45 mA/cm2 (note the y-axis rescale between 6a and 6b needed just to see Pt's curve at all). Combined with the earlier TEM+XRD result (RGO sheets wrap Cu2S spheres, with XRD reflection attenuation ordering -1/-2 ~ bare Cu2S > -3, consistent with more GO giving more wrapping/masking of crystalline Cu2S), the new pairing shows that this wrapping does not come at an electrochemical cost: the RGO-Cu2S composite's peak current (~-43) is essentially the same as, or marginally larger than, bare Cu2S's (~-41), even though the wrapping is inferred to reduce exposed/detectable Cu2S surface.
