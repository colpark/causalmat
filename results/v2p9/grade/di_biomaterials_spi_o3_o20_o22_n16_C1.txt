What it leaves open is substantial. First, there is no matched, dose-response comparison: the XRD data span three compositions (MBG, 2Co, 5Co) with slightly different peak positions, but the release curve is reported for only one of them (2Co-MBG), so no one has shown that more or less ordered samples release faster or slower -- the link between degree of order and release rate is untested. Third, the evidentiary base is sparse (one peak, four time points), far too thin to fit a rate law, diffusion model, or any quantitative structure-kinetics relationship. In short: co-occurrence of order and sustained release is established; causation, mechanism, and any order-dependent kinetics are not.
