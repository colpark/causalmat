What it leaves open: whether the gain is durable over long-term cycling/outdoor conditions, whether it scales to larger-area or flexible modules, whether the specific hierarchical microsphere morphology is necessary versus other Cu2S/rGO composite geometries, and whether the observed PCE gain generalizes across different quantum-dot sensitizers or electrolyte systems.
