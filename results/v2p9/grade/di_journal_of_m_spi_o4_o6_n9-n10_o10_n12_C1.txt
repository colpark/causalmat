This combination justifies a qualitative mechanistic conclusion, not a quantitative or exclusive one. What it supports: the DFT band structure shows Se substitution raises the Fermi level so that the M, K, and L conduction-band minima (starting ~0.15 eV above E_F in the undoped case) are pulled down to E_F, while the Gamma minimum remains well above it (~0.35-0.4 eV); this is directionally consistent with the Hall measurement showing carrier concentration rising monotonically with Se content, from ~0.5e19 cm^-3 at x=0 up to 3.3e19 cm^-3 at x=0.04. So the causal/mechanistic story is plausible and consistent, but not quantitatively verified or uniquely established.
