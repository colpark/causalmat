This result does not settle anything about STR (structure), microstructure, or shape/morphology directly — the underlying observations are purely spectroscopic (N 1s NEXAFS peak presence/absence and relative peak-height ranking), not structural or morphological data. What it does support is a chemical/electronic-structure claim: the nitrogen-containing sample (CnC HS) carries a nitrogen species whose local electronic environment changes specifically when zinc is deposited — the ~398 eV feature collapses and the ~401 eV feature grows to dominate — while the nitrogen-free sample (C HS) shows no such features to begin with.
