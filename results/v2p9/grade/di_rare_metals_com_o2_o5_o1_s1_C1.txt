It supports this via two independent lines of evidence converging: a structural signal (extra XRD reflections at ~21-37, 50, and 67 deg appearing only in the La pattern) and a thermal signal (a separate DSC dissolution shoulder B at ~638 C beyond the Al2Cu-related peak A at ~605 C, again only in La), with the Sm alloy showing neither.
