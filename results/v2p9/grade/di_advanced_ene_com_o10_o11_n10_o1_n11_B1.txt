The DFT panel (F1a) plots computed ΔG for two sequential Co oxidation steps across four model (oxy)hydroxides. CoFeOOH has the lowest ΔG for the Co3+→Co4+ step (~6.2 eV) and CoCrOOH the lowest for Co2+→Co3+ (~1.05 eV), with CoFeCrOOH intermediate on both axes and Co(OH)2 highest on the Co3+→Co4+ axis (~6.7 eV). This strengthens the case that the effect is a genuine, energetically-grounded compositional phenomenon rather than a spectroscopic artifact, since an independent theoretical calculation points the same direction as two independent experimental probes.

Critically, CoFeCrOOH's intermediate/mixed behavior (not lowest on either axis) still does not cleanly isolate whether Fe alone, or Fe-Cr jointly, drives the effect — the same ambiguity flagged earlier persists.
