Combined, the two results support a specific causal-adjacent inference about the composite's mechanical behavior: the monotonic UCS increase (~310→363 MPa across 0-25 wt% SiCp) is consistent with a classic particulate-reinforcement strengthening mechanism (load transfer/Orowan-type effects from reasonably well-dispersed particles) rather than being an artifact of gross segregation.
