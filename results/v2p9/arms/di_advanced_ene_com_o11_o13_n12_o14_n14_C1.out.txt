This measurement (the paired XAS N 1s + Raman ~1200 cm-1 result) settles a chemistry/electronic-structure claim, not a structural, microstructural, or morphological (STR/shape) one. It supports the conclusion that zinc deposition co-occurs with a change in bonding/electronic environment specifically at or near nitrogen sites in the carbon host — evidenced convergently by a shift in nitrogen oxidation/coordination states (XAS) and a weakening of a vibrational feature (Raman) between pristine and zinc-loaded samples. That is the full extent of what it justifies: a cross-technique, nitrogen-localized chemical/bonding change tracking zinc loading in a two-point (before/after) comparison.

It does not settle anything about STR, microstructure, or particle/crystallite shape. Nothing in the reported data — a core-level electronic transition and a Raman peak intensity — speaks to morphology, particle size/shape, lattice structure at the microstructural scale, porosity, or spatial distribution of zinc.
