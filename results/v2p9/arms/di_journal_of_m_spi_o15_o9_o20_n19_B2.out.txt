Combined with the settled EPT result (EPT-treated: grain refinement to 0.3-0.8 um, UTS 420→447 MPa, elongation 0.095→0.15, but yield knee 350→330 MPa), the two together support: the strength-ductility combination achieved by this study's processing route(s) — of which EPT is at least one contributing step — is not just marginally but categorically better than the full span of alternative Mg-AZ61 processing technologies surveyed in the literature, on both the YS and UTS axes simultaneously. This upgrades the earlier paired co-occurrence (EPT correlates with microstructural refinement and altered tensile response within one study) to a claim of comparative superiority against an external benchmark set of established techniques.

What remains unsettled: F14 does not label which of the three red points corresponds to the EPT-treated condition versus other conditions in "this study" (e.g., an as-cast, forged, or partially treated state), so the figure cannot confirm that the specific point exceeding literature UTS (~447) is the EPT-refined one from F10d/F13.
