Panel F5d confirms the stated readout: all five BNs-BSA+Lasers (dual 660/808 nm) tumors stay flat at ~0 mm3 through day ~20, while four of five BNs-BSA+808 nm-alone tumors regrow starting around day 14, reaching several hundred mm3 by day 20-22.

What this settles in combination with the established band-gap/DPBF result: The earlier work shows bismuthene's ~0.69 eV band gap is compatible with, and its DPBF decay is consistent with, singlet-oxygen generation specifically under 660 nm illumination (photodynamic capability), independent of any photothermal effect from 808 nm. The in vivo curves now show that 808 nm alone (photothermal channel) gives only a partial, non-durable response — most tumors ablate initially but the majority regrow by day ~14-20 — whereas adding the dual-wavelength regimen that includes the 660 nm component (which the earlier data ties to singlet-oxygen production) converts this into a complete, durable, 5/5 response. Together, this supports a two-pathway mechanistic picture: an 808 nm-driven photothermal effect that alone is insufficient for durable tumor control, plus a 660 nm-driven pathway (independently linked to bismuthene's narrow band gap and singlet-oxygen generation) that supplies the additional efficacy needed for complete, sustained regression — i.e., combination therapy (PTT+PDT) outperforms either single modality, consistent with bismuthene serving a dual optical/therapeutic role matching its band structure.

What it leaves open: this panel alone cannot attribute the improved durability specifically to singlet oxygen in vivo — no in vivo ROS/singlet-oxygen marker, dose-response, or action-spectrum data link the 0.69 eV gap value to the tumor-regrowth outcome; the comparison is confounded by any thermal or immune synergy between the two wavelengths rather than isolating a purely PDT contribution; and the BNs-BSA+660 nm-alone tumor curve (present in the same figure but not analyzed here) would be needed to isolate the 660 nm/PDT contribution's own durability independent of 808 nm, which this reasoning has not directly examined.
