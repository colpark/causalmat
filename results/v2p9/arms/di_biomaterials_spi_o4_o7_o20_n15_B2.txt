The qualifications recorded on that earlier result:
  - The XRD peak height is a measure of long-range structural order/periodicity (arbitrary intensity units), while the N2 uptake is a measure of total adsorbed amount reflecting pore volume/surface area (mmol/g) -- these are different kinds of quantities, so the pair shows they move together with Co but not that the magnitude of order loss corresponds to the magnitude of porosity loss.

  - The combination does not establish which change is upstream of the other -- whether loss of ordered channel structure drives the loss of total pore volume, or whether both are independent consequences of Co incorporation.
