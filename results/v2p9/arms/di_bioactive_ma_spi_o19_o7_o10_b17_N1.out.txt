What it leaves open: whether the effect is additive or synergistic (i.e., contribution of Ce alone vs Er alone vs combined), the underlying mechanism (band-gap narrowing vs Er upconversion vs surface trap states), dose/wavelength dependence, durability/reusability of the catalyst, cytotoxicity in real tissue settings, and generalization beyond the tested bacterial strains and lab conditions.
