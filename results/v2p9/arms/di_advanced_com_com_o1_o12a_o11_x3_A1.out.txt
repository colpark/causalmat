Figure 5a shows four DSC traces (130–190 °C) with a single downward endothermic step per curve: pure PAEN at 176 °C, and BST@Ag composites at 146, 149, and 157 °C for 1, 3, and 5 wt% Ag loading respectively. The step is broad and shallow in the image, consistent with a glass-transition-like feature rather than a sharp melting peak, but the crop alone does not label the y-axis quantitatively (only ENDO/EXO direction) and there is no ΔCp value printed.

What this measurement supports: (1) all BST@Ag composites show their transition well below that of pure PAEN (146–157 °C vs 176 °C), so filler incorporation is associated with a large downward shift relative to the neat polymer — consistent with a plasticizing or chain-mobility effect from the filler/interface. (2) Within the composite series, the transition temperature rises monotonically with increasing Ag content, 146→149→157 °C for 1%→3%→5%, so more Ag is associated with a partial recovery toward the neat-polymer value, not a monotonic decrease with loading.

What it leaves open: the panel gives one trace per composition, so there is no indication of run-to-run reproducibility, error bars, or whether the "step" is truly Tg versus some other transition; the caption fragment ("DSC and") is truncated, so the intended full claim/units cannot be confirmed from the image; and the mechanism behind the non-monotonic-with-loading recovery (interfacial interaction, crystallinity change, filler dispersion) is not addressed by this panel — that would require additional data (e.g., XRD, rheology) not shown here.
