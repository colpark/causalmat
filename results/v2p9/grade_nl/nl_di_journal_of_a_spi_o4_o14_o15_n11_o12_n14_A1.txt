Panel F6b (fracture toughness vs. sintering temperature for three PcBN composite series, S1/S2/S3) supports a modest, temperature- and composition-dependent effect on K_IC, not a clean monotonic PRP relationship.

So the data support: (a) sintering temperature can raise K_IC substantially in some compositions (S1), (b) in other compositions the effect is negligible (S3), and (c) a third composition shows a smaller, more gradual rise over a hotter range (S2).

What it leaves open: because S1, S2, and S3 differ in composition (not just processing), the panel cannot isolate whether the toughness gain is driven by sintering temperature per se, by composition, or by an interaction between the two — no single series spans the full temperature range, so direct comparison across the three "at a fixed temperature" is not really possible.
