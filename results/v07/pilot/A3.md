**Every verdict is model against model.** The second read is net-reread (Sonnet, one image, no label, no question), graded by net-grader against the graph's evidence node.

## What changed
- **A1, skip rule** (`trace_kit/cut_traces.py`; R5 masking is now off and runs only behind `--r5-mask`). The rule checks every given panel of a trace:
  - A panel with no crop closes the trace as `no crop`. There is no whole-figure fallback any more.
  - A panel with no OCR record counts as annotated and closes the trace as `answer written on the image`.
  - A panel whose annotation text shares a content word (5-letter stem) or a number with a graded target also closes the trace as `answer written on the image`.
  - Unit exponents (Å-1, x10²), round scale bars, temperature labels and numbers already in the context do not count.
  - The v1 ceramic regression still shows 0 differences.
- **A2, second read** (`trace_kit/reread.py`, `trace_kit/v07.py rrgrade`):
  - Every given panel of every open trace was read: 23 panels.
  - Each evidence node is graded once, against the reads of all the given panels it cites (13 units). Grading it panel by panel flagged 11 of 35 checks WRONG only because one panel cannot show a trend drawn across several. That first pass is kept in `reread/*/perpanel/`.
  - A WRONG grade sends the trace to inspect with cause `graph`.
- The open traces are unchanged from v06c: same walk, same given panels, and none was masked in v06c. Their writer packets and gate prompts are therefore byte-identical, and their v06c gate verdicts carry over.
