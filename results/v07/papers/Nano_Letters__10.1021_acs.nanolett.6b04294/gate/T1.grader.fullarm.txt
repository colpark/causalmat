Candidate answer:
Panel F1a shows a Rietveld refinement (HEXRD) of the as-prepared porous cobalt oxide powder over a 1-8° 2-theta range. The refinement models the pattern as a two-phase mixture: Co3O4 (78.6 wt%, black tick marks) and CoO (21.4 wt%, red tick marks), indicating the "porous cobalt oxide" is not phase-pure but a mixed Co3O4/CoO material, with Co3O4 the dominant phase. The calculated curve (Cacl, red line) tracks the observed data (Obs, black x symbols) very closely across all major and minor reflections, including correctly reproducing relative peak intensities of the multiple closely spaced peaks. The difference/residual curve (Diff, blue trace) plotted below the pattern is flat and near zero across the whole 2-theta range, with only small oscillations at the strongest peak positions, indicating a good, low-residual fit between the model and the measured diffraction data.
