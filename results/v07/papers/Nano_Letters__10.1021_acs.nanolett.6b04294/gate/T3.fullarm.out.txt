Panel F6d (image: /home/aid1/Documents/causalmat/matmech/Nano_Letters/10.1021_acs.nanolett.6b04294/panels/crops/6139e4c8123fd9f6c0fd401273b557c309b586226a6facebff2d2d727296e604_D.jpg) shows three curves over 2 ps of ab initio MD: Li-Co3O4 (black), Na-Co3S4 (red), and Na-Co3O4 (blue).

By the end of the run (2 ps), Li-Co3O4 reaches a degree of oxidation of roughly 90%, while Na-Co3O4 only reaches about 35-37% — a gap of roughly 50-55 percentage points between lithiated and sodiated cobalt oxide. (Na-Co3S4 ends at an intermediate ~58-60%.)

The sodiated cobalt-oxide curve (Na-Co3O4, blue) tracks closely with the other curves during the first ~0.4-0.8 ps, rising to about 30%, but then largely plateaus/saturates for the remainder of the simulation, only creeping up slightly (to ~35-37%) by 2 ps with some minor fluctuation — in contrast to Li-Co3O4, which keeps climbing steadily and near-linearly throughout the full 2 ps window.
