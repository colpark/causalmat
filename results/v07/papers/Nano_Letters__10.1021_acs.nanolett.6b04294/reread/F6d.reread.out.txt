This is a line/scatter plot (labeled panel "d"), not a microscopy image — it plots simulation output rather than showing a physical technique with a scale bar. The x-axis is "MD simulation time (ps)" ranging from 0 to 2 ps, and the y-axis is "Degree of oxidation" ranging from 0% to 100%. Three data series are plotted with distinct markers/colors: black circles for Li-Co3O4, red triangles for Na-Co3S4, and blue squares for Na-Co3O4, connected by lines. All three series start at 0% at t=0 and rise monotonically. Li-Co3O4 (black) increases fastest and most linearly, reaching roughly 90% oxidation by 2 ps. Na-Co3S4 (red) and Na-Co3O4 (blue) track closely together up to about 0.8 ps (~30%), after which Na-Co3S4 continues rising to about 58-60% while Na-Co3O4 plateaus/saturates around 35-37% by 2 ps. No scale bar is present since this is a data plot, not a micrograph.
