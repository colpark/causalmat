Panel (a) shows three stacked open-circuit potential (OCP) vs. time electrochemical measurement plots, one for each of three dissimilar-metal couples: Al/Ti (black, top), Al/Mg (red, middle), and Al/Cu (blue, bottom). The x-axis (shared, ~600–1450 s) is Time (s); each y-axis is OCP (V vs. Ag/AgCl) but with a different scale for each metal pair. All three traces show a relatively stable/oscillating OCP baseline that sharply drops between marked "Start" and "End" vertical dashed lines (around 900–1000 s), followed by partial recovery afterward. Each panel is annotated with an arrow and the magnitude of the potential drop: 0.086 V for Al/Ti, 0.008 V for Al/Mg, and 0.0016 V for Al/Cu — indicating the Al/Ti couple experiences the largest transient OCP drop and Al/Cu the smallest. No scale bar is present (this is a graph/plot, not a micrograph); the technique is OCP monitoring, likely during a contact/immersion or scratch-type galvanic corrosion test given the "Start"/"End" markers.
