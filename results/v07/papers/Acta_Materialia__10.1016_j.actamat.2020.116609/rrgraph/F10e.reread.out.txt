This is a two-panel schematic (labeled (e) and (f)), not a microscopy image — no scale bar is present since it depicts an atomistic/computational model rather than an experimental micrograph. Both panels show the same interface orientation relationship, "Al (111) || Ti (0002)", modeled as a ball-and-stick/sphere packing diagram: large blue spheres represent a Ti substrate layer, orange spheres represent an Al surface layer arranged atop it, with an arrow labeling the "Surface layer."

Panel (e) shows a small teal sphere labeled "O" (oxygen) adsorbed on the Al surface layer, with a calculated adsorption energy given as E_ad^Oxy = -4.16 eV. Panel (f) shows the analogous structure with a magenta sphere labeled "Cl" (chlorine) adsorbed instead, with E_ad^Cl = -1.72 eV. The trend conveyed is that oxygen adsorption on the Al(111)/Ti(0002) interface is substantially more energetically favorable (more negative binding energy) than chlorine adsorption, consistent with a DFT-style comparison of adsorbate binding strength at this metal interface.
