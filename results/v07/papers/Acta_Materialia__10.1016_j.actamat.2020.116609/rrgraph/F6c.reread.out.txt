Panel (c) is a line plot, not a micrograph. X-axis is "Distance (μm)" ranging roughly 100–290; Y-axis is "Depth (μm)" ranging from about +0.2 down to −1.0. Three colored traces represent depth profiles for three diffusion-couple/interface conditions: Al/Ti (black), Al/Mg (red), and Al/Cu (blue), per the legend in the lower right. The Al/Ti and Al/Cu traces stay near zero depth across the full distance range (flat, with Al/Ti showing a small sharp spike/dip near ~200–220 μm reaching about −0.15 to 0.1 μm), while the Al/Mg trace shows a pronounced, jagged depression reaching roughly −0.6 to −0.9 μm between about 170 and 250 μm distance, then returning to baseline outside that region. No scale bar is present since this is a plotted graph rather than an image; the trend indicates a much deeper material loss/penetration for the Al/Mg interface compared to the essentially flat Al/Ti and Al/Cu interfaces.
