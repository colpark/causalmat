Question: The claim under test says Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS gives the most efficient photocarrier separation, ranking Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS above Pd-ZIS, Ag-ZIS, and ZIS. Does the TR-PL decay spectra panel (F10d) for these four samples support this ranking?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): No. The TR-PL decays of the four samples nearly coincide; only a marginally longer tail is seen for Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS, so the panel does not resolve the claimed four-way ranking (o17).

Candidate answer:
Panel F10d (Fig. 9d) shows four overlapping TR-PL decay curves — Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (blue), Pd-ZIS (red), Ag-ZIS (green), and ZIS (black) — plotted as relative intensity vs. time (0–60 ns). All four curves rise to a peak near t≈9 ns and decay along nearly indistinguishable trajectories in the tail region; the Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS curve has a modestly higher peak intensity than the others, but peak height reflects signal intensity, not necessarily faster decay/lifetime. No numerical lifetime (τ) values are printed on the panel, and the decay curves are too closely overlapping visually to discern a clear, ordered separation among the four samples (Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS > Pd-ZIS > Ag-ZIS > ZIS) by eye. Therefore, this panel qualitatively shows that all cocatalyst-loaded samples decay similarly and does not visually establish the specific claimed ranking — quantitative fitted lifetime values (not shown in this crop) would be needed to confirm that ordering. The panel is consistent with the general idea that decay behavior differs slightly among samples but does not on its own visually support the precise four-way ranking asserted in the claim.
