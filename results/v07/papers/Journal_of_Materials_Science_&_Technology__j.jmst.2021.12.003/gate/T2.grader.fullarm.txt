Candidate answer:
Panel F9a (Fig. 8a) plots the hydrogen adsorption free-energy diagram (ΔG_H*) for five surfaces. Reading the labeled values directly from the diagram: Ag = 0.49 eV, Pd0.25Ag0.75 = 0.28 eV, Pd0.5Ag0.5 = 0.12 eV, Pd0.75Ag0.25 = -0.08 eV, and pure Pd = -0.31 eV. The surface whose free energy sits closest to zero (the thermoneutral/optimal value for hydrogen evolution) is Pd0.75Ag0.25(111), at -0.08 eV.
