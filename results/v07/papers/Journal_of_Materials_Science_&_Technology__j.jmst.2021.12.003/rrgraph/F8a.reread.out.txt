Panel (a): a line-and-marker plot of photocatalytic H2 evolution versus irradiation time, not a microscopy image (no scale bar). X-axis is "Irradiation time (h)" from 0 to 5 h; y-axis is "H2 evolution (μmol)" from 0 to 700 μmol. Six catalyst compositions are compared with distinct colored markers/lines: Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (dark blue), Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS (cyan), Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS (magenta), Pd-ZIS (red), Ag-ZIS (green), and bare ZIS (black), each sampled at 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 h. All curves rise roughly monotonically/near-linearly from the origin, but with very different slopes: Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS shows by far the steepest increase, reaching ~625 μmol at 5 h; Ag0.5Pd0.5-ZIS and Ag0.75Pd0.25-ZIS are intermediate, ending near ~290 μmol and ~220 μmol respectively; Pd-ZIS is lower still (~170 μmol at 5 h); Ag-ZIS and unmodified ZIS remain nearly flat near the baseline (roughly 10–15 μmol at 5 h), indicating negligible H2 evolution activity. The overall trend shows Ag/Pd bimetallic co-doping (especially the Ag0.25Pd0.75 ratio) substantially enhances photocatalytic hydrogen evolution compared to single-metal-doped or undoped ZIS.
