Panel (d) shows time-resolved photoluminescence (PL) decay spectra, plotting relative PL intensity (a.u., y-axis) versus time in nanoseconds (x-axis, 0–60 ns). Four overlaid decay curves are compared for different photocatalyst compositions: Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (blue), Ag-ZIS (green), Pd-ZIS (red), and pristine ZIS (black).
