The panel (labeled "(a)") is a photoelectrochemical transient photocurrent response plot — current density (μA/cm²) versus time (s), no scale bar (this is a graph, not a micrograph). It shows chopped-light (on/off) chronoamperometry cycles from ~0 to 280 s, with repeated square-wave spikes and decay as the light is switched on and off. Four traces are compared: ZIS (black, lowest response, ~0.5-0.6 μA/cm² peaks), Ag-ZIS (green, ~1.5-2.1 μA/cm² peaks), Pd-ZIS (red, ~1.8-2.5 μA/cm² peaks), and Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (blue, highest response, initial spike to ~3.0-3.5 μA/cm² then stabilizing around 2.0-2.4 μA/cm² per cycle). The trend across all curves is a sharp rise upon illumination followed by gradual decay/stabilization during each light-on period and a rapid drop to near-zero when light is off, with the bimetallic Ag-Pd co-doped ZIS sample consistently showing the highest and most stable photocurrent density, indicating superior charge separation/photoresponse compared to the single-metal-doped and undoped ZIS samples.
