Panel (b) is a Tauc plot for indirect bandgap determination: (αhν)^1/2 (in (eV)^1/2) on the y-axis versus photon energy hν (eV, 1.5–6.0) on the x-axis. Four curves are overlaid, each corresponding to a different photocatalyst sample: ZIS (black, Eg = 2.53 eV), Ag-ZIS (green, 2.40 eV), Pd-ZIS (red, 2.46 eV), and Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS (blue, 2.35 eV), with the bandgap values given in the legend. Dotted lines near the onset of each curve (around 2.3–2.9 eV) show linear extrapolation to the x-axis, the standard method for extracting the bandgap value from a Tauc plot. All curves rise steeply from near-zero baseline starting around 2.3–3.0 eV, reach a broad peak/plateau roughly between 4.5–5.5 eV, then decline slightly toward 6.0 eV; the Ag-ZIS (green) and ZIS (black) curves show visibly lower absorption intensity in the 3.5–5.5 eV plateau region compared to the Pd-containing (red) and bimetallic (blue) samples. No scale bar is present, as this is a spectroscopic/optical plot rather than a micrograph. The overall trend indicates that Pd doping and Ag/Pd co-doping reduce the bandgap relative to pristine ZIS, with the bimetallic Ag0.25Pd0.75-ZIS sample showing the smallest bandgap (2.35 eV) among the four.
