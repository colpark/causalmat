Panel (a) is not a microscopy image but a computational free-energy diagram for the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER), plotted as Free Energy (eV, y-axis, range -0.6 to 0.8) along a reaction coordinate from H⁺+e⁻ (left) through the adsorbed H* intermediate to ½H₂ (right). Five colored curves compare different Pd-Ag compositions: Ag (cyan, ΔG_H* = +0.49 eV), Pd0.25Ag0.75 (purple, +0.28 eV), Pd0.5Ag0.5 (yellow, +0.12 eV), Pd0.75Ag0.25 (red, -0.08 eV), and pure Pd (black, -0.31 eV). The trend shows increasing Pd content shifts the H* adsorption free energy from strongly positive (Ag, poor HER kinetics) toward near-zero/slightly negative (Pd0.75Ag0.25, closest to the thermoneutral optimum ~0 eV expected for good HER catalysts), with pure Pd becoming too strongly binding (too negative).
