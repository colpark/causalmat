Panel d shows magnetic hysteresis (M–H) loops measured by vibrating-sample magnetometry (VSM or similar), plotting mass magnetization (emu/g) versus applied magnetic field (kOe, swept roughly -10 to +20 kOe) for four composite samples with different ratios of magnetic microparticles (MMPs) to liquid crystal elastomer (LCE): 2:1 (black circles), 1:1 (red diamonds), 1:2 (orange triangles), and 1:4 (blue squares). All four curves show classic S-shaped saturating hysteresis loops with visible coercivity/remanence (loops open near zero field) and approach saturation magnetization at high field. Saturation magnetization scales monotonically with MMP content: 2:1 saturates highest (~68-70 emu/g), followed by 1:1 (~52-53 emu/g), 1:2 (~30 emu/g), and 1:4 lowest (~15 emu/g), consistent with higher magnetic particle loading giving greater magnetic response.
