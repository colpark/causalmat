This is a line/scatter plot (panel "a"), not a microscopy image — there is no scale bar. It plots the nematic-to-isotropic transition temperature (T_NI, °C, y-axis, range ~61.5–65.5) versus volume ratio of MMPs (magnetic microparticles, presumably) in a composite (x-axis, 0–25%). Two data series are shown: filled black circles connected by a black line for "Increasing temperature" runs, and open/gray squares connected by a gray line for "Decreasing temperature" runs, each point with visible error bars. The increasing-temperature series sits consistently higher (~64.5–64.7 °C) and is nearly flat across the composition range, while the decreasing-temperature series is lower (~62.0–62.7 °C), rising slightly from 0% to ~4% MMP loading and then remaining roughly flat/slightly increasing out to 24%. The gap between the two curves (thermal hysteresis of the transition) stays roughly constant (~2–2.5 °C) across all volume ratios, indicating the MMP loading has minimal effect on the transition temperature or hysteresis width in this composite system.
