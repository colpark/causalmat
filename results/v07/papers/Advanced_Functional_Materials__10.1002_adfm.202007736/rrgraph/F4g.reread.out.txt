Panel g shows EDS (energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy) point spectra plotted as intensity (arbitrary units) versus channel energy (eV, x-axis spans ~340–580 eV). Two spectra are shown in a pseudo-3D "staircase" layout comparing two sample conditions, labeled "Untreated" (front, gray shaded plane) and "Citrate Treated" (back, tan/yellow shaded plane). Each spectrum displays two labeled peaks/edges: Ca-L (around 345–355 eV) as a sharp peak, and O-K (around 525–530 eV rising edge followed by a broad plateau). No scale bar is present since this is a spectral plot, not a micrograph. The qualitative trend visible is that both Ca-L and O-K signals appear present in both the untreated and citrate-treated samples, with the citrate-treated spectrum (back plane) showing peaks at similar relative shapes/positions to the untreated one — the panel is used to compare elemental composition (Ca and O signatures) between the two treatment conditions rather than to show a numeric intensity trend.
