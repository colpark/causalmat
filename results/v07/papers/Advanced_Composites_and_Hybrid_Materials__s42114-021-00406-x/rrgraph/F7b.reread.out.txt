The panel (labeled "(b)") is a line graph, not a microscopy image. It plots EMI Shielding Effectiveness (EMI SE, in dB) on the y-axis (range ~20–45 dB) versus Frequency (GHz) on the x-axis (range 8–12.5 GHz) — this is the X-band frequency range typically used for EMI shielding characterization. Four curves are shown for different sample thicknesses of a PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs composite: 2 mm (black), 4 mm (red), 6 mm (green), and 8 mm (olive/dark yellow). There is no scale bar since this is a data plot, not a micrograph. Trend: EMI SE generally increases with sample thickness, with the 6 mm sample showing the highest shielding effectiveness (peaking near 42 dB around 8.7 GHz) and the 2 mm sample the lowest (around 30–33 dB); all curves show mild oscillatory/wavy behavior across the frequency range rather than a flat or strictly monotonic trend, with local peaks around 8.5–9 GHz and 10.5–11 GHz.
