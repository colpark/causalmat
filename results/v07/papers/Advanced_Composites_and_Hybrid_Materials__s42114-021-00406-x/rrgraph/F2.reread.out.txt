The panel is a 4x3 grid of SEM (scanning electron microscopy, secondary electron mode, SE(UL) detector) micrographs of fracture/cross-section surfaces from PLA/PCL nanocomposite blends containing 8 wt% carbon nanotubes (CNTs), comparing samples without and with 4% polymeric ionic liquid (PIL) compatibilizer across four different processing/mixing times (2, 4, 6, 8 min, labeled "-2m" through "-8m"). Column 1 shows PLA/PCL/8CNTs at 10,000x magnification (5.00 µm scale bar); column 2 shows PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs at the same 10,000x magnification (5.00 µm scale bar) with a yellow boxed region; column 3 shows a 20,000x zoomed-in inset of that boxed region (2.00 µm scale bar), connected by an arrow. All images show rough, porous, fibrous fracture surfaces typical of phase-separated polymer blends, with visible voids/pores, torn ligaments, and fine fibrillar/nanoscale texture (likely CNT-related) more pronounced in the zoomed insets. Across increasing mixing time (2→8 min), the surface morphology appears to become somewhat more uniform/finer with smaller, more numerous pores in the PIL-containing samples compared to the coarser, larger-void morphology in the non-PIL samples, suggesting improved phase dispersion/interfacial compatibilization with PIL addition and longer mixing time. Each SEM frame includes standard instrument metadata (accelerating voltage 5.0 kV, working distance, magnification, detector mode) at the bottom.
