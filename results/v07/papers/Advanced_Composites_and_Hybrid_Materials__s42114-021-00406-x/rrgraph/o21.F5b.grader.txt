Axes: Strain (%) on the x-axis (0-140%) and Stress (MPa) on the y-axis (0-40 MPa); there is no scale bar since this is a graph, not a micrograph. Four line traces are shown, each a different composite formulation with varying compatibilizer/CNT-processing time: PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs-2m (black), -4m (red), -6m (blue), and -8m (green).

All four curves show a similar shape: a sharp initial stress rise and yield peak near ~25 MPa at very low strain (~2-5%), a slight post-yield drop/plateau around 22-24 MPa extending through moderate strain (up to ~60-80%), followed by a gradual strain-hardening rise in stress up to failure. Failure (curve termination, marked by an abrupt vertical drop) occurs at different strains: the black (-2m) curve fails earliest near ~60% strain at about 24 MPa; red (-4m) and green (-8m) fail near ~115-120% strain around 27-31 MPa; the blue (-6m) curve extends furthest, to roughly 135% strain, reaching the highest final stress of about 33-34 MPa before termination. This indicates that the -6m sample has the best combination of ductility (elongation at break) and ultimate strength among the four formulations, while -2m is the most brittle/least elongating.
