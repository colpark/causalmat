Eight sample series are plotted with distinct markers/colors: PLA/PCL/8CNTs at 2, 4, 6, 8 min mixing times (gray squares, red circles, blue up-triangles, teal down-triangles) and PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs at 2, 4, 6, 8 min mixing times (purple diamonds, olive left-triangles, cyan right-triangles, brown hexagons). All curves show the same overall trend: G' is relatively flat/low at low frequency (a rubbery/terminal plateau region) then rises steeply and roughly monotonically with increasing ω at higher frequencies, characteristic of viscoelastic polymer nanocomposite melts. The brown hexagon series (PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs-8m) sits highest across nearly the whole frequency range, while the purple diamond series (PLA/PCL/4PIL/8CNTs-2m) sits lowest, indicating G' increases with longer mixing time and that the 4PIL-containing formulations show a wider spread between mixing times than the plain 8CNTs series. No error bars are shown; data points are dense and evenly sampled on the log axis.
