Panel (b) is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image. It shows electronic conductivity (S/m, log scale from 10^-14 to 10^2) on the y-axis versus CNT content (wt%, linear scale 0–10) on the x-axis, comparing two composite formulations: PLA/PCL/CNTs-6m (black squares) and PLA/PCL/PIL/CNTs-6m (red circles). Both curves show a classic percolation-type trend: conductivity starts near the instrument floor (~10^-14 S/m) at 0 wt% CNT, rises steeply by many orders of magnitude between 1–4 wt% CNT (the percolation threshold region), and then plateaus toward ~10–30 S/m at 8–10 wt% CNT. The red (PIL-containing) curve sits consistently above the black curve at every intermediate CNT loading, indicating the PIL additive lowers the percolation threshold / boosts conductivity at a given CNT content, though both converge to similar high-loading conductivity by 10 wt%. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph); it's a standard Cartesian/log-linear data plot with axis labels, a legend box, and panel label "(b)" in the upper right.
