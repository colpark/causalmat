This is a grouped bar chart (not a microscopy image), plotting EMI shielding effectiveness (EMI SE, dB) on the y-axis (0-50 dB) against mixing times (2, 4, 6, 8 min) on the x-axis. Four series are shown per mixing time via a legend: PLA/PCL/8CNTs-SE_R (gray), PLA/PCL/4IL/8CNTs-SE_R (green), PLA/PCL/8CNTs-SE_A (blue), and PLA/PCL/4IL/8CNTs-SE_A (orange), where "R" and "A" likely denote reflection and absorption contributions to shielding (stacked as small colored base segments topped by taller blue/orange bars). There is no scale bar since this is a data plot, not a micrograph. Trends: the IL-containing composite (orange, SE_A) increases with mixing time, peaking near 41 dB at 6 min before dropping to ~34 dB at 8 min, while the IL-free composite (blue, SE_A) decreases fairly steadily from ~37 dB at 2 min to ~27 dB at 8 min; the small gray/green base segments (SE_R) stay roughly constant (~2-3 dB) across all mixing times, indicating absorption dominates the total shielding effectiveness in both formulations.
