Panel (a) is a schematic + waveform figure illustrating the working mechanism of a piezoelectric/triboelectric fiber-based multilayer generator, not a microscopy image (no scale bar present, since it is diagrammatic rather than an electron/optical micrograph).

Left side: four labeled schematic cross-sections (i–iv) arranged in a cyclic loop showing a layered device stack (yellow PET layers, green Ni electrodes, gray fiber film containing oriented dipole ellipses with +/- charge poles). Stage i shows the resting/aligned state with a dashed line at top and + charges at bottom electrode; stage ii ("Pressing," force F applied) shows a small downward displacement Δx with dipoles beginning to tilt and induced charge redistribution; stage iii ("Pressing," continued compression to Δx_max) shows maximal dipole reorientation/tilt under full compression; stage iv ("Releasing," force removed) shows the film relaxing back with reversed charge polarity (now +/- flipped at top vs bottom) as it returns toward state i, completing the cycle. Arrows connect the four stages in a circular sequence, and a central 3D rendering shows the actual multilayer stack (alternating yellow PET, green Ni electrode, and gray fiber film sheets).

Right side: an output voltage-vs-time trace (red curve) correlated with the four stages (labeled i, ii, iii, iv, i along the time axis) — voltage is near zero during resting stage i, shows a sharp positive spike during pressing (stage ii) that decays during continued compression (stage iii), then a sharp negative spike during releasing (stage iv), before returning to baseline, demonstrating the alternating-polarity output signal generated by the press-release cycle.
