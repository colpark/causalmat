Panel (f), labeled "Fist beating": a voltage-vs-time line chart (blue trace) with axes Voltage (V) from -20 to 40 on the y-axis and Time (s) from 0 to 16 on the x-axis. It is not a microscopy image but an output signal plot from a triboelectric/piezoelectric nanogenerator device test, showing repetitive sharp alternating voltage spikes (roughly 25-35 V positive peaks and -10 to -15 V negative peaks) produced by cyclic mechanical impact ("fist beating"), with fairly consistent peak amplitude and frequency (~2-2.5 Hz) across the ~15 s recording, and a brief flat/zero segment near the end (~15-16 s) indicating cessation of beating. An inset photograph in the upper right shows a gloved fist pressing down on a device connected via red and yellow alligator-clip wires, mounted on a grid-lined benchtop background; no scale bar is present since this is an electrical waveform, not a micrograph.
