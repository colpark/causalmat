Panel (g) is a grouped bar chart with three sample types on the x-axis (Cast film, F ∥ fibers, F ⊥ fibers), plotted against three separate y-axes for three different mechanical properties: E_mod (MPa, gold bars, left axis, 0–1800), F_max (MPa, blue bars, middle axis, 0–60), and Breakage elongation (%, gray bars, right axis, 0–400). Error bars (standard deviation, thin black whiskers with caps) are shown on all nine bars. No scale bar is present (this is a data plot, not a micrograph). Trend: E_mod and F_max are both highest for the cast film (~1600 MPa and ~1200 MPa/50 respectively) and decrease progressively for fibers parallel (F ∥) and perpendicular (F ⊥) to the fiber direction; conversely, breakage elongation shows the opposite trend, lowest for the cast film (~2–3%) and increasing substantially through F ∥ fibers (~20%) to F ⊥ fibers (highest, ~40%). This indicates an inverse relationship between stiffness/strength and ductility across the three sample geometries.
