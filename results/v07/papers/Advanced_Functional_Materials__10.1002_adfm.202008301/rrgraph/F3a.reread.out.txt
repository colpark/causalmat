The two curves are essentially superimposed throughout the entire energy range, showing the characteristic Co K-edge absorption rise near ~7725–7730 eV to a peak around 1.55–1.6, followed by post-edge oscillations (a dip near 7735 eV and a broad hump around 7742–7748 eV) before gradually declining toward ~0.8 at 7760 eV. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph; this is a spectral plot). The near-total overlap of the NCM-0 and NCM-2 curves indicates no detectable shift in Co oxidation state or local coordination environment between the two samples at the Co K-edge.
