Panel (a) shows an atomic-resolution HAADF-STEM (or HRTEM) lattice-fringe image of a single crystalline particle/domain, with a 5 nm scale bar in the lower right. Four dashed-outline boxes mark distinct sub-regions of the lattice, each connected by orange arrows to an inset showing its fast Fourier transform (FFT) diffraction pattern, used to index local crystal symmetry. The FFT insets are labeled with space groups: R-3m (rhombohedral, top-left region), R-3m/Fm-3m (mixed/transitional region, lower-middle), and Fm-3m (cubic, upper-right region, also implicitly attributed to the lower-right box). A faint white line/boundary runs diagonally through the image, apparently marking a phase or domain boundary separating the differently indexed regions. The overall trend illustrated is a spatial phase gradient/coexistence within one particle, transitioning from rhombohedral (R-3m) symmetry to cubic (Fm-3m) symmetry across the imaged area, with an intermediate mixed-phase region in between.
