Panel (b), labeled "Mn K-edge," shows X-ray absorption near-edge structure (XANES) spectra. The x-axis is Energy (eV) from about 6530 to 6590 eV, and the y-axis is Normalized Absorption from -0.2 to 1.8. Two curves are plotted — a black trace for "NCM-0" and an olive/green trace for "NCM-2" — and they overlap almost perfectly across the entire energy range, indicating essentially identical Mn oxidation state/local coordination environment between the two samples. The spectrum shows a small pre-edge feature near 6540 eV, a steep main absorption edge rising sharply around 6555–6560 eV to a strong white-line peak near 6560 eV (~1.75 normalized absorption), followed by a dip and a smaller secondary peak near 6578 eV before gradually declining toward 6590 eV. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph); this is a standard XANES spectral plot with a legend in the lower right and panel label "(b)" in the upper left.
