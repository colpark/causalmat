Panel (e) is an EXAFS Fourier-transform plot: FT[k²χ(k)] (arbitrary units, y-axis 0–2.5) versus radial distance R (Å, x-axis 0–6), i.e., a pseudo-radial distribution function derived from X-ray absorption fine-structure data. Two overlaid traces are shown, NCM-0 (black) and NCM-2 (olive/dark-yellow), which coincide almost exactly across the whole range. Both curves show two dominant peaks near R ≈ 1.5 Å (intensity ~2.2) and R ≈ 2.5 Å (intensity ~2.3, the tallest peak), corresponding to the first- and second-shell coordination (e.g., metal–O and metal–metal/transition-metal shells, uncorrected for phase shift), followed by smaller oscillations/peaks between ~4–5.5 Å (intensity ~0.4–0.7) representing higher coordination shells. No scale bar is present (not a micrograph); axes are labeled "FT[k²X(k)] (a.u.)" and "R (Å)". The near-total overlap of the NCM-0 and NCM-2 curves indicates that the local atomic/coordination structure around the absorbing element is essentially unchanged between the two samples, with only very minor differences visible in the smaller peaks beyond ~4 Å.
