This is panel "b," a UV-Vis absorbance spectrum plot (not microscopy), with Absorbance (a.u.) on the y-axis (0 to 1.00) and Wavelength (nm) on the x-axis (500-800 nm). No scale bar (not applicable — it's a spectroscopy plot, not an image). Four traces are shown corresponding to different reaction conditions: TMB+H2O2 (black), TMB+H2O2+Cu(II) (red), TMB+H2O2+MoO3 (green), and TMB+H2O2+Cu(II)+MoO3 (purple). The black, red, and green traces remain low and roughly flat (~0.0-0.1 a.u.) across the wavelength range, while the purple trace (combined Cu(II)+MoO3 condition) shows a pronounced absorbance peak centered around 650 nm reaching roughly 0.6 a.u., indicating a strong synergistic colorimetric/catalytic (peroxidase-mimicking, likely oxidized TMB) signal only when both Cu(II) and MoO3 are present together, consistent with a combined catalytic effect not seen with either component alone.
