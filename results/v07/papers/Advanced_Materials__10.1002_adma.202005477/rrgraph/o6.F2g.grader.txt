Panel g shows electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR/ESR) spectra (spin-trapping assay with DMPO) plotted as Intensity vs. Magnetic Field (G), x-axis spanning 3460–3550 G. Four overlaid traces represent different reaction conditions: H2O2 alone (black), H2O2+MoO3 (green), H2O2+Cu(II) (red), and H2O2+Cu(II)+MoO3 (purple). Peaks are annotated with three symbols denoting radical adducts: filled circle = DMPO-OH, filled triangle = DMPOX, star = DMPO-C radical species.

The black (H2O2 only) and green (H2O2+MoO3) traces are essentially flat baselines with no discernible peaks, indicating no significant radical generation under those conditions. The red trace (H2O2+Cu(II)) shows a moderate multi-peak pattern with a mix of DMPO-OH, DMPOX, and DMPO-C signals of modest amplitude. The purple trace (H2O2+Cu(II)+MoO3) shows a much larger-amplitude, sharp four-line derivative pattern with strong DMPO-OH peaks and additional DMPOX/DMPO-C features, indicating that the combination of Cu(II) and MoO3 with H2O2 produces markedly enhanced radical (Fenton-like) signal intensity compared to Cu(II) alone or MoO3 alone. No scale bar is present (not applicable to a spectral plot); the panel is purely a line-spectrum comparison.
