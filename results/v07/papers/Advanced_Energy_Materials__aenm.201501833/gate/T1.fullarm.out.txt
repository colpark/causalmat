In F4a (Ni-MOF), the rods have smooth, faceted, well-defined surfaces with sharp edges — they look like clean, crystalline bars packed together with visible flat facets and few loose particles attached.

In F4b (MOF-derived NixPyOz), the same rod-like scaffold is still present, but the rod surfaces are now rough and textured, densely coated with small granular/nodular particles, and the sharp facet edges are largely obscured; additional loose agglomerated particulate clusters are visible around/between the rods.

Summary: the rod template shape is retained after conversion, but the surface transitions from smooth and faceted (Ni-MOF) to rough, particle-coated/nodular with extra loose aggregates (MOF-derived NixPyOz).
