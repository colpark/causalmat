Question: The claim under test says NixPyOz keeps the Ni-MOF micro-rod outline, with expanded rods and rough, loosened surfaces. Looking at panel F4b (SEM), does this claim hold up as the full picture of what the panel shows?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): The rod outline claim holds, but it is not the whole picture: panel b also shows abundant detached nanoparticle aggregates between and on the rods, a feature the claim does not mention.

Grading note: Answer key: the audit ruling on the panel (o8), read against the given claim n8.

Candidate answer:
Panel F4b (SEM, X16,000, 1 μm scale) shows elongated rod-like structures that are consistent with the claimed retained micro-rod outline, and the rod surfaces are indeed rough and textured rather than smooth. However, the claim as stated is not the full picture: a large amount of loose, granular/particulate material is aggregated on and around the rods, in some areas nearly burying or obscuring the rod outlines rather than simply "roughening" them. This heavy particulate/aggregate coverage is a distinct, prominent feature of the panel that goes beyond "rough, loosened surfaces" — it looks more like separate nanoparticle/cluster deposition than surface texture of the rod itself. So the panel partially supports the claim (rod outline present, surface not smooth) but omits/understates the significant loose particulate aggregation that dominates much of the field of view.
