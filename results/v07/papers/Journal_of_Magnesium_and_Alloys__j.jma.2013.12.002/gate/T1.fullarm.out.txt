Panel F1c is an XRD pattern (2θ = 20-90°) with a legend identifying two markers: a filled black triangle (▼) for α-Mg and an open/unfilled triangle (▽) for β-Li. The diffraction peaks in the pattern are labeled with these two symbols throughout the scan (e.g., strong peaks near ~32°, ~34°, ~37°, ~58°, ~70° marked α-Mg, and peaks near ~36°, ~50°, ~65°, ~66°, ~78° marked β-Li). So the reflections index to only these two phases: α-Mg and β-Li, consistent with the caption's description of the as-cast LA91 duplex alpha-Mg + beta-Li microstructure — no Al2Ca or other secondary-phase peaks are indexed in this particular pattern (this is the base LA91 alloy, prior to Ca addition).
