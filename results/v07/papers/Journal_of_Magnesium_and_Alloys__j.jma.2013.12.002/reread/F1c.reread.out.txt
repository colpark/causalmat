Panel (c) is an X-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern, plotted as Intensity (Counts, ×10^3) on the y-axis (0 to 25.0) versus 2-Theta (degrees) on the x-axis (20 to 90). No scale bar is present since this is a spectral/diffraction plot, not a micrograph. Peaks are indexed and labeled with two phase markers: filled black triangles (▼) for α-Mg and open triangles (▽) for β-Li. The dominant peak (~23×10^3 counts) occurs near 2θ ≈ 37°, identified as α-Mg, with additional α-Mg peaks near 2θ ≈ 33°, 47°, 57°, 63°, 70°, 73°, and 79° of varying intensity; β-Li peaks appear as smaller shoulders near 2θ ≈ 36°, 50°, 65°, and 77°. The pattern confirms a two-phase Mg-Li alloy microstructure (α-Mg and β-Li phases) with α-Mg as the majority phase based on relative peak intensities.
