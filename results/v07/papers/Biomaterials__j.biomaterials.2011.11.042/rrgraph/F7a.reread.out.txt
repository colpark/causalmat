This is a bar chart (panel "a"), not a micrograph — it shows quantitative assay data rather than an imaging technique. The y-axis is "Absorbance @ 495nm" (0.00–0.50), consistent with an MTT-type cell viability/proliferation assay, plotted against "Culture time (day)" on the x-axis at two time points: day 1 and day 7. Four groups are compared via colored bars with error bars (SD): Blank (red), MBG (green), 2Co-MBG (blue), 5Co-MBG (cyan). At day 1, absorbance values are low and similar across groups (roughly 0.06–0.14). At day 7, all groups increase substantially, with Blank showing the highest absorbance (~0.40) and MBG, 2Co-MBG, and 5Co-MBG progressively lower (~0.17, ~0.15, ~0.11 respectively), indicating a dose-dependent trend with cobalt-doped mesoporous bioactive glass. Statistical significance brackets are annotated at day 7: p<0.05 between Blank and 5Co-MBG, and p>0.05 between MBG and 2Co-MBG. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a bar chart).
