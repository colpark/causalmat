This is a line/scatter plot (panel "a") showing a cumulative in vitro drug release profile, not a microscopy image (no scale bar present). The x-axis is "Release time (h)" ranging from 0 to about 180 h, and the y-axis is "Released amount of drug (µg)" ranging from -100 to 1000 µg. Filled black circles connected by a line show cumulative released drug amount at time points roughly 0, 8, 24, 48, 72, 120, and 168 h, each with vertical error bars (standard deviation). The trend is a classic burst-then-plateau release curve: rapid rise from 0 to about 675 µg within the first 24 h, followed by a slower increase to about 730-780 µg by 48-72 h, and then a plateau near 790-800 µg from 72 h through 168 h, indicating the drug release approaches saturation/equilibrium after about 3 days.
