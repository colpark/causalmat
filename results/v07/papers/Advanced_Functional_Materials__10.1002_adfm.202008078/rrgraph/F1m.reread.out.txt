Panel M is a line/scatter plot (not microscopy — no scale bar) titled by y-axis "O2 generation (ppm)" vs x-axis "Time (min)" ranging from 5 to 30 minutes in 5-minute increments. Two conditions are compared: "MVs" (red squares) and "Dissociated MVs" (blue circles), each with visible error bars (mean ± SD/SEM presumably from n replicate measurements, likely from an O2 electrode/probe or fluorescence-based O2 assay).

Trend: The MVs (red) trace stays flat and low (roughly 0-3 ppm) across all time points, showing negligible O2 generation. The Dissociated MVs (blue) trace rises steadily and roughly monotonically from about 14 ppm at 5 min to about 55 ppm at 30 min, with the rate of increase appearing to plateau slightly between 20-30 min. This indicates that dissociation of the MVs (membrane vesicles, likely catalase- or peroxidase-containing, e.g., for O2 generation from H2O2) substantially increases O2-generating activity compared to intact/non-dissociated MVs.
