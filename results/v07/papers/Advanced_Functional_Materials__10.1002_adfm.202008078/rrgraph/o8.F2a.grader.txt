Panel A shows fluorescence microscopy of cultured cells assessing intracellular H2O2 levels, compared across two oxygen conditions (Normoxia, left; Hypoxia, right) and three treatment groups (PBS control, PBS+H2O2, and PBS+H2O2+MVs, i.e., microvesicles). For each condition/group, four columns are shown: Hoechst nuclear stain (blue), H2O2 tracker fluorescent probe (orange/yellow), a Merge of the two channels, and a pseudo-colored 3D surface-intensity plot rendering H2O2 tracker signal height/topography. No scale bar is visible in the cropped panel. Trend: PBS-alone rows show sparse, low orange signal and short 3D peaks; PBS+H2O2 rows show markedly increased orange/H2O2 tracker signal with tall, dense 3D peaks in both normoxia and hypoxia, indicating oxidative stress induction; the PBS+H2O2+MVs row shows visibly reduced orange signal and lower/sparser 3D peaks relative to PBS+H2O2 in both oxygen conditions (most pronounced under hypoxia, where signal is nearly back to baseline), consistent with MVs attenuating H2O2-induced oxidative stress.
