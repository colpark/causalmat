Panel F3b is a top-down SEM image of the fabricated metasurface, showing a grid array of discrete plasmonic units against a dark background. Each unit is a single rectangular (roughly square-to-elongated-rectangle) nanoantenna occupying one pixel site, consistent with the described 40 nm Au nanoantenna-on-MIM design. The nanoantennas are arranged in a regular, evenly spaced lattice of rows and columns (columns of antennas are visibly grouped, spanning the imaged region up to the 40 μm scale bar), but the individual antenna sizes vary noticeably from pixel to pixel — some appear as small squares, others as larger or more elongated rectangles, and orientation/aspect ratio also differs somewhat between units. This pixel-to-pixel variation in rectangle size/shape (while maintaining a fixed grid pitch) is consistent with each e-beam-written unit cell encoding a distinct local phase value needed for the phase-matrix (P1/P2) hologram-switching design. No other antenna shapes (e.g., crosses, rings, split rings) are visible — all units appear to be simple rectangular/square patches.
