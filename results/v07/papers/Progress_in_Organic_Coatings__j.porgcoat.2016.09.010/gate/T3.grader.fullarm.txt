Figure 2b (Al 2p high-resolution XPS spectrum) shows two fitted components: a large, dominant peak centered at about 78.3 eV labeled "Al" (metallic aluminum, intensity ~72 counts) and a smaller, broader shoulder peak at about 75.5 eV labeled "Al2O3" (aluminum oxide, intensity ~17 counts). This is consistent with, and supports, the claim: it demonstrates that native Al nanoparticles carry only a thin, incomplete oxide layer leaving substantial exposed metallic Al, which explains why they are prone to self-corrosion and aggregation and motivates the need for additional protective conversion coatings (Ce oxide/hydroxide, APTES) described in the paper.
