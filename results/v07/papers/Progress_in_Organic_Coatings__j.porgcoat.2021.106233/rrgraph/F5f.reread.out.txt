Panel (f) is an X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) high-resolution scan of the N 1s core level, plotted as Count/s (y-axis) versus Binding Energy in eV (x-axis, range ~392–410 eV, decreasing left to right). The raw spectrum (black data points with red fitted envelope) shows a single asymmetric peak centered near 398 eV, deconvoluted into three color-filled Gaussian/Lorentzian component peaks: an orange peak assigned to -NH- at 398.0 eV, a yellow peak assigned to -NH2+ at 399.0 eV, and a smaller green peak assigned to -N= at 397.1 eV. No scale bar is present (this is a spectroscopy plot, not a micrograph). The dominant contribution is the -NH- (398.0 eV) component, indicating that amine/imine-type nitrogen bonding is the primary nitrogen species detected in the sample, with lesser protonated amine and imine-type nitrogen present.
