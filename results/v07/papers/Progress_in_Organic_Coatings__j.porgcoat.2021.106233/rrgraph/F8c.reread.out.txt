The image at /home/aid1/Documents/causalmat/.v07work/rr/ba3c67047fa2bc08.jpg is a line/scatter plot, labeled panel (c), not a microscopy image (no scale bar). It plots capacitance C_c (in pF, y-axis, 0–800+) versus Time (in days, x-axis, 0–60) for four coating/composite samples: pure WPU (black squares), GO/WPU (red circles), FGO/WPU (blue triangles), and FGO/PANI_PA/WPU (magenta inverted triangles). Pure WPU and GO/WPU both rise sharply from ~200 pF at day 0-15 to a peak near 830-840 pF around day 40, then decline to ~480-500 pF (pure WPU) or ~410 pF (GO/WPU) by day 60. FGO/WPU stays lower overall, rising more gradually to ~360 pF near day 40, dropping to a minimum near 40-50 pF at day 50, then spiking sharply to ~870 pF at day 60. FGO/PANI_PA/WPU remains nearly flat and low throughout (roughly 110-155 pF across the full 60-day span), indicating the most stable barrier/anticorrosion performance among the four coatings, with the least fluctuation in capacitance over time.
