Panel F8a: Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image. It shows corrosion resistance (contact resistance, R_c, in Ω·cm⁻², log scale y-axis) versus immersion time (0–60 days, x-axis) for four coating systems on WPU (waterborne polyurethane) substrates: pure WPU (black squares), GO/WPU (red circles), FGO/WPU (blue triangles), and FGO/PANI_PA/WPU (magenta inverted triangles). All curves start near 10^9 Ω·cm² at day 0 and drop sharply within the first 10–15 days. The FGO/PANI_PA/WPU curve declines more gradually and plateaus around 1×10^8 Ω·cm² from day 20 onward, remaining roughly one to two orders of magnitude higher than the other three formulations, which stabilize between 1×10^6 and 1×10^7 Ω·cm² after day 15–20. No scale bar is present (this is a plotted graph, not a micrograph); a legend in the upper right identifies the four sample series.

Panel F8b: Panel (b) is a line/scatter plot from electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) analysis, showing charge-transfer resistance (R_ct, in Ω·cm², log scale from 10^3 to 10^9) versus immersion time in days (linear scale, 0 to 60 days) for coated samples in a corrosive medium. Four sample series are plotted with distinct markers/colors: pure WPU (black squares), GO/WPU (red circles), FGO/WPU (blue triangles), and FGO/PANI_PA/WPU (magenta inverted triangles), each connected by lines through data points sampled roughly every 5-10 days. No scale bar is present (this is a data plot, not a micrograph). Trend: all series start near ~5×10^8 Ω·cm² at day 0 and decline over time, but at markedly different rates — GO/WPU drops most steeply to ~5×10^3 by day 40-60, FGO/WPU declines more gradually to ~10^5 by day 60, pure WPU declines slowly and plateaus around 1-2×10^6, while FGO/PANI_PA/WPU maintains the highest R_ct throughout (staying above 10^6-10^7) despite an initial sharp drop and a mid-range dip/recovery around day 15-30, indicating it provides the best long-term anti-corrosion barrier performance among the four coatings.
