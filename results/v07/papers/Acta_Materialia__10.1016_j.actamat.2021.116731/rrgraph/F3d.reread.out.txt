Panel (d) is an engineering stress–strain curve, not a microscopy image. It plots engineering stress (MPa, y-axis, 0–1000) versus engineering strain (%, x-axis, 0–15) for a specimen labeled with crystallographic orientation [112]. The curve shows linear elastic loading to roughly 450 MPa, a small serration/yield drop, then continued rise to a peak of about 590–600 MPa near 2% strain, followed by a long, gently declining/plateau region (with fine serrations, likely Portevin–Le Chatelier-type flow) extending out to about 13–14% strain before a sharp final drop (fracture) near 510–520 MPa. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a plot) and no imaging technique involved — this is a uniaxial tensile test result for a single-crystal-oriented sample.
