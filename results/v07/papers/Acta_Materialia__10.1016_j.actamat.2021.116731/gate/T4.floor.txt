Context from a materials paper:
- Why microscale NiTi deforms by stress-induced transformation in some orientations and by plasticity in others is unexplained
- Because transformation, slip and deformation twinning have different orientation-dependent Schmid factors, the active mode should switch with the compression axis
- Comparison axis: compression along [001] versus [112] of the B2 austenite
- Machine ~2 um diameter micro-pillars from grains of the two chosen orientations
- Compress the pillars: three load-unload cycles to 3, 5 and 10% engineering strain, then loading to ~10-13% strain
- Plastic deformation of the [001] pillar is localized to one narrow band; the rest of the pillar shaft stays featureless
