Reading panel F8c (the herringbone B2-B19' transformation CRSS map over the [001]-[1̄11]-[011] standard triangle, scale 0-400 MPa): the CRSS is clearly not uniform across orientations. The [001] corner is dark blue, the lowest value on the entire map (roughly 50-80 MPa), while the rest of the triangle is mostly green-yellow (~150-250 MPa) with an orange local maximum (~300-350 MPa) near the middle of the triangle close to the [1̄11]-[011] edge. So [001] stands out as the orientation with the minimum CRSS for the herringbone transformation, markedly lower than essentially everywhere else in the triangle, consistent with [001] being the orientation where stress-induced transformation is most favored.
