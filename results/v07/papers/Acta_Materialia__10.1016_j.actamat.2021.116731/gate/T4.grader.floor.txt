Based on the caption of panel F5b, the deformed [112]-oriented pillar contains two phases: B19 martensite (the majority/matrix microstructure) and B2 austenite, which appears as discrete islands within the martensite. The corresponding selected-area diffraction pattern (SADP) confirms both phases are present and shows the B2 austenite as isolated, retained regions surrounded by transformed B19 martensite rather than as a continuous matrix. Additionally, dislocations on (i10) slip planes are observed specifically within the B2 austenite islands, indicating that plasticity (slip) occurred locally in the untransformed austenite regions while the surrounding material transformed to B19 martensite.
