Panel F1a shows an XRD pattern (2θ = 20–120°) with two indexed phases, marked by diamonds (B2) and circles (Ti4Ni2O). The B2 austenite peaks are (100), (110), (200), (210), (211), and (220) — with (110) at ~49° being by far the most intense peak, and (200) at ~73° the second strongest, while (100), (210), (211), and (220) are much weaker. The Ti4Ni2O peaks are (511), (440), (822)/(660), and (10 2 2), all comparatively weak/minor reflections interspersed among the B2 peaks. Overall the pattern is dominated by the B2 (cubic austenite) reflections, consistent with the alloy being mostly B2 austenite, with only a trace amount of the Ti4Ni2O secondary phase as stated in the caption — no martensite (B19′) peaks are indexed.
