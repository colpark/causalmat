Candidate answer:
No, the rising trend does not hold for S2 from 1650 to 1700 C. In F6a, S2's flexural strength decreases over that range, from about 170 MPa at 1650 C to about 150 MPa at 1700 C (a dip, not a rise). This contrasts with S1, which peaks at 1600 C (~465 MPa) and is not plotted beyond that temperature in this panel.

In F4a, S2's apparent porosity decreases slightly over the same range, from roughly 1.2% at 1650 C to about 0.7% at 1700 C. So while porosity continues to drop modestly (as expected with further densification), S2's flexural strength does not track this improvement and instead falls slightly, indicating the general "strength rises with temperature" claim does not apply uniformly to S2 in the 1650-1700 C interval.
