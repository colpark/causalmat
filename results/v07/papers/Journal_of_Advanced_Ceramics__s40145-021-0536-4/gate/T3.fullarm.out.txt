Both panels (F8b and F8e) show the S2 sample (SPS at 1700 C) with a yellow dotted line tracing the crack path through the fracture surface, magnified further in (b) via the circled inset.

The crack path is markedly tortuous/zigzag rather than straight, deflecting sharply back and forth as it threads between individual grains. It does not cut through the grains themselves; instead it follows the boundaries around the angular, polyhedral cBN grains, consistent with an intergranular fracture mode. The cBN grains appear as distinct, sharp-edged, blocky particles that sit embedded in a finer-grained, more granular-textured matrix (the Al2O3–ZrO2 binder phase) — the two phases are texturally distinguishable, with the crack preferentially propagating along the cBN grain/matrix interfaces rather than transgranularly through either phase. This zigzagging, boundary-hugging crack path is consistent with weak interfacial bonding between the cBN grains and the Al2O3-based matrix, as would be expected from the thermal-expansion-mismatch-driven residual stress described for the S2 binder system.
