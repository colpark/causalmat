Panel (a) [BCN, 2 µm scale] shows large, thick, compact, elongated/rod-like or blocky particles with smooth-ish continuous solid surfaces, densely packed grains typical of bulk multilayer stacking. Panel (b) [CNS, 200 nm scale] shows a much finer, thin, irregular flake/sheet-like morphology with a porous, mesh-like network of small interconnected fragments and visible interstitial voids/pores, consistent with exfoliated thin nanosheets. In short: BCN exhibits large, dense, solid bulk particles, while CNS exhibits fine, thin, porous, interconnected sheet fragments with abundant mesoporosity.
