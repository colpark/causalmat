Panel (a) BCN: shows large, thick, smooth-surfaced bulk particles/grains several micrometers across (scale bar 2 µm), densely packed with compact, layered stack-like morphology and no visible internal porosity.

Panel (b) CNS: shows a much finer, porous, sponge-like/mesh-like network of small interconnected fragments with numerous irregular pores and voids, imaged at a scale roughly 10x finer (scale bar 200 nm).

Difference: BCN consists of large, dense, non-porous bulk particles, whereas CNS consists of a fine, thin, mesoporous interconnected sheet/fragment network with visible open porosity — consistent with exfoliation breaking the bulk stack into thinner, porous nanosheet fragments.
