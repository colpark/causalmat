In sharp contrast, the SWNT + Laser group remains essentially flat/near baseline (~0.2–0.7) throughout the 14-day period, indicating strong tumor growth suppression from combined SWNT nanoparticle and laser (photothermal) treatment. A bracket on the right indicates statistical significance (p < 0.0001) comparing the SWNT + Laser group to the other groups.
