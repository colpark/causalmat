Candidate answer:
Panel c shows stacked XPS (X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy) survey spectra plotted as Intensity (a.u., y-axis, no scale bar) versus Binding energy (eV, x-axis, 0–1000 eV, decreasing left to right). Five traces are overlaid/offset vertically for different samples: CeO2 (olive), MXene (blue), F-d@Ce (magenta), F-d@MX (red), and PCM (black), from bottom to top. Vertical shaded bands mark characteristic elemental peaks labeled Ce 3d (~880 eV), F 1s (~685 eV), O 1s (~530 eV), N 1s (~400 eV), C 1s (~285 eV), and Ti 3s/Ti 3p near 0–60 eV. The CeO2 and F-d@Ce traces show a strong Ce 3d peak absent in MXene-containing samples, while MXene, F-d@MX, and PCM show a pronounced Ti 3s/Ti 3p signal near 0 eV that is absent in the Ce-only samples; all traces show O 1s and C 1s peaks, indicating compositional differences confirming successful incorporation of MXene and CeO2/fluorine components across the sample series.
