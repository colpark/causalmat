Candidate answer:
In panel F3d, both the Fe K-edge position and the V pre-edge position shift monotonically to higher energy as the normalized state of charge increases from 0 to 1.0 (i.e., both metals are oxidized on charging).

- Fe: the edge energy rises from about 7124.6 eV (near C/C=0) to about 7125.8 eV (at C/C=1.0), a shift of roughly 1.2 eV, moving upward within/toward the Fe3+ reference band (the Fe3+ dashed line sits near 7125.9 eV, well above the Fe2+ line near 7120.5 eV).
- V: the pre-edge peak position rises from about 5469.8 eV to about 5470.5 eV over the same charge range, a shift of roughly 0.7 eV, moving from near the V4+ reference line (~5470.0 eV) up toward the V5+ reference line (~5470.5–5470.8 eV).

So both Fe and V oxidize progressively with charging, with the Fe edge shift (~1.2 eV) somewhat larger in absolute terms than the V pre-edge shift (~0.7 eV) over the same 0→1.0 normalized capacity range.
