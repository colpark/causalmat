Question: The claim under test holds that charge is compensated by full valence swings for both V (V3+ to V4+ to V5+) and Fe (Fe2+ to Fe3+). Does the Fe K-edge XAS panel (F3d) fully bear this out?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): No, only partially. At zero charge the Fe absorption edge sits about two-thirds of the way toward the Fe3+ reference line, and the edge shift is only about 1.6 eV out of the roughly 5.9 eV span expected across the full Fe2+ to Fe3+ range; the V edge sits somewhere between the V3+ and V5+ references rather than at a defined endpoint (o19). So the panel shows a partial Fe shift, not the complete Fe2+-to-Fe3+ swing the claim asserts.
