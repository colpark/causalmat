Panel D shows a scatter/line plot, not a micrograph — there is no scale bar since this is a spectroscopic data plot rather than an image of a physical sample.

Technique: appears to be XANES (X-ray absorption near-edge structure) analysis, plotting X-ray absorption edge energy (eV) as a function of normalized charge capacity (C/C at 1.15 V), used to track oxidation-state changes of two elements during electrochemical cycling.

Layout: the y-axis is split into two stacked sub-panels sharing the same x-axis. The upper sub-panel (labeled "Fe", dark blue open circles) spans roughly 7122.0–7126.5 eV, with reference dashed lines/labels for Fe3+ and Fe2+ oxidation states. The lower sub-panel (labeled "V", magenta/maroon open circles) spans roughly 5469.0–5471.5 eV, with reference lines for V5+, V4+, and V3+.

Trends: for Fe, the edge energy rises modestly and roughly monotonically from about 7124.5 eV at C/C≈0 to about 7125.8 eV at C/C≈1.0, staying close to the Fe3+ reference level throughout (little sign of reduction to Fe2+). For V, the edge energy also increases with charge capacity, from about 5469.8 eV near C/C≈0 to about 5470.5 eV at C/C≈1.0, moving from near the V4+/V3+ region toward higher energy but not clearly reaching the V5+ reference. Both elements thus show a gradual increase in oxidation state (edge energy) as charge capacity increases, consistent with oxidation of both Fe and V during charging.
