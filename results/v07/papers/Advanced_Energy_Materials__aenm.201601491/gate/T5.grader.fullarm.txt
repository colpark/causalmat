Question: You're handed the XAS panel F3d from the V/Fe PBA cathode study (galvanostatic cycling in the 0.5 M Na2SO4 + H2SO4 acidic electrolyte described above). Reading the Fe edge and the V pre-edge positions in that panel, how do they change as the electrode is charged?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): Both edges rise monotonically with normalized charge: the Fe edge shifts from about 7124.3 to 7125.9 eV, and the V pre-edge shifts from about 5469.75 to 5470.5 eV.

Grading note: Answer key: the numbers and labels on the evidence node o18, judge-confirmed against the panel F3d read.

Candidate answer:
In panel F3d, both the Fe K-edge energy (top, normalized intensity = 0.5) and the V pre-edge peak energy (bottom) shift monotonically to higher X-ray energy as the normalized charge capacity increases from 0 to 1.0 (i.e., as the electrode is charged).

- Fe edge: rises from about 7124.5 eV near C/C=0 to about 7126 eV near C/C=1.0, moving up toward the dashed Fe3+ reference line (i.e., iron becomes progressively more oxidized, Fe2+→Fe3+, on charging).
- V pre-edge: rises from about 5469.8 eV near C/C=0 to about 5470.5 eV near C/C=1.0, moving up toward the V5+ reference line (i.e., vanadium also becomes progressively more oxidized, roughly V4+→V5+, on charging).

So charging drives simultaneous oxidation of both the Fe and V sublattices in the V/Fe PBA, evidenced by the correlated rise in Fe edge and V pre-edge energies with increasing charge capacity.
