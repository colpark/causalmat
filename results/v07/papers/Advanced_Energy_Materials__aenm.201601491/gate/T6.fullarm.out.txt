Panel F3d (crop D) shows Fe K-edge energy on the top axis (scale ~7120.5–7126.5 eV) plotted against normalized charge capacity, with dashed reference lines for Fe2+ (near 7120.5 eV) and Fe3+ (near 7126.5 eV). The four data points sit at roughly 7124.5, 7124.8, 7125.2, and 7125.8 eV across the 0→1.0 capacity range — a narrow band clustered close to the Fe3+ reference line, shifting upward by only about 1.3 eV overall, and never approaching the Fe2+ reference line at the bottom of the scale. This indicates Fe stays in a largely Fe3+-like oxidation state throughout charging with only a modest further increase, rather than showing a full swing spanning Fe2+ to Fe3+. So the panel does not fully bear out the claim of a complete Fe2+→Fe3+ valence swing; it shows a partial/modest edge shift confined to the higher (Fe3+-adjacent) end of the range. (The lower half of the same crop, for V, does show a clearer progression from near the V3+ line up through V4+ toward V5+.)
