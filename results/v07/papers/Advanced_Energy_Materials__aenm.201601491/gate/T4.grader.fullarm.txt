Candidate answer:
Panel F3c shows the V K pre-edge feature (marked by triangles) shifting in energy position as the applied potential changes across the cycle (0.45 V, 0.60 V, 0.90 V, 1.15 V), rather than staying fixed. At the lower potential (0.45 V), the pre-edge peak sits at a lower X-ray energy, aligning closely with the VO2 and V2O3 reference spectra positions. At the higher potential (1.15 V), the pre-edge peak sits at a higher X-ray energy, aligning closely with the V2O5 reference spectrum position. So the two conditions differ in that the pre-edge feature tracks toward the reduced-vanadium-oxide reference energy at low potential and toward the oxidized-vanadium-oxide (V2O5) reference energy at high potential, indicating the pre-edge position moves between these two reference positions rather than remaining at one fixed energy.
