Panel F3b (Fe K-edge XAS, panel B) shows five stacked spectra: Fe2O3 reference at top, then 1.15 V, 0.90 V, 0.60 V, 0.45 V, and FeO reference at bottom, each with a colored triangle marking the pre-edge/edge feature position (dotted circles in the inset show the pre-edge peaks).

Difference between the two potential conditions: at 0.45 V the marked edge/pre-edge feature sits at a lower X-ray energy, essentially overlapping the FeO reference position, while at 1.15 V the marked feature sits at a distinctly higher X-ray energy, overlapping the Fe2O3 reference position. The intermediate 0.60 V and 0.90 V traces show the marker positions progressively shifted between these two endpoints, tracking with the applied potential. So the two conditions differ specifically in where the Fe K-edge feature falls relative to the reference compounds: 0.45 V aligns with the FeO (lower-energy) reference, whereas 1.15 V aligns with the Fe2O3 (higher-energy) reference, consistent with a change in the iron local electronic/oxidation environment between low and high applied potential.
