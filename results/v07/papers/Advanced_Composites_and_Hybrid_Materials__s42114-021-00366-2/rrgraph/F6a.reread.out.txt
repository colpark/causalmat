Panel (a): a line plot of dielectric constant vs. frequency (log scale, 100 Hz to 1,000,000 Hz on the x-axis) for four composite samples, likely BST@Ag/polymer (PAEN) nanocomposite films, measured by dielectric spectroscopy (no scale bar, as this is a data plot not a micrograph). Four curves are shown: Pure PAEN (gray, lowest, ~3.3 to ~3.05), BST@Ag1% (teal, ~5.3 to ~4.7), BST@Ag3% (orange, ~5.65 to ~5.0), and BST@Ag5% (red, highest, ~6.05 to ~5.35), with the y-axis (dielectric constant) ranging from about 3.0 to 6.5. All four curves show the same trend: dielectric constant decreases monotonically and smoothly (gently sloping, roughly logarithmic decay) as frequency increases across the full measured range. The curves are ordered and separated by Ag loading percentage, with higher Ag content corresponding to a higher dielectric constant at every frequency, and pure PAEN sitting well below all the filled composites throughout.
