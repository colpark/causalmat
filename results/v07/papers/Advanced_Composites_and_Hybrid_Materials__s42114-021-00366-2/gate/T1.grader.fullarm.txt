Panel F3e (BST@Ag5%) shows a dense field of aggregated, rough-surfaced particles at 500 nm scale bar; individual particle/grain sizes are consistent with roughly 50-100 nm, matching the "grown to about 100 nm" size claim and showing a visibly roughened (bumpy, non-smooth) particle surface texture. This supports the "roughening" and approximate size portion of the claim. However, the image is a plain secondary-electron SEM micrograph with no elemental contrast, EDS mapping, or higher-magnification/backscatter view distinguishing Ag nanoparticles from the underlying BST-SH core — so it cannot by itself confirm that the roughness is specifically caused by Ag nanoparticle decoration rather than simply BST particle agglomeration. Overall the panel is consistent with and partially supports the claim (rough ~100 nm particles) but does not independently verify the "Ag coats the BST-SH surface" mechanism.
