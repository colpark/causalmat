Panel F5c (2-18 GHz) plots the dielectric loss tangent (tanδε), ranging roughly 0.01-0.03 through S/C band and rising with resonance-like humps to ~0.06-0.10 in the X/Ku band. Panel F5f plots the magnetic loss tangent (tanδμ = μ''/μ'), which is actually higher overall: it starts near 0.14-0.16 in S/C band, dips with a resonance hump around 8-10 GHz to ~0.10-0.13, and gradually decreases to ~0.06-0.10 by 18 GHz.

Taken at face value, these two panels do NOT clearly support the claim that magnetic loss contributes little: tanδμ (F5f) is comparable to or larger than tanδε (F5c) across most of the 2-18 GHz range, not obviously smaller. A loss-tangent comparison alone therefore does not demonstrate that magnetic loss is negligible relative to dielectric loss - that conclusion would require also weighing the absolute permeability values (μ'≈1, so μ'' itself is small even if the ratio μ''/μ' is sizable), which isn't shown in these two panels. So the panels as presented are, at best, ambiguous/non-supportive of the "little magnetic loss" claim, and could even be read as contradicting it if interpreted by tangent magnitude alone.
