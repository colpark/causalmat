Panel F3c is an XPS survey spectrum (binding energy 0–800 eV vs. intensity) with three labeled peaks: C1s (~285 eV, by far the tallest/most intense peak), O1s (~530 eV), and N1s (~400 eV). Comparing O1s and N1s directly: the O1s peak is noticeably taller and sharper than the N1s peak, which appears as a smaller, more modest bump. So the panel shows O1s > N1s in intensity, with both being minor relative to the dominant C1s signal — consistent with the caption's claim that the spectrum shows the presence of C, N, and O, with N1s being the weakest of the three signals.
