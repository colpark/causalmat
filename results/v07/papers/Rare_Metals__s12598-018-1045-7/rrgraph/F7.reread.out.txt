X-axis is Strain (%) from 0 to 25%, Y-axis is Stress (MPa) from 0 to 350 MPa. Two curves are plotted: "As-cast" (black line) which rises steeply to a peak of about 200 MPa around 9% strain and then drops abruptly (fracture), and "As-extruded (350 °C)" (red/maroon line) which rises to a higher peak of roughly 275-280 MPa, plateaus between about 5-15% strain, then gradually declines to about 235 MPa before ending near 23% strain (also a fracture point). No scale bar is present since this is a plotted chart, not a microscopy image; the as-extruded condition shows both higher strength and much greater ductility (elongation) than the as-cast condition.
