This is panel B, an FTIR spectroscopy plot showing Reflectance (%) on the y-axis versus Wavenumber (cm⁻¹) on the x-axis (4000 to 500, decreasing left to right). Four stacked/offset spectra are shown for different sample conditions: PPAmF (black), BVLD-PPAmF (green), NO-PPAmF (red), and BVLD/NO-PPAmF (blue), each vertically offset for clarity (no shared baseline, no scale bar as is typical for spectra plots). Labeled peaks/bands are annotated with arrows and text: -COOH stretch, -NH stretch, C-H stretch (circled region ~2900 cm⁻¹), -C=O, and C-F for the black/green pair; and -OH stretch (H-bonded), N-N stretch, N=N stretch, NO vibration, and N-O stretch (marked with arrows and magenta text) for the red/blue pair, with several peaks also marked by asterisks. The black and green traces (PPAmF, BVLD-PPAmF) sit in the upper portion of the plot and share similar peak positions (C=O ~1700 cm⁻¹, C-F ~1150 cm⁻¹), while the red and blue traces (NO-PPAmF, BVLD/NO-PPAmF) sit lower and show a broad -OH stretch band near 3400-3500 cm⁻¹ plus a cluster of NO-related bands in the 1000-1700 cm⁻¹ region. Overall the panel is comparing how BVLD treatment and NO incorporation alter the surface chemical functional groups (via characteristic IR absorption/reflectance bands) of the PPAmF material.
