Panel G (labeled "G" in upper left) is a bar chart, not a microscopy image — there is no scale bar or imaging technique here (no SEM/TEM/optical micrograph). It plots "Blood Flow Rate (%)" on the y-axis (0–120%) across six groups on the x-axis: Initial Value, 316LSS, PPAmF, BVLD-PPAmF, NO-PPAmF, and BVLD/NO-PPAmF. Bars are teal-colored with error bars (SD/SEM), and significance brackets with *** are drawn between groups.

Trend: Initial Value is set at 100%; 316LSS and PPAmF drop sharply to ~20% and ~17% respectively (indicating occlusion/thrombosis reducing blood flow); BVLD-PPAmF and NO-PPAmF partially recover to ~64% and ~68%; and the combined BVLD/NO-PPAmF group shows the highest recovery at ~90%, which is marked as significantly different (***) from both the low-flow groups and from BVLD-PPAmF/NO-PPAmF alone. This suggests the combined surface treatment (likely a coating releasing NO, evaluated against a control PPAmF-coated and bare 316L stainless steel surface) best preserves/restores blood flow rate in what appears to be a vascular graft or stent patency assay.
