Question: The claim under test is that TiO6 octahedral distortion strengthens with x. Checking this against Raman panels F2d and F5b, what does a careful read of the two panels show for the x=0.06 sample, and does it support the claim?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): The two panels do not agree with each other for x=0.06: the room-temperature wavenumbers read off F2d and F5b disagree, with nu1 ~136 cm-1 in one versus ~113 cm-1 in the other, nu2 ~185 cm-1 versus ~170 cm-1, and nu8 ~818 cm-1 versus ~865 cm-1 (o24). Because the two readings of the same composition conflict, the panels cannot be used to confirm the claim.

Grading note: Answer key: the audit ruling on the panel, node o24.

Candidate answer:
Panel F2d (wavenumber vs. x, room T) shows mixed, non-monotonic trends across the 8 Raman modes as x rises to 0.06: the low-frequency A-site modes ν1 and ν2 increase steadily (ν1 ~128→136 cm⁻¹, ν2 ~162→184 cm⁻¹), and ν3 rises markedly (~238→268 cm⁻¹), but ν5 decreases (~540→527 cm⁻¹), ν4/ν6/ν7 stay essentially flat, and ν8 actually dips slightly (~832→818 cm⁻¹) before rising again at x=0.08. Since the higher-wavenumber modes (ν5–ν8) are generally the ones most sensitive to TiO6 octahedral (Ti–O stretch/bend) vibrations, and these show flat-to-decreasing behavior rather than a clear monotonic shift, F2d alone does not present a clean, uniform signature of strengthening octahedral distortion with x — only the lower-frequency, largely A-site-related modes (ν1–ν3) increase consistently.

Panel F5b, plotted for the fixed x=0.06 sample as a function of temperature, only shows all eight modes softening (shifting to lower wavenumber) with increasing temperature, a normal thermal effect; it carries no information about how distortion varies with composition x, so it cannot itself support or refute the "distortion strengthens with x" claim. Notably, the room-temperature (lowest-T) values in F5b for x=0.06 (e.g., ν8 ≈865, ν1≈113, ν2≈170) do not closely match the x=0.06 values read off F2d (ν8≈818, ν1≈136, ν2≈184), an inconsistency between the two panels for the same nominal sample/condition that further weakens confidence in using them together as corroborating evidence. Overall, a careful read shows the two panels give at best mixed, partially contradictory support for the claim that TiO6 octahedral distortion strengthens with x — some modes trend as expected, others do not, and the panels are not fully self-consistent at x=0.06.
