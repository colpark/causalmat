Panel (d) is a dual-y-axis line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image (no scale bar). X-axis: Frequency (Hz), 0–100. Left y-axis (red, square markers): Energy density (J/cm³), 0–1.0. Right y-axis (blue, circle markers): Energy efficiency (%), 0–100. Both curves are plotted for several frequency points clustered at low frequency (roughly 1, 2, 5, 10 Hz) plus two points at 50 and 100 Hz. Energy density starts around 0.73 J/cm³ at the lowest frequency and declines gradually to about 0.63 J/cm³ at 100 Hz. Energy efficiency starts around 80% at low frequency and declines gradually to about 72% at 100 Hz. Both trends are monotonically decreasing with increasing frequency, with the density curve consistently below the efficiency curve on their respective scales; a legend box in the plot identifies the two series.
