The panel (labeled "e") is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image — there is no scale bar since it is not a micrograph. It plots Viscosity (Pa·s, log scale from 10^-2 to 10^6) on the y-axis versus Time (s, 0–120) on the x-axis, consistent with a rotational rheometer step-shear (thixotropy/shear-recovery) test comparing two MXene inks. Two datasets are shown: red squares for "H-MXene ink" (high-concentration or high-viscosity MXene ink) and blue triangles for "L-MXene ink" (low-concentration/low-viscosity MXene ink). Both curves show a step pattern: viscosity starts moderate/high (roughly 10^3 Pa·s for H-MXene, ~10^1 Pa·s for L-MXene) during an initial low-shear interval (0–30 s), drops sharply during a middle interval (30–60 s, presumably high shear rate applied) — H-MXene falling to ~1–3 Pa·s and L-MXene falling to ~0.1 Pa·s — and then recovers back toward the initial higher viscosity plateau once the shear rate returns to low (60–120 s). The H-MXene ink maintains consistently higher viscosity and shows more complete/faster recovery to its original plateau than the L-MXene ink, indicating a demonstration of shear-thinning and thixotropic self-recovery behavior, with the higher-concentration ink exhibiting stronger structural recovery.
