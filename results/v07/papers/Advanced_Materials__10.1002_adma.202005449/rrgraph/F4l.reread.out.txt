This is panel "I" showing a Ragone plot (log-log scale) comparing energy density (µW h cm⁻²) versus power density (mW cm⁻²) for several energy-storage devices. The red curve/points labeled "This work" trace a decreasing trend from roughly 150 µW h cm⁻² at low power density (~0.03 mW cm⁻²) down to about 20 µW h cm⁻² at higher power density (~0.4 mW cm⁻²), forming the characteristic Ragone-plot falloff. Six comparison literature data points (single markers, not curves) are overlaid: Zn//MnO2-PPy (purple square), Zn//MnO2 (purple star), NiSn//LixMnO2 (green right-triangle), Li//LiCoO2 (yellow down-triangle), Li//LiFePO4 (orange half-circle), C//PPy (blue circle), and Li//TiO2 (teal diamond), each positioned as isolated points in the 10⁻² to ~1 mW cm⁻² power range and roughly 10–100 µW h cm⁻² energy range. No scale bar is present since this is a graph/chart panel rather than a micrograph. The overall trend shows "This work" achieving comparable or superior energy density at low-to-moderate power densities relative to the benchmark devices, with performance converging toward or falling below some comparators (e.g., NiSn//LixMnO2, Li//LiCoO2) at the higher power density end.
