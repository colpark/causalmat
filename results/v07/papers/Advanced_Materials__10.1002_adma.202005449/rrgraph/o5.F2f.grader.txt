Panel f is a rheology plot showing storage modulus G' and loss modulus G'' (Pa, log scale y-axis, 10^-1 to 10^5) versus shear stress (Pa, log scale x-axis, 1 to ~10^3), comparing two MXene-based inks: "H-MXene ink" (red circles = G', purple/lavender circles = G'') and "L-MXene ink" (red triangles = G', blue triangles = G''). This is an oscillatory shear rheology amplitude/stress sweep, not a microscopy image — no scale bar is present (not applicable to this data type). For the H-MXene ink, both G' and G'' remain roughly constant (plateau) around 2000–4000 Pa across a wide stress range (~1–100 Pa) before both drop sharply together near ~100–300 Pa, indicating a yield-stress/gel-like behavior with a well-defined yield point. For the L-MXene ink, G' and G'' are much lower in magnitude (starting near 1–5 Pa) and both decline steadily and continuously from the start of the stress sweep, dropping off by ~10–20 Pa, indicating a much weaker, less structured gel with an early onset of flow/yielding. Overall the panel demonstrates that the H-MXene ink has substantially higher storage/loss moduli and a much higher yield stress (better shape retention/printability) than the L-MXene ink.
