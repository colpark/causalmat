The panel (labeled "b") is a log-log rheology plot showing Viscosity (Pa·s, y-axis, 10^-2 to 10^5) versus Shear rate (1/s, x-axis, 10^-2 to 10^3). Two datasets are plotted with continuous marker series: blue circles for "MXene-LTO" and red squares for "MXene-LFP." Both curves show classic shear-thinning behavior — viscosity decreases monotonically and roughly linearly (on the log-log scale) as shear rate increases, spanning about 4-5 orders of magnitude in viscosity. At low shear rates (~10^-2 to 10^-1 s^-1), MXene-LFP (red) has slightly higher viscosity than MXene-LTO (blue); the two curves cross around shear rate ~5-10 s^-1; at high shear rates (>~100 s^-1) MXene-LTO (blue) ends up higher than MXene-LFP (red), which drops to the lowest viscosity value (~0.2-0.3 Pa·s) at the highest shear rate (~10^3 s^-1). No scale bar is present (this is a graph, not a micrograph); axes are labeled in bold black text with a legend box in the upper right.
