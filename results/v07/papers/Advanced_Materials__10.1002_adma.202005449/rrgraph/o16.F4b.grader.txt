The panel (labeled "b") is a log-log rheology plot showing Viscosity (Pa·s, y-axis, 10^-2 to 10^5) versus Shear rate (1/s, x-axis, 10^-2 to 10^3). Two datasets are plotted with continuous marker series: blue circles for "MXene-LTO" and red squares for "MXene-LFP."
