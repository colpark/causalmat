Panel F2i (cross-sectional SEM of the screen-printed MXene line) shows the Ti3C2Tx nanosheets stacked as flat, parallel-oriented flakes lying roughly horizontal (parallel to the substrate/printed-line plane), forming a laminated, layered morphology rather than a random or porous arrangement. This lamellar stacking is consistent from the bottom to the top of the imaged cross-section, indicating the alignment is fairly uniform through the line's depth. There is some gentle waviness/undulation in individual layers (slight curvature of flake stacks), but no clear region of disorder, delamination gaps, or reorientation is visible at any depth — the layered texture looks continuous throughout the ~few-micron-thick cross-section shown (scale bar 1 µm).

This supports the described mechanism: shear flow through the screen-printing mesh aligns the nanosheets in-plane, and the image confirms that alignment is preserved uniformly through the full thickness of the printed film, not just at the surface.
