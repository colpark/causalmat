Panel F2h (top-view SEM, scale bar 1 µm) shows a continuous, fully covered MXene film with no visible gaps, pinholes, or cracks that expose an underlying substrate. The surface has a characteristic wrinkled/crumpled texture typical of restacked MXene nanosheets, with numerous fine, irregular ridge-like folds and creases running across the field of view, giving a "wavy terrain" or crumpled-paper appearance rather than a flat, smooth surface. These wrinkles/ridge lines appear to be intrinsic flake-edge/overlap features (sheet-to-sheet stacking boundaries) rather than printing defects — there are no obvious pores, particulate contamination, or delamination visible at the ~1 µm scale. Overall the image supports a dense, continuous, conformal printed film with a rough, folded nanosheet texture but no coarse cracking or discontinuity at this magnification.
