Panel (c) is a stress–plastic strain plot from a mechanical (compression/tension) test at 300°C, not a microscopy image — there is no scale bar in the micron sense, but a "1%" horizontal reference bar is provided in the upper right of the plotting area to indicate strain magnitude. The y-axis is Stress (MPa), ranging 0–350 MPa; the x-axis is Plastic strain (%), unlabeled numerically (only the 1% reference interval shown). Four crystallographic loading/slip orientations are compared: [0001] (black, plotted as a single vertical line/point near the y-axis reaching ~260 MPa with an "X" marker, i.e., essentially no plastic strain before failure/termination), [11-20] (red curve, rising steeply to a plateau around 130–140 MPa with a small stress dip mid-curve), [01-10] (blue curve, rising to a lower plateau around 110–115 MPa), and [1 1 -2 1.85] (green curve, remaining flat and low, near 0–15 MPa) — each curve terminated with a small arrow indicating continued strain beyond the plot. The trend shows a clear hierarchy of flow stress/hardening among orientations: [0001] > [11-20] > [01-10] >> [1 1 -2 1.85], consistent with anisotropic plastic deformation behavior (likely a hexagonal metal such as Mg or Ti tested along different crystallographic axes).
