Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot of yield stress (MPa, y-axis, 0–400) versus temperature (°C, x-axis, 0–400), not a microscopy image — no scale bar is present since it's a mechanical-property graph. It compares yield stress for a textured Mg alloy tested along multiple crystallographic loading directions (colored/symbol-coded per the inset stereographic/orientation triangle showing 0001, 01-10, 11-20, and a 45°/[11-2 1.85] direction: red circles, blue triangles, green diamonds) against "pure-Mg" reference data (gray open squares). A thick black curve with black square markers labeled "fracture" traces the fracture stress envelope, which decreases from about 400 MPa near room temperature toward roughly 170 MPa at 400 °C. The oriented-alloy yield stress curves (red, blue) start around 130–160 MPa at room temperature and decline gently to ~100–110 MPa by 400 °C, while the green diamond curve stays near 0 MPa across all temperatures. The gray pure-Mg curve is highest at low temperature (~270 MPa at 25 °C) and drops sharply, converging toward near-zero by 300–400 °C, showing a much steeper temperature dependence than the alloy's colored curves.
