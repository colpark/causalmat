Looking at F7c: the right-axis Coulombic-efficiency traces for all three temperatures (30, 45, 60 C) do sit essentially flat near ~100% across the full 100 cycles, so that part of the claim is visually supported. So the panel does not cleanly back the "stable capacity at all three temperatures" part of the claim: two of the three capacity curves show mild fade rather than true stability, and the third (45 C) is not credibly shown as a real capacity trajectory at all — only the CE-near-100% portion of the claim is well supported by the image.
