Context from a materials paper:
- PEO-based solid polymer electrolytes conduct Li+ too poorly near room temperature for all-solid-state Li metal batteries
- Add ball-milled Li alloy particles LixM (Li21Si5, Li21Ge5, Li21Sn5, ~200-300 nm) to PEO-LiTFSI as filler
- PEO, LiTFSI and LixM mixed in DOL-DME and dried into free-standing flexible PEOm-LixM membranes
- The shell formed on Li21Si5 contains Li-rich SEI species RCOO-Li, ROLi and LiF, absent with Si or SiO2 fillers
- Li spreads from the alloy particle into PEO as a gradient over tens of nm while Si stays confined to the particle
- Conductivity of the Li-alloy/PEO interface (~5e-4 S/cm) is about an order higher than Si/PEO or SiO2/PEO interfaces
- PEOm-Li21Si5 has the highest ionic conductivity of all fillers from 20 to 80 C, ~4e-5 S/cm at 30 C
- LiFePO4 cells with PEOm-Li21Si5 cycle stably for 100 cycles at 30, 45 and 60 C with ~100% Coulombic efficiency
