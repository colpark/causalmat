Panel (c) is a line-plus-scatter plot (no scale bar; not a micrograph) showing ionic conductivity versus filler volume fraction for composite PEO-based electrolytes. Y-axis: Conductivity (S cm⁻¹) on a log scale from 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻³; X-axis: Volume fraction (V₀) from 0% to 30%. Three dashed "theoretical σ" curves are shown (teal = PEOₘ-Li alloy, purple = PEOₘ-Si, red/pink = PEOₘ-SiO₂), each rising from ~7×10⁻⁶ S cm⁻¹ near 0% and leveling off/saturating at higher volume fractions, with the Li-alloy curve reaching the highest conductivity (~2×10⁻⁴ S cm⁻¹) and the Si curve the lowest (~1×10⁻⁵ S cm⁻¹). Overlaid experimental data points (green circles = PEOₘ-Li₂₁Si₅, blue circles = PEOₘ-Li₂₁Ge₅, yellow circles = PEOₘ-Li₂₁Sn₅) generally track along/near the theoretical Li-alloy and SiO₂ curves, rising from ~1% to ~13% volume fraction. A vertical dashed line labeled "Pc" (percolation threshold) is marked near 28% volume fraction at the right edge of the plot. Overall trend: conductivity increases with volume fraction and then plateaus, with the Li-alloy composite system showing consistently higher conductivity than Si or SiO₂-based systems across the measured range.
