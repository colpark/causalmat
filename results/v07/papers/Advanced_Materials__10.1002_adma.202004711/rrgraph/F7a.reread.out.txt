Panel (a) is a voltage–time profile from a galvanostatic symmetric-cell cycling test (Li plating/stripping), not a microscopy image. It plots Voltage (V, y-axis, range -1.5 to 1.5) against Time (h, x-axis, 0 to ~300 h) for three cell configurations: PEOm-Si (purple/blue trace), PEOm-SiO2 (red/pink trace), and PEOm-Li21Si5 (green trace). No scale bar is present since this is a line/data plot, not an image. Test conditions are annotated in the plot: 0.2 mA cm^-2, 0.1 mA h cm^-2, 45 °C. Trend: the PEOm-SiO2 cell shows the largest voltage envelope/overpotential (widest pink band, roughly ±0.5 V) and appears noisiest, while PEOm-Li21Si5 (green) shows a narrow, stable band near 0 V dashed line, and PEOm-Si (purple) shows a brief high-voltage transient near t=0 then drops out/overlaps near baseline — indicating PEOm-Li21Si5 provides the most stable, lowest-overpotential cycling among the three, while PEOm-SiO2 is least stable.
