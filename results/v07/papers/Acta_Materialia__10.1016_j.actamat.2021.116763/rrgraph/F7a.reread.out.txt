Panel (a) is a stress-strain plot from tensile testing, "Strength (MPa)" on the y-axis (0-800+ MPa) vs "Strain (%)" on the x-axis (0-50%). Six curves are shown for a sample series processed/annealed at different temperatures: Cast (black), 200°C (red), 400°C (blue), 600°C (magenta), 800°C (green), and 1000°C (dark navy, labeled with an arrow pointing to a very short, near-vertical curve near the origin). The 600°C curve reaches the highest strength (~820-830 MPa) at moderate strain (~18%) before fracture; Cast, 200°C, and 400°C curves cluster together reaching ~650-700 MPa with the largest strains (~30-36%, ductile behavior with strain hardening then drop-off); the 800°C curve is lower in strength (~520 MPa) with moderate strain (~14-15%); the 1000°C curve fails almost immediately at very low strain and low strength (~100 MPa), indicating brittle/embrittled behavior. No scale bar (this is a plot, not a micrograph). An inset diagram in the lower right shows the dog-bone-shaped tensile specimen geometry with dimensions (28 mm overall length, 24 mm grip width, 10 mm gauge length, 2 mm gauge width, R2 fillet radius, 1 mm thickness).
