This is a panel (labeled "a") of stacked X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) spectra showing the Co 2p region (Co 2p3/2 and Co 2p1/2 spin-orbit doublet, binding energy 780–810 eV) for four samples arranged vertically: Co, Co5Fe5, Co5Cr5, and Co5Fe3Cr2 (from top to bottom). Each spectrum shows raw data points (open circles) overlaid with a fitted envelope (black line) and deconvoluted component peaks shown as colored/shaded sub-peaks assigned to Co3+ and Co2+ oxidation states within each spin-orbit doublet, plus a shake-up satellite feature labeled "Sat." (indicated in the bottom spectrum). Vertical dotted guide lines mark the Co3+ and Co2+ peak positions for Co 2p3/2 (~780 eV) and Co 2p1/2 (~795–797 eV) across all four spectra for comparison. The y-axis is intensity in arbitrary units (a.u.) and the x-axis is binding energy in eV; there is no scale bar (not applicable to a spectral plot). The trend across the stack shows the relative Co3+/Co2+ peak-shape and intensity ratio shifting somewhat as alloying composition changes from pure Co to the Fe- and Cr-containing alloys, with broader/more overlapping doublet peaks in the Cr-containing samples.
