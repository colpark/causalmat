Panel C shows linear-sweep voltammetry (LSV) polarization curves for an oxygen evolution reaction (OER) electrocatalysis study, plotting current density j (mA cm⁻²) on the y-axis (0–20) against applied potential vs. RHE (V) on the x-axis (1.0–1.8).

Five curves are compared: Co5Fe3Cr2, Co5Fe5, Co5Cr5, Co, and IrO2 (color-coded legend, top left). All curves are flat near zero current at low potential and rise steeply above roughly 1.5–1.6 V, consistent with onset of the OER.

Co5Fe3Cr2 (black) shows the earliest onset and highest current density at high potential, reaching about 17–18 mA cm⁻² near 1.8 V, indicating the best catalytic activity among the samples. Co5Fe5 and Co5Cr5 (pink/light curves) perform intermediately, while Co (olive) and IrO2 (dark red, the benchmark) lag behind, plateauing around 6–7 mA cm⁻² and 15 mA cm⁻² respectively by 1.8 V.

A dashed horizontal reference line is drawn at j = 10 mA cm⁻², a standard benchmark for comparing overpotentials, with an annotation "η10 = 452 mV" pointing to where the curves cross this line. No scale bar is present, as this is a graph/plot rather than a micrograph.
