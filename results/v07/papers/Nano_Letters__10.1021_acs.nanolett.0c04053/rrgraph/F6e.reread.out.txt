Panel E is a set of ex vivo fluorescence/near-infrared (NIR) imaging images comparing biodistribution of two nanoparticle formulations across excised organs. Two rows of six false-color heat-map images (dark background, rainbow intensity scale from 0 to 7500 at right) show signal in Liver, Kidney, Spleen, Lungs, Heart, and Tumor.
