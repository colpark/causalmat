This panel shows two stacked dynamic light scattering (DLS) histograms plotting size distribution by intensity. The x-axis is "Size (nm)" on a logarithmic scale from 100 to 10000 nm (shared by both subplots), and the y-axis is "Intensity (%)" from 0 to roughly 30-35%. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph; it is a plotted graph).

Top subplot (teal/cyan bars): a single, relatively narrow unimodal peak centered around 300-400 nm, with maximum intensity near 30-32%.

Bottom subplot (magenta/pink bars): a broader unimodal peak shifted to larger sizes, centered around 900-1000 nm, with maximum intensity around 22-25%.

Overall trend: the two distributions represent two different particle/aggregate populations of markedly different hydrodynamic size (roughly 300-400 nm vs. ~900-1000 nm by DLS intensity), each unimodal and reasonably narrow, with the pink/bottom population being both larger and slightly broader than the teal/top population.
