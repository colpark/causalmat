This is a line/scatter plot (not a micrograph) showing interdiffusion layer growth kinetics, plotting "layer thickness [µm]" (y-axis, 0–10 µm) against "annealing time √t [h^1/2]" (x-axis, 0–7 h^1/2), with a secondary top axis marking actual annealing times of 1h, 4h, 6h, 18h, and 48h. Three data series are shown with diamond markers and error bars, each fit with a linear trend line through near the origin: red = "Ni3Al into NiAl" (steepest slope, reaching ~8 µm at 48h), blue = "Al-enriched (Ni) into (Ni)" (intermediate slope, ~5 µm at 48h), and green = "Ni3Al into (Ni)" (shallowest slope, ~2 µm at 48h). All three series show an approximately linear increase in layer thickness with √t, consistent with diffusion-controlled (parabolic) growth kinetics, with error bars generally growing larger at longer times. There is no scale bar since this is a plotted graph, not an image with physical magnification.
