Panel (e) shows a Rietveld refinement plot of an X-ray diffraction (XRD) pattern. Red filled circles are the observed intensity data, the overlaid black line is the calculated fit, and green vertical tick marks below the peaks mark the expected Bragg reflection positions for the refined phase(s). A blue trace at the bottom represents the difference curve (observed minus calculated), which is nearly flat with small residual bumps, indicating a good fit between model and data. The x-axis is 2θ (degree), spanning roughly 10–90°, and the y-axis is intensity (arbitrary/unlabeled units, no scale bar as is typical for XRD plots). The pattern shows a dominant sharp peak near 2θ ≈ 31–32° along with numerous smaller peaks across the 20–90° range, consistent with a polycrystalline/powder sample; peak intensities decrease in a broadly irregular trend with increasing 2θ, and the dense clustering of green tick marks suggests multiple reflections or possibly more than one phase being fitted.
