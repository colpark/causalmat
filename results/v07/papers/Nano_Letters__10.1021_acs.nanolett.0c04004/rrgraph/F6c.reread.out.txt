Panel C shows a set of six stacked X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) spectra (Cu K-edge or L-edge XANES-type near-edge absorption profiles) plotted as Intensity (a.u., y-axis, arbitrary/offset scale) versus Photon energy (eV) on the x-axis, ranging from 920 to 970 eV. No scale bar is present (this is a spectroscopic plot, not a micrograph).

The six curves, offset vertically for clarity and color-coded, correspond to (top to bottom): La2CuO4 NBs (red), La2CuO4 NRs (green), bulk La2CuO4 (blue), CuO (gray), Cu2O (orange), and Cu foil (cyan). Each spectrum shows one or two absorption peaks/features near ~930-935 eV and ~950-955 eV (consistent with Cu L3 and L2 edges), with the peak shapes, intensities, and relative sharpness varying across samples — the La2CuO4 nanobelt (NBs) and nanorod (NRs) samples closely resemble bulk La2CuO4, while CuO, Cu2O, and Cu foil show progressively different peak shapes/positions reflecting different Cu oxidation states/chemical environments. The overall trend illustrates that the two nanostructured La2CuO4 samples (NBs, NRs) match the bulk La2CuO4 reference spectrum, distinguishing them from the Cu, Cu2O, and CuO reference standards.
