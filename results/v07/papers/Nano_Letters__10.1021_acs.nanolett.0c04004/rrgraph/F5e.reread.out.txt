This is a dual-axis, split time-course plot from an electrocatalysis stability/selectivity test, plotting current density j (mA cm⁻², left axis, range -10 to 10) and Faradaic efficiency FE (%, right axis, range 0 to 80) against time (h, 0–12) over an 11 h run. The plot is split into two stacked halves: the upper half (red) shows La₂CuO₄ NBs (nanobelts) with j staying nearly flat near 0 (slightly declining trace, drawn near y=−4 baseline) while FE toward C₂H₄ (red filled circles) stays roughly constant around 55–60% across all time points. The lower half (blue) shows bulk La₂CuO₄, with j also flat/stable near a baseline (~−7) and FE toward CO (blue filled circles) staying roughly constant around 70–75% throughout. No scale bar is present (this is a line/scatter chart, not a micrograph). The overall trend conveyed is long-term electrochemical stability for both catalysts: essentially constant current density and constant, high product-specific Faradaic efficiency (C₂H₄ for the nanobelt form, CO for the bulk form) sustained over 11 hours.
