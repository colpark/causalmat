The main difference among traces is a slight shift/change in the edge position and shoulder intensity: the black (in-situ, -1.0 V) and green (after reaction) curves appear to have a small edge shift to lower energy and altered post-edge oscillation amplitude relative to the red (before reaction) curve, consistent with a change in Cu oxidation state/local coordination during electrochemical reaction.
