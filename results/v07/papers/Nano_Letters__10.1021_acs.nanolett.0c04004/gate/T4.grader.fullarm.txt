Question: Panel F6d shows Cu K-edge EXAFS Fourier-transform spectra for the La2CuO4 nanobamboos, nanorods and bulk sample, plotted alongside Cu2O, CuO and Cu-foil references. What peak positions appear in common across the three La2CuO4 sample traces, and how do they compare in that region to the Cu-foil reference trace?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): All three La2CuO4 samples (nanobamboos, nanorods, bulk) show EXAFS Fourier-transform peaks near 1.5 A and near 3.0 A. The peak near 2.2 A that the Cu-foil reference shows (the Cu-Cu peak) is absent from all three La2CuO4 sample traces.

In panel F6d (Cu K-edge EXAFS Fourier-transform, /home/aid1/Documents/causalmat/matmech/Nano_Letters/10.1021_acs.nanolett.0c04004/panels/crops/0796412a23d1fb4a81c8328bd0ec4c58a640cc33a11bdd21adcf1deb2ad00409_D.jpg), the three La2CuO4 traces (NBs-red, NRs-green, bulk-blue) share two common peak positions: a smaller peak near ~1.3-1.4 Å and a larger, more intense peak near ~3.0-3.1 Å radial distance. These two peaks are common to all three La2CuO4 samples (with bulk showing the highest intensity, NBs intermediate, NRs lowest, but at essentially the same radial-distance positions).

In contrast, the Cu-foil reference (cyan) trace shows its dominant peak shifted to a distinctly different radial distance, around ~2.2 Å, with an additional shoulder/second peak near ~4.2-4.5 Å — clearly offset from both of the La2CuO4 peak positions (~1.3 Å and ~3.0 Å). So in the region where the La2CuO4 samples peak (~1.3 and ~3.0 Å), the Cu-foil trace is comparatively low/flat, and Cu foil's own characteristic peak (metallic Cu-Cu scattering) lies at a longer radial distance than the second (~3.0 Å) La2CuO4 peak, indicating the Cu local coordination in La2CuO4 (NBs, NRs, bulk) is distinct from metallic Cu foil.
