This is a two-part composite figure (labeled (c) and (d)), not a microscopy image — no scale bar is present.

Panel (c): A schematic diagram (illustration, not an imaging technique) comparing two polymer electrolyte architectures for Li-ion transport. Left box, titled "Crystalline polymer with inorganic fillers," shows blue wavy lines (crystalline domains) interspersed with brown coiled regions (amorphous domains) and black dots (inorganic filler particles); a green arrow labeled "Li+ easy" curves around the crystalline regions while a red arrow labeled "Li+ hard" with an X shows Li+ blocked when trying to cross crystalline domains directly. Right box, titled "Hybrid polymer with fillers," shows a more homogeneous mesh of brown amorphous chains interconnected by red branched "Y-type oligomer" linkages and black filler dots, with a straight green arrow labeled "Li+ easy" passing directly through, indicating unimpeded ion transport. A legend at the bottom defines the four symbols: crystalline domain (blue stripes), amorphous domain (brown coil), inorganic filler (black dot), and Y-type oligomer (red branch).

Panel (d): A line/scatter plot of Specific Capacity (mAh g⁻¹, y-axis, 0–200) versus Cycle Number (x-axis, 0–25) for lithium battery cells under stepped C-rates (0.2C, 0.5C, 1C, 2C, 1C, 0.2C). Three data series are shown: red circles = LFP/15PBA-Li/Li at 30°C, blue circles = LFP/15PBA-Li/Li at 45°C, and purple circles = LFP/PA-Li/Li at 30°C, with grey circles apparently marking a reference/overlay trace at rate transitions. Both PBA-containing cells (red, blue) show a stepwise capacity decline as C-rate increases (from ~145–165 down to ~70–100 mAh g⁻¹ at 2C) and largely recover to near-initial values when the rate returns to 0.2C, with the 45°C (blue) cell consistently outperforming the 30°C (red) cell. The PA-only cell (purple) shows markedly lower and more degraded capacity throughout (roughly 10–40 mAh g⁻¹), indicating poor rate capability compared to the PBA-hybrid electrolyte.
