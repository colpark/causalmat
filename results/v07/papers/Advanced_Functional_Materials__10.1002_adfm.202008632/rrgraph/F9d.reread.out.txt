Panel (d) is a dual-axis line/scatter plot of battery cycling performance versus cycle number (1–100). The left y-axis shows discharge capacity (mAh g⁻¹, 0–200) and the right y-axis shows Coulombic efficiency (%, 0–120), both plotted against cycle number on the x-axis (0–100). Three data series (large filled circles connected by lines) compare electrolyte/electrode systems: PEO-SN-LiTFSI (blue), DSM-SPE (dark red), and PEO-LiTFSI (green). The blue PEO-SN-LiTFSI series shows steep, near-linear capacity fade from ~135 to below 40 mAh g⁻¹, failing entirely by roughly cycle 45–47. The green PEO-LiTFSI series starts near ~150 mAh g⁻¹ and declines gradually with step-like drops (around cycles 55–60 and 80) down to ~60 mAh g⁻¹ by cycle 100. The dark red DSM-SPE series is by far the most stable, maintaining a capacity plateau near 140–170 mAh g⁻¹ with only slight gradual decline and high, stable Coulombic efficiency (~95–100%, indicated by the arrow pointing to the right axis) throughout all 100 cycles, indicating superior cycling stability of the DSM-SPE system relative to the other two.
