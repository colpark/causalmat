Candidate answer:
Panel (a) shows stacked X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns, plotted as intensity (a.u.) versus 2θ (°) from about 5° to 70°, no scale bar (not applicable to XRD plots). Three traces are shown, offset vertically for clarity, bottom to top: "MF POMs" (green), "MXenes" (purple), and "MF POMs/MXenes" (pink/magenta) composite. Peaks belonging to MXenes are marked with purple dots and peaks belonging to MF POMs are marked with green diamonds, per the legend at top left. The MF POMs trace alone shows numerous sharp, well-resolved diffraction peaks across the range (strongest near 2θ ≈ 26°), the MXenes trace alone shows fewer, broader/weaker peaks (a prominent one near low angle ~6-7° and another near ~26°), and the composite MF POMs/MXenes trace at top retains essentially all the sharp MF POM peaks together with the marked MXene peaks superimposed, indicating both phases coexist in the hybrid material without loss of crystallinity of either component.
