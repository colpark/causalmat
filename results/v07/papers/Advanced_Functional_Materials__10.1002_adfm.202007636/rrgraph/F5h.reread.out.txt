This is a line/scatter plot, not a micrograph — no imaging technique or scale bar is present. The x-axis is "Mass loading (mg cm⁻²)" with data points at roughly 0.5, 1.5, 3.0, 4.6, and 6.0 mg cm⁻². The left y-axis is "Mass capacity (mA h g⁻¹)" (0–300+ range, green squares/line) and the right y-axis is "Area capacity (mA h cm⁻²)" (0–1.5 range, magenta/pink circles/line), both measured at a fixed current density of 1.0 A g⁻¹ (noted in-panel). The green mass-capacity curve decreases monotonically from about 270 down to about 200 mA h g⁻¹ as mass loading increases, while the magenta area-capacity curve increases monotonically and roughly linearly from about 0.15 up to about 1.4 mA h cm⁻² over the same mass-loading range. The two curves cross near a mass loading of about 3.5 mg cm⁻², illustrating the typical tradeoff between gravimetric (mass) capacity and areal capacity as electrode mass loading increases — consistent with a battery/electrode-performance figure.
