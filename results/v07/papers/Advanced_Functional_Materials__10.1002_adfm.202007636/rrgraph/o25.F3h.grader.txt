This is a line/scatter plot (not a micrograph — no scale bar present) showing electrochemical performance versus mass loading of an electrode, tested at a fixed current density of 1.0 A g⁻¹. The x-axis is "Mass loading (mg cm⁻²)" ranging from about 0 to 6.0. Two y-axes are used: a left axis "Mass capacity (mA h g⁻¹)" (0–600+) plotted in green circles, and a right axis "Area capacity (mA h cm⁻²)" (0–2.4) plotted in magenta/purple squares, each with an arrow indicating which axis it corresponds to. The green mass-capacity curve declines gradually from roughly 500 mA h g⁻¹ at low loading (~0.3 mg cm⁻²) to about 430 mA h g⁻¹ at 6.0 mg cm⁻², showing a slight downward trend as loading increases. The magenta area-capacity curve shows the opposite/rising trend, increasing roughly linearly from about 0.25 mA h cm⁻² at low loading to about 2.2 mA h cm⁻² at 6.0 mg cm⁻², with the two curves crossing near a mass loading of about 4 mg cm⁻².
