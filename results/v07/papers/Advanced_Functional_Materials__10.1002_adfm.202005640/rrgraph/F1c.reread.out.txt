Bottom plot: a reference stick pattern (black vertical lines, presumably a standard/database reference pattern) showing the full set of expected FCC reflections — (111), (200), (220), (311), (222), (400), (331), (420) — plotted at their expected 2θ positions with varying relative stick heights, used for comparison/indexing against the measured pattern above.
