This is a Nyquist plot (electrochemical impedance spectroscopy) showing -Z'' (kΩ) versus Z' (kΩ), with both axes ranging roughly 0–200 kΩ (Z') and 0 to -200 kΩ (-Z''). Six data series are plotted as semicircular arcs, each corresponding to a different temperature: 50°C, 60°C, 70°C, 80°C, 90°C, and 100°C, color-coded (orange, green, yellow, blue, gray, and a lighter shade respectively) and labeled directly on the curves. An inset table gives peak frequency (ω_peak) values increasing with temperature (50°C → 15 kHz up to 100°C → 59 kHz). A circuit-diagram inset (equivalent circuit model with R_s in series with two parallel CPE/R sub-circuits, plus a CPE_d element) is shown at upper right. The clear trend is that arc diameter (impedance magnitude) decreases systematically and peak frequency increases as temperature rises from 50°C to 100°C, indicating decreasing resistance/faster relaxation with increasing temperature. No scale bar is present (not a micrograph); this is a plotted electrical/impedance dataset, not an imaging technique.
