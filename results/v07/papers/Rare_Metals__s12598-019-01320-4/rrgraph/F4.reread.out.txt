This is a line/scatter plot, not a micrograph — no imaging technique or scale bar is present. Two data series are plotted: black squares labeled "This work" (experimental grain-size measurements) and red circles labeled "Modified Z-S model" (predicted values from a modified Zener-Smith grain-growth model), each with its own linear fit line (solid brownish-red line for "This work," dashed blue line for the model). Both series show a clear positive linear trend of average grain size increasing with t^(1/3), consistent with expected grain-growth kinetics, and the two datasets track each other closely across most of the range (roughly 5 μm at t^(1/3)≈14 up to ~30–40 μm at t^(1/3)≈24), with the "This work" fit line ending slightly higher (~40 μm) than the model fit (~32 μm) at the largest t^(1/3) value, indicating good but imperfect agreement between measured and modeled grain growth.
