Candidate answer:
In panel F5b, a thin, lighter-gray halo/shell is visible wrapping the outer surface of the dark Au nanorod core, and it is labeled "PEG-thiol" with an arrow pointing to this coating layer. This coating appears as a faint, low-contrast rim running along the top and bottom edges of the rod, distinct from the dense black nanorod interior. Relative to the 10 nm scale bar shown in the lower left, the coating layer is quite thin — visually only a small fraction of the bar's length, roughly on the order of 1–2 nm (i.e., well under one-fifth of the 10 nm scale bar), consistent with a thin molecular (PEG-thiol) functionalization layer rather than a substantial shell.
