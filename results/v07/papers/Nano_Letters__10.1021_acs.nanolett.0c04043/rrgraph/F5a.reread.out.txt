This is a nano-FTIR absorption spectrum (infrared nanospectroscopy, likely AFM-IR or scattering-type near-field optical microscopy, s-SNOM) plotted as Nano-FTIR Absorption (a.u., y-axis, no scale marks) versus Wavenumber (cm⁻¹, x-axis, ranging from 1400 down to ~980, decreasing left to right). No spatial scale bar is present since this is a spectral plot, not a real-space image. An inset schematic shows the chemical structure of PEG-thiol (HS-CH2CH2-O-(CH2CH2O)n-CH3), the molecule whose vibrational signature is being probed.

The spectrum shows a single blue curve with three labeled/shaded peak regions (highlighted in light orange bands): a broad "C-H bend" peak near ~1350 cm⁻¹, a smaller "C-O stretch" shoulder near ~1250 cm⁻¹, and a large, dominant "C-O stretch" peak near ~1100–1120 cm⁻¹ that is markedly taller and broader than the other two features. Baseline absorption is low and relatively flat outside these three peak regions, with the curve dipping to local minima between peaks and at the low-wavenumber end (~1000–980 cm⁻¹). The overall trend is a stepwise increase in peak intensity from the C-H bend feature to the two C-O stretch features, culminating in the strongest, broadest absorption band at the lowest wavenumbers shown, consistent with characteristic C-O and C-H vibrational modes of the PEG backbone.
