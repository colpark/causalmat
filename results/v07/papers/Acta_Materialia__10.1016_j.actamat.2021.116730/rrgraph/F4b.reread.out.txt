Panel (b) is a mechanical tensile test plot showing engineering stress (MPa, y-axis, 0 to ~1.4) versus nominal strain (dimensionless, x-axis, 0 to 1.0). It is a line graph, not a micrograph, so there is no scale bar or imaging technique involved. Four stress-strain curves are overlaid, color-coded by treatment/condition duration per the legend: 0 min (blue), 1 min (red), 10 min (green), and 60 min (orange).

All four curves rise steeply from the origin to a peak stress of roughly 1.2–1.4 MPa at very low strain (~0.03–0.05), with the 60 min (orange) curve reaching the highest peak (~1.4 MPa). After the initial peak, all curves decline gradually and nearly overlap through the strain range ~0.1–0.6, indicating similar post-yield softening behavior across conditions. Beyond strain ~0.6 the curves diverge in failure/elongation behavior: the 0 min (blue) and 10 min (green) curves drop to zero stress earliest (around strain 0.65–0.75), while the 1 min (red) and 60 min (orange) curves extend further, failing at higher strains (~0.85–0.95), suggesting the 1 min and 60 min treatments produce greater ductility/elongation at break than the 0 min and 10 min conditions.
