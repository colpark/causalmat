Trends: Both samples show emission bands in the green region (~520–570 nm, appearing as a split/multi-peak feature with two sub-peaks around 525 nm and 550 nm) and a smaller red band (~640–680 nm, peaking near 660 nm), consistent with characteristic Er3+ upconversion/emission transitions (green: 2H11/2,4S3/2→4I15/2; red: 4F9/2→4I15/2). The Er0.5Ti-O sample (teal) shows much higher intensity than Er0.75Ti-O (yellow) across both bands — the green peak reaches ~1.2×10^5 a.u. for Er0.5Ti-O versus only ~2×10^4 a.u. for Er0.75Ti-O, and similarly the red peak is markedly stronger for Er0.5Ti-O (~1.5×10^4) than Er0.75Ti-O (~0.5×10^4). This indicates emission intensity decreases with increasing Er doping from 0.5 to 0.75 (likely concentration quenching), while the spectral peak positions/shapes remain similar between the two compositions.
