X-axis: Time (s), 0–~300 s; Y-axis: Current (A/cm²), 0 to 8.0×10⁻⁶, with square-wave "Light on"/"Light off" cycles labeled (period roughly 40 s), showing repeated on/off photoresponse over about eight cycles. The Er0.5Ce0.2Ti-O sample consistently shows higher current amplitude (both peak and trough) than plain TiO2 across all cycles, indicating enhanced photoelectrochemical response from the doped material; no scale bar is present since this is a graph, not a micrograph.
