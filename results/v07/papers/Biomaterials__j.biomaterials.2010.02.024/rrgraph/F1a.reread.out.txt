Panel (a) is a scatter/line plot, not a micrograph technique per se, but it contains an inset micrograph.

Main plot: x-axis "ionic strength [M]" from 0 to 1.5, y-axis "salting out efficiency [%]" from 0 to 100. Filled black circles with error bars (vertical, small) show salting out efficiency rising monotonically with ionic strength: from ~19% at ~0.15 M, to ~52% at ~0.33 M, ~75% at ~0.5 M, ~84% at ~0.65 M, then a jump to ~86% at ~0.83 M, ~89% at ~1.0 M, and ~93% at ~1.25 M — a saturating increasing trend (steep rise at low ionic strength, plateauing near 90% at high ionic strength).

A vertical dotted line at ~0.75 M divides the plot into two labeled regimes with horizontal gray arrows: "gelation" (pointing left, ionic strength < ~0.75 M) and "particle formation" (pointing right, ionic strength > ~0.75 M).

Inset (bottom right of plot, within the particle-formation region): a grayscale micrograph (likely optical or SEM) showing numerous small spherical particles clustered together, with a scale bar labeled "5 µm," illustrating the discrete particle morphology formed at higher ionic strength.
