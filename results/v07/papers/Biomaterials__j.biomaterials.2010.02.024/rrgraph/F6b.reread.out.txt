This is a scatter/line-free XY plot (panel "b" of a larger figure), not a microscopy image — it shows quantitative data rather than an imaging technique. The x-axis is "molar ratio (MD:SF)" ranging from 0 to 100 (data points at approximately 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 60, 100), and the y-axis is "loading [%]" ranging from 0 to 16. Three dye/marker series are plotted with distinct symbols: filled circles = Crystal violet, open triangles = Alcian blue, filled diamonds = Rhodamin B, each with vertical error bars. All three series show an increasing trend in loading (%) as the molar ratio increases, with Alcian blue consistently showing the highest loading values across the range (rising from ~1.5% at ratio 5 to ~13% at ratio 100), while Crystal violet and Rhodamin B track more closely together at lower values (rising from under 1% to roughly 5-7% at ratio 100). There is no scale bar since this is a data plot, not a micrograph.
