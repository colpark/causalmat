Panel F4d shows a bright-field TEM image of the FCC lamella at 1.5% strain (scale bar 0.2 μm). The dislocations no longer appear as isolated, sparse planar-slip traces as at 0.6% strain; instead they form a denser, interconnected network of long, curved/wavy dislocation lines that cross and intersect one another at multiple points across the field of view, consistent with the caption's mention of "intersecting dislocation." The lines run in several different orientations rather than a single planar-slip direction, indicating that multiple slip systems have become active and dislocations are interacting/tangling with each other, producing a web-like or criss-crossing substructure within the FCC lamella rather than the simple, well-separated planar slip bands seen at the lower (0.6%) strain level.
