Answer: The F3a panel (TEM at 0.6% strain) is consistent with and provides supporting evidence for one part of the claim — it shows dislocation pileup occurring at the FCC(L12)/B2 phase boundaries early in tensile deformation, along with dislocations/planar slip confined to FCC lamellae while B2 stays dislocation-free. This corroborates the described microstructural observation of boundary pile-up. However, the panel alone (a single static TEM micrograph) cannot establish the full causal claim that this pile-up is specifically "driven by lattice-mismatch internal stress" or that it quantitatively "raises the yield strength" — those are mechanistic/quantitative inferences that would require additional evidence (e.g., stress-strain curves, lattice-mismatch calculations, or comparisons across strain levels/phases) beyond what one TEM image can show. So the panel partially supports the claim (the pile-up observation) but does not by itself substantiate the causal link to yield strength.
