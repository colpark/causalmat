Panel F3a shows a bright-field TEM image at 0.6% tensile strain containing a single lamellar boundary running diagonally across the field, separating an upper-left region (darker/mottled contrast, consistent with one phase, likely B2) from a lower-right lamella (lighter, more uniformly granular contrast, likely the FCC/L12 matrix). Along the upper edge of this interphase boundary, a row of short, closely spaced parallel dark tick-marks/striations is visible — this is the dislocation pileup referenced in the caption, indicating dislocations have accumulated against (and are blocked by) the lamellar boundary at this early strain stage. No dislocations or slip traces are yet visible penetrating into the lower lamella, consistent with the boundary acting as a barrier to slip transmission between the two phases at 0.6% strain.
