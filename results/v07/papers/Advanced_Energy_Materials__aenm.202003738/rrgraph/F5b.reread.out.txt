This is panel (b), a voltage-vs-capacity plot for an Al2O3-coated NCM523 || Graphite full cell (galvanostatic cycling technique, not microscopy — no scale bar present). The x-axis is "Specific capacity / mAh g⁻¹" (0–500) and the y-axis is "Cell voltage / V" (2.5–5.0), showing charge/discharge curves at cycle no. 85 for two electrolytes: 1.0 M LiPF6 in EC:EMC (3:7 by wt.) (solid dark curve) and in EMC alone (dashed blue curve). Both traces rise steeply on charge to a plateau near 4.4–4.5 V before discharging; the EC:EMC cell shows a charge capacity around 150–170 mAh g⁻¹ before hitting a voltage plateau, while the EMC-only cell charges to a noticeably higher capacity (~380 mAh g⁻¹) before its plateau. An arrow labeled "Voltage noise" points to an irregular, jagged/fluctuating segment on the charge plateau near 4.5 V (most visible on the solid EC:EMC curve), indicating unstable voltage response during that region. On discharge, the EMC-only cell (dashed) delivers substantially more capacity (extending to ~520 mAh g⁻¹) than the EC:EMC cell, whose discharge curve is truncated/not fully shown beyond the charge plateau in this crop.
