Candidate answer:
Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image — it shows cycling performance of NCM523||Graphite battery cells at 4.5 V. The x-axis is "Cycle no." (0–100) and the y-axis is "Specific capacity / mAh g⁻¹" (0–200). Two electrolyte conditions are compared: 1.0 M LiPF6 in EC:EMC (3:7 by wt.) alone (dark gray/black circles with large error bars) and the same electrolyte plus 1 wt.% LiDFP additive (green circles, small error bars). The green (LiDFP-added) curve stays high and stable, declining only slightly from ~185 to ~170 mAh g⁻¹ over 100 cycles with tight error bars (good reproducibility). The black (baseline) curve tracks closely with the green curve until about cycle 45–50, then drops sharply to a minimum near ~60 mAh g⁻¹ around cycle 85–90 (with very large error bars indicating poor cell-to-cell reproducibility/failure), followed by a partial, noisy recovery to ~80–85 mAh g⁻¹ by cycle 100. No scale bar is present since this is a graph, not a micrograph. Overall the panel demonstrates that the LiDFP additive substantially improves capacity retention and cycling stability compared to the baseline electrolyte.
