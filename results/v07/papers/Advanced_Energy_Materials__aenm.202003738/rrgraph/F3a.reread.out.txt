Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot titled "NCM523||Graphite" with an inset label "4.5 V", from a battery cycling study (no scale bar, not a microscopy image). X-axis: Cycle no. (0–100); Y-axis: Specific capacity / mAh g⁻¹ (0–200). Two datasets are plotted with 1.0 M LiPF6 electrolyte: black circles with error bars for "EC:EMC (3:7 by wt.)" and blue/purple circles for "EMC" (solvent alone), per the legend inside the plot.

Trends: both curves start near ~185–195 mAh g⁻¹ over the first ~10 cycles, then converge and track closely together (~165–170 mAh g⁻¹) through roughly cycle 40. After that, the two series diverge sharply — the blue "EMC" curve declines only gradually and smoothly, ending around ~155–160 mAh g⁻¹ at cycle 100, while the black "EC:EMC" curve drops steeply starting around cycle 45–50, reaching a minimum of roughly 55–60 mAh g⁻¹ near cycle 85, then partially recovers to about 80–85 mAh g⁻¹ by cycle 100. The black curve also carries large, widening error bars (vertical whiskers) especially in the decline/recovery region (cycles 60–100), indicating substantial cell-to-cell variability, whereas the blue curve's error bars are small/tight throughout, indicating much more reproducible/stable cycling behavior with the EMC-only electrolyte.
