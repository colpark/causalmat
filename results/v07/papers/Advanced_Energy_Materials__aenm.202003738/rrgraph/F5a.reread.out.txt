Panel (a): a battery cycling/electrochemical performance plot, not a microscopy image. Title reads "Al2O3-NCM523||Graphite" tested at a 4.5 V cutoff. X-axis is "Cycle no." (0–100), y-axis is "Specific capacity / mAh g-1" (0–400).

Two data series are plotted: (1) cells with 1.0 M LiPF6 in EC:EMC (3:7 by wt.) electrolyte, shown as black up-triangles (Charge) and down-triangles (Discharge); (2) cells with 1.0 M LiPF6 in EMC electrolyte, shown as blue/purple up-triangles (Charge) and open down-triangles (Discharge). The blue (LiPF6 in EMC) series stays smooth and stable, declining gradually from ~200 to ~150–170 mAh g-1 over 100 cycles with charge/discharge closely overlapping (high coulombic efficiency). The black (EC:EMC) series is stable and low (~180→130 mAh g-1) until roughly cycle 65, after which the charge capacity becomes erratic and spikes sharply upward (values scattering between ~250–400 mAh g-1 through cycle ~90), indicating an onset of instability/parasitic reactions or cell failure at high voltage for that electrolyte, while discharge values in that regime are not clearly distinguishable from the noise. There is no scale bar since this is a graph, not a micrograph.
