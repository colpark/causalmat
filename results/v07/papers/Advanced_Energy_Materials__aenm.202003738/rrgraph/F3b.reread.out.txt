Panel (b) is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image, so there is no scale bar or imaging technique involved. It shows electrochemical cycling data for an "Al2O3-NCM523 || Graphite" full cell tested at 4.5 V cutoff. The x-axis is "Cycle no." (0–100) and the y-axis is "Specific capacity / mAh g⁻¹" (0–200). Two data series with error bars are plotted for different electrolytes: black circles for "1.0 M LiPF6 in EC:EMC (3:7 by wt.)" and blue circles for "EMC" (presumably also 1.0 M LiPF6 in EMC solvent). Both start near ~185–190 mAh g⁻¹ and stay close together (~175–180 mAh g⁻¹) through about cycle 60; after that the black (EC:EMC) series declines steadily and with growing error bars to about 125 mAh g⁻¹ by cycle 100, while the blue (EMC) series degrades much more gradually, ending near 170 mAh g⁻¹, indicating better capacity retention with the EMC-only electrolyte at this high voltage.
