Candidate answer:
SEM appearance differs between the two conditions. In panel F4a (EC-based electrolyte), the graphite surface shows a mottled, patchy texture with several lighter, raised lump/agglomerate regions scattered across the field; these patches spatially coincide with the localized EDX hot spots (F, Co, Mn, Ni signals clustered in the same blob-shaped areas), consistent with deposited transition-metal/Li-dendrite material sitting on the graphite. In panel F4b (EC-free electrolyte), the graphite surface instead looks like a more uniform, fine-grained texture without any distinct lumps or patchy regions, and the corresponding EDX maps show F spread evenly while Co, Mn, and Ni are essentially absent (no localized clusters). So the two conditions do not look the same: EC-based graphite exhibits visible patchy surface deposits/aggregates, while EC-free graphite shows a more uniform surface lacking those features.
