Panel F4a (EC-based, 1.0 M LiPF6 in EC:EMC 3:7) and F4b (EC-free, 1.0 M LiPF6 in EMC) look different, not the same.

In F4a, the F, Co, Mn, and Ni EDX maps all show distinct bright clustered/blob-shaped regions that spatially coincide with one another and with darker mottled regions in the SEM image — i.e., localized co-located deposits of F together with Co, Mn, and Ni at the same spots.

In F4b, the EDX F map shows a diffuse, speckled signal spread fairly uniformly across the field with no distinct clusters; the Mn map similarly shows diffuse scattered speckling with no localized clusters; and the Co and Ni maps show essentially no signal (dark/empty), i.e. no detectable Co or Ni at all.

So the two conditions differ: EC-based anodes show co-located, clustered F/Co/Mn/Ni deposits at discrete spots, while EC-free anodes show only diffuse (non-clustered) F and Mn signal and no visible Co or Ni signal anywhere on the surface.
