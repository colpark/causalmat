The two panels show visibly different graphite anode surfaces between the two electrolyte conditions. In the EC-based electrolyte (F4a), the cycled graphite surface shows Li dendrites along with accumulated transition metals (TMs), with the dendrites and TM deposits co-located at the same spots. In the EC-free electrolyte (F4b), these features are suppressed — the Li dendrites are largely absent, and the co-located spots of TM accumulation and Li dendrites seen in the EC-based case cannot be observed. So the surfaces differ: EC-based shows dendrite/TM deposits, EC-free shows a comparatively cleaner surface without those co-located features.
