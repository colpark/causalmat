This is panel (f) of a multi-panel figure, showing an X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) high-resolution spectrum of the C 1s region, labeled "C1s" in the upper right. The x-axis is Binding Energy (eV) from 295 to 275 (decreasing left to right), and the y-axis is Intensity (a.u.) from 3000 to 7000. The raw spectrum (black jagged line) is fit with a flat magenta background/baseline and two deconvoluted peaks: a larger dark-green/teal peak centered at 284.60 eV labeled "C-C," and a smaller red peak centered at 283.00 eV labeled "N-C," with an overall fitted envelope (blue) matching the raw data. No scale bar is present (this is a spectral plot, not a micrograph). The dominant C-C peak indicates typical adventitious/graphitic carbon, while the smaller N-C shoulder suggests nitrogen-carbon bonding in the sample, consistent with a doped or nitrogen-containing material.
