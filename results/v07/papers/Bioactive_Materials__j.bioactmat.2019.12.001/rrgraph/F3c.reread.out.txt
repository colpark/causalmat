Panel (c), labeled "Ta4f + O2s": an X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) spectrum plotting Intensity (a.u., y-axis 0–12000) versus Binding Energy (eV, x-axis 35–15, plotted decreasing left to right). The raw spectrum (black) is deconvoluted into fitted component peaks (a linear/curved background baseline, plus doublet peaks in magenta and teal, with an overall fit envelope in red overlaid on the raw data). Two dominant peaks are labeled at 27.28 eV and 25.36 eV and attributed to Ta2O5 (annotated with an arrow pointing to the peak pair), with a much smaller peak/shoulder near 21.07 eV assigned to N-Ta. No scale bar is present (not a micrograph; this is a spectral plot). The overall trend shows the Ta2O5-related doublet as the major intensity contribution (~9000 counts peak), while the N-Ta component near 21 eV is a low-intensity feature only slightly above the background baseline.
