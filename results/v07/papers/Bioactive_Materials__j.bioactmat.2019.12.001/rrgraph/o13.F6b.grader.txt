Panel (b) is a line/scatter plot of hydrogen evolution rate (mL·cm⁻²·h⁻¹, y-axis, 0 to 0.030) versus immersion time (h, x-axis, 0 to ~50), for three magnesium-alloy sample conditions: AZ31 (black squares), MAO (red circles), and MAO/Ta2O5 (blue triangles), each with visible vertical error bars. Overall trend: the coated samples (MAO, and especially MAO/Ta2O5) evolve hydrogen much more slowly than bare AZ31, indicating improved corrosion resistance from the coatings, with MAO/Ta2O5 performing best.
