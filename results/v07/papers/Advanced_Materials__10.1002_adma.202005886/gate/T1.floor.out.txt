No, panel F2c as described does not by itself support the claim that irradiated zones contain crystalline silicon grains. The caption for F2c only reports how Raman signal intensity at 578 cm⁻¹ and PL intensity at 1530 nm change with laser fluence — a trend/intensity-vs-fluence plot, not a spectral fingerprint. Crystalline silicon's diagnostic Raman signature is the sharp phonon peak near ~520 cm⁻¹ (with amorphous Si giving a broad hump near ~480 cm⁻¹); 578 cm⁻¹ is not the standard crystalline-Si Raman shift, so this panel's chosen peak position does not itself establish crystallinity. Identifying crystalline Si grains would require showing the actual Raman spectrum (peak position/shape, as presumably shown in the referenced panels F2a/b) rather than just an intensity-vs-fluence dependency curve. Based solely on the text given, F2c demonstrates a fluence-dependent signal trend, not direct evidence of crystalline silicon.
