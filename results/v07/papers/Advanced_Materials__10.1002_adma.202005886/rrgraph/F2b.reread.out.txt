Panel (b) is a photoluminescence (PL) spectroscopy plot, not a micrograph. X-axis: Wavelength (nm), spanning roughly 1420–1620 nm. Y-axis: PL Intensity (counts), from 0 to about 2500. No scale bar is present (it is a spectrum, not an image).

Three fluence-dependent spectra are overlaid with shaded fill under each curve: green (1.0 mJ/cm²), blue (3.8 mJ/cm²), and magenta/pink (8.9 mJ/cm²). All three curves share a broad, somewhat noisy emission band centered near 1530 nm with a sharper peak there and several smaller shoulder features (fine substructure/ripples) extending out to about 1600 nm, plus a smaller shoulder near 1550 nm. As excitation fluence increases from 1.0 to 8.9 mJ/cm², the overall PL intensity increases substantially (peak rising from roughly 850 counts at low fluence to about 2500 counts at high fluence), while the spectral shape/peak position stays largely similar, indicating a monotonic fluence-dependent enhancement of emission intensity rather than a spectral shift.
