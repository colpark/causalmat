Panel (a): X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) survey spectra comparing two samples, "HA" (black curve) and "Si-HA" (red curve), plotted as intensity vs. binding energy (0–1300+ eV, x-axis in eV). Labeled peaks include P 2p, P 2s, Si 2p, Si 2s, C 1s, Ca 2p, Ca 2s, and O 1s (the dominant, tallest peak near ~530 eV for both curves); the Si-HA curve additionally shows distinct Si 2p and Si 2s peaks at low binding energy (~100–150 eV) that are essentially absent in the HA curve. An inset table gives the elemental composition in atomic % for C, O, P, Ca, and Si: HA = 19.1, 49.9, 12.5, 18.5, 0; Si-HA = 23.3, 47.2, 10.4, 14.2, 4.9 — showing Si substitution reduces P and Ca content while introducing measurable Si. No scale bar (not a micrograph); curves are vertically offset for clarity with the Si-HA trace stacked above the HA trace. Overall trend: Si incorporation into hydroxyapatite (Si-HA) is confirmed by the appearance of Si 2p/2s peaks and a corresponding decrease in Ca and P atomic percentages relative to undoped HA.
