It plots "Relative mRNA levels of CTGF" (y-axis, 0-3) against "Incubation time (Days)" on the x-axis, with two time points: Day 3 and Day 7. Three grouped bars per time point compare three sample conditions: Ti (gray), HA (red), and Si-HA (blue), each with error bars (SEM/SD whiskers). At Day 7, values rise for all groups and diverge more: Ti ~1.55, HA ~2.0 (**), Si-HA ~2.4, with Si-HA also marked ## (indicating significance vs HA).
