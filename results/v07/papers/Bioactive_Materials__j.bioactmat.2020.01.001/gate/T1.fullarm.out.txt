Panels F1a and F1b show no visible difference in nanorod morphology between the bare HA-coated and Si-HA-coated Ti surfaces. Both images display dense clusters of vertically-oriented, hexagonal-faceted nanorods with rounded tips, of comparable diameter (~70 nm scale) and inter-rod spacing, packed similarly at the same 100 nm scale bar. The rods in both panels appear equally straight, densely packed, and randomly oriented with overlapping bundles - no qualitative change in rod shape, faceting, diameter, or spacing is discernible between (a) and (b). The only distinguishing feature visible is in panel (b)'s inset EDS/low-mag overlay noting "Si 3.11 Wt%," which confirms Si incorporation but does not itself reveal a morphological difference at the nanorod scale. Based on these top-view SEM crops alone, the Si substitution does not produce an observable change in nanorod surface morphology.
