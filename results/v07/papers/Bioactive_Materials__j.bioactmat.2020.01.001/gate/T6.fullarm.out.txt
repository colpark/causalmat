Panels 8b-8d partially support the claim but not uniformly at every time point.

At day 7, all three panels show the claimed ranking Si-HA > HA > Ti with statistical markers: Si-HA is marked ** (vs Ti) and ## (vs HA) in every panel (CTGF ~2.4 vs 2.0 vs 1.55; alpha-SMA ~2.0 vs 1.6 vs 1.25; Col-I ~2.0 vs 1.75 vs 1.35), so the ordering and significance hold clearly at this later time point.

At day 3, the picture is weaker: in CTGF (b) and Col-I (d), HA and Si-HA are both marked ** vs Ti but are essentially tied with each other (no ## marker), so Si-HA is not shown as significantly higher than HA at day 3. In alpha-SMA (c) at day 3, none of the groups carry significance markers at all, and HA is numerically slightly higher than Si-HA, contradicting the stated ranking.

So the "same ranking at every time point" claim is only supported at day 7; at day 3 Si-HA does not show a clear or significant advantage over HA, and for alpha-SMA there is no significant elevation over Ti at day 3 either.
