Panel B shows X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns comparing four samples, labeled "As grown." The x-axis is 2-theta (degrees) from 10 to 45, and the y-axis is Intensity (a.u.), with no numerical intensity scale or scale bar (not applicable to XRD plots). Four stacked traces are shown, offset vertically for clarity: SC (single crystal, blue) at top, HOC (red) below it, PC (polycrystalline, black) below that, and a Theoretical pattern (green) at the bottom.

The SC trace shows only two sharp, isolated peaks (near 2θ ≈ 20° and a strong peak near 41°), consistent with a highly oriented single crystal exhibiting few allowed reflections. The HOC trace also shows a dominant single sharp peak near 41° with minimal other features, indicating a highly oriented/textured sample similar to but slightly less clean than SC. In contrast, the PC trace displays multiple peaks (prominent ones near 14° and 29°, plus several smaller peaks across the range), reflecting a polycrystalline sample with multiple crystallographic orientations present. The Theoretical pattern at the bottom shows the richest set of peaks spanning the full 10–45° range, serving as a reference simulated diffraction pattern; several of its peak positions (e.g., near 14°, 20°, 23–25°, 29°, 31–33°, 41–43°) align with peaks seen in the PC, SC, and HOC traces, supporting phase identification of the experimental samples against the theoretical structure.
