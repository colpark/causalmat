Question: Panel F3a presents XAS spectra for W foil, WO2, and Ni-WO2. Based on that panel, how does the near-edge absorption peak intensity compare across the three samples?
state the difference between the two conditions; do not rank

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): The W L3 white-line intensity is highest for Ni-WO2, next highest for WO2, and lowest for W foil -- i.e. Ni-WO2 > WO2 > W foil (o10).

Grading note: answer key: the numbers and labels on the evidence node o10, judge-confirmed

Candidate answer:
In panel F3a, the W foil trace shows a near-edge (white-line) peak that is distinctly broader and lower in intensity than the peaks for WO2 and Ni-WO2, and it is positioned at somewhat lower photon energy; the oxide samples (WO2 and Ni-WO2) both show a sharper, taller, and narrower near-edge peak located at higher photon energy (~10215 eV) relative to W foil. Between WO2 and Ni-WO2, the two peaks are very close in both energy position and shape (nearly overlapping in the main plot), with only a small difference in peak height visible in the zoomed inset — Ni-WO2's peak is slightly more intense than WO2's, but the two occupy essentially the same energy position, unlike the clear broadening/shift seen for metallic W foil. So the key contrast is: metallic W foil (broad, weaker, lower-energy peak) versus the two oxide/oxide-alloy samples (sharp, stronger, higher-energy peak), while WO2 and Ni-WO2 remain nearly indistinguishable from each other in peak position.
