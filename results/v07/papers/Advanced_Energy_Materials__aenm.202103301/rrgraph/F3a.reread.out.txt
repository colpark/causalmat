Panel "a" shows X-ray absorption near-edge structure (XANES) spectra — plotted as Intensity (a.u.) vs. Photon energy (eV) from roughly 10175 to 10450 eV, consistent with the W L3-edge. Three overlaid curves are compared: WO2 (blue), Ni-WO2 (red), and W foil (gray reference metal). Each curve shows a sharp white-line absorption peak near ~10210-10215 eV followed by a smaller broad oscillation feature around 10250-10270 eV, then a flat/declining tail out to 10450 eV. An inset in the upper right zooms into the white-line peak region (10200-10240 eV), showing that the W foil peak is shifted to lower energy and has a different (broader, less intense) shape than the WO2 and Ni-WO2 peaks, while Ni-WO2 has a slightly higher and shifted peak intensity relative to WO2, indicating a change in W oxidation state/local coordination between metallic W, WO2, and Ni-doped WO2. No scale bar is present (this is a spectroscopic line plot, not a micrograph); axes are labeled with units and a color-coded legend identifies the three samples.
