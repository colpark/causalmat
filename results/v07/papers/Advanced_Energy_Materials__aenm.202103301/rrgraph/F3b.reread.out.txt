Panel b shows EXAFS (extended X-ray absorption fine structure) analysis: Fourier-transformed k3-weighted chi(k) spectra plotted as FT K3 chi(K) (arbitrary units, y-axis) versus R+ΔR (Å, x-axis, range 0–4 Å). Three stacked, vertically offset curves are shown for W foil (gray, bottom), WO2 (blue, middle), and Ni-WO2 (red, top), with no scale bar (line plot, not micrograph). The W foil curve shows a single dominant peak near ~2.7 Å labeled W–W (metallic W–W bonding, no oxygen shell). The WO2 curve shows a strong peak near ~1.6 Å labeled W–O and a smaller peak near ~3.2 Å labeled W–W. The Ni-WO2 curve shows the same W–O peak near ~1.6 Å (with somewhat increased intensity/broadening) and a peak near ~3.2 Å labeled W–W/Ni, suggesting Ni incorporation into the W coordination shell and a shift/change in the second-shell scattering path compared to plain WO2.
