Panel c is a voltage-time plot from a symmetric-cell electrochemical cycling (galvanostatic) test, not a microscopy image. Axes: Voltage (V), range roughly -0.06 to +0.06 V, versus Time (h), 0 to about 1000 h. Two overlaid traces are shown as dense oscillating (square-wave-like) bands: a black "Blank" trace that runs from 0 to about 250-500 h before becoming erratic/spiky (indicating short-circuiting or instability), and a red "NH4+ additive" trace that persists smoothly and stably out to nearly 1000 h with a fairly constant, slightly narrowing voltage amplitude envelope (~±0.02-0.03 V). Test conditions are annotated in the plot: 0.5 mA cm-2, 0.5 mAh cm-2. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph); the trend shown is that the NH4+-additive condition maintains stable, long-term cycling voltage polarization, while the Blank condition degrades/fails earlier (shorter cycling life before voltage fluctuation increases sharply).
