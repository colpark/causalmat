This is panel (d), a rheology time-sweep plot titled "Gelation kinetics." It shows Storage Modulus, G' (Pa), on a log scale y-axis (10^-1 to 10^4 Pa) versus Time (min) on a linear x-axis (0 to 400 min). Two datasets are plotted: filled black circles labeled "PEGSH/(2% MoS2)" and open gray circles labeled "PEGSH." The PEGSH/(2% MoS2) curve stays flat near ~0.1-0.2 Pa until roughly 130-150 min, then rises sharply through a sigmoidal transition to reach a plateau around 1000-2000 Pa by ~400 min, indicating gelation/crosslinking onset and completion. The plain PEGSH curve remains flat at roughly 0.1 Pa across the entire 400 min window, showing no gelation. No scale bar is present (this is a graph, not a micrograph); technique is oscillatory rheometry (storage modulus vs. time).
