Candidate answer:
Panel b shows a Tauc plot: (αhν)^1/2 (cm)^-1/2, y-axis, vs photon energy hν (eV), x-axis from 1.5 to 3.0. Two curves are plotted — black "TKL" (pristine) and red "Li-TKL" (lithiated) — both derived from UV-Vis diffuse-reflectance/absorbance data assuming an indirect band-gap transition (the 1/2 exponent). Both curves are flat near zero at low energy and rise steeply starting around 2.1–2.4 eV, with linear extrapolation (dashed guide lines visible under the rising edges) used to extract the optical band gap via x-intercept. An inset table lists the extracted band gaps: Lithiated TKL = 2.41 eV, Pristine TKL = 2.67 eV, showing the lithiated sample has a smaller (red-shifted) band gap than the pristine material. There is no scale bar (not a micrograph); axes are the only calibration. The red Li-TKL curve's onset is shifted to lower energy relative to the black TKL curve, consistent with the tabulated band-gap narrowing upon lithiation.
