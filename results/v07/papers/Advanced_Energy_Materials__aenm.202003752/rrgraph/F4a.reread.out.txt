Technique: X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) narrow-scan spectra of the F 1s core level, comparing three samples/electrolytes: "commercial" (top), "ILE" (middle), and "LHCE" (bottom).

Features: Each spectrum is plotted as intensity (counts per second, y-axis) versus binding energy (692–680 eV, x-axis, decreasing left to right). A large deconvoluted peak assigned to LiF (shaded blue/purple, centered near 685 eV, marked with a dashed vertical guideline) dominates all three spectra. In the top "commercial" spectrum, an additional smaller shoulder peak labeled "P-OF" (shaded orange/yellow) is present at higher binding energy (~687–688 eV) alongside the LiF peak; this P-OF component is not indicated in the ILE and LHCE spectra below.

Trends/scale: No scale bar (spectral plot, not micrograph). The LiF component appears prominent and similarly positioned (~685 eV) across all three conditions, suggesting LiF is a common decomposition/interphase product, while the P-OF species appears specific to or more visible in the commercial sample's surface chemistry. Raw data are shown as red curve/dots with fitted colored peak envelopes underneath.
