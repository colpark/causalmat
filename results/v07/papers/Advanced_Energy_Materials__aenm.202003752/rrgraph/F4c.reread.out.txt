This is line graph (panel "c") showing computed projected density of states (PDOS) as a function of energy, not a microscopy image. The x-axis is "Energy (eV)" spanning roughly 0 to 6 eV, and the y-axis is "PDOS" ranging from 0 to about 400 (arbitrary/density units); there is no scale bar since this is not a micrograph. Five curves are plotted, distinguished by color/linestyle per the legend: FSI⁻ (ILE) as a blue dashed line, FSI⁻ (LHCE) as a red solid line, PP1,3⁻ (ILE) as a yellow/gold dashed line, PP1,3⁻ (LHCE) as a magenta/purple solid line, and HFE (LHCE) as a green solid line. All curves start near zero at low energy, remain flat/near-zero through roughly 0–4 eV, then rise sharply into a peak centered around 4.5–5.5 eV — the FSI⁻ (ILE) and FSI⁻ (LHCE) curves show the tallest peaks (~350–400), the HFE (LHCE) curve peaks lower (~220), and the two PP1,3⁻ curves remain low (~50) throughout. An annotation "LUMO at FSI⁻" with an arrow points to this high-energy peak region, indicating the lowest unoccupied molecular orbital is localized on the FSI⁻ species and lies near 4.5–5.5 eV above the reference energy.
