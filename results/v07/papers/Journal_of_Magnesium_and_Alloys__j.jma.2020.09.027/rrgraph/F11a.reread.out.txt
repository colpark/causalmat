Panel (a) is a pressure–composition isotherm (PCT) plot for hydrogen absorption/desorption, plotted as H2 pressure (MPa, log scale, 10^-3 to 10^1) on the y-axis versus H2 content (wt%, 0–5.5) on the x-axis. Three curves are shown for different temperatures: 360 °C (black), 340 °C (red), and 320 °C (blue), each with a distinct marker/line style per the legend. Each curve shows the classic sigmoidal PCT shape: a steep rise at very low H2 content, a broad flat plateau region extending from roughly 0.5 to ~5 wt%, and a sharp upturn near ~5–5.5 wt% as the material approaches saturation. The plateau pressure increases with increasing temperature (360 °C plateau ~0.4–0.5 MPa, 340 °C ~0.2–0.3 MPa, 320 °C ~0.1–0.15 MPa), consistent with typical van't Hoff behavior for a metal hydride system.
