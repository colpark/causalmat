Panel (b) is a pressure-composition isotherm (PCT) plot for a metal hydride system, showing H2 pressure (MPa, log scale, 10^-3 to 10^1) on the y-axis versus H2 content (wt%, 0 to 5) on the x-axis. Three curves are plotted for absorption/desorption at different temperatures: 360 °C (black), 340 °C (red), and 320 °C (blue), each with distinct marker shapes. Each curve shows the classic sigmoidal PCT shape: a steep initial rise at low H2 content, a broad plateau region (roughly 0.2–4.3 wt%) where pressure stays nearly constant while hydrogen content increases, and a sharp final rise near 4.5–5 wt% as the material approaches saturation. The plateau pressure increases with temperature (highest for 360 °C, lowest for 320 °C), consistent with van't Hoff behavior for hydride equilibrium. No scale bar is present (this is a data plot, not a micrograph); axes are linear-log with gridlines, and a legend in the lower right identifies the three temperature series.
