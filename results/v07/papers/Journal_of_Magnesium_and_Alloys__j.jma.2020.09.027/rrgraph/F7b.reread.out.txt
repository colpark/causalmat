Panel (b) is a line/scatter plot of hydrogen absorption kinetics, plotting H2 absorption (wt%, y-axis, 0–5) versus time (min, x-axis, 0–~250). It is not a microscopy image — no scale bar is present, as this is a quantitative kinetics curve (likely PCT/Sieverts-type measurement), not an SEM/TEM/optical micrograph.

Four curves are shown for different absorption temperatures, distinguished by color/marker: black squares = 250 °C, red circles = 200 °C, blue triangles = 150 °C, magenta inverted triangles = 100 °C. All curves show rapid initial uptake within the first ~10-20 min followed by a plateau (saturation) over the remaining time to 240 min. The saturation (plateau) H2 absorption capacity increases with temperature: ~3.2 wt% at 100 °C, ~4.0 wt% at 150 °C, ~4.5 wt% at 200 °C, and ~4.5-4.6 wt% at 250 °C (250 °C and 200 °C curves nearly overlap at the highest capacity). Both the absorption rate and the final capacity trend upward with increasing temperature, though the effect saturates between 200 °C and 250 °C.
