This is a line/scatter plot (not a micrograph) labeled "(a)", plotting hydrogen desorption kinetics. The x-axis is "Time (min)" ranging from 0 to ~500, and the y-axis is "H2 desorption (wt%)" ranging from 0 down to -6. Four datasets are shown, each corresponding to a different isothermal temperature: 360°C (black squares), 320°C (red circles), 300°C (blue up-triangles), and 280°C (magenta down-triangles), per the legend in the upper right. Each curve starts near 0 wt% at t=0 and decreases monotonically (desorbing hydrogen) before leveling off in a plateau around -5 to -5.2 wt%. There is a clear trend of desorption rate with temperature: the 360°C curve drops fastest and plateaus earliest (within ~20-30 min), while curves for progressively lower temperatures (320°C, 300°C, 280°C) desorb more slowly, with the 280°C curve taking roughly 400-450 min to reach a similar plateau.
