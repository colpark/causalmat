The panel is a DSC (differential scanning calorimetry) plot showing heat flow (mW/mg) vs. temperature (°C) for two samples measured over roughly 240–460 °C. An "endo" arrow at top left indicates the endothermic direction. Two curves are plotted: a black curve labeled "+ 0 wt.% Co@C" and a red curve labeled "+ 10 wt.% Co@C," each offset vertically (black curve sits lower, around -5 to -8 mW/mg; red curve sits higher, around -3 to -5 mW/mg). Each curve shows a broad endothermic dip (shaded/hatched region) with an onset temperature and peak temperature labeled directly on the plot: the black (0 wt.% Co@C) curve has features at 341.0 °C and a deeper minimum at 396.5 °C, with total enthalpy ΔW_C0 = 616.3 J/g; the red (10 wt.% Co@C) curve has features at 267.0 °C and a minimum at 338.5 °C, with enthalpy ΔW_C10 = 603.1 J/g. The overall trend is that adding 10 wt.% Co@C shifts the endothermic decomposition/melting event to lower temperature (by roughly 60–90 °C) while only slightly reducing the total enthalpy of the transition (616.3 vs. 603.1 J/g).
