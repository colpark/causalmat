The Fig. 4a survey spectrum shows only broad elemental peaks (C 1s ~285 eV, N 1s ~400 eV, O 1s ~530 eV) for both g-C3N4-E and S-g-C3N4-E, with "S 2p" merely labeled near baseline (~160-170 eV) — no distinct, resolvable S 2p peak is visible above the background in the S-g-C3N4-E trace. A survey scan at this resolution and scale cannot distinguish bonding environments; it only indicates elemental composition at low resolution, and even elemental S detection here is marginal/unlabeled as a real peak. Therefore Figure 4a does NOT support the specific claim that sulfur is bonded as C-S-C on N sites — that chemical-bonding assignment would require the corresponding high-resolution S 2p XPS spectrum (with peak fitting/binding-energy assignment), which is referenced in the caption but not shown in panel 4a itself. At most, 4a is consistent with S being present as a dopant in S-g-C3N4-E, but it provides no evidence for the specific C-S-C/N-site bonding configuration.
