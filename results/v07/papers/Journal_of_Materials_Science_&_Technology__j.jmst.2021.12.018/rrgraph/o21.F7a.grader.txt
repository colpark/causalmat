Candidate answer:
Panel (a) is a UV-Vis diffuse reflectance/absorbance spectrum plot, not a micrograph. Axes: y-axis "Absorbance (a.u.)" from 0.0 to 1.0; x-axis "Wavelength (nm)" from 300 to 700 nm. No scale bar (not applicable to this data-plot type).

Four curves are shown for different sample variants, per the legend:
- g-C3N4 (black/gray)
- g-C3N4-E (teal)
- S@g-C3N4-E (blue)
- S-g-C3N4-E (red)

Trends: all four curves show a strong absorption edge/rising absorbance below ~430-450 nm (peak near 380-400 nm, absorbance ~0.7-0.87), consistent with the intrinsic band-gap absorption of g-C3N4. Beyond ~450 nm, g-C3N4 and g-C3N4-E drop sharply to low absorbance (~0.05-0.1) by 700 nm, while S@g-C3N4-E retains a moderate absorbance tail (~0.15-0.2), and S-g-C3N4-E (red) shows the strongest red-shifted/extended visible-light absorption, staying elevated (~0.2-0.45) across 450-700 nm. This indicates that sulfur doping/loading (S@ and especially S-g-C3N4-E) extends light absorption into the visible range compared to undoped g-C3N4.
