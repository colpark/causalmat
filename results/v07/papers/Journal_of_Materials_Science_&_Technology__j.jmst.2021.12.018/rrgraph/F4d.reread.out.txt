This is XPS (X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy) data, panel (d), showing the S 2p core-level region over a binding energy range of roughly 160–174 eV (x-axis: "Binding Energy (eV)"; y-axis: "Intensity (a.u.)"). Two spectra are overlaid: a lower, essentially flat/noisy trace labeled "g-C3N4-E" (no sulfur signal, as expected for the undoped material), and an upper trace labeled "S-g-C3N4-E" showing a clear doublet peak structure. The upper spectrum is fitted with a Shirley-type baseline (magenta) and two deconvoluted component peaks (green and red Gaussian/Lorentzian fits) centered at 164.5 eV and 168.5 eV, consistent with C–S–C (thiophene-like S 2p3/2) and oxidized sulfate/sulfonate-type S species, respectively. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a spectral plot). The trend illustrated is the presence of sulfur-containing bonding states only in the S-doped sample, confirming successful sulfur incorporation into the g-C3N4 framework.
