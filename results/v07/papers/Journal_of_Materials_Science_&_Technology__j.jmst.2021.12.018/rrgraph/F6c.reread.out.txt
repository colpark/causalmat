Panel C (labeled "c" upper-left) is a line-and-marker plot, not a micrograph. It shows cycling stability of photocatalytic H2 evolution over six consecutive 4-hour runs (1st through 6th, spanning 0–24 h total). X-axis: Time (h), 0–24 in steps of 4. Y-axis: H2 Evolution (µmol g⁻¹), 0–25000. Each cycle is plotted in a different color/marker (gray squares, red circles, blue up-triangles, teal down-triangles, purple diamonds, gold up-triangles) with a solid line connecting points within each cycle and a vertical dashed blue line marking the reset/boundary between consecutive cycles. Each cycle rises roughly linearly from 0 to about 20,000–22,500 µmol g⁻¹ over 4 h, then drops back to near zero at the start of the next cycle. The peak evolution amount is fairly consistent across the six cycles (slight decline visible by the 5th–6th cycle, e.g., ~20,500 and ~20,000 vs. ~22,500 for cycles 1–3), indicating good but not perfectly stable photocatalytic recyclability. There is no scale bar since this is a graph, not a micrograph.
