In panel F1e, the GIWAXS map of PEA2MA4(Pb0.5Sn0.5)5I16 shows a single sharp, intense diffraction arc/spot located directly along the out-of-plane axis (qz), centered near qxy ≈ 0 and at roughly qz ≈ 20 nm⁻¹, labeled as the (202) reflection. This spot sits essentially on the meridian (qz axis) rather than being spread azimuthally around the full Debye-Scherrer ring, indicating a preferential out-of-plane crystallographic orientation rather than random polycrystalline texture. The corresponding intensity line-cut, plotted along qz (shown as the white curve on the left side of the panel), is largely flat/near-zero at low qz and then displays a single sharp, narrow peak at qz ≈ 20 nm⁻¹ coinciding with the (202) spot position — consistent with a highly oriented film where the (202) crystal planes are aligned parallel to the substrate.
