Panel (f) is a time-resolved photoluminescence (TRPL) decay plot, shown as semi-log axes: y-axis "Counts" (log scale, 0.01 to 1, normalized/decaying intensity) vs x-axis "Time / ns" (linear, 0 to 80 ns). Two sample decay curves are plotted with distinct markers: pink/red circles for PEA2MA4Pb5I16 and open squares for PEA2MA4(Pb0.5Sn0.5)5I16, each overlaid with a solid blue fitting curve. The Pb-only sample (circles) shows a much slower, long-lived decay persisting out to ~80 ns before reaching the ~0.05-0.1 count level, while the mixed Pb/Sn sample (squares) decays far more rapidly, dropping to the ~0.01 noise floor by roughly 20-25 ns. No scale bar is present (this is a data plot, not a micrograph). Overall trend: substituting half the Pb with Sn substantially shortens the photoluminescence lifetime/decay compared to the pure-Pb perovskite.
