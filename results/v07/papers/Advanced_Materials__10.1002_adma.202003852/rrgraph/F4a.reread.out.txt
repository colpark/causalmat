Both traces show a shoulder/plateau feature near 350–450 nm before rising to the main peak, and both decay toward zero near 1000–1100 nm, indicating the polarization-dependent (anisotropic) photoresponse of the device is consistent in spectral shape but differs by roughly a factor of ~2 in magnitude between the two polarization angles.
