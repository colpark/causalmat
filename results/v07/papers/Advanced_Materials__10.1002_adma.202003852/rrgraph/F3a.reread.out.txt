Panel (a): a cross-sectional scanning electron microscope (SEM) image of a perovskite solar cell device stack, imaged in a top-to-bottom layered view. From top (bright, thin edge) to bottom the labeled layers are: Ag (silver top electrode, appears as a thin bright line against the black background), PCBM (electron transport layer), Perovskite (thick, textured grey layer forming the bulk of the image), PEDOT:PSS (hole transport layer), and ITO (transparent conductive oxide substrate at the bottom). The image is grayscale with visible grain/textural contrast distinguishing the perovskite layer from the thinner PCBM and PEDOT:PSS layers, though the PCBM/perovskite/PEDOT:PSS boundaries are not sharply resolved (fairly low contrast between them). A scale bar of 300 nm is shown in the lower right corner. No quantitative trend is depicted (single static cross-section image), it simply documents the layer stack architecture and relative layer thicknesses of the fabricated device.
