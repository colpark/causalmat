Panel (b), labeled "IPF" at top and "Ref" in the upper right, shows an EBSD inverse pole figure (IPF) map with color-coded crystallographic orientation relative to the ND (normal direction) direction, using the standard triangular IPF-ND color key (001-101-111 corners) shown in the bottom-left legend. The map displays a mosaic of irregularly shaped, differently colored (mostly purple/violet, with patches of green, yellow, orange, red, cyan) grains. Black dotted lines overlaid on the map outline PAGBs (prior austenite grain boundaries, per the legend box at bottom right), tracing an irregular polygonal network across the field of view, with a prominent dark/black band running diagonally through the upper-middle portion of the image. A scale bar of 5 µm is shown in the lower left. The grains vary substantially in size and shape, with fine substructure/coloring variation within some larger grains suggesting subgrain or lath-like orientation gradients, consistent with a martensitic or bainitic microstructure reconstructed to prior austenite grains.
