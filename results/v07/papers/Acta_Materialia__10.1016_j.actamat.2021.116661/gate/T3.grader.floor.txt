At low magnification, the overall fracture surface of the H-charged Ref steel (F7b) is divided into two distinct regions — a dark-gray area and a light-gray area. In contrast, the H-charged Mo steel fracture surface (F7f) shows only a single light-gray area, with no separate dark-gray region present. This is consistent with the differing microcrack paths noted for each steel: in the Ref steel, cracking along prior-austenite grain boundaries produces the mixed dark-/light-gray fracture appearance, while in the Mo steel, cracking crossing through the grain interiors along {011}M planes results in the more uniform light-gray fracture surface.
