Candidate answer:
Panel F4a only shows Coulombic-efficiency (CE) cycling data for C HS vs. CnC HS at 1 mAh/cm2 and 4 mA/cm2 — this is a plating/stripping half-cell (or asymmetric) test measured in cycle counts, so it can at most support the "~200 cycles vs. ~50 cycles" portion of the claim. The claim also states CnC HS sustains >7000 min and C HS fails near 4400 min of "symmetric cycling," which is a time-based metric from a symmetric Zn||Zn cell — a distinct experiment reported as voltage-vs-time, not as Coulombic efficiency. Nothing in F4a's caption or described content (CE at a fixed areal capacity/current density) speaks to symmetric-cell voltage-time cycling life. So the panel does not fully support the claim as a whole: it can corroborate only the cycle-count/CE comparison between the two hosts, while the >7000 min/~4400 min symmetric-cycling figures must come from a separate panel (a symmetric-cell voltage profile, not F4a). In short, F4a is consistent with CnC HS outperforming C HS in CE-cycling stability, but it is silent on — and cannot verify — the symmetric-cycling duration claim, which requires different data.
