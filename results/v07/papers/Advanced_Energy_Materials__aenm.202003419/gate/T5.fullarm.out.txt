Both curves display both features — i.e., neither peak appears or disappears between the two conditions; the two nitrogen features are present in both the pristine and zinc-deposited spectra. However, the two spectra are not simply overlapping/identical: in the zinc-containing sample the peaks appear slightly shifted to higher photon energy relative to the pristine sample, and the second (yellow-region) feature appears somewhat broader/more intense with a stronger rising edge into the higher-energy shoulder compared to pristine. So this is not "no visible difference" — both features persist in both conditions, but their peak positions/shapes show a small shift and change in relative intensity upon zinc deposition, consistent with nitrogen sites interacting with zinc.
