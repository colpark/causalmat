Question: Panels F3d and F3e show the plated zinc surface on CnC HS and on C HS with Zn plating, respectively. Based on these SEM images, how does the morphology of the deposited zinc compare between the two hosts?
state how the two conditions compare, including if they look the same; do not rank. Answer "no visible difference" when they look the same, or "cannot determine" when the panels cannot settle it

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): Zinc plated on CnC HS forms a largely flat deposit with only sparse protruding platelets, whereas zinc plated on C HS forms a dense mat of sharp platelets covering the surface (o14).

Grading note: Answer key: the description on the evidence node o14, judge-confirmed against the two panel labels (F3d = CnC HS, F3e = C HS with Zn plating).

Candidate answer:
Panels F3d (CnC HS) and F3e (C HS) both show similar-shaped platelet/flake-like zinc deposits at the same 10 µm scale, but the surface coverage differs visibly between the two conditions. In F3d (CnC HS), the flake-like deposits are sparse and scattered, leaving much of the underlying substrate surface visible/smooth between them. In F3e (C HS), flake-like deposits densely and uniformly cover nearly the entire imaged surface, with little to no bare substrate visible. So the individual deposit morphology (thin platelet/flake shape) looks similar between the two panels, but the areal density/coverage of these deposits is clearly different — sparse on CnC HS versus dense, near-complete coverage on C HS.
