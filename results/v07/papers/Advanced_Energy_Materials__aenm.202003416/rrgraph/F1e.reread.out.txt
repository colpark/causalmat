Panel (e) is a scatter plot of Coulombic Efficiency (CE, %) versus cycle number, comparing two battery cycling conditions: "With pressure" (red circles) and "Without pressure" (blue circles). The y-axis spans roughly 40–110% CE and the x-axis spans cycle numbers 0–20. This is electrochemical cycling data (not a micrograph), so there is no scale bar or imaging technique involved. Both series start near 90-95% CE around cycle 1-2; the "With pressure" (red) points remain tightly clustered near 90-95% CE fairly consistently through about cycle 15, then gradually decline to roughly 83-88% by cycle 20-21. The "Without pressure" (blue) points are noisier, fluctuating between about 81% and 106% (with an outlier near 107% at cycle ~14 and one near 110% at cycle ~20), and the blue series appears to terminate earlier (around cycle 18-20) than the red series, suggesting the "with pressure" condition yields more stable, less scattered, and longer-lasting CE cycling performance.
