Orange square data points connected by a line show space utilization starting around 29–30% at 0 MPa, dipping slightly near 2 MPa, then rising steadily through 4–10 MPa to a peak of about 38% at 10 MPa, followed by a slight decline/plateau to roughly 37–37.5% at 12–14 MPa. An inset schematic defines "Space utilization = S_dendrite / (L × b)", showing a rectangular cell region of width L and height b containing a blue dendrite-like growth of width a, illustrating how the metric is calculated from dendrite growth area within a confined space (likely from a battery/lithium dendrite pressure study).
