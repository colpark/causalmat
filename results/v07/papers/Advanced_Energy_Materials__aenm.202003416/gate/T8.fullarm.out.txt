In panel F5d, the boundary line separating the gray "fails to work" region from the white "pressure shaping domain" is a curve that rises from left to right across the elastic-modulus axis (0.2–2.0 GPa).

- At the low end of the modulus range (~0.2 GPa), the boundary sits at a low pressure, roughly 1–2 MPa.
- At the high end of the modulus range (2.0 GPa), the boundary has risen to roughly 10–11 MPa.

So the dividing curve is low and nearly flat near the low-modulus end, then curves upward steeply toward higher modulus, ending at roughly 10–11 MPa at 2.0 GPa — meaning stiffer electrolytes require substantially more external pressure to reach the enhanced-performance (white) region.
