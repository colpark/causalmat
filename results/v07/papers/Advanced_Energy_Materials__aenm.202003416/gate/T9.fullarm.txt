- How the stress state from external pressure shapes Li dendrites is unclear; coin and pouch cells differ in stress accumulation
- Mechano-electrochemical phase-field model coupling electroplating, ion transport and mechanics (eigen strain, Gibbs energy)
- External pressure 0-14 MPa (electrolyte 1.0 GPa); electrolyte modulus 0.5-2.0 GPa at 6 MPa; map 0.2-2.0 GPa
- Simulated potentiostatic Li plating (0.10 V) from one nucleus in a 20x20 um domain to 0.40 mAh cm-2 under pressure
- Pressure turns dendrite stress from tensile to compressive, maximal at the tip, inhibiting tip growth and promoting lateral growth
- Dendrites become shorter, less branched and stockier as external pressure rises
- External pressure yields denser, less porous plated Li (higher space utilization)
- External pressure raises and stabilises Coulombic efficiency of Li|Cu pouch cells
