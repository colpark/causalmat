Panel (h) shows X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) depth-profile spectra of the Mn 2p region, comparing two conditions stacked vertically: the top spectrum is labeled "SEI" (solid electrolyte interphase, i.e., the as-formed surface before etching) and the bottom spectrum is labeled "Etching ~100 nm SEI" (after ~100 nm of sputter etching into the SEI layer). Both plots share the x-axis "B.E. (eV)" (binding energy) ranging from about 650 to 635 eV, and the y-axis is "Intensity (a.u.)" with no numerical scale or scale bar (as expected for a spectroscopy plot, not a micrograph). In the top (SEI) spectrum, the signal is essentially flat/noisy with no discernible peaks, indicating no detectable Mn at the outer SEI surface. In the bottom (etched) spectrum, two clear peaks appear at roughly 648 eV and 637 eV, labeled Mn 2p1/2 and Mn 2p3/2 respectively, with the raw data (black) overlaid by fitted component curves (red envelope fit, plus deconvoluted magenta/purple and teal/green sub-peaks representing different chemical states or background). This trend shows Mn is absent at the pristine SEI surface but becomes detectable after etching ~100 nm into the SEI, suggesting Mn dissolution/deposition products are buried within or beneath the outer SEI layer rather than at its surface.
