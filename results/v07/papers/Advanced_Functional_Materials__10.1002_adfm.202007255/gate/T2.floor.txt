Context from a materials paper:
- Li metal anodes suffer dendritic, low-efficiency plating; low-cost ways to regulate Li deposition are needed
- A transition-metal-oxide layer on the separator, facing Li, should react with Li to form a passivating interlayer regulating deposition
- Coat TMO particles (TiO2, MnO, FeO, CoO, NiO, CuO) onto porous PP separator, TMO side contacting Li
- TMO particle layer ~3.5 um thick coated on PP separator (single-sided; double-sided MnO/PP/MnO for F7)
- With every TMO/PP separator, Li plates on Cu dendrite-free as a dual layer (sheets over spheres)
- MnO chosen for further study: dense deposit, slightly higher efficiency and lower cost than CoO
- MnO/PP-separated Li||Cu cells: MnO contacts Li and Li is plated on Cu (0.5 mA cm-2, LiTFSI DOL/DME)
- Contact with Li reduces MnO to metallic Mn0 and Li2O, leaving residual MnO
- SEI on the plated Li spheres contains Mn0 and Li2O nanocrystals
- Continuously dissolving MnO self-heals a Mn0/Li2O mixed-conducting SEI that homogenises Li+ flux and suppresses dendrites
