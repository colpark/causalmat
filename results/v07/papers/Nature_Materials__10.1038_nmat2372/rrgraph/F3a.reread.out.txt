Panel (a) shows a powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) Rietveld refinement plot: intensity (arb. units) vs 2θ° (Cu Kα) from 10° to 50°. The observed data are plotted as red dots, the calculated fit as a solid black line overlaid on them, and a blue difference (residual) curve is shown below the pattern. Green vertical tick marks between the pattern and residual indicate the calculated Bragg peak positions for the identified phase(s), with one peak near 2θ ≈ 34° labeled "α". Several sharp, strong diffraction peaks appear in the 19–31° range, decreasing in intensity and becoming more closely spaced/overlapping at higher angles, consistent with a crystalline phase with a moderately large unit cell. An inset in the upper right shows a scanning electron microscopy (SEM) image of the powder morphology, displaying irregular, plate-like/flake-shaped particles with a 5 μm scale bar.
