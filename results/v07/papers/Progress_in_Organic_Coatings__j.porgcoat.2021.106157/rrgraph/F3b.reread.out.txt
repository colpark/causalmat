PCL and PCL-LS (polycaprolactone and PCL with an added component, likely a coating/composite) both show much higher contact angles (~95–97°), close to each other, with the droplet insets appearing more rounded/beaded, indicating more hydrophobic surfaces. The clear trend is a sharp increase in hydrophobicity going from the bare/treated Mg alloy substrates (AZ31, AZ31-OH) to the PCL-based coatings (PCL, PCL-LS), with the LS additive causing negligible change relative to plain PCL.
