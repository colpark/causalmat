This is a Bode plot (impedance modulus vs. frequency) from electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), labeled panel (B). The x-axis is Log frequency (Hz) ranging from -2 to 5, and the y-axis is Log Z (Ω·cm²) ranging from 0 to ~6. Four datasets are plotted with distinct markers/colors: PCL (black diamonds), 1 day (red squares), 3 Day (green triangles), and 7 Day (blue circles). No scale bar is present, as this is a graph/plot rather than a micrograph. All four curves show the same general trend — high impedance (~4.3–5.5 Log Z) at low frequency plateauing/decreasing gradually through mid-frequencies, then converging and dropping sharply above ~10^4 Hz down to roughly 3–3.3 Log Z at the highest frequencies. The curves are ordered by magnitude at low-to-mid frequency with PCL (uncoated/reference) showing the highest impedance, followed by 1 day, 3 Day, and 7 Day showing progressively lower impedance, suggesting decreasing corrosion/coating resistance with increasing immersion/degradation time, though all converge at high frequency.
