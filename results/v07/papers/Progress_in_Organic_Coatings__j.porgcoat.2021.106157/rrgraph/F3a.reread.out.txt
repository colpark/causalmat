Four stacked spectra are shown, offset vertically for clarity and color-coded per the legend: AZ31 (black, flat/featureless baseline with minor broad hump near 3300–3600 cm⁻¹), AZ31-OH (red, showing a distinct sharp peak at 3700 cm⁻¹ labeled with an arrow, attributed to hydroxyl groups from the surface treatment), PCL (green, showing labeled peaks at 2944 cm⁻¹ and 2865 cm⁻¹ - C-H stretching - and a strong sharp peak at 1723 cm⁻¹ - carbonyl C=O stretch - plus a fingerprint region cluster of peaks between ~1400–1000 cm⁻¹), and PCL-LS (purple, showing essentially the same peak pattern as PCL, including the strong 1723 cm⁻¹ carbonyl peak and fingerprint region peaks, indicating the PCL coating chemistry is preserved after adding the LS component). The trend across the four traces (bottom to top: AZ31 → AZ31-OH → PCL → PCL-LS) illustrates progressive surface functionalization: bare magnesium alloy AZ31 has no distinctive IR bands, alkaline/hydroxide treatment (AZ31-OH) introduces a hydroxyl signature at 3700 cm⁻¹, and subsequent PCL and PCL-LS coatings both display characteristic polyester (PCL) absorption bands, with PCL-LS closely matching PCL's spectrum, suggesting the added LS filler does not significantly alter or mask the polymer's core IR signature.
