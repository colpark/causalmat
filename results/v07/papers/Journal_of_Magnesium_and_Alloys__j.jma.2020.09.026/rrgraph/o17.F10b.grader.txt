Panel (b) is a grouped bar chart, not a microscopy/imaging panel — no scale bar is present. It plots mechanical properties of a Ti-6Al-4V composite/alloy system as a function of Ti-6Al-4V volume fraction (x-axis: 0, 7, 14, 21 vol.%). Three series are shown as colored bars: UTS (orange, left y-axis "Stress (MPa)", 0–400 range), offset yield strength (green, same left axis), and elongation (red, right y-axis "Elongation (%)", 0–20 range). Trends: UTS and offset yield strength both increase roughly monotonically with increasing Ti-6Al-4V content (UTS rising from ~230 MPa at 0% to ~325 MPa at 21%; yield strength rising from ~100 MPa at 0% to ~205 MPa at 21%), while elongation shows the opposite trend, decreasing from its highest value (~14.5%) at 0% Ti-6Al-4V down to ~9% at 7%, with smaller fluctuations (~11%, ~9%) at 14% and 21%. This indicates a classic strength–ductility trade-off as reinforcement/second-phase content increases.
