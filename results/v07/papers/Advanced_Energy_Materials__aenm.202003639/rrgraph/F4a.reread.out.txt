Panel (a) is a composite of three sub-plots related by shared 2-theta axes, showing in-situ/operando powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) of an electrode material during electrochemical cycling. Left sub-plot: a stacked (waterfall) series of XRD intensity-vs-2theta traces (10-40 degrees), each trace offset vertically and colored differently, spanning two labeled cycling stages ("1st" and "2nd"), representing spectra collected at successive charge/discharge states with a few peaks (notably one near ~17-18 degrees and a broader one near ~25 degrees) visible superimposed on noisy baselines. Middle sub-plot: a voltage-vs-capacity curve (Voltage 0.5-2.0 V on x-axis, Capacity 0-300+ mAh/g on y-axis) tracing the electrochemical charge/discharge profile, with arrows indicating the 1st and 2nd cycle directions and four colored markers/labels (D-1.60V, D-1.75V, C-1.77V, C-1.69V) marking specific voltage points corresponding to discharge (D) and charge (C) states used for XRD sampling. Right sub-plot: another stacked waterfall of XRD traces (10-35 degrees) but here each trace is labeled with a crystallographic phase assignment (Monoclinic, Mon/Cub, Cubic, alternating through the stack), showing phase evolution/transition between monoclinic and cubic structures as a function of state of charge, again showing peaks near ~17-18 and ~25 degrees. No scale bar is present (not a micrograph); intensity is in arbitrary units. Overall trend: the panel correlates electrochemical voltage/capacity states with corresponding diffraction patterns to demonstrate a reversible monoclinic-to-cubic (and back) phase transformation during cycling.
