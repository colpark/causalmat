Panel (c) shows XPS (X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy) survey spectra, plotting Intensity (a.u., y-axis, no scale bar) versus Binding energy (eV, x-axis, 0–1400 eV) for three samples, stacked vertically with a vertical offset for clarity: GB (green, top), GO (blue, middle), and GB@rGO (red, bottom). Each spectrum shows characteristic peaks labeled Si2p (~100 eV), C1s (~285 eV), and O1s (~530 eV), plus smaller unlabeled peaks near 970–990 eV and ~1070 eV likely corresponding to O KLL Auger and Na1s/other minor features. The C1s and O1s peaks are the dominant sharp features in all three traces, with C1s appearing notably taller relative to O1s in the GO trace compared to GB and GB@rGO. Baselines show a general upward slope/step across the binding energy range typical of XPS survey background, consistent across all three curves. No scale bar is present since intensity is in arbitrary units; the panel is a standard elemental-composition comparison across the three material states (GB, GO, GB@rGO).
