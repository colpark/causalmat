Panel (b) shows FTIR (Fourier-transform infrared) spectra, plotting Intensity (a.u.) on the y-axis versus Wavenumber (cm⁻¹) on the x-axis from 500 to 4000 cm⁻¹. There is no scale bar (spectroscopic plot, not a micrograph). Four stacked/offset traces are shown, labeled at right: GB (green, top), GO (blue), GB@GO (black), and GB@rGO (red, bottom).

Labeled peaks/bands are annotated with arrows: Si-O and Si-O-Si stretching bands near 800-1100 cm⁻¹ (prominent dips, strongest in GB and GB@rGO/GB@GO traces), C-O-C near ~1050-1200 cm⁻¹, C=C near ~1600 cm⁻¹, C=O near ~1700-1730 cm⁻¹, and a broad O-H band near 3300-3600 cm⁻¹ (present in all four traces, most pronounced as a broad hump). The GO and GB@GO traces show characteristic graphene-oxide oxygen functional group bands (C-O-C, C=C, C=O, O-H) superimposed on or alongside the glass-bead (GB) Si-O-Si features, consistent with a composite/functionalization comparison across the four sample traces.
