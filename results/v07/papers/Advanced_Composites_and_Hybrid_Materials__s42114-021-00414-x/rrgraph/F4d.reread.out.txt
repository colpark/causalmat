Panel (d) is a thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) plot showing weight percent (%) on the y-axis (40-100%) versus temperature (°C) on the x-axis (room temperature to ~900°C), for four samples: GB (green), GO (blue), GB@GO (dark gray/black), and GB@rGO (red). No scale bar (not a micrograph). Trends: GB shows minimal weight loss, staying near 98-99% across the whole temperature range, indicating high thermal stability. GO shows the largest and sharpest weight loss, dropping steeply from 100% to about 65% between roughly 150-250°C (attributed to loss of oxygen-containing functional groups), then declining more gradually to about 44% by 900°C. GB@GO shows an intermediate, more gradual continuous decline from 100% down to about 88% by 900°C. GB@rGO also declines gradually but less than GB@GO, ending around 92-93% at 900°C, consistent with rGO having fewer labile oxygen functional groups than GO after reduction.
