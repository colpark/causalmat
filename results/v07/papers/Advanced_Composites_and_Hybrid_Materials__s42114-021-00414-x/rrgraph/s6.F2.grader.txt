The panel (aca6731e03665d32.jpg) is a schematic process-flow diagram, not a microscopy or spectroscopy image — no scale bar is present. It illustrates the fabrication of a short-carbon-fiber (SCF)/graphene-ball@reduced-graphene-oxide (GB@rGO)/PDMS composite: short carbon fibers are mechanically stirred into PDMS (conical twin-screw mixer) to form an "SCF/PDMS homogeneous compound," then GB@rGO particles are added with further stirring to form an "SCF/GB@rGO/PDMS homogeneous compound." This compound is hot-embossed under applied force, then subjected to "Free compression" (driven by thermodynamics/hydrodynamic conditions) which drives self-assembly of the fiber network, followed by "Spatial confining compression" that produces a denser "Forced-assembled network." Below the flow arrows, schematic cross-sections (green circles) show progressively increasing fiber/particle density and interconnection from the homogeneous compound through the self-assembled to the forced-assembled network. The final panel on the right is an actual photograph of the finished black composite sample. Overall the figure conveys a fabrication route in which mechanical compression is used to tune internal network density/connectivity of a conductive filler network within a polymer matrix.
