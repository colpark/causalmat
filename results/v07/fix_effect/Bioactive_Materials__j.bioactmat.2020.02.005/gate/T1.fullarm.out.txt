However, the actual red/black trace is dominated by noise with no visibly resolved peaks rising clearly above baseline at most of the marked positions; only the u and v''' region show some signal above noise, so the panel does not visually demonstrate well-resolved Ce3+ and Ce4+ components — the dashed lines appear to be reference assignments rather than fitted peaks clearly extracted from the data.

Overall: the panels are directionally consistent with the text's claim (presence of Er and Ce, with conventional binding-energy assignments matching Er3+ and a Ce3+/Ce4+ mixture), but the signal-to-noise in both spectra is low enough that the specific oxidation-state assignments are not strongly demonstrated by the raw data shown — the support is weak/qualitative rather than quantitatively conclusive.
