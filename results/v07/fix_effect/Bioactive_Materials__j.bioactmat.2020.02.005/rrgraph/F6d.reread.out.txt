Panel (d) shows an XPS spectrum of the Ce 3d region, plotting intensity (a.u., y-axis) versus binding energy from roughly 918 eV down to about 880 eV (x-axis, decreasing left to right). The raw spectrum is a noisy black line without an apparent fitted/deconvoluted envelope overlay; eight dashed red vertical guide lines mark the standard Ce 3d spin-orbit doublet peak positions, labeled u''', u'', u', u (Ce 3d3/2 components) and v''', v'', v', v (Ce 3d5/2 components) from left to right. The signal-to-noise ratio is low, so individual peaks are not clearly resolved above the noise except for a general rise in intensity toward the low-binding-energy (right) side of the spectrum near the v/v' labels. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a spectral plot), and no obvious systematic trend beyond the labeled peak positions and overall noisiness of the trace.
