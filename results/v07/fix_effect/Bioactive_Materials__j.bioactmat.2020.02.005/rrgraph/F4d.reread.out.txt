Panel (d) is a Raman spectroscopy plot, Intensity (a.u.) on the y-axis versus Raman shift (cm⁻¹, 0–~700) on the x-axis, no scale bar (not a microscopy image). Four overlaid spectra are shown: a light-blue reference curve labeled TiO2 (Anatase), and three colored curves for Er0.5Ce0.1Ti-O, Er0.5Ce0.2Ti-O, and Er0.5Ce0.3Ti-O, all annealed at 700 °C (dark blue, orange-red, and yellow, respectively). Characteristic anatase TiO2 Raman-active modes are labeled: a strong sharp peak near ~140 cm⁻¹ (Eg(1)), a weak shoulder near ~200 cm⁻¹ (Eg(2)), and broader bands around ~400 cm⁻¹ (B1g), ~510 cm⁻¹ (A1g), and ~630–640 cm⁻¹ (Eg(3)). The pure TiO2 reference shows sharp, well-defined peaks with low baseline intensity, while the Ce/Er-doped samples show progressively broadened and less resolved peaks with increasing baseline/background intensity as Ce content increases (0.1→0.2→0.3), and the Er0.5Ce0.3Ti-O curve (yellow) sits highest overall with a notably enhanced Eg(3) feature near 630 cm⁻¹. Overall trend: increasing Ce doping concentration correlates with peak broadening, reduced peak sharpness relative to the undoped anatase, and elevated background intensity, consistent with increasing lattice disorder/defects from dopant incorporation.
