Panel (a) is a photoluminescence (PL) emission spectrum plot, with Intensity (a.u.) on the y-axis (ranging 0 to ~3.6×10^5) and Wavelength (nm) on the x-axis (480–700 nm). Two curves are overlaid: a teal/cyan line labeled "Er0.5Ti-O" and a yellow/gold line labeled "Er0.75Ti-O." Both traces show a series of sharp emission peaks in the 520–575 nm range (multiple sub-peaks around 525, 540, 555, and a dominant sharp peak near 565 nm), consistent with characteristic Er3+ green upconversion/emission transitions, with the signal falling to near-zero baseline outside this range (500–520 nm and 575–700 nm). The Er0.5Ti-O sample (teal) shows substantially higher peak intensities than Er0.75Ti-O (yellow) across all peaks, most notably at the dominant ~565 nm peak (~3.5×10^5 vs ~1.3×10^5), indicating higher Er content is associated with reduced emission intensity (likely concentration quenching). There is no scale bar (not a micrograph); this is a line/spectral plot, not an imaging technique.
