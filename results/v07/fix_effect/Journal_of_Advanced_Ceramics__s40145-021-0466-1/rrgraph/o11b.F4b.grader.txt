Panel (b): a linear-sweep/rotating disk voltammetry (ORR polarization) plot showing current density J (mA·cm⁻²) on the y-axis (range about -6 to 1) versus potential E (V vs. RHE) on the x-axis (range about 0.2 to ~1.1+). Five curves are plotted for different catalyst samples: NC-1000 (purple), NPC-800 (green), NPC-900 (blue), NPC-1000 (red), and Pt/C (black), distinguished by a color-coded legend in the upper left. No scale bar is present (this is a data plot, not a micrograph). All curves show the typical ORR S-shape: near-zero current at high potential (~1.0–1.1 V), a steep onset/mixed kinetic-diffusion drop between roughly 0.65–0.9 V, and a diffusion-limited plateau at negative current (around -3 to -5 mA·cm⁻²) at low potential (0.2–0.6 V). NPC-1000 (red) and NC-1000/purple show the most positive onset/half-wave potentials (~0.8–0.85 V) and reach the most negative limiting current (~-5 mA·cm⁻²), performing comparably to or better than Pt/C (black, onset ~0.8 V, limiting current ~-5 mA·cm⁻²), while NPC-800 (green) has the least negative shift, lowest onset potential (~0.65–0.7 V), and smallest limiting current magnitude (~-3 mA·cm⁻²), indicating it is the weakest ORR catalyst among the set; NPC-900 (blue) is intermediate, with onset near 0.75 V and limiting current near -3 mA·cm⁻².
