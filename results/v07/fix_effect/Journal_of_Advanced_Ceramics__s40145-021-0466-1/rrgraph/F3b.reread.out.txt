Panel (b) is a stacked bar chart showing "Nitrogen species content (at%)" on the y-axis (0–6) for four samples on the x-axis: NPC-800, NPC-900, NPC-1000, and NC-1000. This is XPS-derived compositional data (deconvolution of N 1s spectra into nitrogen species), not a microscopy image — there is no scale bar. Each bar is segmented into four colored components (bottom to top): Pyridinic N (red), Pyrrolic N (green), Graphitic N (blue), and Oxidized N (purple), per the legend.

Trend: total nitrogen content decreases from NPC-800 (~5.9 at%, the tallest bar) to NPC-900 (~3.8 at%) to NPC-1000 (~1.8 at%, the lowest), then increases slightly for NC-1000 (~2.3 at%). Pyrrolic N is the dominant species in NPC-800 (largest green segment), while pyridinic N (red) becomes relatively more prominent as a fraction of the total in the lower-temperature-derived or NC-1000 samples. Graphitic N (blue) content is relatively stable to slightly increasing in relative proportion at higher pyrolysis temperatures (NPC-1000, NC-1000), consistent with the known trend of pyrrolic/pyridinic N converting to graphitic N with increasing carbonization temperature.
