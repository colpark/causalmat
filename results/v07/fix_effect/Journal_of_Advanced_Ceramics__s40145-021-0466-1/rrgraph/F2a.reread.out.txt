This is a composite of three scanning electron microscopy (SEM) images, labeled (a), showing surface morphology of three samples: "NPC-800", "NPC-900", and "NPC-1000" (likely nanoporous/porous carbon materials pyrolyzed at 800, 900, and 1000 °C). Each panel has a white scale bar reading "1 μm" in the bottom-left corner. The leftmost panel (NPC-800) is annotated with labels "flakes" (top-left, pointing to thin sheet-like structures) and "spheres" (center, pointing to small rounded particles), showing a mixed morphology of aggregated spherical nanoparticles interspersed with some flake-like debris and voids/pores. NPC-1000 shows the spherical particle background largely obscured/overlaid by larger, wrinkled, crumpled flake or sheet-like structures draping across the upper right portion of the image, suggesting a shift toward flake/sheet morphology at higher temperature. Overall trend across the series: increasing pyrolysis temperature correlates with a visible increase in sheet/flake-like features relative to the spherical particle morphology.
