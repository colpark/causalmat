Panel F4c is a chronoamperometric (i-t) durability plot: relative current density (%) vs. time (0-8 h) in O2-saturated 0.1 M KOH at 0.35 V, 1600 rpm, for NPC-1000 (red) and Pt/C (black). It clearly supports the durability portion of the claim: NPC-1000 retains a higher relative current (~75-77% at 8 h) than Pt/C (~70%), and NPC-1000's curve stays above Pt/C's across essentially the whole 8 h window, so "holds its ORR current advantage over Pt/C across the 8 h test" is supported.

However, the panel shows no methanol addition/injection event (no spike or discontinuity in either curve), so it does not demonstrate methanol tolerance at all — there is no visual evidence in F4c that methanol was introduced or that NPC-1000 was unaffected while Pt/C dropped 35-40%. That specific quantitative methanol-tolerance behavior must come from a different panel/experiment not shown here. So the claim as stated conflates two distinct results: the 8 h durability advantage (supported by F4c) and methanol tolerance (not shown/supported by F4c).
