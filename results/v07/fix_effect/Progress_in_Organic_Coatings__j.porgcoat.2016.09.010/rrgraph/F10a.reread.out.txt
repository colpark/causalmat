Panel (a) is a Bode plot from electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), showing log(f/Hz) on the x-axis (-2 to 4) against two y-axes: log|Z| (ohm·cm², left, 0–6) and -Phase Angle (deg, right, 0–90). No scale bar is present (not a micrograph).
