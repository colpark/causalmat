Axes: x-axis "Time (min)" from 0 to 60; y-axis "Turbidity (NTU)" from 0 to 6000. Five curves are plotted for different coagulant samples: Al (triangles), Si-Al-1 (x markers), Si-Al-2 (asterisks), Si-Al-3 (filled circles), and Si-Ce-Al (filled squares), each with data points at t = 0, 15, 30, 45, and 60 min connected by smoothed lines. All curves show turbidity decreasing monotonically with time. The Al curve starts highest (~3750 NTU) and decays more slowly, ending near 1250 NTU at 60 min, while the other four curves (Si-Al-1, Si-Al-2, Si-Al-3, Si-Ce-Al) start lower (~1500–2000 NTU) and converge to low values (~400–600 NTU) by 60 min, indicating faster/more effective turbidity removal for the Si- and Ce-modified coagulants compared to plain Al.
