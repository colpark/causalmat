This is an X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) narrow-scan spectrum, labeled panel (b), showing the Al 2p core-level region. The x-axis is Binding Energy (eV), spanning roughly 73–83 eV, and the y-axis is Intensity (arbitrary units, 0–80). The raw data (gray noisy trace) is overlaid with a peak-fitted envelope (solid black curve) that is deconvoluted into two Gaussian/Voigt component peaks shown as dashed colored curves: a smaller peak centered near 75–76 eV assigned to Al2O3 (oxidized aluminum, blue dashed) and a larger, dominant peak centered near 78 eV assigned to metallic Al (red dashed). The metallic Al peak has much higher intensity (~70) than the Al2O3 oxide peak (~17), indicating the sample is predominantly metallic aluminum with a smaller oxide component. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a spectral plot); the plot uses standard axis tick marks/gridlines instead.
