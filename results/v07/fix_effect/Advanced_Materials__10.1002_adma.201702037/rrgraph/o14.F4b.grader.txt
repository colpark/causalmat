Panel (b) shows four X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) plots comparing individual components (MoS2, PEGSH) with the crosslinked PEGSH/(2% MoS2) hydrogel ("Gel"), arranged as two columns: Mo 3d region (left) and C 1s region (right).

Top-left: MoS2 alone, Mo 3d region (Binding Energy 226–240 eV), showing two deconvoluted peaks — Mo 3d5/2 (~229 eV) and Mo 3d3/2 (~232 eV), each fitted with a single shaded Gaussian/Lorentzian component under a dashed envelope curve.

Top-right: PEGSH alone, C 1s region (Binding Energy 281–290 eV), with a broad dashed envelope deconvoluted into three shaded components labeled sp2 (~283 eV), C-C (~285 eV), and C-O/C-S (~286–288 eV, two overlapping peaks labeled C-O and C-S).

Bottom-left: the crosslinked Gel, Mo 3d region, showing a more complex deconvolution with four shaded peaks labeled (MoS2-PEG) at low binding energy (~227 eV), two (MoS2) peaks (~229–231 eV, shifted/broadened relative to pristine MoS2), and a new peak labeled (MoS2-PEG) at higher binding energy (~235 eV), indicating a shifted chemical environment upon crosslinking.

Bottom-right: the crosslinked Gel, C 1s region, again deconvoluted into sp2, C-C, C-O peaks similar to pristine PEGSH but with an added/shifted component labeled C-S (MoS2-PEG) around 286–288 eV, suggesting new bonding interactions between MoS2 and PEG in the gel.

All four subplots have y-axis "Intensity (a.u)" and x-axis "Binding Energy (eV)"; there is no scale bar (not a microscopy image), and no numerical intensity values are given — only relative peak shapes/positions with dashed raw-data envelopes and shaded deconvoluted fit components. The overall trend across the panel is a comparison showing how binding energies and peak shapes shift/broaden/split upon crosslinking of PEGSH with MoS2 into a hydrogel, interpreted as evidence of new chemical bonding (MoS2-PEG interactions) not present in the pure components.
