This is a photographic panel (labeled "(a)"), not a microscopy technique, showing four digital camera images of a black, bow-tie/dogbone-shaped composite film labeled "PEGSH/2%MoS2 (as prepared)". The top-left image shows the as-prepared flat sample laid on a ruler marked in centimeters (0–6 cm) for scale, used to indicate sample size (roughly 3–4 cm long). The remaining three images ("Bending," "Stretching," "Twisting") show the same black film being mechanically deformed by hand/tweezers into a tight loop, stretched into an elongated strip (also over a cm ruler, showing it stretches to ~5+ cm), and twisted into a helical shape, respectively. No quantitative data, error bars, or trends are plotted — the panel qualitatively demonstrates the material's flexibility and mechanical deformability (bendable, stretchable, twistable) rather than presenting measurement results.
