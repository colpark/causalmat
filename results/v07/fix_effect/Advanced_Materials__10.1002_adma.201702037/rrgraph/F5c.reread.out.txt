The panel (labeled "(c)") shows a "Cell Cycle Analysis" stacked bar chart, not a microscopy/imaging technique — it is a flow-cytometry-derived quantitative plot with no scale bar. The x-axis is "MoS2 Concentration (µg/mL)" with four categories: 0, 10, 100, and 1000. The y-axis is "Percent Population (%)" from 0 to 100. Each bar is stacked into four cell-cycle stages (legend at right): G1 (light blue, bottom), S (medium blue), G2/M (dark blue), and Apoptosis (dark red, top). Trend: as MoS2 concentration increases from 0 to 1000 µg/mL, the G1 fraction decreases modestly (~70% to ~63%) while the Apoptosis fraction increases markedly, from near 0% at 0 and 10 µg/mL to a small band at 100 µg/mL and a substantially larger red segment (roughly 15-20% of the bar) at 1000 µg/mL, indicating dose-dependent induction of apoptosis with increasing MoS2 concentration.
