Panel (f), labeled "Stress Sweep," is a rheology plot showing storage modulus G' (filled circles) and loss modulus G'' (open circles) versus oscillatory shear stress (Pa), on log-log axes (G', G'' from 10^-2 to 10^4 Pa; stress from 10^-1 to 10^3 Pa). Two MoS2 concentrations are compared: 2% MoS2 (black symbols) and 0.25% MoS2 (gray symbols). For both concentrations G' is roughly flat/plateau at low stress and higher than G'' (indicating gel-like/solid-like behavior), then both moduli drop sharply and G'' crosses above G' at a "crossover point" marking the yield/flow transition — labeled at ~50 Pa for the 2% MoS2 sample and ~5 Pa for the 0.25% MoS2 sample. No scale bar is present (this is a graph, not a micrograph); the trend shows the higher MoS2 concentration sample withstands roughly an order of magnitude higher stress before yielding than the lower concentration sample.
