Panel (c) shows characterization of a PEGSH/2%MoS2 porous scaffold/composite material, combining SEM imaging with EDS elemental mapping and spectroscopy, arranged in a 2x2 grid.

Top row: two SEM micrographs of the PEGSH/2%MoS2 sample at different magnifications, both showing an irregular, sponge-like/foam porous microstructure with interconnected struts and large void spaces. The left image (10 μm scale bar) shows a lower-magnification overview with a dashed white box indicating the region enlarged in the right image; the right image (5 μm scale bar) shows a higher-magnification close-up revealing rougher, wrinkled/folded surface texture on the pore walls and some rounded nodular features.

Bottom-right: an EDS spectrum plot (Intensity a.u. vs Energy in keV, 0 to ~3.5 keV) showing a small carbon (C) peak near 0.3 keV, a small platinum (Pt) peak around 2.0 keV (likely from sputter coating), and a dominant overlapping peak near 2.3 keV with arrows labeled "Mo (Lα)" and "S (Kα)" pointing to it, confirming the presence of Mo and S in the sample consistent with MoS2 incorporation.
