Two MoS2 concentrations are compared: 2% MoS2 (black symbols) and 0.25% MoS2 (gray symbols). For both concentrations G' is roughly flat/plateau at low stress and higher than G'' (indicating gel-like/solid-like behavior), then both moduli drop sharply and G'' crosses above G' at a "crossover point" marking the yield/flow transition — labeled at ~50 Pa for the 2% MoS2 sample and ~5 Pa for the 0.25% MoS2 sample.
