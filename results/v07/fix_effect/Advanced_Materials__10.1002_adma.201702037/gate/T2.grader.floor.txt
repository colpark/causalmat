Question: The claim under test says PEG thiols chemisorb at MoS2 vacancy sites, forming C-S-Mo crosslinks. Looking at the Mo 3d XPS panel (F4b) for the crosslinked gel, does the fitted spectrum support this claim?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): No. The fitted gel Mo 3d components do not form 3.1 eV doublets (the spin-orbit pairs that should sit ~1.6 and ~6.8 eV apart); the 235.3 eV peak instead sits at Mo6+ 3d3/2, not a signature of a new C-S-Mo crosslink (o14).
