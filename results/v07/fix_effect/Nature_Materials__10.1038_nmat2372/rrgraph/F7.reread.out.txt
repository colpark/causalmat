Panel shows differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) traces, plotting heat flow (mW mg⁻¹, exo up) versus temperature (80–300 °C), for four different lithiated/delithiated electrode materials stacked with vertical offsets and broken y-axis segments to fit varying signal magnitudes. The top (green) curve, Li_xC_6 (x=1, lithiated graphite), shows the largest exothermic response with peaks at 148, 171, and 195 °C and an integrated heat release of −709 J g⁻¹. The blue curve, Li_{2+x}C_8H_4O_4 (x=2), has a sharp peak near 140 °C releasing −245 J g⁻¹ with a smaller shoulder near 195 °C. The black curve, Li_{4+x}Ti_5O_12 (x=3), shows two peaks (~140 °C and ~210 °C) releasing −95 and −71 J g⁻¹ respectively. The bottom red curve, Li_{2+x}C_6H_4O_4 (x=-1), has the weakest response, with small peaks releasing −53 and −19 J g⁻¹. Shaded/hatched regions under each curve indicate the integrated exothermic heat, and no scale bar is present (axes are quantitative, not micrographic); overall the trend shows decreasing thermal reactivity (lower exothermic heat release) from the lithiated graphite anode material down to the other lithiated/organic and titanate compounds.
