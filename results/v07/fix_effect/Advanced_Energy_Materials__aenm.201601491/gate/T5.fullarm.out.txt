Panel F3d shows both the Fe K-edge energy (top, at normalized intensity 0.5) and the V pre-edge energy (bottom, at the pre-edge peak) plotted against normalized charge capacity (C/C1.15V), from 0 to 1.0.

Both edge energies shift monotonically upward as the electrode is charged:
- Fe: rises from ~7124.6 eV near the bottom of the Fe2+/Fe3+ range at low charge capacity to ~7125.7 eV, approaching the Fe3+ reference line, at full charge.
- V: rises from ~5469.8 eV (near the V3+/V4+ boundary) at low charge capacity to ~5470.5 eV (near the V4+/V5+ boundary) at full charge.

This positive shift in absorption-edge/pre-edge energy for both elements during charging indicates that both Fe and V are oxidized as the electrode is charged (electrons removed, consistent with Na+ deextraction), i.e., the charge capacity is supported by simultaneous, roughly parallel oxidation of both the Fe and V redox centers in the V/Fe PBA framework, rather than by only one metal center — consistent with the paper's goal of achieving higher capacity than earlier PBAs.
