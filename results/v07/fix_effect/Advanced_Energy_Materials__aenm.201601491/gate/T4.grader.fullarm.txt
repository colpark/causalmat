Candidate answer:
Panel F3c (V K-edge XAS, panel C) shows a pre-edge feature (marked by triangular arrowheads) around 5470-5472 eV in each spectrum. Reading top to bottom, the arrowhead position shifts progressively to lower X-ray energy as the applied potential decreases: it sits farthest right (highest energy, closest to the V2O5 reference) at 1.15 V, and moves stepwise leftward through 0.90 V, 0.60 V, and 0.45 V, ending closest to the VO2 and V2O3 reference positions (lowest energy) at 0.45 V. So the sample pre-edge position at 1.15 V is closer to/aligned with the V2O5 reference, while at 0.45 V it is closer to the VO2/V2O3 reference positions — the two potentials (0.45 V vs 1.15 V, as an example pair) show a visible difference, with the pre-edge shifted to lower energy at the lower potential relative to the higher potential. This is a gradual, monotonic shift across all four intermediate potentials shown (1.15, 0.90, 0.60, 0.45 V), not an abrupt jump.
