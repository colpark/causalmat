Panel F3b (image: aenm.201601491 panel B) shows Fe K-edge XAS spectra stacked with arrowheads marking the pre-edge/edge peak position for FeO, 0.45 V, 0.60 V, 0.90 V, 1.15 V, and Fe2O3 (bottom to top). The arrowhead positions progressively shift toward higher X-ray energy as potential increases: the 0.45 V arrow sits closest to the FeO reference position, and the peak position shifts rightward through 0.60 V, 0.90 V, to 1.15 V, which sits closest to the Fe2O3 reference. So the four potentials are NOT the same — the Fe K-edge position moves progressively to higher energy (from near-FeO toward near-Fe2O3) as charge potential increases from 0.45 to 1.15 V. This is a directional shift, not "no visible difference."
