Panel (e) shows STEM-EDS elemental mapping across four sub-panels, each with a 25 nm scale bar. The leftmost sub-panel is a high-angle annular dark-field (HAADF) STEM image showing several bright, rounded/spherical particles (nanoparticles or grains, tens of nm in diameter) sitting against a darker background, imaged near an edge/interface. The second sub-panel is the Bi elemental map (blue signal), the third is the O elemental map (cyan/teal signal), and the fourth is the merged Bi+O overlay map. The O map shows a more uniform, dense cyan speckle distributed across nearly the entire field, including regions where Bi signal is weak, and the merged map confirms overlap of Bi and O in the upper region while showing O-dominant (little Bi) signal in the lower/darker region — consistent with a Bi-containing phase (e.g., oxide/particle) sitting on or embedded in an O-rich (or oxide) matrix/interface.
