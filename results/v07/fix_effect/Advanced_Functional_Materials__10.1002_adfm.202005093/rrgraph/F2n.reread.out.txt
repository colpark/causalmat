Panel (n) shows a Tauc plot used to determine optical band gap from UV-Vis diffuse reflectance/absorbance data. The plot is (F(∞)hν)^2 in eV^-2 (y-axis, 0 to 2.0) versus photon energy in eV (x-axis, 0.5 to 2.5). A blue curve traces the measured data, rising monotonically and showing a change in slope around 1.5-2 eV. A red straight line is fit to the linear region of the curve and extrapolated down to the x-axis (y=0), intersecting it at approximately 0.69 eV, which is labeled with an arrow and the text "0.69 eV" — this is the extracted optical band gap value. No scale bar is present (this is a plot, not a micrograph); a legend in the upper area identifies the blue curve as "Tauc plot."
