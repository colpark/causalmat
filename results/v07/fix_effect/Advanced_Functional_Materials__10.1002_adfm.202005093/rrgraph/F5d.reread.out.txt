Panel (d) is a set of six line-graph subplots showing individual-mouse tumor growth curves over time for an in vivo antitumor efficacy study (not microscopy/imaging technique — this is quantitative/graphical data, no scale bar present since it is not an image-based panel).

Each subplot has axes "Tumor volume (mm³)" (y, 0–1500) versus "Time (day)" (x, 0–~20), with multiple colored lines representing individual animals/tumors within each treatment group. The six groups, left to right, are: PBS (orange), BNs-BSA (purple), Lasers (green), BNs-BSA + 660 nm (yellow), BNs-BSA + 808 nm (blue), and BNs-BSA + Lasers (red).

Trend: the PBS, BNs-BSA, Lasers, and BNs-BSA+660 nm groups all show steep, largely unimpeded tumor growth curves reaching or exceeding 1000–1500 mm³ within about 15–20 days, indicating no meaningful therapeutic effect from these single/control treatments. The BNs-BSA+808 nm group shows markedly suppressed growth, with tumor volumes staying near zero for the first ~15 days before a modest late regrowth to ~250–700 mm³ in some animals. The BNs-BSA+Lasers (combination, both wavelengths) group shows near-complete tumor growth suppression across all animals for the full ~20-day observation period, indicating the strongest therapeutic effect among the six conditions.
