Panel (I): Raman spectroscopy comparison plot. It shows Raman shift (cm⁻¹, x-axis, 0–~550) versus intensity (a.u., y-axis, arbitrary units, no numeric scale) for three samples overlaid: bismuthene oxide (blue, top, offset upward), bulk Bi (green, middle), and bismuthene (orange, bottom). No scale bar (not a micrograph; this is a spectral plot with axis units instead).

Trends: All three curves show two sharp low-frequency peaks near ~65-70 cm⁻¹ and ~95-100 cm⁻¹ (marked with red dashed vertical guide lines), consistent with A1g and Eg Raman-active phonon modes of bismuth/bismuthene. The bulk Bi (green) and bismuthene (orange) traces are relatively flat and featureless above ~150 cm⁻¹, with orange (bismuthene) showing the weakest/broadest low-frequency peaks and least additional structure, suggesting a thinner/more exfoliated or oxidized-suppressed signal. The bismuthene oxide (blue) trace, offset to higher apparent intensity, shows the same two low-frequency peaks plus several additional broad humps/bands at higher Raman shift (roughly 150-200 cm⁻¹, ~250-300 cm⁻¹, ~330-400 cm⁻¹, and ~480-530 cm⁻¹), consistent with additional vibrational modes introduced by oxidation (e.g., Bi-O bonding features) not present or much weaker in the pristine bulk Bi and bismuthene spectra.
