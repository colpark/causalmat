Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot, not a micrograph — no scale bar is present. It plots pH (y-axis, 6.0–10.0) versus immersion time in days (x-axis, 0–28) for a sample labeled "HP-Mg," comparing two immersion media: GS (black squares/line) and SBF (red diamonds/line). Both curves start high (GS ~8.4, SBF ~7.8) and decline over the first ~6–8 days before leveling off. After the initial drop, the SBF curve stabilizes and gently oscillates around pH 7.3–7.5 for the remainder of the 28-day period, staying fairly flat. The GS curve is noisier, dipping as low as ~7.0 around day 13 and again toward day 25, with a local peak near day 19 (~7.4), overall trending slightly lower than the SBF curve after day ~14 and ending around pH 7.0–7.1 at day 27–28.
