Panel (d) is an EDS/EDX (energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy) point/area spectrum, not a micrograph. The x-axis is energy in keV (0 to ~11), and the y-axis (unlabeled, no scale shown) is X-ray counts/intensity. Four labeled peaks appear: a small Ca peak near 0 keV, a Mg peak at roughly 1.2-1.3 keV, a dominant Al peak at roughly 1.4-1.5 keV (the tallest, sharpest peak in the spectrum), and a second, smaller Ca peak near 3.6-3.7 keV. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a spectrum plot), and the trend/finding conveyed is simply the relative elemental composition detected at the analyzed spot/region — Al and Mg as major constituents with minor Ca content, consistent with an Al-Mg based alloy or coating with trace Ca.
