Panel (c) is an EDS (energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy) point/area spectrum, plotted as X-ray intensity (counts, y-axis, unlabeled/arbitrary) versus energy in keV (x-axis, 0–~11 keV). Four labeled peaks appear: two overlapping low-energy peaks near ~0.3–0.5 keV labeled "Ca" (likely Ca L-line plus possibly O or C overlap) and "Mg"/"Al" together, a very tall sharp peak just after 1 keV that is the dominant Al K-line with Mg K-line as a shoulder/smaller peak just before it (~1.25 and ~1.49 keV region), and a smaller Ca K-line peak near ~3.7 keV. No scale bar (not a micrograph). The spectrum shows Al as the overwhelmingly dominant element by intensity, with much weaker Mg and Ca signals, consistent with an Al-rich phase or matrix containing minor Mg and Ca. Baseline is flat/near-zero beyond ~5 keV out to 11 keV, indicating no heavier elements detected in this energy range.
