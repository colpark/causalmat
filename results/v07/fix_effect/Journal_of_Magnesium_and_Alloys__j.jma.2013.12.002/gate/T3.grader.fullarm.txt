Given the 2 µm scale bar in F2b, the individual particles are quite fine—roughly submicron to about 1–2 µm across—rather than large discrete blocks, and they appear as clusters/strings of fine particles rather than single coarse precipitates. This distribution (fine particles decorating alpha/beta boundaries) is consistent with the stated hypothesis that Ca addition forms Al2Ca particles that nucleate at/along the alpha-Mg–beta-Li phase boundaries to refine the duplex structure. Overall: boundary-segregated, chain-like distribution of fine (sub-micron to ~1–2 µm) particles, not a uniform or bulk dispersion through the grains.
