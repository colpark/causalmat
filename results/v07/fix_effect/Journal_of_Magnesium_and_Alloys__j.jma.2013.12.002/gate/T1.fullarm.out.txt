In panel F1c, the XRD pattern for the as-cast alloy is indexed using two symbols per the legend: filled inverted triangles (▼) labeled "α-Mg" and open inverted triangles (▽) labeled "β-Li." This is consistent with the panel showing the base LA91 (Mg-9Li-1Al, no Ca) as-cast alloy, whose duplex microstructure is composed of α-Mg and β-Li phases only.
