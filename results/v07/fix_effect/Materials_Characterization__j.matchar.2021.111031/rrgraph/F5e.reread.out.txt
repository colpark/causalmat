Panel (e) is a Nyquist plot from electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS), showing -Z'' (imaginary impedance, in KOhm) on the y-axis versus Z' (real impedance, in KOhm) on the x-axis, both ranging roughly from 0 to 45 KOhm. Two datasets are plotted: black filled circles labeled "BCN" and red filled squares labeled "CNS." The BCN curve lies consistently above the CNS curve at all Z' values, reaching a higher plateau (~33-34 KOhm) compared to CNS (~22-23 KOhm), indicating BCN has higher impedance response than CNS across the measured frequency range.
