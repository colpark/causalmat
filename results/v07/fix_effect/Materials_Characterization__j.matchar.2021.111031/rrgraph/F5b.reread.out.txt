Panel (b) shows electron paramagnetic/spin resonance (EPR/ESR) spectra used to detect hydroxyl radicals via a spin-trapping technique (likely DMPO-OH adduct), labeled "Hydroxyl radical" at top. The x-axis is Magnetic Field (G) spanning ~3460–3560 G; the y-axis is Intensity (a.u.), with no numeric scale/tick values shown and no physical length scale bar (not applicable to this spectroscopic plot type). Three stacked traces are shown, offset vertically for clarity: black "Dark" (flat, no signal, baseline noise only), red "5 min" (moderate four-line characteristic hyperfine splitting pattern with 1:2:2:1 intensity ratio typical of DMPO-OH), and blue "10 min" (same four-line pattern but with markedly higher peak intensities than the 5 min trace). The trend indicates that hydroxyl radical signal is absent in the dark control and increases with irradiation/reaction time from 5 to 10 minutes, consistent with progressive photocatalytic or reaction-driven generation of hydroxyl radicals over time.
