This panel is a Western blot image showing two stacked blot strips for three sample lanes labeled "MBG", "2Co-MBG", and "5Co-MBG" (left to right). The upper strip is probed for HIF-1α at a molecular weight marker of 120 kDa, showing a faint band in the MBG lane that increases in intensity progressively through 2Co-MBG to the strongest, sharpest band in 5Co-MBG, indicating a dose-dependent increase in HIF-1α expression with increasing cobalt content. The lower strip is the loading control α-Tubulin at 50 kDa, showing three bands of comparable, consistently strong intensity across all three lanes, confirming equal protein loading. No scale bar is present (not applicable to blot images); molecular weight markers (120 kDa, 50 kDa) are given instead, placed to the left of each strip, with protein/antibody labels (HIF-1α, α-Tubulin) to the right.
