Panel "b" is a pore-size distribution plot (dV/dD in cm³/g vs. pore size in nm, 2–30 nm range), most likely derived from BJH analysis of N2 adsorption-desorption isotherms for mesoporous bioactive glass (MBG) samples. Three curves are plotted with square markers: 0Co-MBG (black), 2Co-MBG (red/dark red), and 5Co-MBG (green), representing cobalt-doped MBG at 0, 2, and 5 mol% (or wt%) Co. All three curves show a peak in the 3–6 nm range followed by a long tail decaying toward larger pore sizes out to 30 nm; 0Co-MBG has the broadest peak (~5 nm, dV/dD ~0.0107) and highest tail values at large pore sizes, while 5Co-MBG has the sharpest, tallest peak (~4–5 nm, dV/dD ~0.0162) but drops to the lowest dV/dD values beyond ~8 nm, and 2Co-MBG is intermediate with a lower, broader peak (~0.006) and a moderate tail. No scale bar is present (this is a graph, not a micrograph). The trend suggests Co doping narrows the pore size distribution and shifts more pore volume into a concentrated small-pore range, at the expense of larger mesopores present in the undoped sample.
