This is a nitrogen adsorption-desorption isotherm plot (labeled panel "a"), not a micrograph. X-axis is "Relative pressure (P/P0)" from 0 to 1.0; y-axis is "Pore volume (mmol/g)" from 0 to 10. Three data series are plotted with distinct markers/colors: black squares "0Co-MBG", red circles "2Co-MBG", and green triangles "5Co-MBG" (MBG likely mesoporous bioactive glass, Co = cobalt-doped variants), each shown as a full adsorption/desorption loop (hysteresis visible, especially in the 0.4-0.6 relative pressure range where curves show a steep rise/step).

Trends: the 0Co-MBG sample has the highest overall pore volume (rising from ~1.5 to ~8.7 mmol/g) and shows a pronounced steep uptake step around P/P0 = 0.45-0.55 with clear hysteresis between adsorption and desorption branches, indicative of mesoporous capillary condensation. The 2Co-MBG (red) and 5Co-MBG (green) curves have progressively lower pore volumes (max ~5.6 and ~4.4 mmol/g respectively) and their steep uptake steps are shifted to a similar P/P0 range but with smaller hysteresis loops, suggesting Co doping reduces both total pore volume and mesopore ordering/size. There is no scale bar (not applicable to a graph), no magnification value — this is a quantitative plot, not an image-based micrograph panel.
