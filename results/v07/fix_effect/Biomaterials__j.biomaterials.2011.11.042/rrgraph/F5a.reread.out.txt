Panel (a) is a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image — it shows Co2+ ion release concentration (mg/L, y-axis, 0–25) versus soaking time in days (x-axis, 0–8) for three mesoporous bioactive glass (MBG) samples with different cobalt doping levels: 0Co-MBG (black squares), 2Co-MBG (red circles), and 5Co-MBG (green triangles). No scale bar or imaging technique is present, since this is a quantitative release/dissolution plot (likely ICP measurement of soaked samples), not an electron/optical micrograph. Trends: 0Co-MBG shows essentially flat/zero Co2+ release across all time points (0–7 days), consistent with no cobalt content. 2Co-MBG and 5Co-MBG both show a rapid rise in Co2+ concentration from day 0 to day 3, then a slower, near-plateauing increase from day 3 to day 7. 5Co-MBG releases consistently more Co2+ than 2Co-MBG at every time point (reaching ~21 mg/L vs ~17 mg/L by day 7), and both curves are still trending slightly upward at the final (day 7) time point rather than fully plateaued.
