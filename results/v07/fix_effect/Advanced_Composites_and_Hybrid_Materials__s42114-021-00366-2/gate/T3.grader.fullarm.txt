Question: The text says dielectric loss stays low (below 0.026 in the text), increasing only slightly as Ag content rises. Does the dielectric-loss panel (F6b) support this for BST@Ag1%?

Answer key (answer_scope: full - the given panels support the whole answer): No. On the BST@Ag1% curve in F6b, loss climbs above 100 kHz and reaches about 0.065 at 1 MHz, and low-frequency losses already exceed 0.026 (o16).

Candidate answer:
Panel F6b (dielectric loss vs frequency, 100 Hz–1 MHz) shows BST@Ag1% (teal line) starting near 0.032 at 100 Hz, dipping to a minimum of about 0.013–0.014 around 10–30 kHz, then rising sharply at high frequency to roughly 0.065 at 1 MHz — well above the stated <0.026 threshold. This is the opposite of "only slight increase": BST@Ag1% shows by far the steepest high-frequency rise of any curve in the panel, while Ag3% and Ag5% (orange/red) stay flat near 0.013–0.015 up to 1 MHz. So the panel does not support the text's claim for BST@Ag1%; it contradicts it, since Ag1% loss exceeds 0.026 at high frequencies and rises much more than the higher-Ag-content samples, even though the higher-Ag curves do fit the "low and only slightly increasing" description.
