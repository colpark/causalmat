Panel 6b (F6b/dielectric loss vs frequency, 100 Hz–1 MHz) shows dielectric loss for Pure PAEN, BST@Ag1%, BST@Ag3%, and BST@Ag5%. Loss values are low (roughly 0.01–0.04) and relatively flat across most of the range for all four curves, and the 3% and 5% Ag composites stay essentially stable (~0.012–0.038) all the way to 1 MHz. However, the BST@Ag1% curve rises sharply above ~100 kHz, reaching ~0.065 near 1 MHz — a clear deviation from stability for that composition. So the panel partially supports the frequency-stability claim (true for pure PAEN and the 3%/5% composites, not fully true for 1% Ag). Critically, this panel only shows frequency-dependence at presumably one temperature; it contains no temperature axis or data, so it provides no evidence for the "stable up to ~135–140 °C" portion of the claim. Overall: partial support for the frequency-stability part (with a notable exception at high frequency for BST@Ag1%), and no support/evidence at all for the temperature-stability part of the claim.
