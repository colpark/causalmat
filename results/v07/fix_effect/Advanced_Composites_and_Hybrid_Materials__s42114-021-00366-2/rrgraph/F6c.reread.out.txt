Panel (c) is a line plot, not a microscopy image — it shows dielectric constant versus temperature (26–180 °C) for four samples: Pure PAEN (gray), BST@Ag1% (teal/blue), BST@Ag3% (orange), and BST@Ag5% (red). No scale bar is present since this is a graph, not a micrograph. All four curves start near room temperature with dielectric constant values of roughly 3–5 and remain fairly flat/slowly rising up to about 130–140 °C, after which each curve rises sharply toward 180 °C. At 180 °C, the values reach approximately 11 (Pure PAEN, lowest), 17 (BST@Ag1%), and about 19–20 (BST@Ag3% and BST@Ag5%, which are highest and nearly overlapping at the high-temperature end). The overall trend shows that Ag-doped BST composites have consistently higher dielectric constants than pure PAEN across the whole temperature range, with the enhancement most pronounced near the high-temperature upturn, and BST@Ag3% and BST@Ag5% performing similarly and above BST@Ag1%.
