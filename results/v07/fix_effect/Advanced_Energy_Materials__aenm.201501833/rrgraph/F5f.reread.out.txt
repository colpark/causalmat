This is an electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) Nyquist plot, showing -Z'' (Ohm) on the y-axis (0–12) versus Z' (Ohm) on the x-axis (0–14). Two data series are overlaid: black squares labeled "MOF" and red circles labeled "MOF derived NiₓPᵧOᵤ," each with a smooth fitted curve through the points. Both traces show the characteristic Nyquist semicircle-to-diffusion-tail shape, starting near the origin and curving upward as Z' increases, consistent with charge-transfer resistance followed by a Warburg diffusion region. The red "MOF derived NiₓPᵧOᵤ" curve rises higher and extends further along Z' (reaching ~12 at Z'~14) compared to the black "MOF" curve, which plateaus lower (~4 at Z'~10), indicating a difference in impedance characteristics between the two materials. An inset in the upper left, labeled "(f)," shows a magnified view of the low-Z' region (near the origin) for both curves, likely highlighting the smaller semicircle/charge-transfer feature not resolvable at the main plot's scale. There is no scale bar (not applicable to this type of plot, which is a graph rather than a micrograph).
