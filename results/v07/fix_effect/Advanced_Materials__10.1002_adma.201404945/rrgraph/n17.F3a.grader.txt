Panel (a): a line/scatter plot, not a microscopy image (no scale bar, no imaging technique). It plots ON/OFF ratio (log scale, y-axis, ranging from ~1 to >100) versus QD (quantum dot) concentration in wt% (x-axis, 0–60 wt%, sampled at 0, 20, 40, and 60 wt%). Nine curves are shown, each corresponding to a different measurement wavelength (365, 400, 455, 530, 625, 680, 740, 850, and 940 nm), distinguished by color per the legend, with error bars at each data point. All curves trend upward with increasing QD concentration, i.e., ON/OFF ratio increases as QD wt% increases. The 625 nm curve (red) has the highest ON/OFF ratio at every concentration, reaching roughly 150–200 at 60 wt%, followed closely by 365 nm (purple), 400 nm (dark blue) and 455 nm (blue), all clustering in the 70–120 range at 60 wt%; 530 nm (cyan) is somewhat lower (~75 at 60 wt%). The longer-wavelength curves (680, 740, 850, 940 nm) form a distinctly lower cluster, rising only to roughly 4–18 at 60 wt%, with 940 nm (orange) the lowest throughout and missing data points at 0 wt%.
