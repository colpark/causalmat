Panel (b) is a pore-size distribution plot from gas-sorption analysis (likely N2 physisorption, e.g., NLDFT/BJH-derived dV(d)), showing incremental pore volume dV(d) (cm3 nm-1 g-1) on the y-axis (0 to ~1.2) versus pore diameter (nm) on the x-axis (0.5 to 6 nm, log-like/expanded low-diameter scale). Four carbon samples are compared: S-carbon (light gray, open circles, solid line), L-carbon (dark gray/black, filled triangles, solid line), WS-carbon (light gray, dashed line), and WL-carbon (dark gray, dashed line with markers).

All four curves show a dominant sharp micropore peak near ~1 nm pore diameter, with S-carbon showing the tallest peak (~1.2 cm3/nm/g), followed by L-carbon and WL-carbon (~0.6-0.65), and WS-carbon slightly lower/broader. After the ~1 nm peak, curves drop steeply by ~1.5-2 nm. S-carbon and L-carbon then show a distinct secondary broad mesopore hump centered around 3-4 nm (S-carbon peaking near 0.3 at ~3.5 nm, L-carbon a smaller bump near 0.1 at ~3.5-4 nm), while WS-carbon and WL-carbon (the "W" variants) show little to no secondary mesopore feature, remaining flat near zero beyond 2 nm. This indicates the W-treated/washed carbons lost or lack the mesoporosity present in the untreated S- and L-carbons, while retaining similar or reduced microporosity.
