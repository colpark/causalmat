Two curves are shown, each with adsorption and desorption branches forming a hysteresis loop: a light gray/open-circle curve labeled "AS-carbon" with surface area 1075 m²/g, reaching ~420-430 cm³/g at P/P0 = 1.0, and a darker gray/filled-circle curve labeled "AL-carbon" with surface area 735 m²/g, reaching ~290-300 cm³/g. The clear trend is that AS-carbon has substantially higher adsorption capacity and surface area than AL-carbon across the entire pressure range.
