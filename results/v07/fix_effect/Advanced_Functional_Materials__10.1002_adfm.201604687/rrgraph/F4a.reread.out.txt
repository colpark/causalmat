Panel (a) is a cyclic voltammetry (CV) plot, x-axis "Potential (V)" from 0.0 to 1.0 V, y-axis "Current density (A g⁻¹)" from about -40 to +40. It overlays CV curves at eight scan rates (5, 10, 20, 50, 80, 100, 200, 500 mV/s), each a different color per the legend, no scale bar (not a micrograph). All curves show a roughly rectangular/quasi-rectangular loop shape typical of electric double-layer capacitive behavior, with slight bulging near the vertex regions (0 and 1 V) rather than perfect rectangles. Curve area/width increases systematically with increasing scan rate: the lowest rates (5–100 mV/s) form tightly nested, low-amplitude rectangles near zero current, while 200 mV/s is visibly wider, and 500 mV/s (orange) is by far the largest loop, reaching roughly ±35–40 A/g at the vertices. The progressive widening with scan rate indicates increasing capacitive current response at higher sweep rates, consistent with supercapacitor-type electrode behavior, and the maintained loop shape across rates suggests reasonably good rate capability/reversibility.
